Sahih al-Bukhari 2498

Book 47: Partnership · Hadith 15

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَاصِمٍ، عَنْ عُثْمَانَ يَعْنِي ابْنَ الأَسْوَدِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ أَبِي مُسْلِمٍ، قَالَ سَأَلْتُ أَبَا الْمِنْهَالِ عَنِ الصَّرْفِ، يَدًا بِيَدٍ فَقَالَ اشْتَرَيْتُ أَنَا وَشَرِيكٌ، لِي شَيْئًا يَدًا بِيَدٍ وَنَسِيئَةً، فَجَاءَنَا الْبَرَاءُ بْنُ عَازِبٍ فَسَأَلْنَاهُ، فَقَالَ فَعَلْتُ أَنَا وَشَرِيكِي زَيْدُ بْنُ أَرْقَمَ، وَسَأَلْنَا النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ ذَلِكَ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ مَا كَانَ يَدًا بِيَدٍ فَخُذُوهُ، وَمَا كَانَ نَسِيئَةً فَذَرُوهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Sulaiman bin Abu Muslim:I asked Abu Minhal about money exchange from hand to hand. He said, "I and a partner of mine bought something partly in cash and partly on credit." Al-Bara' bin `Azib passed by us and we asked about it. He replied, "I and my partner Zaid bin Al-Arqam did the same and then went to the Prophet (ﷺ) and asked him about it. He said, 'Take what was from hand to hand and leave what was on credit

Sulaiman bin Abu Muslim bataate hain: Maine Abu Minhal se haath-se-haath paise ki adla-badli ke baare mein pucha. Unhone kaha: "Main aur mera sajhedaaar ne kuch khareeda tha, kuch nagad aur kuch udhaar par." Al-Bara bin Azib humare paas se guzre aur humne unse iske baare mein pucha. Unhone jawab diya: "Main aur mera sajhedaaar Zaid bin Al-Arqam ne bhi yehi kiya tha aur phir Nabi ke paas gaye aur unse iske baare mein pucha. Unhone kaha: 'Jo haath se haath hua use lo aur jo udhaar par hua use chhod do.'"
