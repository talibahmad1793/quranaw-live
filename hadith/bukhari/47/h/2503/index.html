Sahih al-Bukhari 2503

Book 47: Partnership · Hadith 19

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا جُوَيْرِيَةُ بْنُ أَسْمَاءَ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ أَعْتَقَ شِرْكًا لَهُ فِي مَمْلُوكٍ وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يُعْتِقَ كُلَّهُ، إِنْ كَانَ لَهُ مَالٌ قَدْرَ ثَمَنِهِ يُقَامُ قِيمَةَ عَدْلٍ وَيُعْطَى شُرَكَاؤُهُ حِصَّتَهُمْ وَيُخَلَّى سَبِيلُ الْمُعْتَقِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Whoever manumits his share of a jointly possessed slave, it is imperative on him to manumit the slave completely if he has sufficient money to pay the rest of its price which is to be estimated justly. He should pay his partners their shares and release him (the freed one)

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Jo koi saanjhi milkiyat wale gulaam mein apna hissa azaad karta hai, uske liye zaroori hai ke gulaam ko poori tarah azaad karvaye agar uske paas baaki qeemat ada karne ke liye kaafi paisa hai, jise munasib tareeke se lagaya jaana chahiye. Use apne sajhedaaron ko unka hissa dena chahiye aur use (azaad kiye gulaam ko) chhod dena chahiye."
