Sahih al-Bukhari 2504

Book 47: Partnership · Hadith 20

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو النُّعْمَانِ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرُ بْنُ حَازِمٍ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنِ النَّضْرِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ بَشِيرِ بْنِ نَهِيكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ أَعْتَقَ شِقْصًا لَهُ فِي عَبْدٍ، أُعْتِقَ كُلُّهُ إِنْ كَانَ لَهُ مَالٌ، وَإِلاَّ يُسْتَسْعَ غَيْرَ مَشْقُوقٍ عَلَيْهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Whoever manumits his share of a jointly possessed slave, it is essential for him to manumit the slave completely if he has sufficient money. Otherwise he should look for some work for the slave (to earn what would enable him to emancipate himself), without overburdening him with work

Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Jo koi saanjhi milkiyat wale gulaam mein apna hissa azaad karta hai, uske liye zaroori hai ke gulaam ko poori tarah azaad karvaye agar uske paas kaafi paisa hai. Warna use gulaam ke liye koi kaam dhoondna chahiye (taake woh apni azaadi ke liye kaafi paisa kama sake), uspar zyada bojh daale bina."
