Sahih al-Bukhari 1780

Book 26: `Umrah (Minor pilgrimage) · Hadith 7

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا هُدْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا هَمَّامٌ، وَقَالَ، اعْتَمَرَ أَرْبَعَ عُمَرٍ فِي ذِي الْقَعْدَةِ إِلاَّ الَّتِي اعْتَمَرَ مَعَ حَجَّتِهِ عُمْرَتَهُ مِنَ الْحُدَيْبِيَةِ، وَمِنَ الْعَامِ الْمُقْبِلِ، وَمِنَ الْجِعْرَانَةِ، حَيْثُ قَسَمَ غَنَائِمَ حُنَيْنٍ، وَعُمْرَةً مَعَ حَجَّتِهِ‏.‏

Narrated Hammam:The Prophet (ﷺ) performed four `Umra (three) in Dhi-l-Qa'da except the (one) `Umra which he performed with his Hajj: His `Umra from Al-hudaibiya, and the one of the following year, and the one from Al-Ji'rana where he distributed the booty (of the battle) of Hunain, and another `Umra with his Hajj

Hammam bataate hain: Nabi ne chaar Umrah kiye (teen) Zul-Qa'da mein, siwaye us (ek) Umrah ke jo unhone apne Hajj ke saath kiya: Unka Al-Hudaibiya se Umrah, aur agle saal wala, aur Al-Ji'rana se wala jahan unhone Hunain ki jung ke maal-e-ghanimat taqseem kiye, aur ek doosra Umrah unke Hajj ke saath.
