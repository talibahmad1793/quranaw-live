Sahih al-Bukhari 1804

Book 26: `Umrah (Minor pilgrimage) · Hadith 29

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ سُمَىٍّ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ السَّفَرُ قِطْعَةٌ مِنَ الْعَذَابِ، يَمْنَعُ أَحَدَكُمْ طَعَامَهُ وَشَرَابَهُ وَنَوْمَهُ، فَإِذَا قَضَى نَهْمَتَهُ فَلْيُعَجِّلْ إِلَى أَهْلِهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Traveling is a kind of torture as it prevents one from eating, drinking and sleeping properly. So, when one's needs are fulfilled, one should return quickly to one's family

Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Safar ek qisam ki takleef hai kyunke yeh insaan ko sahi tarah khaane, peene aur sone se rokta hai. Isliye jab kisi ki zaroortein poori ho jaayein, use jaldi apne pariwara ke paas wapas jaana chahiye."
