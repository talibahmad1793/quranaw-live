Sahih al-Bukhari 6744

Book 85: Laws of Inheritance (Al-Faraa'id) · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُوسَى، عَنْ إِسْرَائِيلَ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ آخِرُ آيَةٍ نَزَلَتْ خَاتِمَةُ سُورَةِ النِّسَاءِ ‏{‏يَسْتَفْتُونَكَ قُلِ اللَّهُ يُفْتِيكُمْ فِي الْكَلاَلَةِ‏}‏

Narrated Al-Bara:The last Qur'anic Verse that was revealed (to the Prophet) was the final Verse of Surat-an-Nisa, i.e., 'They ask you for a legal verdict Say: Allah directs (thus) About those who leave No descendants or ascendants as heirs

Al-Bara bataate hain: (Nabi par) naazil ki gayi aakhri Quran ki Aayat, Surah An-Nisa ki aakhri Aayat thi, yaani 'Wo tumse Fatwa (kanooni faisla) maangte hain. Kaho: Allah ye faisla deta hai (in logon ke baare mein) jo waaris nahi chhodte na aulaad na ma-baap...'
