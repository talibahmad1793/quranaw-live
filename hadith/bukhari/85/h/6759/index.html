Sahih al-Bukhari 6759

Book 85: Laws of Inheritance (Al-Faraa'id) · Hadith 36

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا حَفْصُ بْنُ عُمَرَ، حَدَّثَنَا هَمَّامٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ أَرَادَتْ عَائِشَةُ أَنْ تَشْتَرِيَ بَرِيرَةَ فَقَالَتْ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِنَّهُمْ يَشْتَرِطُونَ الْوَلاَءَ‏.‏ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ اشْتَرِيهَا، فَإِنَّمَا الْوَلاَءُ لِمَنْ أَعْتَقَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:When Aisha intended to buy Barira, she said to the Prophet, "Barira's masters stipulated that they will have the Wala." The Prophet (ﷺ) said (to Aisha), "Buy her, as the Wala is for the one who manumits

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Jab Aisha ne Barira ko khareedne ka iraada kiya, unhone Nabi (SAW) se kaha, 'Barira ke maalikon ne shart rakhi hai ke unke paas Wala' rahega.' Nabi (SAW) ne (Aisha se) kaha, 'Use khareedo, kyunke Wala' us shaks ke liye hai jo azaad karta hai.'
