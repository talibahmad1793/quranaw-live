Sahih al-Bukhari 1656

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 137

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ الْهَمْدَانِيِّ، عَنْ حَارِثَةَ بْنِ وَهْبٍ الْخُزَاعِيِّ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ صَلَّى بِنَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَنَحْنُ أَكْثَرُ مَا كُنَّا قَطُّ وَآمَنُهُ بِمِنًى رَكْعَتَيْنِ‏.‏

Narrated Haritha bin Wahab Al-Khuza`i:The Prophet (ﷺ) led us in a two-rak`at prayer at Mina although our number was more than ever and we were in better security than ever

Haritha bin Wahab Al-Khuza'i (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne humari Mina mein do rakat namaz ki imamat ki, jabke humari tadaad pehle se kabhi zyada nahi thi aur hum pehle se zyada mehfooz the.
