Sahih al-Bukhari 1712

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 190

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سَهْلُ بْنُ بَكَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وُهَيْبٌ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ أَبِي قِلاَبَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ وَذَكَرَ الْحَدِيثَ ـ قَالَ وَنَحَرَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِيَدِهِ سَبْعَ بُدْنٍ قِيَامًا، وَضَحَّى بِالْمَدِينَةِ كَبْشَيْنِ أَمْلَحَيْنِ أَقْرَنَيْنِ‏.‏ مُخْتَصَرًا‏.‏

Narrated Sahl bin Bakkar:The narration of Anas abridged, saying, "The Prophet (ﷺ) slaughtered seven Budn (camels) while standing, with his own hands. On the day of `Id-ul-Adha he slaughtered (sacrificed) two horned rams, black and white in color

Sahl bin Bakkar bataate hain: Anas ki (choti) riwayat: "Nabi ne saat Budn (oont) khade hokar apne haathon se zabah kiye. Eid-ul-Adha ke din unhone do singon wale menghon ko, kaale-safed rang ke, zabah kiya."
