Sahih al-Bukhari 1770

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 246

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُثْمَانُ بْنُ الْهَيْثَمِ، أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ، قَالَ عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ كَانَ ذُو الْمَجَازِ وَعُكَاظٌ مَتْجَرَ النَّاسِ فِي الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ، فَلَمَّا جَاءَ الإِسْلاَمُ كَأَنَّهُمْ كَرِهُوا ذَلِكَ حَتَّى نَزَلَتْ ‏{‏لَيْسَ عَلَيْكُمْ جُنَاحٌ أَنْ تَبْتَغُوا فَضْلاً مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ‏}‏ فِي مَوَاسِمِ الْحَجِّ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn ' `Abbas:Dhul-Majaz and `Ukaz were the markets of the people during the Pre-Islamic period of ignorance. When the people embraced Islam, they disliked to do bargaining there till the following Holy Verses were revealed:-- There is no harm for you If you seek of the bounty Of your Lord (during Hajj by trading, etc)

Ibn Abbas (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Dhul-Majaz aur Ukaz jahiliyat ke zamane mein logon ke bazaar the. Jab log Islam laaye, unhone wahan tijarat karna napasand kiya, jab tak yeh paak ayaat nazil hui: "Isme koi harj nahi agar tum apne Rab ka fazl (Hajj ke dauran tijarat karke) talaash karo."
