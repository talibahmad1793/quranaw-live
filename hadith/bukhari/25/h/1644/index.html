Sahih al-Bukhari 1644

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 126

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عُبَيْدِ بْنِ مَيْمُونٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عِيسَى بْنُ يُونُسَ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا طَافَ الطَّوَافَ الأَوَّلَ خَبَّ ثَلاَثًا وَمَشَى أَرْبَعًا، وَكَانَ يَسْعَى بَطْنَ الْمَسِيلِ إِذَا طَافَ بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ لِنَافِعٍ أَكَانَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ يَمْشِي إِذَا بَلَغَ الرُّكْنَ الْيَمَانِيَ قَالَ لاَ‏.‏ إِلاَّ أَنْ يُزَاحَمَ عَلَى الرُّكْنِ فَإِنَّهُ كَانَ لاَ يَدَعُهُ حَتَّى يَسْتَلِمَهُ‏.‏

Narrated Nafi`:Ibn `Umar said, "When Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) performed the first Tawaf he did Ramal in the first three rounds and then walked in the remaining four rounds (of Tawaf of the Ka`ba), where as in performing Tawaf between Safa and Marwa he used to run in the midst of the rainwater passage," I asked Nafi`, "Did `Abdullah (bin `Umar) use to walk steadily on reaching the Yemenite Corner?" He replied, "No, unless people were crowded at the Corner; otherwise he would not leave it without touching it

Nafi bataate hain: Ibn Umar ne kaha: "Jab Allah ke Rasool ne pehla Tawaf kiya, pehli teen chakkaron mein Ramal kiya aur baaki chaar mein aam chaal se chale (Ka'ba ke Tawaf mein), jabke Safa-Marwa ke beech Tawaf karte waqt woh baarish ke paani ki nehr ke beech mein daudte." Maine Nafi se pucha: "Kya Abdullah (bin Umar) Yemeni Kone tak pahunchte huye aaram se chalte?" Unhone jawab diya: "Nahi, siwaye jab log Kone ke qareeb bhirr mein hote; warna woh use chhote bina nahi jate."
