Sahih al-Bukhari 1711

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 189

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ الْمُنْذِرِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَنَسُ بْنُ عِيَاضٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ عُقْبَةَ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، أَنَّ ابْنَ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ كَانَ يَبْعَثُ بِهَدْيِهِ مِنْ جَمْعٍ مِنْ آخِرِ اللَّيْلِ، حَتَّى يُدْخَلَ بِهِ مَنْحَرُ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَعَ حُجَّاجٍ فِيهِمُ الْحُرُّ وَالْمَمْلُوكُ‏.‏

Narrated Nafi`:Ibn `Umar used to send his Hadi from Jam' (to Mina) in the last third of the night with the pilgrims amongst whom there were free men and slaves, till it was taken into the Manhar (slaughtering place) of the Prophet (ﷺ)

Nafi bataate hain: Ibn Umar apna Hadi Jam' (Al-Muzdalifa) se (Mina) raat ke aakhri tihai hisse mein Hajjiyon ke saath bhejte, jinme azaad log aur gulaam donon shamil hote, yahan tak ke woh Nabi ke Manhar (qurbani ki jagah) mein le jaaayaa jata.
