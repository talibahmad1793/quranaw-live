Sahih al-Bukhari 1518

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 6

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَاصِمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيْمَنُ بْنُ نَابِلٍ، حَدَّثَنَا الْقَاسِمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ أَنَّهَا قَالَتْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، اعْتَمَرْتُمْ وَلَمْ أَعْتَمِرْ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ يَا عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ اذْهَبْ بِأُخْتِكَ فَأَعْمِرْهَا مِنَ التَّنْعِيمِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَأَحْقَبَهَا عَلَى نَاقَةٍ فَاعْتَمَرَتْ‏.‏

Narrated Al-Qasim bin Muhammad:`Aisha said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! You performed `Umra but I did not." He said, "O `Abdur-Rahman! Go along with your sister and let her perform `Umra from Tan`im." `Abdur-Rahman made her ride over the packsaddle of a she-camel and she performed `Umra

Al-Qasim bin Muhammad bataate hain: Ayesha ne kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Aapne Umrah kiya magar maine nahi kiya." Aap ne kaha: "Aye Abdur-Rahman! Apni behn ke saath jaao aur unhe Tan'im se Umrah karne do." Abdur-Rahman ne unhe oontni ki paiksaddal par sawar karwaya aur unhone Umrah kiya.
