Sahih al-Bukhari 1754

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 232

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ الْقَاسِمِ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَاهُ ـ وَكَانَ أَفْضَلَ أَهْلِ زَمَانِهِ ـ يَقُولُ سَمِعْتُ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ تَقُولُ طَيَّبْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِيَدَىَّ هَاتَيْنِ حِينَ أَحْرَمَ، وَلِحِلِّهِ حِينَ أَحَلَّ، قَبْلَ أَنْ يَطُوفَ‏.‏ وَبَسَطَتْ يَدَيْهَا‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur-Rahman bin Al-Qasim:I heard my father who was the best man of his age, saying, "I heard `Aisha saying, 'I perfumed Allah's Apostle with my own hands before finishing his Ihram while yet he had not performed Tawaf-al- Ifada.' She spread her hands (while saying so)

Abdur-Rahman bin Al-Qasim bataate hain: Maine apne waalid ko, jo apne zamane ke behtareen shaks the, kehte suna: "Maine Ayesha ko kehte suna: 'Maine Allah ke Rasool ko apne haathon se khushbu lagai isse pehle ke unhone apna Ihram khatam kiya, isse pehle ke unhone Tawaf-al-Ifada kiya.'" Unhone apne haath phailaate hue yeh batayaa.
