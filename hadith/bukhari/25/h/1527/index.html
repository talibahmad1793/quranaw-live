Sahih al-Bukhari 1527

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 15

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيٌّ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَفِظْنَاهُ مِنَ الزُّهْرِيِّ عَنْ سَالِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، وَقَّتَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏

Narrated Salim from his father who said:"The Prophet (ﷺ) had fixed the Mawaqit as follows: (No)

Salim bataate hain, apne waalid ke hawale se: "Nabi ne Mawaqit (Ihram ki jagahein) muqarrar ki thi (koi tafseel nahi)."
