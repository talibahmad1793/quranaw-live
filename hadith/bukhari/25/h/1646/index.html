Sahih al-Bukhari 1646

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 127

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ دِينَارٍ، قَالَ سَأَلْنَا ابْنَ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنْ رَجُلٍ، طَافَ بِالْبَيْتِ فِي عُمْرَةٍ، وَلَمْ يَطُفْ بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ أَيَأْتِي امْرَأَتَهُ فَقَالَ قَدِمَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَطَافَ بِالْبَيْتِ سَبْعًا، وَصَلَّى خَلْفَ الْمَقَامِ رَكْعَتَيْنِ، فَطَافَ بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ سَبْعًا ‏{‏لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللَّهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ‏}‏‏.‏ وَسَأَلْنَا جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ فَقَالَ لاَ يَقْرَبَنَّهَا حَتَّى يَطُوفَ بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ‏.‏

Narrated `Amr bin Dinar:We asked Ibn `Umar whether a man who, while performing `Umra, had performed Tawaf of the Ka`ba; and had not yet performed Tawaf between Safa and Marwa, could have sexual relation with his wife, Ibn `Umar replied "The Prophet (ﷺ) reached Mecca and performed the seven rounds (of Tawaf) of the Ka`ba and then offered a two-rak`at prayer behind Maqam Ibrahim and then performed the seven rounds (of Tawaf) between Safa and Marwa." He added, "Verily! In Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) you have a good example." We asked Jabir bin `Abdullah (the same question) and he said, "He (that man) should not come near (his wife) till he has completed Tawaf between Safa and Marwa

Amr bin Dinar bataate hain: Humne Ibn Umar se pucha kya koi shaks, jisne Umrah mein Ka'ba ka Tawaf kiya ho aur abhi Safa-Marwa ka Tawaf na kiya ho, apni biwi se humbistari kar sakta hai. Ibn Umar ne jawab diya: "Nabi Mecca pahunche, Ka'ba ke saat chakkar (Tawaf) lagaae, phir Maqam-e-Ibrahim ke peechhe do rakat padhi, phir Safa-Marwa ke saat chakkar lagaae." Unhone joda: "Beshak, Allah ke Rasool mein tumhare liye behtareen namoona hai." Humne Jabir bin Abdullah se (yehi sawaal) pucha, unhone kaha: "Woh (shaks) apni biwi ke qareeb tab tak na jaaye jab tak Safa-Marwa ka Tawaf mukammal na kar le."
