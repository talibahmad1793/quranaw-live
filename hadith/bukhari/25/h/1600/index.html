Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 86

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ أَبِي خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي أَوْفَى، قَالَ اعْتَمَرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَطَافَ بِالْبَيْتِ وَصَلَّى خَلْفَ الْمَقَامِ رَكْعَتَيْنِ، وَمَعَهُ مَنْ يَسْتُرُهُ مِنَ النَّاسِ فَقَالَ لَهُ رَجُلٌ أَدَخَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم الْكَعْبَةَ قَالَ لاَ‏.‏

Narrated Isma'il bin Abu Khalid:`Abdullah bin Abu `Aufa said, "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) performed the `Umra. He performed Tawaf of the Ka`ba and offered two rak`at behind the Maqam (Abraham's place) and was accompanied by those who were screening him from the people." Somebody asked `Abdullah, "Did Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) enter the Ka`ba?" `Abdullah replied in the negative

Ismail bin Abu Khalid bataate hain: Abdullah bin Abu Aufa ne kaha: "Allah ke Rasool ne Umrah kiya. Aap ne Ka'ba ka Tawaf kiya aur Maqam (Ibrahim ki jagah) ke peechhe do rakat padhi, aur unke saath woh log the jo unhe logon se parda kar rahe the." Kisi ne Abdullah se pucha: "Kya Allah ke Rasool Ka'ba mein daakhil hue the?" Abdullah ne na mein jawab diya.
