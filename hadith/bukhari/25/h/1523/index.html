Sahih al-Bukhari 1523

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ بِشْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شَبَابَةُ، عَنْ وَرْقَاءَ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ كَانَ أَهْلُ الْيَمَنِ يَحُجُّونَ وَلاَ يَتَزَوَّدُونَ وَيَقُولُونَ نَحْنُ الْمُتَوَكِّلُونَ، فَإِذَا قَدِمُوا مَكَّةَ سَأَلُوا النَّاسَ، فَأَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى ‏{‏وَتَزَوَّدُوا فَإِنَّ خَيْرَ الزَّادِ التَّقْوَى‏}‏‏.‏ رَوَاهُ ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ عَنْ عَمْرٍو عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ مُرْسَلاً‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The people of Yemen used to come for Hajj and used not to bring enough provisions with them and used to say that they depend on Allah. On their arrival in Medina they used to beg the people, and so Allah revealed, "And take a provision (with you) for the journey, but the best provision is the fear of Allah

Ibn Abbas (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Yemen ke log Hajj ke liye aate the aur apne saath kaafi saaman nahi laate the, aur kehte: "Hum Allah par bharosa rakhte hain." Madina pahunchkar woh logon se maangte the, isliye Allah ne yeh ayat nazil ki: "Aur safar ke liye saaman lekar chalo, magar behtareen saaman Allah ka khauf hai."
