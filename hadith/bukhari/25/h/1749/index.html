Sahih al-Bukhari 1749

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 227

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا الْحَكَمُ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، أَنَّهُ حَجَّ مَعَ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ فَرَآهُ يَرْمِي الْجَمْرَةَ الْكُبْرَى بِسَبْعِ حَصَيَاتٍ، فَجَعَلَ الْبَيْتَ عَنْ يَسَارِهِ، وَمِنًى عَنْ يَمِينِهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ هَذَا مَقَامُ الَّذِي أُنْزِلَتْ عَلَيْهِ سُورَةُ الْبَقَرَةِ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur-Rahman bin Yazid:I performed Hajj with Ibn Mas`ud , and saw him doing Rami of the big Jamra (Jamrat-ul-Aqaba) with seven small pebbles, keeping the Ka`ba on his left side and Mina on his right. He then said, "This is the place where the one on whom Surat-al-Baqara was revealed (i.e. Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) ) stood

Abdur-Rahman bin Yazid bataate hain: Maine Ibn Mas'ud ke saath Hajj kiya, aur unhe bade Jamra (Jamrat-ul-Aqaba) ki Rami saat chhoṭe kankaron se karte dekha, Ka'ba ko baayi taraf aur Mina ko dahini taraf rakhte hue. Phir unhone kaha: "Yeh woh jagah hai jahan woh shaks (yaani Allah ke Rasool), jinpar Surah Al-Baqarah nazil hui, khade the."
