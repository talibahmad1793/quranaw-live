Sahih al-Bukhari 1580

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 66

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْوَهَّابِ، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمٌ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، دَخَلَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَامَ الْفَتْحِ مِنْ كَدَاءٍ مِنْ أَعْلَى مَكَّةَ‏.‏ وَكَانَ عُرْوَةُ أَكْثَرَ مَا يَدْخُلُ مِنْ كَدَاءٍ وَكَانَ أَقْرَبَهُمَا إِلَى مَنْزِلِهِ‏.‏

Narrated Hisham:`Urwa said, "The Prophet (ﷺ) entered Mecca in the year of the conquest of Mecca from the side of Kada' which is at the higher part of Mecca." `Urwa often entered from Kada' which was nearer of the two to his dwelling place

Hisham bataate hain: Urwa ne kaha: "Nabi Fatah-e-Mecca ke saal Kada ki taraf se Mecca mein daakhil hue, jo Mecca ke oonche hisse mein hai." Urwa aksar Kada se hi daakhil hote the kyunke woh do jagahon mein unke ghar ke zyada qareeb tha.
