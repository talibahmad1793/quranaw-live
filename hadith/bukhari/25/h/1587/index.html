Sahih al-Bukhari 1587

Book 25: Hajj (Pilgrimage) · Hadith 73

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْحَمِيدِ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، عَنْ طَاوُسٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ فَتْحِ مَكَّةَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ هَذَا الْبَلَدَ حَرَّمَهُ اللَّهُ، لاَ يُعْضَدُ شَوْكُهُ، وَلاَ يُنَفَّرُ صَيْدُهُ، وَلاَ يَلْتَقِطُ لُقَطَتَهُ إِلاَّ مَنْ عَرَّفَهَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:On the Day of the Conquest of Mecca, Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Allah has made this town a sanctuary. Its thorny bushes should not be cut, its game should not be chased, and its fallen things should not be picked up except by one who would announce it publicly

Ibn Abbas (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Fatah-e-Mecca ke din, Allah ke Rasool ne farmaya: "Allah ne is shehar ko haram (muqaddas jagah) banaya hai. Iski kaante wali jhaadiyan na kaati jaayen, iske shikaar ka peechha na kiya jaaye, aur isme giri hui koi cheez sirf woh shaks uthaaye jo use elaan kare (public tor par)."
