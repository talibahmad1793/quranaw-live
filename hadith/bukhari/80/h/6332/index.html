Sahih al-Bukhari 6332

Book 80: Invocations · Hadith 29

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ أَبِي أَوْفَى ـ رضى الله عنهما كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا أَتَاهُ رَجُلٌ بِصَدَقَةٍ قَالَ ‏"‏ اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى آلِ فُلاَنٍ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَأَتَاهُ أَبِي فَقَالَ ‏"‏ اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى آلِ أَبِي أَوْفَى ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Abi `Aufa:Whenever a man brought his alms to the Prophet, the Prophet (ﷺ) would say, "O Allah! Bestow Your Blessing upon the family of so-and-so." When my father came to him (with his alms), he said, "O Allah! Bestow Your Blessings upon the family of Abi `Aufa

Ibn Abi Aufa bataate hain: Jab bhi koi aadmi apna sadqa Nabi (SAW) ke paas laata, Nabi (SAW) kehte, 'Aye Allah! Falaan khaandaan par apni barkat naazil kar.' Jab mere waalid unke paas (apna sadqa lekar) aaye, unhone kaha, 'Aye Allah! Abi Aufa ke khaandaan par apni barkat naazil kar.'
