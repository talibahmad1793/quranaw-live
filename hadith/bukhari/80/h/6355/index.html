Sahih al-Bukhari 6355

Book 80: Invocations · Hadith 52

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، أَخْبَرَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتْ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُؤْتَى بِالصِّبْيَانِ فَيَدْعُو لَهُمْ، فَأُتِيَ بِصَبِيٍّ فَبَالَ عَلَى ثَوْبِهِ، فَدَعَا بِمَاءٍ فَأَتْبَعَهُ إِيَّاهُ، وَلَمْ يَغْسِلْهُ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:The boys used to be brought to the Prophet (ﷺ) and he used to invoke for Allah's blessing upon them. Once an infant was brought to him and it urinated on his clothes. He asked for water and poured it over the place of the urine and did not wash his clothes

Aisha bataati hain: Bachchon ko Nabi (SAW) ke paas laaya jaata tha aur wo unke liye Allah se barkat ki dua karte the. Ek baar ek chhota bacha unke paas laaya gaya aur usne unke kapdon par peshaab kar diya. Unhone paani maangaya aur peshaab ki jagah par daala, aur apne kapde dhoye nahi.
