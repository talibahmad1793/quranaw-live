Book 80: Invocations · Hadith 95

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، أَخْبَرَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ أَبُو الْقَاسِمِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ فِي الْجُمُعَةِ سَاعَةٌ لاَ يُوَافِقُهَا مُسْلِمٌ وَهْوَ قَائِمٌ يُصَلِّي يَسْأَلُ خَيْرًا إِلاَّ أَعْطَاهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَقَالَ بِيَدِهِ قُلْنَا يُقَلِّلُهَا يُزَهِّدُهَا‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Abu-l-Qasim (the Prophet) said, "On Friday there is a particular time. If a Muslim happens to be praying and invoking Allah for something good during that time, Allah will surely fulfill his request." The Prophet (ﷺ) pointed out with his hand. We thought that he wanted to illustrate how short that time was

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Abu-l-Qasim (Nabi) ne kaha, 'Jumme ke din ek khaas waqt hota hai. Agar koi Musalmaan us waqt namaz mein ho aur Allah se kisi achhi cheez ki dua kare, to Allah zaroor uski darkhwaast poori karega.' Nabi (SAW) ne apne haath se ishara kiya. Hamne socha wo dikhaana chahte the ke wo waqt kitna chhota hai.
