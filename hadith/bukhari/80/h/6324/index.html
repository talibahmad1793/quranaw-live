Sahih al-Bukhari 6324

Book 80: Invocations · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ بْنِ عُمَيْرٍ، عَنْ رِبْعِيِّ بْنِ حِرَاشٍ، عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ، قَالَ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا أَرَادَ أَنْ يَنَامَ قَالَ ‏"‏ بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا ‏"‏‏.‏ وَإِذَا اسْتَيْقَظَ مِنْ مَنَامِهِ قَالَ ‏"‏ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا، وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Hudhaifa:Whenever the Prophet (ﷺ) intended to go to bed, he would recite: "Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya (With Your name, O Allah, I die and I live)." And when he woke up from his sleep, he would say: "Al-hamdu lil-lahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana; wa ilaihi an-nushur (All the Praises are for Allah Who has made us alive after He made us die (sleep) and unto Him is the Resurrection

Hudhaifa bataate hain: Jab bhi Nabi (SAW) so-ne ka iraada karte, wo padhte, 'Bismika Allahumma Amutu Wa Ahya' (Tere naam se, Aye Allah, main marta hoon aur jeeta hoon). Aur jab wo apni neend se uthte, wo kehte, 'Al-Hamdu Lillahil-ladhi Ahyana Ba'da Ma Amatana; Wa Ilaihi An-Nushur' (Sab tareef Allah ke liye jisne humein maut (neend) dene ke baad zinda kiya; aur usi ki taraf Qayamat hai).
