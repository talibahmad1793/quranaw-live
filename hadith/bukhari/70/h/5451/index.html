Sahih al-Bukhari 5451

Book 70: Food, Meals · Hadith 80

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، قَالَ قِيلَ لأَنَسٍ مَا سَمِعْتَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي الثُّومِ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ أَكَلَ فَلاَ يَقْرَبَنَّ مَسْجِدَنَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdul `Aziz:It was said to Anas "What did you hear the Prophet (ﷺ) saying about garlic?" Anas replied, "Whoever has eaten (garlic) should not approach our mosque

Abdul Aziz bataate hain: Anas se pucha gaya, 'Aapne Nabi (SAW) ko lehsun ke baare mein kya kehte suna?' Anas ne jawab diya, 'Jisne (lehsun) khaaya ho, use hamaari Masjid ke paas nahi aana chaahiye.'
