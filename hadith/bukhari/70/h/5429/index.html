Sahih al-Bukhari 5429

Book 70: Food, Meals · Hadith 57

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ سُمَىٍّ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ السَّفَرُ قِطْعَةٌ مِنَ الْعَذَابِ، يَمْنَعُ أَحَدَكُمْ نَوْمَهُ وَطَعَامَهُ، فَإِذَا قَضَى نَهْمَتَهُ مِنْ وَجْهِهِ فَلْيُعَجِّلْ إِلَى أَهْلِهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Traveling is a kind of torture, as it prevents one from sleeping and eating! So when one has finished his job, he should return quickly to his family

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Safar ek qism ki takleef hai, kyunke ye insaan ko sone aur khaane se rokta hai. To jab koi apna kaam khatam kar le, use jaldi apne khaandaan ke paas laut jaana chaahiye.'
