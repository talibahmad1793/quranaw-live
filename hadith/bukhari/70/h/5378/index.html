Sahih al-Bukhari 5378

Book 70: Food, Meals · Hadith 6

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ وَهْبِ بْنِ كَيْسَانَ أَبِي نُعَيْمٍ، قَالَ أُتِيَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِطَعَامٍ وَمَعَهُ رَبِيبُهُ عُمَرُ بْنُ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ سَمِّ اللَّهَ، وَكُلْ مِمَّا يَلِيكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Wahb bin Kaisan Abi Nu'aim:A meal was brought to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) while his step-son, `Umar bin Abi Salama was with him. Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said to him, "Mention the Name of Allah and eat of the dish what is nearer to you

Wahb bin Kaisan Abi Nu'aim bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ke paas khaana laaya gaya jabke unke saudari bete, Umar bin Abi Salama, unke saath the. Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne unse kaha, 'Allah ka naam lo, aur thaali mein se wahi khaao jo tumhaare qareeb ho.'
