Sahih al-Bukhari 5421

Book 70: Food, Meals · Hadith 49

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا هُدْبَةُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا هَمَّامُ بْنُ يَحْيَى، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، قَالَ كُنَّا نَأْتِي أَنَسَ بْنَ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ وَخَبَّازُهُ قَائِمٌ قَالَ كُلُوا فَمَا أَعْلَمُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم رَأَى رَغِيفًا مُرَقَّقًا حَتَّى لَحِقَ بِاللَّهِ، وَلاَ رَأَى شَاةً سَمِيطًا بِعَيْنِهِ قَطُّ‏.‏

Narrated Qatada:We used to visit Anas bin Malik while his baker was standing (and baking). Anas would say, "Eat! I do not know that the Prophet (ﷺ) had ever seen well-baked bread till he met Allah, nor had he ever seen a roasted sheep with his own eyes

Qatada bataate hain: Hum Anas bin Malik se milne jaate the jab unka baawarchi khada hokar roti bana raha hota. Anas kehte, 'Khaao! Mujhe nahi lagta ke Nabi (SAW) ne kabhi khoob pakayi hui roti dekhi ho apni maut tak, na hi unhone kabhi apni aankhon se bhuni hui bakri dekhi ho.'
