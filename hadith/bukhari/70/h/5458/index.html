Sahih al-Bukhari 5458

Book 70: Food, Meals · Hadith 87

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ ثَوْرٍ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ مَعْدَانَ، عَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ إِذَا رَفَعَ مَائِدَتَهُ قَالَ ‏ "‏ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا فِيهِ، غَيْرَ مَكْفِيٍّ، وَلاَ مُوَدَّعٍ وَلاَ مُسْتَغْنًى عَنْهُ، رَبَّنَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Umama:Whenever the dining sheet of the Prophet (ﷺ) was taken away (i.e., whenever he finished his meal), he used to say: "Al-hamdu li l-lah kathiran taiyiban mubarakan fihi ghaira makfiy wala muWada` wala mustaghna'anhu Rabbuna

Abu Umama bataate hain: Jab bhi Nabi (SAW) ki dastarkhwaan uthaayi jaati (yaani jab bhi wo khaana khatam karte), wo kehte, 'Al-Hamdu Lillahi Kathiran Tayyiban Mubaarakan Feehi, Ghaira Makfiyyin wala Mu-adda'in wala Mustaghnan 'anhu Rabbuna' (Bahut zyaada, paak aur mubaarak shukr Allah ke liye, jo na kaafi samjha jaaye, na chhoda jaaye, na jiski hamare Rab ko zaroorat na ho).
