Sahih al-Bukhari 2313

Book 40: Representation, Authorization, Business by Proxy · Hadith 13

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، قَالَ فِي صَدَقَةِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ لَيْسَ عَلَى الْوَلِيِّ جُنَاحٌ أَنْ يَأْكُلَ وَيُؤْكِلَ صَدِيقًا ‏{‏لَهُ‏}‏ غَيْرَ مُتَأَثِّلٍ مَالاً، فَكَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ هُوَ يَلِي صَدَقَةَ عُمَرَ يُهْدِي لِلنَّاسِ مِنْ أَهْلِ مَكَّةَ، كَانَ يَنْزِلُ عَلَيْهِمْ‏.‏

Narrated `Amr:Concerning the Waqf of `Umar: It was not sinful of the trustee (of the Waqf) to eat or provide his friends from it, provided the trustee had no intention of collecting fortune (for himself). Ibn `Umar was the manager of the trust of `Umar and he used to give presents from it to those with whom he used to stay at Mecca

Amr bataate hain: Umar ke Waqf (charity endowment) ke baare mein: Isme gunah nahi tha ke Waqf ka trustee usme se khaye ya apne doston ko khilaye, bashart ke trustee ka apne liye daulat jama karne ka koi iraada na ho. Ibn Umar Umar ke trust ke manager the aur unse mila-jula tohfa dete the un logon ko jinke saath woh Mecca mein rahte the.
