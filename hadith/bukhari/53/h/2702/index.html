Book 53: Peacemaking · Hadith 12

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا بِشْرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ بُشَيْرِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ أَبِي حَثْمَةَ، قَالَ انْطَلَقَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ سَهْلٍ وَمُحَيِّصَةُ بْنُ مَسْعُودِ بْنِ زَيْدٍ إِلَى خَيْبَرَ، وَهْىَ يَوْمَئِذٍ صُلْحٌ‏.‏

Narrated Sahl bin Abu Hathma:`Abdullah bin Sahl and Muhaiyisa bin Mas`ud bin Zaid went to Khaibar when it had a peace treaty (with the Muslims)

Sahl bin Abu Hathma bataate hain: Abdullah bin Sahl aur Muhaiyisa bin Mas'ud bin Zaid Khaibar gaye jab wahan (Musalmanon ke saath) ek sulah ka muahida tha.
