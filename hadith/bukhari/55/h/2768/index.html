Sahih al-Bukhari 2768

Book 55: Wills and Testaments (Wasaayaa) · Hadith 31

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنِ كَثِيرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ عُلَيَّةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَدِمَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم الْمَدِينَةَ لَيْسَ لَهُ خَادِمٌ، فَأَخَذَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ بِيَدِي، فَانْطَلَقَ بِي إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّ أَنَسًا غُلاَمٌ كَيِّسٌ، فَلْيَخْدُمْكَ‏.‏ قَالَ فَخَدَمْتُهُ فِي السَّفَرِ وَالْحَضَرِ، مَا قَالَ لِي لِشَىْءٍ صَنَعْتُهُ لِمَ صَنَعْتَ هَذَا هَكَذَا وَلاَ لِشَىْءٍ لَمْ أَصْنَعْهُ لِمَ لَمْ تَصْنَعْ هَذَا هَكَذَا

Narrated Anas:When Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) came to Medina; he did not have any servant. Abu Talha (Anas' step-father) took me to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Anas is a wise boy, so let him serve you." So, I served him at home and on journeys. If I did anything, he never asked me why I did it, and if I refrained from doing anything, he never asked me why I refrained from doing it

Anas bataate hain: Jab Allah ke Rasool Madina aaye, unke paas koi khadim nahi tha. Abu Talha (Anas ke sautele waalid) mujhe Allah ke Rasool ke paas laaye aur kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Anas ek samajhdaar ladka hai, isliye ise aapki khidmat karne dijiye." Isliye maine ghar aur safar mein unki khidmat ki. Agar maine kuch kiya, unhone kabhi nahi pucha maine woh kyun kiya, aur agar maine kuch nahi kiya, unhone kabhi nahi pucha maine woh kyun nahi kiya.
