Sahih al-Bukhari 2754

Book 55: Wills and Testaments (Wasaayaa) · Hadith 17

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم رَأَى رَجُلاً يَسُوقُ بَدَنَةً، فَقَالَ لَهُ ‏"‏ ارْكَبْهَا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّهَا بَدَنَةٌ‏.‏ قَالَ فِي الثَّالِثَةِ أَوِ الرَّابِعَةِ ‏"‏ ارْكَبْهَا، وَيْلَكَ، أَوْ وَيْحَكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) saw a man driving a Badana (i.e. camel for sacrifice) and said to him, "Ride on it." The man said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! It is a Badana." (The Prophet (ﷺ) repeated his order) and on the third or fourth time he said, "Ride it, (woe to you" or said: "May Allah be merciful to you)

Anas bataate hain: Nabi ne ek shaks ko Badana (qurbani ka unt) hankate dekha aur kaha: "Ispar sawar ho jaao." Shaks ne kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Yeh Badana hai." (Nabi ne apna hukum dohraya) aur teesri ya chauthi baar unhone kaha: "Ispar sawar ho jaao, (afsos tumpar)" ya kaha, "Allah tumpar reham kare."
