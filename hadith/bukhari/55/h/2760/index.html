Sahih al-Bukhari 2760

Book 55: Wills and Testaments (Wasaayaa) · Hadith 23

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، رضى الله عنها أَنَّ رَجُلاً، قَالَ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِنَّ أُمِّي افْتُلِتَتْ نَفْسَهَا، وَأُرَاهَا لَوْ تَكَلَّمَتْ تَصَدَّقَتْ، أَفَأَتَصَدَّقُ عَنْهَا قَالَ ‏ "‏ نَعَمْ، تَصَدَّقْ عَنْهَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:A man said to the Prophet, "My mother died suddenly, and I think that if she could speak, she would have given in charity. May I give in charity on her behalf?" He said, "Yes! Give in charity on her behalf

Ayesha bataati hain: Ek shaks ne Nabi se kaha: "Meri maa achanak guzar gayi, aur mujhe lagta hai agar woh bol pati, woh sadqa deti. Kya main uski taraf se sadqa de sakta hoon?" Nabi ne kaha: "Haan! Uski taraf se sadqa do."
