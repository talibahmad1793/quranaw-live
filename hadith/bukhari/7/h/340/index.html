Sahih al-Bukhari 340

Book 7: Rubbing hands and feet with dust (Tayammum) · Hadith 7

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ، عَنْ ذَرٍّ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ أَبْزَى، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّهُ شَهِدَ عُمَرَ وَقَالَ لَهُ عَمَّارٌ كُنَّا فِي سَرِيَّةٍ فَأَجْنَبْنَا، وَقَالَ تَفَلَ فِيهِمَا‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur Rahman bin Abza:that while he was in the company of `Umar, `Ammar said to `Umar, "We were in a detachment and became Junub and I blew the dust off my hands (performed the rolling over the earth and prayed)

Abdur Rahman bin Abza bataate hain ke woh Umar ke saath the, Ammar ne Umar se kaha: "Hum ek toli mein the aur Junub ho gaye, maine apne haathon se dhool phoonk kar (zameen par lot kar namaz padhi)."
