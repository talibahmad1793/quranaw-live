Sahih al-Bukhari 2391

Book 43: Loans, Payment of Loans, Freezing of Property, Bankruptcy · Hadith 7

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ، عَنْ رِبْعِيٍّ، عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ مَاتَ رَجُلٌ، فَقِيلَ لَهُ قَالَ كُنْتُ أُبَايِعُ النَّاسَ، فَأَتَجَوَّزُ عَنِ الْمُوسِرِ، وَأُخَفِّفُ عَنِ الْمُعْسِرِ، فَغُفِرَ لَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو مَسْعُودٍ سَمِعْتُهُ مِنَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏

Narrated Hudhaifa:I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) saying, "Once a man died and was asked, 'What did you use to say (or do) (in your life time)?' He replied, 'I was a businessman and used to give time to the rich to repay his debt and (used to) deduct part of the debt of the poor.' So he was forgiven (his sins.)" Abu Mas`ud said, "I heard the same (Hadith) from the Prophet

Hudhaifa (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Maine Nabi ko farmate suna: "Ek baar ek shaks mar gaya aur usse pucha gaya: 'Tum kya kehte (ya karte) the (apni zindagi mein)?' Usne jawab diya: 'Main ek vyapaari tha aur maldaar ko apna qarz chukane ke liye waqt deta tha, aur gareeb ke qarz ka kuch hissa maaf kar deta tha.' Isliye usse maaf kar diya gaya (uske gunah)." Abu Mas'ud ne kaha: "Maine yehi (hadith) Nabi se suni."
