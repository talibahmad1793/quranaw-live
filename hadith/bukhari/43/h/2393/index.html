Sahih al-Bukhari 2393

Book 43: Loans, Payment of Loans, Freezing of Property, Bankruptcy · Hadith 9

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كَانَ لِرَجُلٍ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم سِنٌّ مِنَ الإِبِلِ فَجَاءَهُ يَتَقَاضَاهُ فَقَالَ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ أَعْطُوهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَطَلَبُوا سِنَّهُ، فَلَمْ يَجِدُوا لَهُ إِلاَّ سِنًّا فَوْقَهَا‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ أَعْطُوهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ أَوْفَيْتَنِي، وَفَّى اللَّهُ بِكَ‏.‏ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ إِنَّ خِيَارَكُمْ أَحْسَنُكُمْ قَضَاءً ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) owed a camel of a certain age to a man who came to demand it back. The Prophet (ﷺ) ordered his companions to give him. They looked for a camel of the same age but found nothing but a camel one year older. The Prophet (ﷺ) told them to give it to him. The man said, "You have paid me in full, and may Allah pay you in full." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The best amongst you is he who pays his debts in the most handsome manner

Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi par kisi ka ek khaas umr ka unt qarz tha, jo use wapas maangne aaya. Nabi ne apne sahaba ko ise dene ka hukum diya. Unhone usi umr ka unt dhoonda magar unhe koi nahi mila siwaye ek unt ke jo ek saal bada tha. Nabi ne unhe use de dene ko kaha. Shaks ne kaha: "Aapne mujhe poora ada kiya, aur Allah aapko poora ada kare." Nabi ne kaha: "Tum mein sabse behtar woh hai jo apna qarz sabse behtareen tareeke se chukata hai."
