Book 43: Loans, Payment of Loans, Freezing of Property, Bankruptcy · Hadith 22

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ دِينَارٍ، سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَجُلٌ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِنِّي أُخْدَعُ فِي الْبُيُوعِ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا بَايَعْتَ فَقُلْ لاَ خِلاَبَةَ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَكَانَ الرَّجُلُ يَقُولُهُ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, "I am often betrayed in bargaining." The Prophet (ﷺ) advised him, "When you buy something, say (to the seller), 'No deception." The man used to say so afterwards

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Ek shaks Nabi ke paas aaya aur kaha: "Main aksar saudebaazi mein dhoka khata hoon." Nabi ne unhe naseehat di: "Jab tum kuch khareedo, (bechne wale se) kaho: 'Koi dhoka nahi.'" Shaks baad mein aisa kehta reha.
