Sahih al-Bukhari 84

Book 3: Knowledge · Hadith 26

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا وُهَيْبٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم سُئِلَ فِي حَجَّتِهِ فَقَالَ ذَبَحْتُ قَبْلَ أَنْ أَرْمِيَ، فَأَوْمَأَ بِيَدِهِ قَالَ وَلاَ حَرَجَ‏.‏ قَالَ حَلَقْتُ قَبْلَ أَنْ أَذْبَحَ‏.‏ فَأَوْمَأَ بِيَدِهِ وَلاَ حَرَجَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Somebody said to the Prophet (during his last Hajj), "I did the slaughtering before doing the Rami.' The Prophet (ﷺ) beckoned with his hand and said, "There is no harm in that." Then another person said, "I got my head shaved before offering the sacrifice." The Prophet (ﷺ) beckoned with his hand saying, "There is no harm in that

Ibn Abbas (رضی اللہ عنہما) se riwayat hai ke kisi ne Nabi (ﷺ) se (aakhri Hajj mein) kaha: "Maine Rami se pehle qurbani kar li." Aap ne haath se ishara karke farmaya: "Koi harj nahi." Doosre ne kaha: "Maine qurbani se pehle sar munwa liya." Aap ne ishara karke farmaya: "Koi harj nahi."
