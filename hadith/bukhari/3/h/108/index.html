Sahih al-Bukhari 108

Book 3: Knowledge · Hadith 50

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، قَالَ أَنَسٌ إِنَّهُ لَيَمْنَعُنِي أَنْ أُحَدِّثَكُمْ حَدِيثًا كَثِيرًا أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ تَعَمَّدَ عَلَىَّ كَذِبًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The fact which stops me from narrating a great number of Hadiths to you is that the Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever tells a lie against me intentionally, then (surely) let him occupy his seat in Hell-fire

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai: "Jo baat mujhe tumhe zyada hadith batane se rokti hai woh yeh hai ke Nabi (ﷺ) ne farmaya: 'Jo mujh par jaan-boojh kar jhoot bandhe, woh apna thikana Dozakh mein bana le.'"
