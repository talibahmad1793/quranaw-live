Sahih al-Bukhari 5146

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 81

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قَبِيصَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عُمَرَ، يَقُولُ جَاءَ رَجُلاَنِ مِنَ الْمَشْرِقِ فَخَطَبَا فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ إِنَّ مِنَ الْبَيَانِ لَسِحْرً‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:Two men came from the east and delivered speeches, and the Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Some eloquent speech has the influence of magic." (e.g., some people refuse to do something and then a good eloquent speaker addresses them and then they agree to do that very thing after his speech)

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Do log poorab se aaye aur taqreerein din, aur Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Kuch bayaan itne shaandar hote hain ke unmein jaadu jaisa asar hota hai.'
