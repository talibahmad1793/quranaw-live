Sahih al-Bukhari 5078

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 16

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أُرِيتُكِ فِي الْمَنَامِ مَرَّتَيْنِ، إِذَا رَجُلٌ يَحْمِلُكِ فِي سَرَقَةِ حَرِيرٍ فَيَقُولُ هَذِهِ امْرَأَتُكَ، فَأَكْشِفُهَا فَإِذَا هِيَ أَنْتِ، فَأَقُولُ إِنْ يَكُنْ هَذَا مِنْ عِنْدِ اللَّهِ يُمْضِهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said (to me), "You have been shown to me twice in (my) dreams. A man was carrying you in a silken cloth and said to me, 'This is your wife.' I uncovered it; and behold, it was you. I said to myself, 'If this dream is from Allah, He will cause it to come true

Aisha bataati hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne (mujhse) farmaya, 'Mujhe tumhein do baar khwaab mein dikhaaya gaya. Ek shaks tumhein resham ke kapde mein leke aaya aur mujhse kaha, "Ye aapki biwi hai." Maine use khola, aur dekha ke wo tum thi. Maine apne dil mein kaha, "Agar ye khwaab Allah ki taraf se hai, to wo ise sach kar dega."'
