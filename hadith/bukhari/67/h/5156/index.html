Sahih al-Bukhari 5156

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 91

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا فَرْوَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُسْهِرٍ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ تَزَوَّجَنِي النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَتَتْنِي أُمِّي فَأَدْخَلَتْنِي الدَّارَ، فَإِذَا نِسْوَةٌ مِنَ الأَنْصَارِ فِي الْبَيْتِ فَقُلْنَ عَلَى الْخَيْرِ وَالْبَرَكَةِ، وَعَلَى خَيْرِ طَائِرٍ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:When the Prophet (ﷺ) married me, my mother came to me and made me enter the house where I saw some women from the Ansar who said, "May you prosper and have blessings and have good omen

Aisha bataati hain: Jab Nabi (SAW) ne mujhse nikaah kiya, meri maa mere paas aayi aur mujhe ghar mein daakhil kiya, jahaan maine Ansaar ki kuch auraton ko dekha jo kaha karti thin, 'Aap khushhaal hon aur aapko barkatein aur achhi qismat mile.'
