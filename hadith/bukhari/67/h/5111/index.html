Sahih al-Bukhari 5111

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 48

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي قَبِيصَةُ بْنُ ذُؤَيْبٍ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، يَقُولُ نَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْ تُنْكَحَ الْمَرْأَةُ عَلَى عَمَّتِهَا وَالْمَرْأَةُ وَخَالَتُهَا‏.‏ فَنُرَى خَالَةَ أَبِيهَا بِتِلْكَ الْمَنْزِلَةِ‏.‏ لأَنَّ عُرْوَةَ حَدَّثَنِي عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ حَرِّمُوا مِنَ الرَّضَاعَةِ مَا يَحْرُمُ مِنَ النَّسَبِ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) forbade that a woman should be married to a man along with her paternal aunt or with her maternal aunt (at the same time). Az-Zuhri (the sub-narrator) said: There is a similar order for the paternal aunt of the father of one's wife, for 'Urwa told me that `Aisha said, "What is unlawful because of blood relations, is also unlawful because of the corresponding foster suckling relations

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne mana kiya ke ek aurat ka nikaah usi mard se ho jiske nikaah mein uski chachi ya khaala (ek hi waqt mein) ho. Az-Zuhri (ravi) kehte hain: Isi tarah ka hukm biwi ke waalid ki chachi ke baare mein bhi hai, kyunke Urwa ne mujhe bataaya ke Aisha ne kaha, 'Jo khoon ke rishte ki wajah se haraam hai, wo doodh-piyaai ke usi tarah ke rishte ki wajah se bhi haraam hai.'
