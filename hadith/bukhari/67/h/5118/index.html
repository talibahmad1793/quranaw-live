Sahih al-Bukhari 5118

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 54

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيٌّ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ عَمْرٌو عَنِ الْحَسَنِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، وَسَلَمَةَ بْنِ الأَكْوَعِ، قَالاَ كُنَّا فِي جَيْشٍ فَأَتَانَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّهُ قَدْ أُذِنَ لَكُمْ أَنْ تَسْتَمْتِعُوا فَاسْتَمْتِعُوا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah and Salama bin Al-Akwa`:While we were in an army, Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) came to us and said, "You have been allowed to do the Mut'a (marriage), so do it

Jabir bin Abdullah aur Salama bin Al-Akwa bataate hain: Jab hum ek fauj mein the, Allah ke Rasool (SAW) humaare paas aaye aur kaha, 'Tumhein Mut'a (waqti nikaah) karne ki ijaazat di gayi hai, isliye ye karo.'
