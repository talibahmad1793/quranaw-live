Sahih al-Bukhari 5095

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 33

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنْ كَانَ فِي شَىْءٍ فَفِي الْفَرَسِ وَالْمَرْأَةِ وَالْمَسْكَنِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Sahl bin Sa`d:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "If at all there is bad omen, it is in the horse, the woman, and the house

Sahl bin Sa'd bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Agar kisi cheez mein bad-shugooni ho sakti hai, to wo ghode, aurat aur ghar mein hai.'
