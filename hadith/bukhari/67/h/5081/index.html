Sahih al-Bukhari 5081

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 19

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ، عَنْ عِرَاكٍ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَطَبَ عَائِشَةَ إِلَى أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ لَهُ أَبُو بَكْرٍ إِنَّمَا أَنَا أَخُوكَ، فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ أَنْتَ أَخِي فِي دِينِ اللَّهِ وَكِتَابِهِ وَهْىَ لِي حَلاَلٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated 'Urwa:The Prophet (ﷺ) asked Abu Bakr for `Aisha's hand in marriage. Abu Bakr said "But I am your brother." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "You are my brother in Allah's religion and His Book, but she (Aisha) is lawful for me to marry

Urwa bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne Abu Bakr se Aisha ka rishta maanga. Abu Bakr ne kaha, 'Magar main to aapka bhai hoon.' Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Aap Allah ke deen aur uski Kitaab mein mere bhai hain, magar Aisha mere liye nikaah ke liye jaayez hai.'
