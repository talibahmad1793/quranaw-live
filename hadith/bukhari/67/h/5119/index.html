Sahih al-Bukhari 5119

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 55

Hadith text

وَقَالَ ابْنُ أَبِي ذِئْبٍ حَدَّثَنِي إِيَاسُ بْنُ سَلَمَةَ بْنِ الأَكْوَعِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أَيُّمَا رَجُلٍ وَامْرَأَةٍ تَوَافَقَا فَعِشْرَةُ مَا بَيْنَهُمَا ثَلاَثُ لَيَالٍ فَإِنْ أَحَبَّا أَنْ يَتَزَايَدَا أَوْ يَتَتَارَكَا تَتَارَكَا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَمَا أَدْرِي أَشَىْءٌ كَانَ لَنَا خَاصَّةً أَمْ لِلنَّاسِ عَامَّةً‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو عَبْدِ اللَّهِ وَبَيَّنَهُ عَلِيٌّ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ مَنْسُوخٌ‏.‏

Salama bin Al-Akwa` said:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "If a man and a woman agree (to marry temporarily), their marriage should last for three nights, and if they like to continue, they can do so; and if they want to separate, they can do so." I do not know whether that was only for us or for all the people in general. Abu `Abdullah (Al-Bukhari) said: `Ali made it clear that the Prophet said, "The Mut'a marriage has been cancelled (made unlawful)

Salama bin Al-Akwa bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Agar koi mard aur aurat (waqti nikaah karne par) razi ho jaayein, to unka nikaah teen raaton tak rahega, aur agar wo jaari rakhna chaahein to rakh sakte hain, aur agar alag hona chaahein to ho sakte hain.' Mujhe nahi pata ke ye sirf humaare liye tha ya sab logon ke liye aam taur par. Abu Abdullah (Al-Bukhari) kehte hain: Ali ne saaf kiya ke Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Mut'a nikaah ab mansookh (haraam) kar diya gaya hai.'
