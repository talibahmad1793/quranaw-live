Sahih al-Bukhari 5134

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 70

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُعَلَّى بْنُ أَسَدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وُهَيْبٌ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم تَزَوَّجَهَا وَهْىَ بِنْتُ سِتِّ سِنِينَ، وَبَنَى بِهَا وَهْىَ بِنْتُ تِسْعِ سِنِينَ‏.‏ قَالَ هِشَامٌ وَأُنْبِئْتُ أَنَّهَا كَانَتْ عِنْدَهُ تِسْعَ سِنِينَ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:that the Prophet (ﷺ) married her when she was six years old and he consummated his marriage when she was nine years old. Hisham said: I have been informed that `Aisha remained with the Prophet (ﷺ) for nine years (i.e. till his death)

Aisha bataati hain: Nabi (SAW) ne unse chhe saal ki umar mein nikaah kiya aur nau saal ki umar mein rukhsati ki. Ravi Hisham kehte hain: Mujhe bataya gaya hai ke Aisha Nabi (SAW) ke saath nau saal (unki wafat tak) rahi.
