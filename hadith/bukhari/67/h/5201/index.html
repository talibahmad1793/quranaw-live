Sahih al-Bukhari 5201

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 135

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ مَخْلَدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي حُمَيْدٌ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ آلَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنْ نِسَائِهِ شَهْرًا وَقَعَدَ فِي مَشْرُبَةٍ لَهُ فَنَزَلَ لِتِسْعٍ وَعِشْرِينَ فَقِيلَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّكَ آلَيْتَ عَلَى شَهْرٍ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ الشَّهْرَ تِسْعٌ وَعِشْرُونَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) took an oath that he would not visit his wives for one month, and he sat in an upper room belonging to him. Then, on the twenty ninth day he came down. It was said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! You had taken an oath not to visit your wives for one month." He said, "The (present) month is of twenty-nine days

Anas bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne qasam khaayi ke wo ek mahine tak apni biwiyon ke paas nahi jaayenge, aur wo apne ek upar wale kamre mein baith gaye. Phir unnattisvein din wo neeche utre. Kaha gaya, 'Aye Allah ke Rasool! Aapne qasam khaayi thi ke aap ek mahine tak apni biwiyon ke paas nahi jaayenge.' Unhone farmaya, 'Ye (mojooda) mahina unnattis dinon ka hai.'
