Sahih al-Bukhari 5173

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 108

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا دُعِيَ أَحَدُكُمْ إِلَى الْوَلِيمَةِ فَلْيَأْتِهَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "If anyone of you is invited to a wedding banquet, he must go for it (accept the invitation)

Abdullah bin Umar bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Agar tum mein se kisi ko shaadi ke Walima ki dawat mile, to use zaroor jaana chaahiye (dawat qubool karni chaahiye).'
