Sahih al-Bukhari 5241

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 174

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصِ بْنِ غِيَاثٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي شَقِيقٌ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ لاَ تُبَاشِرِ الْمَرْأَةُ الْمَرْأَةَ فَتَنْعَتَهَا لِزَوْجِهَا كَأَنَّهُ يَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "A woman should not look at or touch another woman to describe her to her husband in such a way as if he was actually looking at her

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Ek aurat ko doosri aurat ko dekh kar ya chhoo kar apne shohar ke saamne is tarah bayaan nahi karna chaahiye jaise wo khud use dekh raha ho.'
