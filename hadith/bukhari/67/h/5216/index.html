Sahih al-Bukhari 5216

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 149

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا فَرْوَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُسْهِرٍ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا انْصَرَفَ مِنَ الْعَصْرِ دَخَلَ عَلَى نِسَائِهِ، فَيَدْنُو مِنْ إِحْدَاهُنَّ، فَدَخَلَ عَلَى حَفْصَةَ، فَاحْتَبَسَ أَكْثَرَ مَا كَانَ يَحْتَبِسُ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:Whenever Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) finished his `Asr prayer, he would enter upon his wives and stay with one of them. One day he went to Hafsa and stayed with her longer than usual

Aisha bataati hain: Jab bhi Allah ke Rasool (SAW) Asr ki namaz khatam karte, wo apni biwiyon ke paas jaate aur unmein se ek ke paas rukte. Ek din wo Hafsa ke paas gaye aur maamool se zyaada der unke paas ruke.
