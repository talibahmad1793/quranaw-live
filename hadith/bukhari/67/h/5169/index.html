Sahih al-Bukhari 5169

Book 67: Wedlock, Marriage (Nikaah) · Hadith 104

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْوَارِثِ، عَنْ شُعَيْبٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَعْتَقَ صَفِيَّةَ، وَتَزَوَّجَهَا وَجَعَلَ عِتْقَهَا صَدَاقَهَا، وَأَوْلَمَ عَلَيْهَا بِحَيْسٍ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) manumitted Safiyya and then married her, and her Mahr was her manumission, and he gave a wedding banquet with Hais (a sort of sweet dish made from butter, cheese and dates)

Anas bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne Safiya ko azaad kiya phir unse nikaah kiya, aur unka Mahr unki azaadi hi thi, aur unhone Hais (makhan, panir aur khajoor se banaayi ek meethi cheez) se Walima ki dawat di.
