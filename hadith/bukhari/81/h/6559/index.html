Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 148

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا هُدْبَةُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا هَمَّامٌ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَنَسُ بْنُ مَالِكٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَخْرُجُ قَوْمٌ مِنَ النَّارِ بَعْدَ مَا مَسَّهُمْ مِنْهَا سَفْعٌ، فَيَدْخُلُونَ الْجَنَّةَ، فَيُسَمِّيهِمْ أَهْلُ الْجَنَّةِ الْجَهَنَّمِيِّينَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Some people will come out of the Fire after they have received a touch of the Fire, changing their color, and they will enter Paradise, and the people of Paradise will name them 'Al- Jahannamiyin' the (Hell) Fire people

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Kuch log Aag se nikaale jaayenge Aag ka thoda sa asar unpar ho jaane ke baad, unka rang badal jaayega, aur wo Jannat mein daakhil honge, aur Jannat waale unhein "Al-Jahannamiyin" (Jahannam ke log) kahenge.'
