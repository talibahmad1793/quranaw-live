Sahih al-Bukhari 6585

Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 173

Hadith text

وَقَالَ أَحْمَدُ بْنُ شَبِيبِ بْنِ سَعِيدٍ الْحَبَطِيُّ حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، عَنْ يُونُسَ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّهُ كَانَ يُحَدِّثُ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَرِدُ عَلَىَّ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ رَهْطٌ مِنْ أَصْحَابِي فَيُحَلَّئُونَ عَنِ الْحَوْضِ فَأَقُولُ يَا رَبِّ أَصْحَابِي‏.‏ فَيَقُولُ إِنَّكَ لاَ عِلْمَ لَكَ بِمَا أَحْدَثُوا بَعْدَكَ، إِنَّهُمُ ارْتَدُّوا عَلَى أَدْبَارِهِمُ الْقَهْقَرَى ‏"‏‏.‏

Abu Huraira narrated that the Prophet (ﷺ) said:"On the Day of Resurrection a group of companions will come to me, but will be driven away from the Lake-Fount, and I will say, 'O Lord (those are) my companions!' It will be said, 'You have no knowledge as to what they innovated after you left; they turned apostate as renegades (reverted from Islam)

Abu Huraira ne bataaya ke Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Qayamat ke din mere sahaabon ka ek giroh mere paas aayega, magar unhein Hauz se dhakel diya jaayega, aur main kahoonga, "Aye Rab! (Wo) mere saathi hain!" Kaha jaayega, "Tumhein nahi pata unhone tumhaare jaane ke baad kya naye kaam shuru kiye; wo murtad (Islam se peechhe hatne wale) ban gaye (Islam se peechhe hat gaye)."'
