Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 53

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ، عَنْ مُوسَى بْنِ عُقْبَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ سَدِّدُوا وَقَارِبُوا، وَاعْلَمُوا أَنْ لَنْ يُدْخِلَ أَحَدَكُمْ عَمَلُهُ الْجَنَّةَ، وَأَنَّ أَحَبَّ الأَعْمَالِ أَدْوَمُهَا إِلَى اللَّهِ، وَإِنْ قَلَّ ‏"‏

Narrated `Aisha:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Do good deeds properly, sincerely and moderately and know that your deeds will not make you enter Paradise, and that the most beloved deed to Allah is the most regular and constant even if it were little

Aisha bataati hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Nek amaal sahi tareeke se, khulusi se, aur mautadili se karo, aur jaan lo ke tumhaare amaal tumhein Jannat mein daakhil nahi karenge, aur Allah ke nazdeek sabse pyaara amal wo hai jo sabse zyaada mustaqil aur lagataar ho, chaahe wo kam hi kyun na ho.'
