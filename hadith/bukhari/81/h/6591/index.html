Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 179

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا حَرَمِيُّ بْنُ عُمَارَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ مَعْبَدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ حَارِثَةَ بْنَ وَهْبٍ، يَقُولُ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَذَكَرَ الْحَوْضَ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ كَمَا بَيْنَ الْمَدِينَةِ وَصَنْعَاءَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Haritha bin Wahb:I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) mentioning the Lake-Fount (Al-Kauthar), saying, "(The width of the Lake-Fount) is equal to the distance between Medina and Sana' (capital of Yemen)

Haritha bin Wahb bataate hain: Maine Nabi (SAW) ko Hauz (Al-Kauthar) ka zikr karte suna, kehte hue, '(Hauz ki chaudai) Madina aur Sana (Yemen ki raajdhaani) ke beech ki doori jitni hai.'
