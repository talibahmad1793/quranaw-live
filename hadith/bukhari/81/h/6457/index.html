Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 46

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا هُدْبَةُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا هَمَّامُ بْنُ يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا قَتَادَةُ، قَالَ كُنَّا نَأْتِي أَنَسَ بْنَ مَالِكٍ وَخَبَّازُهُ قَائِمٌ وَقَالَ كُلُوا فَمَا أَعْلَمُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم رَأَى رَغِيفًا مُرَقَّقًا، حَتَّى لَحِقَ بِاللَّهِ، وَلاَ رَأَى شَاةً سَمِيطًا بِعَيْنِهِ قَطُّ‏.‏

Narrated Qatada:We used to go to Anas bin Malik and see his baker standing (preparing the bread). Anas said, "Eat. I have not known that the Prophet (ﷺ) ever saw a thin well-baked loaf of bread till he died, and he never saw a roasted sheep with his eyes

Qatada bataate hain: Hum Anas bin Malik ke paas jaaya karte the aur unke rotiwaale (baker) ko khaana taiyaar karte dekhte. Anas ne kaha, 'Khaao. Mujhe nahi maaloom ke Nabi (SAW) ne kabhi apni maut tak ek patli, achhi tarah pakaayi hui roti dekhi ho, aur unhone kabhi apni aankhon se bhuni hui bakri nahi dekhi.'
