Sahih al-Bukhari 6429

Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 18

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، عَنْ أَبِي حَمْزَةَ، عَنِ الأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَبِيدَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ خَيْرُ النَّاسِ قَرْنِي، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ يَجِيءُ مِنْ بَعْدِهِمْ قَوْمٌ تَسْبِقُ شَهَادَتُهُمْ أَيْمَانَهُمْ وَأَيْمَانُهُمْ شَهَادَتَهُمْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The best people are those of my generation, and then those who will come after them (the next generation), and then those who will come after them (i.e. the next generation), and then after them, there will come people whose witness will precede their oaths, and whose oaths will precede their witness

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Sabse behtareen log mere daur ke log hain, phir wo jo unke baad aayenge, phir wo jo unke baad aayenge (yaani agla daur), aur unke baad, aise log aayenge jinki gawaahi unki qasam se pehle hogi, aur jinki qasam unki gawaahi se pehle hogi.'
