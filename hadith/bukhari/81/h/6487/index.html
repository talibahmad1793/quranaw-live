Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 76

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ حُجِبَتِ النَّارُ بِالشَّهَوَاتِ، وَحُجِبَتِ الْجَنَّةُ بِالْمَكَارِهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "The (Hell) Fire is surrounded by all kinds of desires and passions, while Paradise is surrounded by all kinds of disliked undesirable things

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, '(Jahannam ki) Aag har qism ki khwaahishon aur jazbaaton se ghiri hui hai, jabke Jannat har qism ki napasandidaa cheezon se ghiri hui hai.'
