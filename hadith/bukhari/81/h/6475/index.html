Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 64

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا، أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ، وَمَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلاَ يُؤْذِ جَارَهُ، وَمَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلْيُكْرِمْ ضَيْفَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should talk what is good or keep quiet, and whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should not hurt (or insult) his neighbor; and whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, should entertain his guest generously

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo Allah aur Aakhirat ke din par Iman rakhta hai, use achhi baat kehni chaahiye ya khaamosh rehna chaahiye, aur jo Allah aur Aakhirat ke din par Iman rakhta hai, use apne padosi ko takleef nahi deni chaahiye; aur jo Allah aur Aakhirat ke din par Iman rakhta hai, use apne mehmaan ki achhi khidmat karni chaahiye.'
