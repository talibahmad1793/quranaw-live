Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 79

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا نَظَرَ أَحَدُكُمْ إِلَى مَنْ فُضِّلَ عَلَيْهِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْخَلْقِ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَسْفَلَ مِنْهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "If anyone of you looked at a person who was made superior to him in property and (in good) appearance, then he should also look at the one who is inferior to him

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Agar tum mein se koi kisi aise shaks ki taraf dekhe jo daulat aur (achhi) soorat mein usse behtar ho, to use us shaks ki taraf bhi dekhna chaahiye jo usse kam ho.'
