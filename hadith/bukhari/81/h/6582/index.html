Sahih al-Bukhari 6582

Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) · Hadith 170

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، حَدَّثَنَا وُهَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لَيَرِدَنَّ عَلَىَّ نَاسٌ مِنْ أَصْحَابِي الْحَوْضَ، حَتَّى عَرَفْتُهُمُ اخْتُلِجُوا دُونِي، فَأَقُولُ أَصْحَابِي‏.‏ فَيَقُولُ لاَ تَدْرِي مَا أَحْدَثُوا بَعْدَكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Some of my companions will come to me at my Lake Fount, and after I recognize them, they will then be taken away from me, whereupon I will say, 'My companions!' Then it will be said, 'You do not know what they innovated (new things) in the religion after you

Anas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Mere kuch sahaaba mere Hauz par mere paas aayenge, aur unhein pehchaanne ke baad, unhein mujhse door kar diya jaayega, isper main kahoonga, "Mere saathi!" Phir kaha jaayega, "Tumhein nahi pata unhone tumhaare baad deen mein kya naye kaam (bidaat) shuru kiye."'
