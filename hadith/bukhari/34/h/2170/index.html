Sahih al-Bukhari 2170

Book 34: Sales and Trade · Hadith 121

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ مَالِكِ بْنِ أَوْسٍ، سَمِعَ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ الْبُرُّ بِالْبُرِّ رِبًا إِلاَّ هَاءَ وَهَاءَ، وَالشَّعِيرُ بِالشَّعِيرِ رِبًا إِلاَّ هَاءَ وَهَاءَ، وَالتَّمْرُ بِالتَّمْرِ رِبًا إِلاَّ هَاءَ وَهَاءَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The selling of wheat for wheat is Riba (usury) except if it is handed from hand to hand and equal in amount. Similarly the selling of barley for barley, is Riba except if it is from hand to hand and equal in amount, and dates for dates is usury except if it is from hand to hand and equal in amount. (See Riba-Fadl in the glossary)

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Gehun ka gehun se badla lena Riba (sood) hai siwaye jab yeh haath se haath ho aur maqdaar barabar ho. Isi tarah jau ka jau se badla lena bhi Riba hai siwaye jab yeh haath se haath ho aur maqdaar barabar ho, aur khajoor ka khajoor se badla lena sood hai siwaye jab yeh haath se haath ho aur maqdaar barabar ho."
