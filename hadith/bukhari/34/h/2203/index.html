Sahih al-Bukhari 2203

Book 34: Sales and Trade · Hadith 149

Hadith text

قَالَ أَبُو عَبْدِ اللَّهِ وَقَالَ لِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ أَخْبَرَنَا هِشَامٌ، أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ، يُخْبِرُ عَنْ نَافِعٍ، مَوْلَى ابْنِ عُمَرَ أَنَّ أَيُّمَا، نَخْلٍ بِيعَتْ قَدْ أُبِّرَتْ لَمْ يُذْكَرِ الثَّمَرُ، فَالثَّمَرُ لِلَّذِي أَبَّرَهَا، وَكَذَلِكَ الْعَبْدُ وَالْحَرْثُ‏.‏ سَمَّى لَهُ نَافِعٌ هَؤُلاَءِ الثَّلاَثَ‏.‏

Narrated Nafi', the freed slave of Ibn 'Umar:If pollinated date-palms are sold and nothing is mentioned (in the contract) about their fruits, the fruits will go to the person who has pollinated them, and so will be the case with the slave and the cultivator. Nafi' mentioned those three things

Nafi, Ibn Umar ke azaad kiye gulaam, bataate hain: Agar pollinated (nar-maada milaaee hui) khajoor ke darakht beche jaayein aur (samjhaute mein) unke phalon ka koi zikar na ho, phal usi shaks ko milenge jisne unme pollination karvaee thi, aur yehi hukum gulaam aur khetihar ke saath bhi hai. Nafi ne yeh teen cheezon ka zikar kiya.
