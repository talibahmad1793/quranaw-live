Sahih al-Bukhari 2229

Book 34: Sales and Trade · Hadith 176

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي ابْنُ مُحَيْرِيزٍ، أَنَّ أَبَا سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيَّ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ، بَيْنَمَا هُوَ جَالِسٌ عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّا نُصِيبُ سَبْيًا، فَنُحِبُّ الأَثْمَانَ، فَكَيْفَ تَرَى فِي الْعَزْلِ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ أَوَإِنَّكُمْ تَفْعَلُونَ ذَلِكَ لاَ عَلَيْكُمْ أَنْ لاَ تَفْعَلُوا ذَلِكُمْ، فَإِنَّهَا لَيْسَتْ نَسَمَةٌ كَتَبَ اللَّهُ أَنْ تَخْرُجَ إِلاَّ هِيَ خَارِجَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:that while he was sitting with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) he said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! We get female captives as our share of booty, and we are interested in their prices, what is your opinion about coitus interruptus?" The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Do you really do that? It is better for you not to do it. No soul that which Allah has destined to exist, but will surely come into existence

Abu Saeed Al-Khudri (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Jabke woh Allah ke Rasool ke saath baithe the, unhone kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Humein maal-e-ghanimat mein aurat qaidiyaan milti hain, aur humein unki qeemat ki fikr hai, Al-Azl (safar se bahar rehna, coitus interruptus) ke baare mein aapki kya raay hai?" Nabi ne kaha: "Kya tum sach mein aisa karte ho? Behtar hai tum aisa na karo. Koi bhi rooh jise Allah ne wujood mein aane ka faisla kiya hai, zaroor wujood mein aayegi."
