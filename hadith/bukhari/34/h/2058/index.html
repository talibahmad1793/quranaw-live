Sahih al-Bukhari 2058

Book 34: Sales and Trade · Hadith 12

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا طَلْقُ بْنُ غَنَّامٍ، حَدَّثَنَا زَائِدَةُ، عَنْ حُصَيْنٍ، عَنْ سَالِمٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي جَابِرٌ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ بَيْنَمَا نَحْنُ نُصَلِّي مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذْ أَقْبَلَتْ مِنَ الشَّأْمِ عِيرٌ، تَحْمِلُ طَعَامًا، فَالْتَفَتُوا إِلَيْهَا، حَتَّى مَا بَقِيَ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلاَّ اثْنَا عَشَرَ رَجُلاً فَنَزَلَتْ ‏{‏وَإِذَا رَأَوْا تِجَارَةً أَوْ لَهْوًا انْفَضُّوا إِلَيْهَا‏}‏

Narrated Jabir:While we were offering the prayer with the Prophet (ﷺ) a caravan carrying food came from Sham. The people looked towards the caravan (and went to it) and only twelve persons remained with the Prophet. So, the Divine Inspiration came; "But when they see some bargain or some amusement, they disperse headlong to it

Jabir (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Jabke hum Nabi ke saath namaz padh rahe the, Sham se khaana lane wala ek qafila aaya. Log qafile ki taraf dekhne lage (aur uski taraf gaye) aur sirf barah log Nabi ke saath rah gaye. Isliye yeh wahi nazil hui: "Magar jab woh koi saudebaazi ya tamaasha dekhte hain, woh usi taraf bhaag jaate hain (namaz chhod kar)."
