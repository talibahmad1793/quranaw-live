Sahih al-Bukhari 2209

Book 34: Sales and Trade · Hadith 155

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، هِشَامُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي بِشْرٍ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ كُنْتُ عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهْوَ يَأْكُلُ جُمَّارًا، فَقَالَ ‏"‏ مِنَ الشَّجَرِ شَجَرَةٌ كَالرَّجُلِ الْمُؤْمِنِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَأَرَدْتُ أَنْ أَقُولَ هِيَ النَّخْلَةُ‏.‏ فَإِذَا أَنَا أَحْدَثُهُمْ قَالَ ‏"‏ هِيَ النَّخْلَةُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:I was with the Prophet (ﷺ) while he was eating fresh dates. He said, "From the trees there is a tree which resembles a faithful believer." I wanted to say that it was the date palm, but I was the youngest among them (so I kept quiet). He added, "It is the date palm

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Main Nabi ke saath tha jabke woh taazi khajoor kha rahe the. Aap ne kaha: "Darakhton mein se ek darakht hai jo ek sacche momin jaisa hai." Main kehna chahta tha ke yeh khajoor ka darakht hai, magar main unme sabse jawan tha (isliye chup raha). Aap ne joda: "Yeh khajoor ka darakht hai."
