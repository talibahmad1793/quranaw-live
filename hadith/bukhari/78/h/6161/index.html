Sahih al-Bukhari 6161

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 187

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادٌ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ الْبُنَانِيِّ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ،‏.‏ وَأَيُّوبَ عَنْ أَبِي قِلاَبَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، قَالَ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي سَفَرٍ، وَكَانَ مَعَهُ غُلاَمٌ لَهُ أَسْوَدُ، يُقَالُ لَهُ أَنْجَشَةُ، يَحْدُو، فَقَالَ لَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ وَيْحَكَ يَا أَنْجَشَةُ رُوَيْدَكَ بِالْقَوَارِيرِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) was on a journey and he had a black slave called Anjasha, and he was driving the camels (very fast, and there were women riding on those camels). Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Waihaka (May Allah be merciful to you), O Anjasha! Drive slowly (the camels) with the glass vessels (women)

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ek safar par the aur unke saath Anjasha naami ek kaale ghulaam the, jo oonton ko (bahut tez) haank rahe the, aur un oonton par auraten sawaar thin. Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne kaha, 'Waihaka (Allah tumpar reham kare), Aye Anjasha! (Oonton ko) dheere-dheere le jao, unmein shishe ke bartan (auraten) hain.'
