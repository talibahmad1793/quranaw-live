Sahih al-Bukhari 5982

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 13

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي ابْنُ عُثْمَانَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ مُوسَى بْنَ طَلْحَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ، قَالَ قِيلَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَخْبِرْنِي بِعَمَلٍ، يُدْخِلُنِي الْجَنَّةَ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Ayyub Al-Ansari:It was said" "O Allah's Messenger! Inform me of a deed which will make me enter Paradise." (continues through a different chain in the next hadith)

Abu Ayyub Al-Ansari bataate hain: Kaha gaya, 'Aye Allah ke Rasool! Mujhe koi aisa amal bataiye jo mujhe Jannat mein daakhil karaaye.' (Ye agli hadees mein ek doosre silsile se jaari hai.)
