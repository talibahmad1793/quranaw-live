Sahih al-Bukhari 6107

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 134

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ، أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو الْمُغِيرَةِ، حَدَّثَنَا الأَوْزَاعِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا الزُّهْرِيُّ، عَنْ حُمَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ مَنْ حَلَفَ مِنْكُمْ فَقَالَ فِي حَلِفِهِ بِاللاَّتِ وَالْعُزَّى‏.‏ فَلْيَقُلْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ‏.‏ وَمَنْ قَالَ لِصَاحِبِهِ تَعَالَ أُقَامِرْكَ، فَلْيَتَصَدَّقْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said: "Whoever amongst you swears, (saying by error) in his oath 'By Al-Lat and Al- Uzza', then he should say, 'None has the right to be worshipped but Allah.' And whoever says to his companions, 'Come let me gamble' with you, then he must give something in charity (as an expiation for such a sin)." (See Hadith No)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo koi tum mein se (galti se) apni qasam mein "Al-Lat aur Al-Uzza ki qasam" kahe, use "La ilaha illallah" (Allah ke siwa koi ibaadat ke laayak nahi) kehna chaahiye. Aur jo koi apne saathiyon se kahe, "Aao, main tumse jua khelun," use kuch sadqa dena chaahiye (is gunaah ke kaffare ke taur par).'
