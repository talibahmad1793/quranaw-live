Sahih al-Bukhari 6198

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 222

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْعَلاَءِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، عَنْ بُرَيْدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي بُرْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي بُرْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى، قَالَ وُلِدَ لِي غُلاَمٌ، فَأَتَيْتُ بِهِ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَسَمَّاهُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، فَحَنَّكَهُ بِتَمْرَةٍ، وَدَعَا لَهُ بِالْبَرَكَةِ، وَدَفَعَهُ إِلَىَّ، وَكَانَ أَكْبَرَ وَلَدِ أَبِي مُوسَى‏.‏

Narrated Abu Musa:I got a son and I took him to the Prophet (ﷺ) who named him Ibrahim, and put in his mouth the juice of a date fruit (which be himself had chewed?, and invoked for Allah's blessing upon him, and then gave him back to me. He was the eldest son of Abu Musa)

Abu Musa bataate hain: Mere ghar ek beta paida hua aur main use Nabi (SAW) ke paas le gaya, jinhone uska naam Ibrahim rakha, khajoor ka ras (jo unhone khud chabaaya tha) uske munh mein daala, Allah se uske liye barkat ki dua ki, phir use mujhe wapas kar diya. (Ye Abu Musa ka sabse bada beta tha.)
