Sahih al-Bukhari 6220

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 244

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عُقْبَةَ بْنَ صُهْبَانَ الأَزْدِيَّ، يُحَدِّثُ عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مُغَفَّلٍ الْمُزَنِيِّ، قَالَ نَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنِ الْخَذْفِ وَقَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّهُ لاَ يَقْتُلُ الصَّيْدَ، وَلاَ يَنْكَأُ الْعَدُوَّ، وَإِنَّهُ يَفْقَأُ الْعَيْنَ، وَيَكْسِرُ السِّنَّ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin Mughaffal Al-Muzani:The Prophet (ﷺ) forbade the throwing of stones (with the thumb and the index or middle finger), and said "It neither hunts a game nor kills (or hurts) an enemy, but it gouges out an eye or breaks a tooth

Abdullah bin Mughaffal Al-Muzani bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne kankar phenkne (angoothe aur shahaadat ya beech ki ungli se) se mana kiya, aur kaha, 'Isse na shikaar hota hai na dushman ko maara ya takleef pahunchaayi jaa sakti hai, magar isse aankh phoot sakti hai ya daant toot sakta hai.'
