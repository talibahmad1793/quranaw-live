Sahih al-Bukhari 6177

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 201

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ الْغَادِرُ يُرْفَعُ لَهُ لِوَاءٌ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ، يُقَالُ هَذِهِ غَدْرَةُ فُلاَنِ بْنِ فُلاَنٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "For every betrayer (perfidious person), a flag will be raised on the Day of Resurrection, and it will be announced (publicly) 'This is the betrayal (perfidy) of so-and-so, the son of so-and-so

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Har dhoke-baaz (badaeenat karne wale) ke liye Qayamat ke din ek jhanda uthaaya jaayega, aur elaan kiya jaayega, "Ye falaan bin falaan ki bewafaai hai."'
