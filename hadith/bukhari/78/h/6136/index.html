Sahih al-Bukhari 6136

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 163

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ مَهْدِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ أَبِي حَصِينٍ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلاَ يُؤْذِ جَارَهُ، وَمَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلْيُكْرِمْ ضَيْفَهُ، وَمَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, should not hurt his neighbor and whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, should serve his guest generously and whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, should speak what is good or keep silent

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo Allah aur Aakhirat ke din par Iman rakhta hai, use apne padosi ko takleef nahi deni chaahiye, aur jo Allah aur Aakhirat ke din par Iman rakhta hai, use apne mehmaan ki achhi khidmat karni chaahiye, aur jo Allah aur Aakhirat ke din par Iman rakhta hai, use achhi baat kehni chaahiye ya khaamosh rehna chaahiye.'
