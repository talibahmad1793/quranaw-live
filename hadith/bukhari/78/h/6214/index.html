Sahih al-Bukhari 6214

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 238

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ بُكَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ عُقَيْلٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَبَا سَلَمَةَ بْنَ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، يَقُولُ أَخْبَرَنِي جَابِرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ ثُمَّ فَتَرَ عَنِّي الْوَحْىُ، فَبَيْنَا أَنَا أَمْشِي سَمِعْتُ صَوْتًا مِنَ السَّمَاءِ، فَرَفَعْتُ بَصَرِي إِلَى السَّمَاءِ فَإِذَا الْمَلَكُ الَّذِي جَاءَنِي بِحِرَاءٍ قَاعِدٌ عَلَى كُرْسِيٍّ بَيْنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالأَرْضِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:That he heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) saying. "Then there was a pause in the revelation of the Divine Inspiration to me. Then while I was walking all of a sudden I heard a voice from the sky, and I raised my sight towards the sky and saw the same angel who had visited me in the cave of Hira,' sitting on a chair between the sky and the earth

Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain ke unhone Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko ye kehte suna, 'Phir wahi (Divine Revelation) mujhpar naazil hone mein ek waqfa aa gaya. Phir jab main chal raha tha, achaanak maine aasman se ek aawaaz suni, aur maine apni nigaah aasman ki taraf uthaayi aur wahi farishta dekha jo Hira ki gufaa mein mere paas aaya tha, ek kursi par baithe hue aasman aur zameen ke beech.'
