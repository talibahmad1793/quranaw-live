Sahih al-Bukhari 6046

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 76

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ سِنَانٍ، حَدَّثَنَا فُلَيْحُ بْنُ سُلَيْمَانَ، حَدَّثَنَا هِلاَلُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ لَمْ يَكُنْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَاحِشًا وَلاَ لَعَّانًا وَلاَ سَبَّابًا، كَانَ يَقُولُ عِنْدَ الْمَعْتَبَةِ ‏ "‏ مَا لَهُ، تَرِبَ جَبِينُهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) was neither a Fahish (one who had a bad tongue) nor a Sabbaba (one who abuses others) and he used to say while admonishing somebody, "What is wrong with him? May dust be on his forehead

Anas bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) na Fahish the (badzabaan) na Sabbaba (doosron ko bura-bhala kehne wale), aur jab kisi ko naseehat dete, to kehte, 'Isko kya hua hai? Iski peshaani par mitti pade!'
