Sahih al-Bukhari 5988

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 19

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ مَخْلَدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ الرَّحِمَ سُجْنَةٌ مِنَ الرَّحْمَنِ، فَقَالَ اللَّهُ مَنْ وَصَلَكِ وَصَلْتُهُ، وَمَنْ قَطَعَكِ قَطَعْتُهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The word 'Ar-Rahm (womb) derives its name from Ar-Rahman (i.e., one of the names of Allah) and Allah said: 'I will keep good relation with the one who will keep good relation with you, (womb i.e. Kith and Kin) and sever the relation with him who will sever the relation with you, (womb, i.e. Kith and Kin)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Lafz "Ar-Rahm" (kokh) apna naam "Ar-Rahman" (yaani Allah ke naamon mein se ek) se leta hai, aur Allah ne farmaya, "Main us se achhe taaluqaat rakhunga jo tumse (kokh, yaani rishtedaaron se) achhe taaluqaat rakhe, aur us se rishta todunga jo tumse rishta tode."'
