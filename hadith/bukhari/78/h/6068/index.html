Sahih al-Bukhari 6068

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 98

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ بُكَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، بِهَذَا وَقَالَتْ دَخَلَ عَلَىَّ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمًا وَقَالَ ‏ "‏ يَا عَائِشَةُ مَا أَظُنُّ فُلاَنًا وَفُلاَنًا يَعْرِفَانِ دِينَنَا الَّذِي نَحْنُ عَلَيْهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Al-Laith:`Aisha said "The Prophet (ﷺ) entered upon me one day and said, 'O `Aisha! I do not think that so-and-so and so-and-so know anything of our religion which we follow

Al-Laith bataate hain: Aisha ne kaha, 'Nabi (SAW) ek din mere paas aaye aur kaha, "Aye Aisha! Mujhe nahi lagta falaan aur falaan hamare us deen ke baare mein kuch bhi jaante hain jiski hum pairvi karte hain."'
