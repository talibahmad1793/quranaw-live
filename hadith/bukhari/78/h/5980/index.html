Sahih al-Bukhari 5980

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ عُقَيْلٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، أَنَّ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ عَبَّاسٍ، أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّ أَبَا سُفْيَانَ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّ هِرَقْلَ أَرْسَلَ إِلَيْهِ فَقَالَ يَعْنِي النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَأْمُرُنَا بِالصَّلاَةِ وَالصَّدَقَةِ وَالْعَفَافِ وَالصِّلَةِ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Sufyan:That Heraclius sent for him and said, "What did he, i.e. the Prophet (ﷺ) order you?" I replied, "He orders us to offer prayers; to give alms; to be chaste; and to keep good relations with our relatives

Abu Sufyan bataate hain: Heraclius ne unhein bulwaaya aur kaha, 'Unhone (yaani Nabi SAW ne) tumhein kya hukm diya hai?' Maine jawab diya, 'Wo humein namaz padhne, sadqa dene, paakdaaman rehne, aur apne rishtedaaron ke saath achhe taaluqaat rakhne ka hukm dete hain.'
