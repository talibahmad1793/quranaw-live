Sahih al-Bukhari 6002

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 33

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَضَعَ صَبِيًّا فِي حِجْرِهِ يُحَنِّكُهُ، فَبَالَ عَلَيْهِ، فَدَعَا بِمَاءٍ فَأَتْبَعَهُ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:The Prophet (ﷺ) took a child in his lap for Tahnik (i.e. he chewed a date in his mouth and put its juice in the mouth of the child). The child urinated on him, so he asked for water and poured it over the place of the urine

Aisha bataati hain: Nabi (SAW) ne ek bacche ko apni god mein liya Tahnik ke liye (yaani unhone apne munh mein khajoor chabaa kar uska ras bacche ke munh mein daala). Bacche ne unpar peshaab kar diya, isliye unhone paani maanga aur peshaab wali jagah par daala.
