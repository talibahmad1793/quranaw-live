Sahih al-Bukhari 6110

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 137

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ إِسْمَاعِيلَ بْنِ أَبِي خَالِدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا قَيْسُ بْنُ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِي مَسْعُودٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ أَتَى رَجُلٌ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ إِنِّي لأَتَأَخَّرُ عَنْ صَلاَةِ الْغَدَاةِ مِنْ أَجْلِ فُلاَنٍ مِمَّا يُطِيلُ بِنَا قَالَ فَمَا رَأَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَطُّ أَشَدَّ غَضَبًا فِي مَوْعِظَةٍ مِنْهُ يَوْمَئِذٍ قَالَ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّ مِنْكُمْ مُنَفِّرِينَ، فَأَيُّكُمْ مَا صَلَّى بِالنَّاسِ فَلْيَتَجَوَّزْ، فَإِنَّ فِيهِمُ الْمَرِيضَ وَالْكَبِيرَ وَذَا الْحَاجَةِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Mas`ud:A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said "I keep away from the morning prayer only because such and such person prolongs the prayer when he leads us in it. The narrator added: I had never seen Allah's Apostle more furious in giving advice than he was on that day. He said, "O people! There are some among you who make others dislike good deeds) cause the others to have aversion (to congregational prayers). Beware! Whoever among you leads the people in prayer should not prolong it, because among them there are the sick, the old, and the needy." (See Hadith No. 670, Vol)

Abu Mas'ud bataate hain: Ek shaks Nabi (SAW) ke paas aaya aur kaha, 'Main subah ki namaz mein nahi aata sirf isliye kyunke falaan shaks jab humein namaz padhaata hai to bahut lambi kar deta hai.' (Ravi ne aage kaha: Maine kabhi Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko naseehat dete waqt itna gussa nahi dekha jitna us din the.) Unhone kaha, 'Aye logo! Tum mein se kuch aise hain jo doosron ko (nek amaal se, yaani jamaat ki namaz se) nafrat karwaate hain. Khabardaar! Tum mein se jo koi logon ko namaz padhaaye, use use lambi nahi karni chaahiye, kyunke unmein bimaar, budhe aur zaroortmand log bhi hote hain.'
