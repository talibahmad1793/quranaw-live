Sahih al-Bukhari 6010

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 41

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَامَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي صَلاَةٍ وَقُمْنَا مَعَهُ، فَقَالَ أَعْرَابِيٌّ وَهْوَ فِي الصَّلاَةِ اللَّهُمَّ ارْحَمْنِي وَمُحَمَّدًا، وَلاَ تَرْحَمْ مَعَنَا أَحَدًا‏.‏ فَلَمَّا سَلَّمَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ لِلأَعْرَابِيِّ ‏ "‏ لَقَدْ حَجَّرْتَ وَاسِعًا ‏"‏‏.‏ يُرِيدُ رَحْمَةَ اللَّهِ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) stood up for the prayer and we too stood up along with him. Then a bedouin shouted while offering prayer. "O Allah! Bestow Your Mercy on me and Muhammad only and do not bestow it on anybody else along with us." When the Prophet (ﷺ) had finished his prayer with Taslim, he said to the Bedouin, "You have limited (narrowed) a very vast (thing)," meaning Allah's Mercy

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) namaz ke liye khade hue aur hum bhi unke saath khade hue. Phir ek badawi ne namaz padhte hue chillaaya, 'Aye Allah! Sirf mujhpar aur Muhammad par apni rehmat naazil kar, hamare siwa kisi aur par nahi.' Jab Nabi (SAW) ne apni namaz Taslim ke saath khatam ki, unhone badawi se kaha, 'Tumne ek bahut wasee cheez ko tang kar diya,' Allah ki rehmat ki taraf ishara karte hue.
