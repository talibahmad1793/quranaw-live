Sahih al-Bukhari 6028

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) · Hadith 58

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْعَلاَءِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، عَنْ بُرَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي بُرْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ كَانَ إِذَا أَتَاهُ السَّائِلُ أَوْ صَاحِبُ الْحَاجَةِ قَالَ ‏ "‏ اشْفَعُوا فَلْتُؤْجَرُوا، وَلْيَقْضِ اللَّهُ عَلَى لِسَانِ رَسُولِهِ مَا شَاءَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Musa: Whenever a beggar or a person in need came to the Prophet, the Prophet would say "Help and recommend him and you will receive the reward for it, and Allah will bring about what he will through His Prophet's tongue

Abu Musa bataate hain: Jab bhi koi bhikaari ya zaroortmand shaks Nabi (SAW) ke paas aata, Nabi (SAW) kehte, 'Uski madad karo aur usse sifaarish karo, tumhein iska ajr milega, aur Allah apne Nabi ki zubaan se jo chaahega wo poora karega.'
