Sahih al-Bukhari 1431

Book 24: Obligatory Charity Tax (Zakat) · Hadith 34

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَدِيٌّ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ خَرَجَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ عِيدٍ فَصَلَّى رَكْعَتَيْنِ لَمْ يُصَلِّ قَبْلُ وَلاَ بَعْدُ، ثُمَّ مَالَ عَلَى النِّسَاءِ وَمَعَهُ بِلاَلٌ، فَوَعَظَهُنَّ وَأَمَرَهُنَّ أَنْ يَتَصَدَّقْنَ، فَجَعَلَتِ الْمَرْأَةُ تُلْقِي الْقُلْبَ وَالْخُرْصَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) went out for the `Id prayer on the `Id day and offered a two rak`at prayer; and he neither offered a prayer before it or after it. Then he went towards the women along with Bilal. He preached them and ordered them to give in charity. And some (amongst the women) started giving their forearm bangles and earrings

Ibn Abbas (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Nabi Eid ke din Eid ki namaz ke liye bahar gaye aur do rakat padhi, aur usse pehle ya baad koi (aur namaz) nahi padhi. Phir Aap Bilal ke saath aurton ki taraf gaye. Aap ne unhe naseehat ki aur sadqa dene ka hukum diya. Kuch aurton ne apni baaliyaan aur kaan ki angoothiyan dena shuru kiya.
