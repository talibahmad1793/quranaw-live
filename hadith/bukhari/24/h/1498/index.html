Sahih al-Bukhari 1498

Book 24: Obligatory Charity Tax (Zakat) · Hadith 98

Hadith text

وَقَالَ اللَّيْثُ حَدَّثَنِي جَعْفَرُ بْنُ رَبِيعَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ هُرْمُزَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أَنَّ رَجُلاً مِنْ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ سَأَلَ بَعْضَ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ بِأَنْ يُسْلِفَهُ أَلْفَ دِينَارٍ، فَدَفَعَهَا إِلَيْهِ، فَخَرَجَ فِي الْبَحْرِ، فَلَمْ يَجِدْ مَرْكَبًا، فَأَخَذَ خَشَبَةً فَنَقَرَهَا فَأَدْخَلَ فِيهَا أَلْفَ دِينَارٍ، فَرَمَى بِهَا فِي الْبَحْرِ، فَخَرَجَ الرَّجُلُ الَّذِي كَانَ أَسْلَفَهُ، فَإِذَا بِالْخَشَبَةِ فَأَخَذَهَا لأَهْلِهِ حَطَبًا ـ فَذَكَرَ الْحَدِيثَ ـ فَلَمَّا نَشَرَهَا وَجَدَ الْمَالَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "A man from Bani Israel asked someone from Bani Israel to give him a loan of one thousand Dinars and the later gave it to him. The debtor went on a voyage (when the time for the payment of the debt became due) but he did not find a boat, so he took a piece of wood and bored it and put 1000 Dinars in it and threw it into the sea. The creditor went out and took the piece of wood to his family to be used as fire-wood." (See Hadith No. 488 B, Vol. 3). And the Prophet (ﷺ) narrated the narration (and said), "When he sawed the wood, he found his money

Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Bani Israel ke ek shaks ne doosre Bani Israel se hazaar Dinar ka qarz maanga aur usne de diya. Qarzdaar ek safar par gaya (jab qarz wapas karne ka waqt aaya) magar usse koi kashti nahi mili, isliye usne ek lakdi ka tukda lekar usme surakh kiya aur usme hazaar Dinar rakhkar samundar mein phenk diya. Qarz dene wala bahar gaya aur us lakdi ke tukde ko apne ghar leke gaya jalane ke liye." Aur Nabi ne yeh kahani (aage) bayan ki (aur kaha): "Jab usne lakdi kaati, usne apna paisa paaya."
