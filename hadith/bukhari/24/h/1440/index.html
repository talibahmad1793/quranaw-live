Book 24: Obligatory Charity Tax (Zakat) · Hadith 43

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، عَنْ شَقِيقٍ، عَنْ مَسْرُوقٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتْ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ إِذَا أَطْعَمَتِ الْمَرْأَةُ مِنْ بَيْتِ زَوْجِهَا غَيْرَ مُفْسِدَةٍ، لَهَا أَجْرُهَا، وَلَهُ مِثْلُهُ، وَلِلْخَازِنِ مِثْلُ ذَلِكَ، لَهُ بِمَا اكْتَسَبَ، وَلَهَا بِمَا أَنْفَقَتْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrate Aisha:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "If a woman gives in charity from her husband's house .." The Prophet (ﷺ) also said, "If a lady gives meals (in charity) from her husband's house without spoiling her husband's property, she will get a reward and her husband will also get a reward likewise. The husband will get a reward because of his earnings and the woman because of her spending

Ayesha (رضی اللہ عنہا) bataati hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Agar aurat apne shohar ke ghar se sadqa deti hai..." Nabi ne yeh bhi farmaya: "Agar koi aurat apne shohar ke ghar se khana (sadqa mein) deti hai, uske shohar ka maal barbaad kiye bina, use ajr milega, aur uske shohar ko bhi utna hi ajr milega. Shohar ko uski kamaai ki wajah se ajr milega, aur aurat ko uske kharch karne ki wajah se."
