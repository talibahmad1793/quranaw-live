Sahih al-Bukhari 1441

Book 24: Obligatory Charity Tax (Zakat) · Hadith 44

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ يَحْيَى، أَخْبَرَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ شَقِيقٍ، عَنْ مَسْرُوقٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا أَنْفَقَتِ الْمَرْأَةُ مِنْ طَعَامِ بَيْتِهَا غَيْرَ مُفْسِدَةٍ فَلَهَا أَجْرُهَا، وَلِلزَّوْجِ بِمَا اكْتَسَبَ، وَلِلْخَازِنِ مِثْلُ ذَلِكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "When a woman gives in charity from her house meals in Allah's Cause without spoiling her husband's property, she will get a reward for it, and her husband will also get the reward for his earnings and the storekeeper will get a reward likewise

Ayesha (رضی اللہ عنہا) bataati hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Jab koi aurat apne ghar se Allah ki raah mein khana sadqa deti hai, apne shohar ka maal barbaad kiye bina, use uska ajr milta hai, aur uske shohar ko bhi uski kamaai ka ajr milta hai, aur bhandaari ko bhi utna hi ajr milta hai."
