Sahih al-Bukhari 1825

Book 28: Penalty of Hunting while on Pilgrimage · Hadith 5

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُتْبَةَ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، عَنِ الصَّعْبِ بْنِ جَثَّامَةَ اللَّيْثِيِّ، أَنَّهُ أَهْدَى لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم حِمَارًا وَحْشِيًّا، وَهْوَ بِالأَبْوَاءِ أَوْ بِوَدَّانَ فَرَدَّهُ عَلَيْهِ، فَلَمَّا رَأَى مَا فِي وَجْهِهِ قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّا لَمْ نَرُدَّهُ عَلَيْكَ إِلاَّ أَنَّا حُرُمٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Abbas:From As-Sa'b bin Jath-thama Al-Laithi that the latter presented an onager to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) while he was at Al-Abwa' or at Waddan, and he refused it. On noticing the signs of some unpleasant feeling of disappointment on his (As-Sab's) face, the Prophet (ﷺ) said to him, "I have only returned it because I am Muhrim

Abdullah bin Abbas (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain, As-Sa'b bin Jath-thama Al-Laithi ke hawale se: Unhone (As-Sa'b ne) Allah ke Rasool ko ek jangli gadha tohfe mein diya jabke Aap Al-Abwa ya Waddan mein the, aur Aap ne ise wapas kar diya. Jab Nabi ne unke (As-Sa'b ke) chehre par naraazgi ke asaraat dekhe, Aap ne unse kaha: "Maine ise sirf isliye wapas kiya kyunke main Muhrim (Ihram mein) hoon."
