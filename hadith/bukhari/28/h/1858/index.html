Sahih al-Bukhari 1858

Book 28: Penalty of Hunting while on Pilgrimage · Hadith 38

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ يُونُسَ، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمُ بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ يُوسُفَ، عَنِ السَّائِبِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، قَالَ حُجَّ بِي مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَنَا ابْنُ سَبْعِ سِنِينَ‏.‏

Narrated As-Sa'ib bin Yazid:(While in the company of my parents) I was made to perform Hajj with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and I was a seven-year-old boy then. (Fath-ul-Bari, p.443, Vol)

As-Saib bin Yazid bataate hain: (Apne maa-baap ki sohbat mein) mujhe Allah ke Rasool ke saath Hajj karwaya gaya jabke main saat saal ka ladka tha.
