Sahih al-Bukhari 2912

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 125

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَبَّاسٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ سُفْيَانَ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ الْحَارِثِ، قَالَ مَا تَرَكَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلاَّ سِلاَحَهُ وَبَغْلَةً بَيْضَاءَ وَأَرْضًا جَعَلَهَا صَدَقَةً‏.‏

Narrated `Amr bin Al-Harith:The Prophet (ﷺ) did not leave behind him after his death, anything except his arms, his white mule, and a piece of land at Khaibar which he left to be given in charity

Amr bin Al-Harith bataate hain: Nabi ne apni maut ke baad apne peeche kuch nahi chhoda siwaye apne hathiyaar, apne safed khachchar, aur Khaibar mein zameen ka ek tukda jise unhone sadqe mein dene ke liye chhoda tha.
