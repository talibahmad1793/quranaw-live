Sahih al-Bukhari 2814

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 30

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ إِسْحَاقَ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ دَعَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى الَّذِينَ قَتَلُوا أَصْحَابَ بِئْرِ مَعُونَةَ ثَلاَثِينَ غَدَاةً، عَلَى رِعْلٍ وَذَكْوَانَ وَعُصَيَّةَ عَصَتِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ، قَالَ أَنَسٌ أُنْزِلَ فِي الَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا بِبِئْرِ مَعُونَةَ قُرْآنٌ قَرَأْنَاهُ ثُمَّ نُسِخَ بَعْدُ بَلِّغُوا قَوْمَنَا أَنْ قَدْ لَقِينَا رَبَّنَا فَرَضِيَ عَنَّا وَرَضِينَا عَنْهُ‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:For thirty days Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) invoked Allah to curse those who had killed the companions of Bir- Mauna; he invoked evil upon the tribes of Ral, Dhakwan, and Usaiya who disobeyed Allah and His Apostle. There was revealed about those who were killed at Bir-Mauna a Qur'anic Verse we used to recite, but it was cancelled later on. The Verse was: "Inform our people that we have met our Lord. He is pleased with us and He has made us pleased

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Teen din tak, Allah ke Rasool ne Allah se dua ki ke woh un logon par laanat bheje jinhone Bir-Mauna ke sahaba ko qatl kiya tha; unhone Ral, Dhakwan, aur Usaiya qabilon par bad-dua ki jinhone Allah aur uske Rasool ki na-farmani ki thi. Bir-Mauna mein shaheed hue logon ke baare mein ek Quranic Ayat nazil hui jise hum padhte the, magar baad mein woh mansookh ho gayi. Woh Ayat thi: "Humari qaum ko batao ke hum apne Rab se mil chuke hain, woh humse khush hai aur usne humein khush kiya hai."
