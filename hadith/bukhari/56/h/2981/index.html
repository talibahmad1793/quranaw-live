Sahih al-Bukhari 2981

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 190

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّابِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ يَحْيَى، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي بُشَيْرُ بْنُ يَسَارٍ، أَنَّ سُوَيْدَ بْنَ النُّعْمَانِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ، خَرَجَ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَامَ خَيْبَرَ، حَتَّى إِذَا كَانُوا بِالصَّهْبَاءِ ـ وَهْىَ مِنْ خَيْبَرَ وَهْىَ أَدْنَى خَيْبَرَ ـ فَصَلَّوُا الْعَصْرَ، فَدَعَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالأَطْعِمَةِ، فَلَمْ يُؤْتَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلاَّ بِسَوِيقٍ، فَلُكْنَا فَأَكَلْنَا وَشَرِبْنَا، ثُمَّ قَامَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَمَضْمَضَ وَمَضْمَضْنَا، وَصَلَّيْنَا‏.‏

Narrated Suwaid bin An-Nu`man:That he went out in the company of the Prophet (ﷺ) during the year of Khaibar (campaign) till they reached a place called As-Sahba', the lower part of Khaibar. They offered the `Asr prayer (there) and the Prophet asked for the food. Nothing but Sawiq was brought to the Prophet. So, they chewed it and ate it and drank water. After that the Prophet (ﷺ) got up, washed his mouth, and they too washed their mouths and then offered the prayer

Suwaid bin An-Nu'man bataate hain: Woh Nabi ke saath Khaibar ke saal (ki muhim mein) nikle jab tak woh As-Sahba naam ki jagah pahunche, jo Khaibar ka neeche wala hissa hai. Unhone (wahan) Asr ki namaz padhi aur Nabi ne khaana maanga. Nabi ke paas sirf Sawiq (bhune jau ka aata) laaya gaya. Isliye unhone use chabaya aur khaya aur paani piya. Iske baad Nabi uthe, apna mun dhoya, aur unhone (sahaba ne) bhi apna mun dhoya aur phir namaz padhi.
