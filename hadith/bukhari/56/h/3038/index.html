Sahih al-Bukhari 3038

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 244

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا وَكِيعٌ، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ أَبِي بُرْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَدِّهِ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَعَثَ مُعَاذًا وَأَبَا مُوسَى إِلَى الْيَمَنِ قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَسِّرَا وَلاَ تُعَسِّرَا، وَبَشِّرَا وَلاَ تُنَفِّرَا، وَتَطَاوَعَا وَلاَ تَخْتَلِفَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Burda:That his father said, "The Prophet (ﷺ) sent Mu`adh and Abu Musa to Yemen telling them. 'Treat the people with ease and don't be hard on them; give them glad tidings and don't fill them with aversion; and love each other, and don't differ

Abu Burda bataate hain apne waalid ke hawale se: Nabi ne Mu'adh aur Abu Musa ko Yemen bheja aur unse kaha, "Logon ke saath narmi se pesh aao aur unpar sakhti mat karo; unhe khushkhabri do aur unhe nafrat mat dilao; aur ek-doosre se mohabbat karo, aur ikhtilaf mat karo."
