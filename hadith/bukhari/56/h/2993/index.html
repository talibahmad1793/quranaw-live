Sahih al-Bukhari 2993

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 202

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ حُصَيْنِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ سَالِمِ بْنِ أَبِي الْجَعْدِ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ كُنَّا إِذَا صَعِدْنَا كَبَّرْنَا، وَإِذَا نَزَلْنَا سَبَّحْنَا‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:Whenever we went up a place we would say, "Allahu--Akbar (i.e. Allah is Greater)", and whenever we went down a place we would say, "Subhan Allah

Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Jab bhi hum kisi oonchi jagah jate, hum kehte, "Allahu Akbar (yaani Allah sabse bada hai)," aur jab bhi hum kisi neeche jagah jate, hum kehte, "Subhan Allah."
