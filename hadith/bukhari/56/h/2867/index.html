Sahih al-Bukhari 2867

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 83

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الأَعْلَى بْنُ حَمَّادٍ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ زُرَيْعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدٌ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه أَنَّ أَهْلَ، الْمَدِينَةِ فَزِعُوا مَرَّةً، فَرَكِبَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَرَسًا لأَبِي طَلْحَةَ كَانَ يَقْطِفُ ـ أَوْ كَانَ فِيهِ قِطَافٌ ـ فَلَمَّا رَجَعَ قَالَ ‏ "‏ وَجَدْنَا فَرَسَكُمْ هَذَا بَحْرًا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَكَانَ بَعْدَ ذَلِكَ لاَ يُجَارَى‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:Once the people of Medina were frightened, so the Prophet (ﷺ) rode a horse belonging to Abu Talha and it ran slowly, or was of narrow paces. When he returned, he said, "I found your (i.e. Abu Talha's) horse very fast. After that the horse could not be surpassed in running

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Ek baar Madina ke log dar gaye, isliye Nabi Abu Talha ka ghoda par sawar hue aur woh dheeme ya sankirin kadam se chala. Jab woh wapas aaye, unhone kaha, "Mujhe tumhara (yaani Abu Talha ka) ghoda bahut tez laga." Uske baad, ghoda daudne mein kisi se paar nahi hua.
