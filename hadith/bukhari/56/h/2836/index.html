Sahih al-Bukhari 2836

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 52

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، سَمِعْتُ الْبَرَاءَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَنْقُلُ وَيَقُولُ ‏ "‏ لَوْلاَ أَنْتَ مَا اهْتَدَيْنَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Al-Bara:The Prophet (ﷺ) went on carrying (i.e. the earth) and saying, "Without You (O Allah!) we would have got no guidance

Al-Bara bataate hain: Nabi (mitti) uthate rahe aur kehte rahe: "Tere bina (Aye Allah!) humein koi hidayat na milti."
