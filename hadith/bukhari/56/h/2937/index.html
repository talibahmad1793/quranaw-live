Sahih al-Bukhari 2937

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 150

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الزِّنَادِ، أَنَّ عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ، قَالَ قَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه قَدِمَ طُفَيْلُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو الدَّوْسِيُّ وَأَصْحَابُهُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالُوا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّ دَوْسًا عَصَتْ وَأَبَتْ، فَادْعُ اللَّهَ عَلَيْهَا‏.‏ فَقِيلَ هَلَكَتْ دَوْسٌ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏ "‏ اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِ دَوْسًا وَائْتِ بِهِمْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Tufail bin `Amr Ad-Dausi and his companions came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! The people of the tribe of Daus disobeyed and refused to follow you; so invoke Allah against them." The people said, "The tribe of Daus is ruined." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "O Allah! Give guidance to the people of Daus, and let them embrace Islam

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Tufail bin Amr Ad-Dausi aur unke sathi Nabi ke paas aaye aur kaha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Daus qabile ke log na-farmani karte hain aur aapki baat manne se inkaar karte hain; isliye unke khilaf Allah se dua kijiye." Logon ne kaha, "Daus qabila barbaad ho gaya." Nabi ne kaha, "Aye Allah! Daus qabile ko hidayat de aur unhe Islam qubool karne de."
