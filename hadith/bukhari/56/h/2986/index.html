Sahih al-Bukhari 2986

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 195

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّابِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ أَبِي قِلاَبَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كُنْتُ رَدِيفَ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ، وَإِنَّهُمْ لَيَصْرُخُونَ بِهِمَا جَمِيعًا الْحَجِّ وَالْعُمْرَةِ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:I was riding behind Abu Talha (on the same) riding animal and (the Prophet's companions) were reciting Talbiya aloud for both Hajj and `Umra

Anas bataate hain: Main Abu Talha ke peeche (usi janwar par) sawar tha aur (Nabi ke sahaba) Hajj aur Umrah dono ke liye zor se Talbiya padh rahe the.
