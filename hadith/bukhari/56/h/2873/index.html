Sahih al-Bukhari 2873

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 89

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عَمْرَو بْنَ الْحَارِثِ، قَالَ مَا تَرَكَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلاَّ بَغْلَتَهُ الْبَيْضَاءَ وَسِلاَحَهُ وَأَرْضًا تَرَكَهَا صَدَقَةً‏.‏

Narrated `Amr bin Al-Harith:The Prophet (ﷺ) did not leave anything behind him after his death except a white mule, his arms and a piece of land which he left to be given in charity

Amr bin Al-Harith bataate hain: Nabi ne apni maut ke baad apne peeche kuch nahi chhoda siwaye ek safed khachchar, apne hathiyaar, aur zameen ka ek tukda jise unhone sadqe mein dene ke liye chhoda tha.
