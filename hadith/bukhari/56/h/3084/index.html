Sahih al-Bukhari 3084

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 288

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا جُوَيْرِيَةُ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ إِذَا قَفَلَ كَبَّرَ ثَلاَثًا قَالَ ‏ "‏ آيِبُونَ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ حَامِدُونَ لِرَبِّنَا سَاجِدُونَ، صَدَقَ اللَّهُ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:When the Prophet (ﷺ) returned (from Jihad), he would say Takbir thrice and add, "We are returning, if Allah wishes, with repentance and worshipping and praising (our Lord) and prostrating ourselves before our Lord. Allah fulfilled His Promise and helped His Slave, and He Alone defeated the (infidel) clans

Abdullah bataate hain: Jab Nabi (Jihad se) wapas aate, woh teen baar Takbir kahte aur jodte, "Hum wapas aa rahe hain, Insha Allah, tauba karte huye, ibadat karte huye, aur (apne Rab ki) hamd aur sajda karte huye. Allah ne apna waada poora kiya, aur apne bande ki madad ki, aur usne akele hi (kaafir) qabilon ko haar di."
