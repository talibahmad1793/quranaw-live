Sahih al-Bukhari 3002

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 211

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، حَمَلَ عَلَى فَرَسٍ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَوَجَدَهُ يُبَاعُ، فَأَرَادَ أَنْ يَبْتَاعَهُ، فَسَأَلَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ تَبْتَعْهُ، وَلاَ تَعُدْ فِي صَدَقَتِكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:`Umar bin Al-Khattab gave a horse to be ridden in Allah's Cause and then he found it being sold. He intended to purchase it. So, he consulted Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) who said, "Don't buy it and don't take back your gift of charity

Abdullah bin Umar bataate hain: Umar bin Al-Khattab ne ek ghoda Allah ki raah mein istemal hone ke liye diya aur phir dekha ke woh becha jaane wala hai. Unhone use khareedne ka iraada kiya. Isliye unhone Allah ke Rasool se salaah li, jinhone kaha, "Ise mat khareedo aur apna sadqe ka tohfa wapas mat lo."
