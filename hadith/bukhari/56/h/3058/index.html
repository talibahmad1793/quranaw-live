Sahih al-Bukhari 3058

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 263

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مَحْمُودٌ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عَلِيِّ بْنِ حُسَيْنٍ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ عُثْمَانَ بْنِ عَفَّانَ، عَنْ أُسَامَةَ بْنِ زَيْدٍ، قَالَ قُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَيْنَ تَنْزِلُ غَدًا فِي حَجَّتِهِ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ وَهَلْ تَرَكَ لَنَا عَقِيلٌ مَنْزِلاً ‏"‏‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏"‏نَحْنُ نَازِلُونَ غَدًا بِخَيْفِ بَنِي كِنَانَةَ الْمُحَصَّبِ، حَيْثُ قَاسَمَتْ قُرَيْشٌ عَلَى الْكُفْرِ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَذَلِكَ أَنَّ بَنِي كِنَانَةَ حَالَفَتْ قُرَيْشًا عَلَى بَنِي هَاشِمٍ أَنْ لاَ يُبَايِعُوهُمْ وَلاَ يُئْوُوهُمْ‏.‏ قَالَ الزُّهْرِيُّ وَالْخَيْفُ الْوَادِي‏.‏

Narrated Usama bin Zaid:I asked the Prophet (ﷺ) during his Hajj, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Where will you stay tomorrow?" He said, "Has `Aqil left for us any house?" He then added, "Tomorrow we will stay at Khaif Bani Kinana, i.e. Al-Muhassab, where (the Pagans of) Quraish took an oath of Kufr (i.e. to be loyal to heathenism) in that Bani Kinana got allied with Quraish against Bani Hashim on the terms that they would not deal with the members of this tribe or give them shelter." (Az-Zuhri said, "Khaif means valley.") (See Hadith No. 659, Vol)

Usama bin Zaid bataate hain: Maine Nabi se unke Hajj ke dauran pucha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Aap kal kahaan rukenge?" Unhone kaha, "Kya Aqil ne humare liye koi ghar chhoda hai?" Phir unhone joda, "Kal hum Khaif Bani Kinana mein rukenge, yaani Al-Muhassab, jahaan (Quraish ke mushrikon ne) Kufr ki qasam khaee thi (yaani but-parasti se wafadaar rehne ki), jismein Bani Kinana Quraish ke saath jude the Bani Hashim ke khilaf is shart par ke woh is qabile ke logon se koi len-den ya panah nahi denge." (Az-Zuhri ne kaha, "Khaif ka matlab hai wadi.") (Dekhen Hadith No. 659, Vol.)
