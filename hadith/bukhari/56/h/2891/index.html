Sahih al-Bukhari 2891

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 106

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ نَصْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هَمَّامٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ كُلُّ سُلاَمَى عَلَيْهِ صَدَقَةٌ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ، يُعِينُ الرَّجُلَ فِي دَابَّتِهِ يُحَامِلُهُ عَلَيْهَا أَوْ يَرْفَعُ عَلَيْهَا مَتَاعَهُ صَدَقَةٌ، وَالْكَلِمَةُ الطَّيِّبَةُ، وَكُلُّ خَطْوَةٍ يَمْشِيهَا إِلَى الصَّلاَةِ صَدَقَةٌ، وَدَلُّ الطَّرِيقِ صَدَقَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Charity is obligatory everyday on every joint of a human being. If one helps a person in matters concerning his riding animal by helping him to ride it or by lifting his luggage on to it, all this will be regarded as charity. A good word, and every step one takes to offer the compulsory Congregational prayer, is regarded as charity; and guiding somebody on the road is regarded as charity

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Har din, insaan ke har jod ke liye sadqa farz hai. Agar koi kisi ki sawari ke janwar mein madad kare use sawar hone mein madad karke ya uska saaman uspar chadhakar, yeh sab sadqa mana jaayega. Achhi baat, aur farz jamaat namaz padhne ke liye uthaya gaya har kadam bhi sadqa mana jata hai; aur kisi ko raaste par raah dikhana bhi sadqa mana jata hai."
