Sahih al-Bukhari 3079

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 283

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُوسَى، أَخْبَرَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ زُرَيْعٍ، عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي عُثْمَانَ النَّهْدِيِّ، عَنْ مُجَاشِعِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ، قَالَ جَاءَ مُجَاشِعٌ بِأَخِيهِ مُجَالِدِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ هَذَا مُجَالِدٌ يُبَايِعُكَ عَلَى الْهِجْرَةِ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ هِجْرَةَ بَعْدَ فَتْحِ مَكَّةَ، وَلَكِنْ أُبَايِعُهُ عَلَى الإِسْلاَمِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu `Uthman An-Nahdi:Mujashi (bin Mas`ud) took his brother Mujalid bin Mas`ud to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, "This is Mujalid and he will give a pledge of allegiance to you for migration." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "There is no migration after the Conquest of Mecca, but I will take his pledge of allegiance for Islam

Abu Uthman An-Nahdi bataate hain: Mujashi (bin Mas'ud) apne bhai Mujalid bin Mas'ud ko Nabi ke paas laaye aur kaha, "Yeh Mujalid hain aur woh hijrat ke liye aapse bai'at lenge." Nabi ne kaha, "Fatah-e-Mecca ke baad koi hijrat nahi, magar main unse Islam ke liye bai'at lunga."
