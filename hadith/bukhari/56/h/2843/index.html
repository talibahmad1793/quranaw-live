Sahih al-Bukhari 2843

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 59

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، حَدَّثَنَا الْحُسَيْنُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي يَحْيَى، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي بُسْرُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي زَيْدُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ جَهَّزَ غَازِيًا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَقَدْ غَزَا، وَمَنْ خَلَفَ غَازِيًا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ بِخَيْرٍ فَقَدْ غَزَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Zaid bin Khalid:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "He who prepares a Ghazi going in Allah's Cause is given a reward equal to that of) a Ghazi; and he who looks after properly the dependents of a Ghazi going in Allah's Cause is (given a reward equal to that of) a Ghazi

Zaid bin Khalid bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne farmaya: "Jo koi Allah ki raah mein jaane wale Ghazi (fauji) ki taiyaari mein madad kare, use bhi Ghazi jitna ajr milta hai; aur jo koi Allah ki raah mein jaane wale Ghazi ke pariwara ki achhi dekhbhal karta hai, use bhi Ghazi jitna ajr milta hai."
