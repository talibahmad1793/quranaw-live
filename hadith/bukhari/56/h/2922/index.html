Sahih al-Bukhari 2922

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 135

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، سَمِعْتُ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، رَخَّصَ أَوْ رُخِّصَ لِحِكَّةٍ بِهِمَا‏.‏

Narrated Anas:(Wearing of silk) was allowed to them (i.e. `AbdurRahman and Az-Zubair) because of the itching they suffered from

Anas bataate hain: (Resham pehnna) unhe (yaani Abdur-Rahman aur Az-Zubair ko) khujli ki wajah se jo unhe hoti thi, ijazat di gayi thi.
