Sahih al-Bukhari 3077

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 282

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ بْنُ أَبِي إِيَاسٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شَيْبَانُ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، عَنْ طَاوُسٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ فَتْحِ مَكَّةَ ‏ "‏ لاَ هِجْرَةَ وَلَكِنْ جِهَادٌ وَنِيَّةٌ، وَإِذَا اسْتُنْفِرْتُمْ فَانْفِرُوا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, on the day of the Conquest of Mecca, "There is no migration (after the Conquest), but Jihad and good intentions, and when you are called for Jihad, you should immediately respond to the call

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi ne Fatah-e-Mecca ke din kaha, "(Fatah ke baad) koi hijrat nahi, magar Jihad aur achhi niyyaten hain, aur jab tumhe Jihad ke liye bulaya jaaye, tumhe foran bulaave ka jawab dena chahiye."
