Sahih al-Bukhari 2925

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 138

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ الْفَرْوِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ تُقَاتِلُونَ الْيَهُودَ حَتَّى يَخْتَبِيَ أَحَدُهُمْ وَرَاءَ الْحَجَرِ فَيَقُولُ يَا عَبْدَ اللَّهِ هَذَا يَهُودِيٌّ وَرَائِي فَاقْتُلْهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "You (i.e. Muslims) will fight with the Jews until some of them will hide behind stones. The stones will (betray them) saying, 'O `Abdullah (i.e. slave of Allah)! There is a Jew hiding behind me; so kill him

Abdullah bin Umar bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne farmaya: "Tum (yaani Musalman) Yahudiyon se jung karoge jab tak unme se kuch pattharon ke peeche chhupne lagenge. Patthar (unhe dhoka dekar) kahenge, 'Aye Abdullah (yaani Allah ke bande)! Ek Yahudi mere peeche chhupa hai; use qatl karo.'"
