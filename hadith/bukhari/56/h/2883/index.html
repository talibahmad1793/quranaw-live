Sahih al-Bukhari 2883

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) · Hadith 98

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا بِشْرُ بْنُ الْمُفَضَّلِ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ ذَكْوَانَ، عَنِ الرُّبَيِّعِ بِنْتِ مُعَوِّذٍ، قَالَتْ كُنَّا نَغْزُو مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَنَسْقِي الْقَوْمَ وَنَخْدُمُهُمْ، وَنَرُدُّ الْجَرْحَى وَالْقَتْلَى إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ‏.‏

Narrated Ar-Rubai' bint Mu'auwidh:We used to take part in holy battles with the Prophet (ﷺ) by providing the people with water and serving them and bringing the killed and the wounded back to Medina

Ar-Rubai bint Mu'auwidh bataati hain: Hum Nabi ke saath muqaddas jungon mein hissa lete the, logon ko paani pilakar aur unki khidmat karke, aur murdon aur zakhmiyon ko Madina wapas lakar.
