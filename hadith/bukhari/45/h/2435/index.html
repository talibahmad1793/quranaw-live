Sahih al-Bukhari 2435

Book 45: Lost Things Picked up by Someone (Luqatah) · Hadith 10

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ يَحْلُبَنَّ أَحَدٌ مَاشِيَةَ امْرِئٍ بِغَيْرِ إِذْنِهِ، أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمْ أَنْ تُؤْتَى مَشْرُبَتُهُ فَتُكْسَرَ خِزَانَتُهُ، فَيُنْتَقَلَ طَعَامُهُ فَإِنَّمَا تَخْزُنُ لَهُمْ ضُرُوعُ مَوَاشِيهِمْ أَطْعِمَاتِهِمْ، فَلاَ يَحْلُبَنَّ أَحَدٌ مَاشِيَةَ أَحَدٍ إِلاَّ بِإِذْنِهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "An animal should not be milked without the permission of its owner. Does any of you like that somebody comes to his store and breaks his container and takes away his food? The udders of the animals are the stores of their owners where their provision is kept, so nobody should milk the animals of somebody else, without the permission of its owner

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne farmaya: "Kisi janwar ka doodh uske maalik ki ijazat ke bina na nikala jaaye. Kya tum mein se koi pasand karega ke koi uske godaam mein aaye, uska bartan tode aur uska khana le jaaye? Janwaron ke than unke maalikon ke godaam hain jahan unka khaana rakha hota hai, isliye kisi ko bhi doosre ke janwar ka doodh uske maalik ki ijazat ke bina nahi nikaalna chahiye."
