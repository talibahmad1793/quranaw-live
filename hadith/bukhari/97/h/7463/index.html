Sahih al-Bukhari 7463

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 89

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ تَكَفَّلَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ جَاهَدَ فِي سَبِيلِهِ، لاَ يُخْرِجُهُ مِنْ بَيْتِهِ إِلاَّ الْجِهَادُ فِي سَبِيلِهِ، وَتَصْدِيقُ كَلِمَتِهِ أَنْ يُدْخِلَهُ الْجَنَّةَ، أَوْ يَرُدَّهُ إِلَى مَسْكَنِهِ بِمَا نَالَ مِنْ أَجْرٍ أَوْ غَنِيمَةٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Allah guarantees (the person who carries out Jihad in His Cause and nothing compelled him to go out but Jihad in His Cause and the belief in His Word) that He will either admit him into Paradise (Martyrdom) or return him with reward or booty he has earned to his residence from where he went out

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah us shaks ki zimmedaari leta hai jo uski raah mein Jihad karta hai, aur jisse Jihad ke ilaawa uski raah mein aur uski Kalima par Iman ke ilaawa kisi aur cheez ne baahar nikalne ke liye majboor nahi kiya, ke wo use ya to Jannat mein daakhil karega (Shahaadat) ya use uske ajr ya maal-e-ganimat ke saath uske ghar wapas laayega jahaan se wo nikla tha.'
