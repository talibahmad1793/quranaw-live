Sahih al-Bukhari 7492

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 118

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَقُولُ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ الصَّوْمُ لِي وَأَنَا أَجْزِي بِهِ يَدَعُ شَهْوَتَهُ وَأَكْلَهُ وَشُرْبَهُ مِنْ أَجْلِي، وَالصَّوْمُ جُنَّةٌ، وَلِلصَّائِمِ فَرْحَتَانِ فَرْحَةٌ حِينَ يُفْطِرُ وَفَرْحَةٌ حِينَ يَلْقَى رَبَّهُ، وَلَخَلُوفُ فَمِ الصَّائِمِ أَطْيَبُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ مِنْ رِيحِ الْمِسْكِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Allah said: The Fast is for Me and I will give the reward for it, as he (the one who observes the fast) leaves his sexual desire, food and drink for My Sake. Fasting is a screen (from Hell) and there are two pleasures for a fasting person, one at the time of breaking his fast, and the other at the time when he will meet his Lord. And the smell of the mouth of a fasting person is better in Allah's Sight than the smell of musk." (See Hadith No. 128, Vol)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah ne farmaaya: Roza Mere liye hai aur Main hi iska ajr dunga, kyunke wo (roza rakhne wala) apni jinsi khwaahish, khaana, aur peena Meri khaatir chhodta hai. Roza ek parda hai (Jahannam se) aur ek roza rakhne wale ke liye do khushiyaan hain, ek roza kholte waqt, aur doosri jab wo apne Rab se milega. Aur roza rakhne wale ke munh ki khushbu Allah ke nazdeek mushk ki khushbu se bhi behtar hai.' (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees No. 128, Jild dekhein.)
