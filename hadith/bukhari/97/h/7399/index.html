Sahih al-Bukhari 7399

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 28

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا حَفْصُ بْنُ عُمَرَ، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامٌ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ ضَحَّى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِكَبْشَيْنِ، يُسَمِّي وَيُكَبِّرُ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) slaughtered two rams as sacrifice and mentioned Allah's Name and said, "Allahu-Akbar" while slaughtering

Anas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne do menDhe qurbaani ke taur par zibah kiye aur Allah ka naam liya aur zibah karte waqt 'Allahu Akbar' kaha.
