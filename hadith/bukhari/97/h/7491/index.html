Sahih al-Bukhari 7491

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 117

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا الزُّهْرِيُّ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ قَالَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى يُؤْذِينِي ابْنُ آدَمَ، يَسُبُّ الدَّهْرَ وَأَنَا الدَّهْرُ، بِيَدِي الأَمْرُ، أُقَلِّبُ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Allah said: "The son of Adam hurts Me by abusing Time, for I am Time; in My Hands are all things and I cause the revolution of night and day.' " (See Hadith No. 351, Vol)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah ne farmaaya: "Aadam ka beta mujhe waqt (zamaana) ko bura-bhala kehkar takleef pahunchaata hai, kyunke main hi Waqt hoon; sab cheezein mere Haathon mein hain aur main hi raat aur din ka badalna karta hoon."' (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees No. 351, Jild dekhein.)
