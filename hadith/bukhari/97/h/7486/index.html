Sahih al-Bukhari 7486

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 112

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَتَعَاقَبُونَ فِيكُمْ مَلاَئِكَةٌ بِاللَّيْلِ وَمَلاَئِكَةٌ بِالنَّهَارِ، وَيَجْتَمِعُونَ فِي صَلاَةِ الْعَصْرِ وَصَلاَةِ الْفَجْرِ، ثُمَّ يَعْرُجُ الَّذِينَ بَاتُوا فِيكُمْ فَيَسْأَلُهُمْ وَهْوَ أَعْلَمُ كَيْفَ تَرَكْتُمْ عِبَادِي فَيَقُولُونَ تَرَكْنَاهُمْ وَهُمْ يُصَلُّونَ، وَأَتَيْنَاهُمْ وَهُمْ يُصَلُّونَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "There are angels coming to you in succession at night, and others during the day, and they all gather at the time of `Asr and Fajr prayers. Then the angels who have stayed with you overnight ascend (to the heaven) and He (Allah) asks them though He perfectly knows their affairs. 'In what state have you left my slaves?' They say, 'When we left them, they were praying and when we came to them they were praying

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Kuch farishte tumhaare paas raat mein baari-baari se aate hain, aur doosre din mein, aur wo sab Asr aur Fajr ki namaz ke waqt jama hote hain. Phir wo farishte jo raat bhar tumhaare saath rahe, oopar (Aasmaan ki taraf) jaate hain aur Wo (Allah) unse poochta hai halaanke wo unke maamlon ko achhi tarah jaanta hai, "Tumne mere bandon ko kis haalat mein chhoda?" Wo kehte hain, "Jab humne unhein chhoda, wo namaz padh rahe the, aur jab hum unke paas pahunche, wo namaz padh rahe the."'
