Sahih al-Bukhari 7546

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 171

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مِسْعَرٌ، عَنْ عَدِيِّ بْنِ ثَابِتٍ، أُرَاهُ عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقْرَأُ فِي الْعِشَاءِ ‏{‏وَالتِّينِ وَالزَّيْتُونِ‏}‏ فَمَا سَمِعْتُ أَحَدًا أَحْسَنَ صَوْتًا أَوْ قِرَاءَةً مِنْهُ‏.‏

Narrated Al-Bara':I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) reciting Surat at-Tin waz Zaitun (By the Fig and the Olive) in the `Isha' prayer and I have never heard anybody with a better voice or recitation than his

Al-Bara bataate hain: Maine Nabi (SAW) ko Isha ki namaz mein Surat At-Tin waz-Zaitun (Anjeer aur Zaitun ki qasam) padhte suna, aur maine kabhi kisi ko unse behtar awaaz aur tilaawat mein nahi suna.
