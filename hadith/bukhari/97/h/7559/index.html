Sahih al-Bukhari 7559

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 184

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْعَلاَءِ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ فُضَيْلٍ، عَنْ عُمَارَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي زُرْعَةَ، سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ قَالَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ وَمَنْ أَظْلَمُ مِمَّنْ ذَهَبَ يَخْلُقُ كَخَلْقِي، فَلْيَخْلُقُوا ذَرَّةً، أَوْ لِيَخْلُقُوا حَبَّةً أَوْ شَعِيرَةً ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) saying, "Allah said, 'Who are most unjust than those who try to create something like My creation? I challenge them to create even a smallest ant, a wheat grain or a barley grain

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Maine Nabi (SAW) ko ye kehte suna, 'Allah ne farmaaya, "Kaun us shaks se zyaada zaalim ho sakta hai jo Meri takhleeq jaisi koi cheez banaane ki koshish kare? Main unhein challenge (chunauti) deta hoon ke wo ek sabse chhoti chionti, gehun ka daana, ya jau ka daana bhi banaayein."'
