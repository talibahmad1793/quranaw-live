Sahih al-Bukhari 7522

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 147

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمُ بْنُ وَرْدَانَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ كَيْفَ تَسْأَلُونَ أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ عَنْ كُتُبِهِمْ وَعِنْدَكُمْ كِتَابُ اللَّهِ أَقْرَبُ الْكُتُبِ عَهْدًا بِاللَّهِ، تَقْرَءُونَهُ مَحْضًا لَمْ يُشَبْ

Narrated `Ikrima:Ibn `Abbas said, "How can you ask the people of the Scriptures about their Books while you have Allah's Book (the Qur'an) which is the most recent of the Books revealed by Allah, and you read it in its pure undistorted form?

Ikrima bataate hain: Ibn Abbas ne kaha, 'Tum Ahl-e-Kitaab se unki Kitaabon ke baare mein kaise poochh sakte ho jabke tumhaare paas Allah ki Kitaab (Quran) hai jo Allah ki naazil ki hui sab Kitaabon mein sabse nayi hai, aur tum ise uski saaf, gair-tehrif-shuda shakl mein padhte ho?'
