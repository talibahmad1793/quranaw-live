Sahih al-Bukhari 7411

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 40

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَدُ اللَّهِ مَلأَى لاَ يَغِيضُهَا نَفَقَةٌ، سَحَّاءُ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ ـ وَقَالَ ـ أَرَأَيْتُمْ مَا أَنْفَقَ مُنْذُ خَلَقَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ، فَإِنَّهُ لَمْ يَغِضْ مَا فِي يَدِهِ ـ وَقَالَ ـ عَرْشُهُ عَلَى الْمَاءِ وَبِيَدِهِ الأُخْرَى الْمِيزَانُ يَخْفِضُ وَيَرْفَعُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Allah's Hand is full, and (its fullness) is not affected by the continuous spending, day and night." He also said, "Do you see what He has spent since He created the Heavens and the Earth? Yet all that has not decreased what is in His Hand." He also said, "His Throne is over the water and in His other Hand is the balance (of Justice) and He raises and lowers (whomever He will)." (See Hadith No. 206, Vol)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah ka Haath bhara hua hai, aur (uski bharpoori) lagataar kharch hone se, din aur raat, mutassir nahi hoti.' Unhone ye bhi kaha, 'Kya tum dekhte ho usne kya kharch kiya hai jab se usne Aasmaan aur Zameen banaayi? Phir bhi wo sab kuch usne jo uske Haath mein hai use kam nahi kiya.' Unhone ye bhi kaha, 'Uska Arsh (Takht) paani ke oopar hai, aur uske doosre Haath mein insaaf ka taraazu hai, aur wo (jise chahe) ooncha aur (jise chahe) neecha karta hai.' (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees No. 206, Jild dekhein.)
