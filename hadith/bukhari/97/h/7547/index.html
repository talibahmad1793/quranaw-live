Sahih al-Bukhari 7547

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 172

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا حَجَّاجُ بْنُ مِنْهَالٍ، حَدَّثَنَا هُشَيْمٌ، عَنْ أَبِي بِشْرٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مُتَوَارِيًا بِمَكَّةَ، وَكَانَ يَرْفَعُ صَوْتَهُ، فَإِذَا سَمِعَ الْمُشْرِكُونَ سَبُّوا الْقُرْآنَ وَمَنْ جَاءَ بِهِ، فَقَالَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ لِنَبِيِّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏{‏وَلاَ تَجْهَرْ بِصَلاَتِكَ وَلاَ تُخَافِتْ بِهَا‏}‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) was hiding himself in Mecca and used to recite the (Qur'an) in a loud voice. When the pagans heard him they would abuse the Qur'an and the one who brought it, so Allah said to His Prophet: 'Neither say your prayer aloud, nor say it in a low tone

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) khud ko Makkah mein chhupaaye hue the aur oonchi awaaz mein (Quran ki) tilaawat karte the. Jab mushrik unhein sunte, wo Quran aur use laane wale ki bura-bhala kehte, isliye Allah ne apne Nabi se kaha: 'Na to apni namaz oonchi awaaz mein padho, na hi use dhime lehje mein padho...'
