Sahih al-Bukhari 7407

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 36

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا جُوَيْرِيَةُ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ ذُكِرَ الدَّجَّالُ عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لاَ يَخْفَى عَلَيْكُمْ، إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَيْسَ بِأَعْوَرَ ـ وَأَشَارَ بِيَدِهِ إِلَى عَيْنِهِ ـ وَإِنَّ الْمَسِيحَ الدَّجَّالَ أَعْوَرُ الْعَيْنِ الْيُمْنَى كَأَنَّ عَيْنَهُ عِنَبَةٌ طَافِيَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:Ad-Dajjal was mentioned in the presence of the Prophet. The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Allah is not hidden from you; He is not one-eyed," and pointed with his hand towards his eye, adding, "While Al-Masih Ad- Dajjal is blind in the right eye and his eye looks like a protruding grape

Abdullah bataate hain: Ad-Dajjal ka zikr Nabi (SAW) ki maujoodgi mein hua. Nabi (SAW) ne kaha, 'Allah tumse chhupa hua nahi hai; wo ek-aankha nahi hai,' aur apne haath se apni aankh ki taraf ishara kiya, aur izaafa kiya, 'Jabke Al-Maseeh Ad-Dajjal daayin aankh se andha hai aur uski aankh ek uthhe hue angoor jaisi dikhti hai.'
