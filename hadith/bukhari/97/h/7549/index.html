Sahih al-Bukhari 7549

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 174

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قَبِيصَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ أُمِّهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَرَأْسُهُ فِي حَجْرِي وَأَنَا حَائِضٌ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:The Prophet (ﷺ) used to recite the Qur'an with his head in my lap while I used to be in my periods (having menses)

Aisha bataati hain: Nabi (SAW) Quran padha karte the apna sar mere godi mein rakhkar jabke main apni haiz (mahwaari) mein hoti thi.
