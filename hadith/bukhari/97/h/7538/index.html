Sahih al-Bukhari 7538

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 163

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ زِيَادٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَرْوِيهِ عَنْ رَبِّكُمْ، قَالَ ‏ "‏ لِكُلِّ عَمَلٍ كَفَّارَةٌ، وَالصَّوْمُ لِي وَأَنَا أَجْزِي بِهِ، وَلَخَلُوفُ فَمِ الصَّائِمِ أَطْيَبُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ مِنْ رِيحِ الْمِسْكِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said that your Lord said, "Every (sinful) deed can be expiated; and the fast is for Me, so I will give the reward for it; and the smell which comes out of the mouth of a fasting person, is better in Allah's Sight than the smell of musk." (See Hadith No)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne kaha ke tumhaare Rab ne kaha, 'Har (gunahgaar) amal ka kaffara diya jaa sakta hai; aur roza Mere liye hai, isliye main hi iska ajr dunga; aur roza rakhne wale ke munh se aane wali khushbu Allah ke nazdeek mushk ki khushbu se bhi behtar hai.' (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees dekhein.)
