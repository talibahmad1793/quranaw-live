Sahih al-Bukhari 7483

Book 97: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawheed) · Hadith 109

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصِ بْنِ غِيَاثٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ يَقُولُ اللَّهُ يَا آدَمُ‏.‏ فَيَقُولُ لَبَّيْكَ وَسَعْدَيْكَ‏.‏ فَيُنَادَى بِصَوْتٍ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُكَ أَنْ تُخْرِجَ مِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِكَ بَعْثًا إِلَى النَّارِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Allah will say (on the Day of Resurrection), 'O Adam!' Adam will reply, 'Labbaik wa Sa`daik! ' Then a loud Voice will be heard (Saying) 'Allah Commands you to take out the mission of the Hell Fire from your offspring

Abu Saeed Al-Khudri bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah (Qayamat ke din) kahega, "Aye Aadam!" Aadam jawab denge, "Labbaik wa Sa'daik! (Main haazir hoon aur tumhaari farmaanbardaari karta hoon!)" Phir ek oonchi Aawaaz sunaayi degi (kehte hue), "Allah tumhein hukm deta hai ke apni nasl mein se Jahannam ki tolii nikaalo."'
