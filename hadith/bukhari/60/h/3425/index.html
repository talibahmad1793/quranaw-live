Sahih al-Bukhari 3425

Book 60: Prophets · Hadith 97

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ التَّيْمِيُّ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ‏.‏ أَىُّ مَسْجِدٍ وُضِعَ أَوَّلُ قَالَ ‏"‏ الْمَسْجِدُ الْحَرَامُ ‏"‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ ثُمَّ أَىٌّ قَالَ ‏"‏ ثُمَّ الْمَسْجِدُ الأَقْصَى ‏"‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ كَمْ كَانَ بَيْنَهُمَا قَالَ ‏"‏ أَرْبَعُونَ ‏"‏‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏"‏ حَيْثُمَا أَدْرَكَتْكَ الصَّلاَةُ فَصَلِّ، وَالأَرْضُ لَكَ مَسْجِدٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Dharr:I said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Which mosque was built first?" He replied, "Al-Masjid-ul-Haram." I asked, "Which (was built) next?" He replied, "Al-Masjid-ul-Aqsa (i.e. Jerusalem)." I asked, "What was the period in between them?" He replied, "Forty (years)." He then added, "Wherever the time for the prayer comes upon you, perform the prayer, for all the earth is a place of worshipping for you

Abu Dharr bataate hain: Maine pucha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Sabse pahle kaunsi masjid banaee gaee?" Unhone jawab diya, "Al-Masjid-ul-Haram." Maine pucha, "Uske baad kaunsi?" Unhone jawab diya, "Al-Masjid-ul-Aqsa (yaani Jerusalem)." Maine pucha, "Un donon ke beech kitne waqt ka faasla tha?" Unhone jawab diya, "Chaalis (saal)." Phir unhone joda, "Jahaan bhi namaz ka waqt tumpar aaye, namaz padh lo, kyunke poori zameen tumhare liye ibaadat ki jagah hai."
