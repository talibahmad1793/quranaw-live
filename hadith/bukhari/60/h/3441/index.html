Sahih al-Bukhari 3441

Book 60: Prophets · Hadith 111

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ الْمَكِّيُّ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنَ سَعْدٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي الزُّهْرِيُّ، عَنْ سَالِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ،، قَالَ لاَ وَاللَّهِ مَا قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم لِعِيسَى أَحْمَرُ، وَلَكِنْ قَالَ ‏ "‏ بَيْنَمَا أَنَا نَائِمٌ أَطُوفُ بِالْكَعْبَةِ، فَإِذَا رَجُلٌ آدَمُ سَبْطُ الشَّعَرِ، يُهَادَى بَيْنَ رَجُلَيْنِ، يَنْطِفُ رَأْسُهُ مَاءً أَوْ يُهَرَاقُ رَأْسُهُ مَاءً فَقُلْتُ مَنْ هَذَا قَالُوا ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ، فَذَهَبْتُ أَلْتَفِتُ، فَإِذَا رَجُلٌ أَحْمَرُ جَسِيمٌ، جَعْدُ الرَّأْسِ، أَعْوَرُ عَيْنِهِ الْيُمْنَى، كَأَنَّ عَيْنَهُ عِنَبَةٌ طَافِيَةٌ‏.‏ قُلْتُ مَنْ هَذَا قَالُوا هَذَا الدَّجَّالُ‏.‏ وَأَقْرَبُ النَّاسِ بِهِ شَبَهًا ابْنُ قَطَنٍ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ الزُّهْرِيُّ رَجُلٌ مِنْ خُزَاعَةَ هَلَكَ فِي الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ‏.‏

Narrated Salim from his father:No, By Allah, the Prophet (ﷺ) did not tell that Jesus was of red complexion but said, "While I was asleep circumambulating the Ka`ba (in my dream), suddenly I saw a man of brown complexion and lank hair walking between two men, and water was dropping from his head. I asked, 'Who is this?' The people said, 'He is the son of Mary.' Then I looked behind and I saw a red-complexioned, fat, curly-haired man, blind in the right eye which looked like a bulging out grape. I asked, 'Who is this?' They replied, 'He is Ad-Dajjal.' The one who resembled to him among the people was Ibn Qatan." (Az-Zuhri said, "He (i.e. Ibn Qatan) was a man from the tribe Khuza`a who died in the pre-Islamic period

Salim, apne waalid ke hawale se, bataate hain: Nahi, Allah ki qasam, Nabi ne yeh nahi kaha ke Isa laal rang wale the, balki unhone kaha, "Jabke main sote hue Ka'ba ka Tawaf kar raha tha (apne khwab mein), achanak maine ek bhure rang ke, seedhe baal wale shaks ko do logon ke beech chalte dekha, aur unke sar se paani tapak raha tha. Maine pucha, 'Yeh kaun hain?' Logon ne kaha, 'Yeh Maryam ke bete hain.' Phir maine peeche dekha aur ek laal rang, mote, ghungrale baal wale shaks ko dekha, jinki dahini aankh andhi thi jo ubri hui angoor jaisi dikhti thi. Maine pucha, 'Yeh kaun hain?' Unhone jawab diya, 'Yeh Ad-Dajjal hain.' Logon mein unse sabse zyada milte-julte Ibn Qatan the." (Az-Zuhri ne kaha, "Woh (yaani Ibn Qatan) Khuza'a qabile ke ek shaks the jo Islam se pahle guzar gaye the.")
