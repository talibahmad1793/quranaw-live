Sahih al-Bukhari 3398

Book 60: Prophets · Hadith 71

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ يَحْيَى، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ النَّاسُ يَصْعَقُونَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ، فَأَكُونُ أَوَّلَ مَنْ يُفِيقُ، فَإِذَا أَنَا بِمُوسَى آخِذٌ بِقَائِمَةٍ مِنْ قَوَائِمِ الْعَرْشِ، فَلاَ أَدْرِي أَفَاقَ قَبْلِي، أَمْ جُوزِيَ بِصَعْقَةِ الطُّورِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Sa`id:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, 'People will be struck unconscious on the Day of Resurrection and I will be the first to regain consciousness, and behold! There I will see Moses holding one of the pillars of Allah's Throne. I will wonder whether he has become conscious before me, or if he has been exempted because of his unconsciousness at the Tur (mountain) which he received (on the earth)

Abu Saeed bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Qiyamat ke din log behosh ho jaayenge, aur main sabse pahle hosh mein aunga, aur dekhunga! Musa Allah ke Arsh ke ek khambe ko pakade hue honge. Mujhe hairat hogi kya woh mujhse pahle hosh mein aaye, ya use Tur (pahad) par behosh hone se mukti mili jo unhe (duniya mein) mila tha."
