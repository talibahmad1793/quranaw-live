Sahih al-Bukhari 3360

Book 60: Prophets · Hadith 35

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصِ بْنِ غِيَاثٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ، عَنْ عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ لَمَّا نَزَلَتِ ‏{‏الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَلَمْ يَلْبِسُوا إِيمَانَهُمْ بِظُلْمٍ‏}‏ قُلْنَا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَيُّنَا لاَ يَظْلِمُ نَفْسَهُ قَالَ ‏"‏ لَيْسَ كَمَا تَقُولُونَ ‏{‏لَمْ يَلْبِسُوا إِيمَانَهُمْ بِظُلْمٍ‏}‏ بِشِرْكٍ، أَوَلَمْ تَسْمَعُوا إِلَى قَوْلِ لُقْمَانَ لاِبْنِهِ ‏{‏يَا بُنَىَّ لاَ تُشْرِكْ بِاللَّهِ إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ ‏}‏‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:When the Verse:--"It is those who believe and do not confuse their belief with wrong ( i.e. joining others in worship with Allah" (6.83) was revealed, we said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Who is there amongst us who has not done wrong to himself?" He replied, "It is not as you say, for 'wrong' in the Verse and 'do not confuse their belief, with wrong means 'SHIRK' (i.e. joining others in worship with Allah). Haven't you heard Luqman's saying to his son, 'O my son! Join not others in worship with Allah, verily joining others in worship with Allah is a great wrong indeed

Abdullah bataate hain: Jab yeh Ayat nazil hui: "Woh log jo iman laaye aur apne iman ko galat na-insaafi (yaani Allah ke saath shareek karne) se milate nahi..." (6:83), humne kaha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Humme se kaun aisa hai jisne khud par koi na-insaafi nahi ki?" Unhone jawab diya, "Yeh waisa nahi hai jaisa tum kehte ho, kyunke is Ayat mein 'na-insaafi' aur 'apne iman ko na-insaafi se milate nahi' ka matlab Shirk hai (yaani Allah ke saath kisi aur ki ibadat mein shareek karna). Kya tumne Luqman ke apne bete se kahe hue lafz nahi sune hain, 'Aye mere bete! Allah ke saath kisi ko na jodo, kyunke Allah ke saath shareek karna beshak ek badi na-insaafi hai.'"
