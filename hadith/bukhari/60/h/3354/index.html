Sahih al-Bukhari 3354

Book 60: Prophets · Hadith 29

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُؤَمَّلٌ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَوْفٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو رَجَاءٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سَمُرَةُ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أَتَانِي اللَّيْلَةَ آتِيَانِ، فَأَتَيْنَا عَلَى رَجُلٍ طَوِيلٍ، لاَ أَكَادُ أَرَى رَأْسَهُ طُولاً، وَإِنَّهُ إِبْرَاهِيمُ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Samura:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Two persons came to me at night (in dream) (and took me along with them). We passed by a tall man who was so tall that I was not able to see his head and that person was Abraham

Samura bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Do log raat ko mere paas aaye (khwab mein) (aur mujhe apne saath le gaye). Hum ek lambe shaks ke paas se guzre jo itne lambe the ke main unka sar nahi dekh pa raha tha, aur woh shaks Ibrahim the."
