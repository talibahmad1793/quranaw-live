Sahih al-Bukhari 3350

Book 60: Prophets · Hadith 25

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي أَخِي عَبْدُ الْحَمِيدِ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي ذِئْبٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدٍ الْمَقْبُرِيِّ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ يَلْقَى إِبْرَاهِيمُ أَبَاهُ آزَرَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَعَلَى وَجْهِ آزَرَ قَتَرَةٌ وَغَبَرَةٌ، فَيَقُولُ لَهُ إِبْرَاهِيمُ أَلَمْ أَقُلْ لَكَ لاَ تَعْصِنِي فَيَقُولُ أَبُوهُ فَالْيَوْمَ لاَ أَعْصِيكَ‏.‏ فَيَقُولُ إِبْرَاهِيمُ يَا رَبِّ، إِنَّكَ وَعَدْتَنِي أَنْ لاَ تُخْزِيَنِي يَوْمَ يُبْعَثُونَ، فَأَىُّ خِزْىٍ أَخْزَى مِنْ أَبِي الأَبْعَدِ فَيَقُولُ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الْجَنَّةَ عَلَى الْكَافِرِينَ، ثُمَّ يُقَالُ يَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ مَا تَحْتَ رِجْلَيْكَ فَيَنْظُرُ فَإِذَا هُوَ بِذِيخٍ مُلْتَطِخٍ، فَيُؤْخَذُ بِقَوَائِمِهِ فَيُلْقَى فِي النَّارِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "On the Day of Resurrection Abraham will meet his father Azar whose face will be dark and covered with dust.(The Prophet Abraham (ﷺ) will say to him): 'Didn't I tell you not to disobey me?' His father will reply: 'Today I will not disobey you.' 'Abraham will say: 'O Lord! You promised me not to disgrace me on the Day of Resurrection; and what will be more disgraceful to me than cursing and dishonoring my father?' Then Allah will say (to him):' 'I have forbidden Paradise for the disbelievers." Then he will be addressed, 'O Abraham! Look! What is underneath your feet?' He will look and there he will see a Dhabh (an animal,) blood-stained, which will be caught by the legs and thrown in the (Hell) Fire

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Qiyamat ke din Ibrahim apne baap Azar se milenge jinka chehra kaala aur dhool se dhaka hoga. (Nabi Ibrahim unse kahega:) 'Kya maine tumse mera kehna na maanne ko nahi kaha tha?' Unke baap jawab denge: 'Aaj main tumhari baat nahi taalunga.' Ibrahim kahenge: 'Aye Rab! Tune mujhse waada kiya tha ke tu mujhe Qiyamat ke din rusva nahi karega; aur mere liye us se zyada rusva karna wali baat kya hogi jab main apne baap ko laanat deta hoon aur unka apman karta hoon?' Phir Allah kahega (unse): 'Maine kaafiron ke liye Jannat haram kar di hai.' Phir unse kaha jaaega, 'Aye Ibrahim! Dekho! Tumhare paanv ke neeche kya hai?' Woh dekhenge aur wahaan ek Dhabh (janwar) dekhenge, khoon se sana hua, jise uske paanv se pakadkar Dozakh mein phenk diya jaayega."
