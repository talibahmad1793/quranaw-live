Sahih al-Bukhari 3098

Book 57: One-fifth of Booty to the Cause of Allah (Khumus) · Hadith 7

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ سُفْيَانَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عَمْرَو بْنَ الْحَارِثِ، قَالَ مَا تَرَكَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلاَّ سِلاَحَهُ وَبَغْلَتَهُ الْبَيْضَاءَ، وَأَرْضًا تَرَكَهَا صَدَقَةً‏.‏

Narrated `Amr bin Al-Harith:The Prophet (ﷺ) did not leave anything (after his death) except his arms, a white mule, and a (piece of) land which he had given as Sadaqa

Amr bin Al-Harith bataate hain: Nabi ne apni maut ke baad kuch nahi chhoda siwaye apne hathiyaar, ek safed khachchar, aur zameen ka ek tukda jise unhone Sadaqa mein diya tha.
