Sahih al-Bukhari 3097

Book 57: One-fifth of Booty to the Cause of Allah (Khumus) · Hadith 6

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامٌ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ تُوُفِّيَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَمَا فِي بَيْتِي مِنْ شَىْءٍ يَأْكُلُهُ ذُو كَبِدٍ، إِلاَّ شَطْرُ شَعِيرٍ فِي رَفٍّ لِي، فَأَكَلْتُ مِنْهُ حَتَّى طَالَ عَلَىَّ، فَكِلْتُهُ فَفَنِيَ‏.‏

Narrated Aisha:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) died, and there was nothing in my house that a living being could eat, except some barley Lying on a shelf. So, I ate of it for a long period and measured it, and (after a short period) it was consumed

Ayesha bataati hain: Allah ke Rasool ka intiqal hua, aur mere ghar mein koi bhi cheez nahi thi jo koi zinda mahluk kha sake, siwaye kuch jau ke jo ek taak par pade the. Isliye maine unhe lambe waqt tak khaya aur naapa, aur (thode waqt baad) woh khatam ho gaye.
