Sahih al-Bukhari 3112

Book 57: One-fifth of Booty to the Cause of Allah (Khumus) · Hadith 21

Hadith text

قَالَ الْحُمَيْدِيُّ حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ سُوقَةَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ مُنْذِرًا الثَّوْرِيَّ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْحَنَفِيَّةِ، قَالَ أَرْسَلَنِي أَبِي، خُذْ هَذَا الْكِتَابَ فَاذْهَبْ بِهِ إِلَى عُثْمَانَ، فَإِنَّ فِيهِ أَمْرَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي الصَّدَقَةِ‏.‏

Muhammad bin Suqa:I heard Mundhir at-Tawri reporting Ibn Hanafiya who said, "My father sent me saying, 'Take this letter to `Uthman for it contains the orders of the Prophet (ﷺ) concerning the Sadaqa

Muhammad bin Suqa bataate hain: Maine Mundhir at-Tawri ko Ibn Hanafiya se riwaayat karte suna jinhone kaha, "Mere waalid ne mujhe yeh kehte hue bheja, 'Yeh khat Uthman ke paas le jaao kyunke isme Nabi ke Sadaqa ke baare mein hukum hain.'"
