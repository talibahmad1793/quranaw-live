Sahih al-Bukhari 3153

Book 57: One-fifth of Booty to the Cause of Allah (Khumus) · Hadith 61

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ هِلاَلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مُغَفَّلٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كُنَّا مُحَاصِرِينَ قَصْرَ خَيْبَرَ، فَرَمَى إِنْسَانٌ بِجِرَابٍ فِيهِ شَحْمٌ، فَنَزَوْتُ لآخُذَهُ، فَالْتَفَتُّ فَإِذَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَاسْتَحْيَيْتُ مِنْهُ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin Mughaffal:While we were besieging the fort of Khaibar, a person threw a leather container containing fat, and I ran to take it, but when I turned I saw the Prophet (standing behind), so I felt embarrassed in front of him

Abdullah bin Mughaffal bataate hain: Jabke hum Khaibar ke qile ka gherav kar rahe the, kisi ne charbi se bhara ek chamde ka thaila fenka, aur main use lene ke liye dauda, magar jab main muda to dekha Nabi (peeche khade the), isliye mujhe unke saamne sharam mahsoos hui.
