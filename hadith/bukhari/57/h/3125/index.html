Sahih al-Bukhari 3125

Book 57: One-fifth of Booty to the Cause of Allah (Khumus) · Hadith 34

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا صَدَقَةُ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ قَالَ عُمَرُ ـ رضى الله عنه لَوْلاَ آخِرُ الْمُسْلِمِينَ مَا فَتَحْتُ قَرْيَةً إِلاَّ قَسَمْتُهَا بَيْنَ أَهْلِهَا كَمَا قَسَمَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَيْبَرَ‏.‏

Narrated Aslam:`Umar said, "Were it not for those Muslims who have not come to existence yet, I would have distributed (the land of) every town I conquer among the fighters as the Prophet (ﷺ) distributed the land of Khaibar

Aslam bataate hain: Umar ne kaha, "Agar un Musalmanon ka khyaal na hota jo abhi paida nahi hue, main har shehar ki (jise main fatah karun) zameen jungjuon mein taqseem kar deta jaisa Nabi ne Khaibar ki zameen taqseem ki thi."
