Sahih al-Bukhari 3106

Book 57: One-fifth of Booty to the Cause of Allah (Khumus) · Hadith 15

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الأَنْصَارِيُّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ ثُمَامَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، أَنَّ أَبَا بَكْرٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ لَمَّا اسْتُخْلِفَ بَعَثَهُ إِلَى الْبَحْرَيْنِ، وَكَتَبَ لَهُ هَذَا الْكِتَابَ وَخَتَمَهُ، وَكَانَ نَقْشُ الْخَاتَمِ ثَلاَثَةَ أَسْطُرٍ مُحَمَّدٌ سَطْرٌ، وَرَسُولُ سَطْرٌ، وَاللَّهِ سَطْرٌ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:That when Abu Bakr became the Caliph, he sent him to Bahrain and wrote this letter for him, and stamped it with the Ring of the Prophet. Three lines were engraved on the Ring, (the word) 'Muhammad' was in a line, 'Apostle' was in another line, and 'Allah' in a third

Anas bataate hain: Jab Abu Bakr Khalifa bane, unhone unhe (yaani Anas ko) Bahrain bheja aur unke liye yeh khat likha aur ise Nabi ki angoothi se muhar lagaee. Angoothi par teen laainen khudi hui thi, ek laain mein 'Muhammad,' doosri mein 'Rasool,' aur teesri mein 'Allah.'
