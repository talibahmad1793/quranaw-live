Sahih al-Bukhari 5565

Book 73: Al-Adha Festival Sacrifice (Adaahi) · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ ضَحَّى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِكَبْشَيْنِ أَمْلَحَيْنِ أَقْرَنَيْنِ، ذَبَحَهُمَا بِيَدِهِ، وَسَمَّى وَكَبَّرَ وَوَضَعَ رِجْلَهُ عَلَى صِفَاحِهِمَا‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) offered as sacrifices, two horned rams, black and white in color. He slaughtered them with his own hands and mentioned Allah's Name over them and said Takbir and put his foot on their sides

Anas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne do seengon waale mendhe, kaale aur safed rang ke, qurbaani ke taur par diye. Unhone unhein apne haathon se zibah kiya aur unpar Allah ka naam liya aur Takbeer kahi, aur unke pehlu par apna pair rakha.
