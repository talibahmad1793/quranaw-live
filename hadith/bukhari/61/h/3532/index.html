Sahih al-Bukhari 3532

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 41

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ الْمُنْذِرِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَعْنٌ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرِ بْنِ مُطْعِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ لِي خَمْسَةُ أَسْمَاءٍ أَنَا مُحَمَّدٌ، وَأَحْمَدُ، وَأَنَا الْمَاحِي الَّذِي يَمْحُو اللَّهُ بِي الْكُفْرَ، وَأَنَا الْحَاشِرُ الَّذِي يُحْشَرُ النَّاسُ عَلَى قَدَمِي، وَأَنَا الْعَاقِبُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jubair bin Mut`im:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "I have five names: I am Muhammad and Ahmad; I am Al-Mahi through whom Allah will eliminate infidelity; I am Al-Hashir who will be the first to be resurrected, the people being resurrected thereafter; and I am also Al-`Aqib (i.e. There will be no prophet after me)

Jubair bin Mut'im bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Mere paanch naam hain: Main Muhammad hoon aur Ahmad; main Al-Mahi hoon jiske zariye Allah kufr ko mitaega; main Al-Hashir hoon jo sabse pahle (Qiyamat ke din) zinda kiya jaayega, iske baad hi doosre log zinda kiye jaenge; aur main Al-Aqib bhi hoon (yaani mere baad koi Nabi nahi hoga)."
