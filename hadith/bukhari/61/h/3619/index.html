Sahih al-Bukhari 3619

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 126

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قَبِيصَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ بْنِ عُمَيْرٍ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ سَمُرَةَ، رَفَعَهُ قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا هَلَكَ كِسْرَى فَلاَ كِسْرَى بَعْدَهُ ـ وَذَكَرَ وَقَالَ ـ لَتُنْفَقَنَّ كُنُوزُهُمَا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin Samura:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "When Khosrau perishes, there will be no more Khosrau after him, and when Caesar perishes, there will be no more Caesar after him." The Prophet (ﷺ) also said, "You will spend the treasures of both of them in Allah's Cause

Jabir bin Samura bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Jab Khusrau khatam ho jaayega, uske baad koi (aur) Khusrau nahi hoga, aur jab Caesar khatam ho jaayega, uske baad koi (aur) Caesar nahi hoga." Nabi ne yeh bhi kaha, "Tum unke donon ke khazane Allah ki raah mein kharch karoge."
