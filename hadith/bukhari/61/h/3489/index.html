Sahih al-Bukhari 3489

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 1

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ الْكَاهِلِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرٍ، عَنْ أَبِي حَصِينٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسِ، رضى الله عنهما‏.‏ ‏{‏وَجَعَلْنَاكُمْ شُعُوبًا وَقَبَائِلَ‏}‏ قَالَ الشُّعُوبُ الْقَبَائِلُ الْعِظَامُ، وَالْقَبَائِلُ الْبُطُونُ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Regarding the Verse: 'And (We) made you into Shu'ub and Qabail-- (49.13) that Shu'ub means the big Qabail (i.e. nations) while the Qabail (i.e. tribes) means the branch tribes

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Is Ayat ke baare mein: "Aur (humne) tumhe Shu'ub aur Qabaail banaya..." (49:13) — Shu'ub ka matlab hai bade Qabaail (yaani rashtriya samuh), jabke Qabaail ka matlab hai unki shaakhaee qabile.
