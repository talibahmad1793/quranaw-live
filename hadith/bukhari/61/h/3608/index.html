Sahih al-Bukhari 3608

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 115

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا الْحَكَمُ بْنُ نَافِعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ، أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ لاَ تَقُومُ السَّاعَةُ حَتَّى يَقْتَتِلَ فِئَتَانِ دَعْوَاهُمَا وَاحِدَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "The Day of (Judgment) will not be established till there is a war between two groups whose claims (or religion) will be the same

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Qiyamat tab tak nahi aayegi jab tak do giroh ke beech jung na ho jinke daave (ya deen) ek jaise honge."
