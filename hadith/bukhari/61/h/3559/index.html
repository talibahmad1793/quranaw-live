Sahih al-Bukhari 3559

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 68

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، عَنْ أَبِي حَمْزَةَ، عَنِ الأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ مَسْرُوقٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ لَمْ يَكُنِ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَاحِشًا وَلاَ مُتَفَحِّشًا وَكَانَ يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ مِنْ خِيَارِكُمْ أَحْسَنَكُمْ أَخْلاَقًا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Amr:The Prophet (ﷺ) never used bad language neither a "Fahish nor a Mutafahish. He used to say "The best amongst you are those who have the best manners and character." (See Hadith No. 56 (B) Vol

Abdullah bin Amr bataate hain: Nabi kabhi buri ya gaali wali bhaasha istemal nahi karte the, na Fahish (aslil baaten) na Mutafahish (jaanboojhkar aslil baaten). Woh kahte the, "Tum mein sabse behtar woh hain jinke adab aur charitra sabse behtar hain."
