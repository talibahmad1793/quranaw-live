Sahih al-Bukhari 3599

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 107

Hadith text

وَعَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، حَدَّثَتْنِي هِنْدُ بِنْتُ الْحَارِثِ، أَنَّ أُمَّ سَلَمَةَ، قَالَتِ اسْتَيْقَظَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ، مَاذَا أُنْزِلَ مِنَ الْخَزَائِنِ وَمَاذَا أُنْزِلَ مِنَ الْفِتَنِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Um Salama said:The Prophet (ﷺ) woke up and said, "Glorified be Allah: What great (how many) treasures have been sent down, and what great (how many ) afflictions have been sent down

Um Salama bataati hain: Nabi jaag gaye aur kaha, "Subhanallah! Kitne khazane naazil kiye gaye hain, aur kitne fitne naazil kiye gaye hain."
