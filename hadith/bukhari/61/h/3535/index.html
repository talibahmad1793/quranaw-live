Sahih al-Bukhari 3535

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 44

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ مَثَلِي وَمَثَلَ الأَنْبِيَاءِ مِنْ قَبْلِي كَمَثَلِ رَجُلٍ بَنَى بَيْتًا فَأَحْسَنَهُ وَأَجْمَلَهُ، إِلاَّ مَوْضِعَ لَبِنَةٍ مِنْ زَاوِيَةٍ، فَجَعَلَ النَّاسُ يَطُوفُونَ بِهِ وَيَعْجَبُونَ لَهُ، وَيَقُولُونَ هَلاَّ وُضِعَتْ هَذِهِ اللَّبِنَةُ قَالَ فَأَنَا اللَّبِنَةُ، وَأَنَا خَاتِمُ النَّبِيِّينَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "My similitude in comparison with the other prophets before me, is that of a man who has built a house nicely and beautifully, except for a place of one brick in a corner. The people go about it and wonder at its beauty, but say: 'Would that this brick be put in its place!' So I am that brick, and I am the last of the Prophets

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Mujhse pahle ke Nabiyon ke muqaable mein meri misaal us shaks jaisi hai jisne ek ghar khoobsurati se aur behtareen tarike se banaya, siwaye ek kone mein ek eent ki jagah ke. Log uske aas-paas ghoomte hain aur uski khoobsurati par hairat hote hain, magar kahte hain: 'Kaash yeh eent apni jagah par lag jaaati!' Isliye main woh eent hoon, aur main sab Nabiyon mein sabse aakhri hoon."
