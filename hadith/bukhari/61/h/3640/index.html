Sahih al-Bukhari 3640

Book 61: Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions · Hadith 144

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ أَبِي الأَسْوَدِ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا قَيْسٌ، سَمِعْتُ الْمُغِيرَةَ بْنَ شُعْبَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ يَزَالُ نَاسٌ مِنْ أُمَّتِي ظَاهِرِينَ حَتَّى يَأْتِيَهُمْ أَمْرُ اللَّهِ وَهُمْ ظَاهِرُونَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Al-Mughira bin Shu`ba:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Some of my followers will remain victorious (and on the right path) till the Last Day comes, and they will still be victorious

Al-Mughira bin Shu'ba bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Mere pairokaron mein se kuch log Qiyamat tak fatah pate rahenge (aur seedhe raaste par rahenge), aur woh phir bhi fatah pate rahenge."
