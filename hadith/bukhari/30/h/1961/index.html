Sahih al-Bukhari 1961

Book 30: Fasting · Hadith 68

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي يَحْيَى، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي قَتَادَةُ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ لاَ تُوَاصِلُوا ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالُوا إِنَّكَ تُوَاصِلُ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ لَسْتُ كَأَحَدٍ مِنْكُمْ، إِنِّي أُطْعَمُ وَأُسْقَى، أَوْ إِنِّي أَبِيتُ أُطْعَمُ وَأُسْقَى ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Do not practice Al-Wisal (fasting continuously without breaking one's fast in the evening or eating before the following dawn)." The people said to the Prophet, "But you practice Al- Wisal?" The Prophet (ﷺ) replied, "I am not like any of you, for I am given food and drink (by Allah) during the night

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Al-Wisal (lagataar roza rakhna bina shaam ko khole ya agli subah tak kuch khaye) na karo." Logon ne Nabi se kaha: "Magar aap to Al-Wisal karte hain?" Nabi ne jawab diya: "Main tum mein se kisi jaisa nahi, kyunke mujhe raat mein khana-paani diya jaata hai (Allah ki taraf se)."
