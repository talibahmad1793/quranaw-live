Sahih al-Bukhari 5506

Book 72: Hunting, Slaughtering · Hadith 32

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قَبِيصَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَبَايَةَ بْنِ رِفَاعَةَ، عَنْ رَافِعِ بْنِ خَدِيجٍ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ كُلْ ـ يَعْنِي ـ مَا أَنْهَرَ الدَّمَ إِلاَّ السِّنَّ وَالظُّفُرَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Rafi` bin Khadij:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Eat what is slaughtered (with any instrument) that makes blood flow out, except what is slaughtered with a tooth or a nail

Rafi' bin Khadij bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo bhi (kisi bhi hathiyaar se) zibah kiya jaaye jo khoon bahaaye, use khaao, siwaaye us jaanwar ke jo daant ya naakhoon se zibah kiya gaya ho.'
