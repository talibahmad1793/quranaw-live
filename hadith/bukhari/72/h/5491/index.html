Sahih al-Bukhari 5491

Book 72: Hunting, Slaughtering · Hadith 17

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنْ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ، مِثْلَهُ إِلاَّ أَنَّهُ قَالَ ‏ "‏ هَلْ مَعَكُمْ مِنْ لَحْمِهِ شَىْءٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Qatada:(the same Hadith above, but he added); The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, "Is there any of its meat left with you?

Abu Qatada bataate hain: (Wahi upar wali hadees, is izaafe ke saath) Nabi (SAW) ne pucha, 'Kya iska kuch gosht tumhaare paas bacha hai?'
