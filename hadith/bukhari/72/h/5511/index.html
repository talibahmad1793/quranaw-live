Sahih al-Bukhari 5511

Book 72: Hunting, Slaughtering · Hadith 37

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ، سَمِعَ عَبْدَةَ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ فَاطِمَةَ، عَنْ أَسْمَاءَ، قَالَتْ ذَبَحْنَا عَلَى عَهْدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَرَسًا وَنَحْنُ بِالْمَدِينَةِ فَأَكَلْنَاهُ‏.‏

Narrated Asma':We slaughtered a horse (by Dhabh) during the lifetime of Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) while we were at Medina, and we ate it

Asma bataati hain: Humne Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ki zindagi mein ek ghoda (Dhabh ke tareeke se) zibah kiya jabke hum Madina mein the, aur humne use khaaya.
