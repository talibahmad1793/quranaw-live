Sahih al-Bukhari 1090

Book 18: Shortening the Prayers (At-Taqseer) · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتِ الصَّلاَةُ أَوَّلُ مَا فُرِضَتْ رَكْعَتَيْنِ فَأُقِرَّتْ صَلاَةُ السَّفَرِ، وَأُتِمَّتْ صَلاَةُ الْحَضَرِ‏.‏ قَالَ الزُّهْرِيُّ فَقُلْتُ لِعُرْوَةَ مَا بَالُ عَائِشَةَ تُتِمُّ قَالَ تَأَوَّلَتْ مَا تَأَوَّلَ عُثْمَانُ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:"When the prayers were first enjoined they were of two rak`at each. Later the prayer in a journey was kept as it was but the prayers for non-travelers were completed." Az-Zuhri said, "I asked `Urwa what made Aisha pray the full prayers (in journey)." He replied, "She did the same as `Uthman did

Ayesha (رضی اللہ عنہا) bataati hain: "Jab pehli baar namazein farz ki gayin, woh do-do rakat thi. Baad mein safar ki namaz waisi hi rahi magar (ghar par) na safar karne walon ki namazein poori kar di gayin." Az-Zuhri ne kaha: "Maine Urwa se pucha kis wajah se Ayesha safar mein bhi poori namaz padhti thi." Unhone jawab diya: "Unhone wahi kiya jo Uthman ne kiya."
