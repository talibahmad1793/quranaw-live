Sahih al-Bukhari 1081

Book 18: Shortening the Prayers (At-Taqseer) · Hadith 2

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَنَسًا، يَقُولُ خَرَجْنَا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنَ الْمَدِينَةِ إِلَى مَكَّةَ، فَكَانَ يُصَلِّي رَكْعَتَيْنِ رَكْعَتَيْنِ حَتَّى رَجَعْنَا إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ‏.‏ قُلْتُ أَقَمْتُمْ بِمَكَّةَ شَيْئًا قَالَ أَقَمْنَا بِهَا عَشْرًا‏.‏

Narrated Yahya bin 'Is-haq:I heard Anas saying, "We traveled with the Prophet (ﷺ) from Medina to Mecca and offered two rak`at (for every prayer) till we returned to Medina." I said, "Did you stay for a while in Mecca?" He replied, "We stayed in Mecca for ten days

Yahya bin Ishaq bataate hain: Maine Anas ko kehte suna: "Hum Nabi ke saath Madina se Mecca gaye aur (har namaz mein) do rakat padhte rahe yahan tak ke wapas Madina aa gaye." Maine pucha: "Kya aap Mecca mein kuch der ruke the?" Unhone jawab diya: "Hum Mecca mein das din ruke the."
