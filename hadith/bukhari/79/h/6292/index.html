Sahih al-Bukhari 6292

Book 79: Asking Permission · Hadith 64

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، عَنْ أَنَس ٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ أُقِيمَتِ الصَّلاَةُ وَرَجُلٌ يُنَاجِي رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَمَا زَالَ يُنَاجِيهِ حَتَّى نَامَ أَصْحَابُهُ، ثُمَّ قَامَ فَصَلَّى‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Iqama for the prayer was announced while a man was talking to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) privately. He continued talking in that way till the Prophet's companions slept, and afterwards the Prophet (ﷺ) got up and offered the prayer with them

Anas bataate hain: Namaz ki Iqama kahi gayi jabke ek shaks Allah ke Rasool (SAW) se chupke se baat kar raha tha. Wo isi tarah baat karta raha yahaan tak ke Nabi (SAW) ke sahaaba so gaye, aur uske baad Nabi (SAW) uthe aur unke saath namaz padhi.
