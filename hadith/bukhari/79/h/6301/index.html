Sahih al-Bukhari 6301

Book 79: Asking Permission · Hadith 73

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ بُكَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ عُقَيْلٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي حُمَيْدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ مَنْ حَلَفَ مِنْكُمْ فَقَالَ فِي حَلِفِهِ بِاللاَّتِ وَالْعُزَّى‏.‏ فَلْيَقُلْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ‏.‏ وَمَنْ قَالَ لِصَاحِبِهِ تَعَالَ أُقَامِرْكَ‏.‏ فَلْيَتَصَدَّقْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Whoever among you takes an oath wherein he says, 'By Al-Lat and Al-`Uzza,' names of two Idols worshipped by the Pagans, he should say, 'None has the right to be worshipped but Allah; And whoever says to his friend, 'Come, let me gamble with you ! He should give something in charity. " (See Hadith No)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo koi tum mein se qasam khaata hai jismein wo kehta hai, "Al-Lat aur Al-Uzza ki qasam" (mushrikon ki poojee jaane wali do buton ke naam), use kehna chaahiye, "Allah ke siwa koi ibaadat ke laayak nahi." Aur jo koi apne dost se kahe, "Aao, main tumse jua khelun," use kuch sadqa dena chaahiye.' (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees dekhein.)
