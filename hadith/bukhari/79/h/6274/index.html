Sahih al-Bukhari 6274

Book 79: Asking Permission · Hadith 48

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا بِشْرٌ، مِثْلَهُ، وَكَانَ مُتَّكِئًا فَجَلَسَ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ أَلاَ وَقَوْلُ الزُّورِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَمَا زَالَ يُكَرِّرُهَا حَتَّى قُلْنَا لَيْتَهُ سَكَتَ‏.‏

Narrated Bishr:as above (No. 290) adding: The Prophet (ﷺ) was reclining (leaning) and then he sat up saying, "And I warn you against giving a false statement." And he kept on saying that warning so much so that we said, "Would that he had stopped

Bishr bataate hain (upar wali riwayat jaisi hi), izaafa karte hue: Nabi (SAW) tek lagaaye hue baithe the, phir seedhe baith gaye aur kaha, 'Aur main tumhein jhoothi baat kehne se khabardaar karta hoon.' Unhone ye chetaawani itni baar dohraayi ke humne kaha, 'Kaash wo ruk jaate.'
