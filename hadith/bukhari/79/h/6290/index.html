Book 79: Asking Permission · Hadith 62

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُثْمَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏{‏إِذَا كُنْتُمْ ثَلاَثَةً فَلاَ يَتَنَاجَى رَجُلاَنِ دُونَ الآخَرِ، حَتَّى تَخْتَلِطُوا بِالنَّاسِ، أَجْلَ أَنْ يُحْزِنَهُ‏}‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "When you are three persons sitting together, then no two of you should hold secret counsel excluding the third person until you are with some other people too, for that would grieve him

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jab tum teen log saath baithe ho, to tum mein se do ko teesre ko chhod kar chupke se baat nahi karni chaahiye, jab tak tum kisi aur (chauthe) ke saath na ho, kyunke isse use dukh pahunchega.'
