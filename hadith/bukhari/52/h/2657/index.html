Sahih al-Bukhari 2657

Book 52: Witnesses · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا زِيَادُ بْنُ يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمُ بْنُ وَرْدَانَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ، عَنِ الْمِسْوَرِ بْنِ مَخْرَمَةَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ قَدِمَتْ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَقْبِيَةٌ فَقَالَ لِي أَبِي مَخْرَمَةُ انْطَلِقْ بِنَا إِلَيْهِ عَسَى أَنْ يُعْطِيَنَا مِنْهَا شَيْئًا‏.‏ فَقَامَ أَبِي عَلَى الْبَابِ فَتَكَلَّمَ، فَعَرَفَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم صَوْتَهُ فَخَرَجَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَمَعَهُ قَبَاءٌ وَهُوَ يُرِيهِ مَحَاسِنَهُ وَهُوَ يَقُولَ ‏ "‏ خَبَأْتُ هَذَا لَكَ، خَبَأْتُ هَذَا لَكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Al-Miswar bin Makhrama:Some outer garments were received by the Prophet (ﷺ) and my father (Makhrama) said to me, "Let us go to the Prophet (ﷺ) so that he may give us something from the garments." So, my father stood at the door and spoke. The Prophet (ﷺ) recognized his voice and came out carrying a garment and telling Makhrama the good qualities of that garment, adding, "I have kept this for you, I have sent this for you

Al-Miswar bin Makhrama bataate hain: Nabi ke paas kuch bahar wale libaas aaye aur mere waalid (Makhrama) ne mujhse kaha: "Chalo Nabi ke paas chalte hain taake woh humein in libaason mein se kuch de sakein." Isliye mere waalid darwaze par khade hokar bole. Nabi ne unki aawaaz pehchaani aur bahar aaye, ek libaas lekar, aur Makhrama ko us libaas ki achhaiyan batate huye kaha: "Maine ise tumhare liye rakha tha, maine ise tumhare liye bheja tha."
