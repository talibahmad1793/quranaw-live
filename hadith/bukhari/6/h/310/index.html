Sahih al-Bukhari 310

Book 6: Menstrual Periods · Hadith 15

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ زُرَيْعٍ، عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتِ اعْتَكَفَتْ مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم امْرَأَةٌ مِنْ أَزْوَاجِهِ، فَكَانَتْ تَرَى الدَّمَ وَالصُّفْرَةَ، وَالطَّسْتُ تَحْتَهَا وَهْىَ تُصَلِّي‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:"One of the wives of Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) joined him in I`tikaf and she noticed blood and yellowish discharge (from her private parts) and put a dish under her when she prayed

Ayesha (رضی اللہ عنہا) se riwayat hai: "Nabi ki ek biwi unke saath I'tikaf mein thi, unhe khoon aur peela sa mawaad (mahwaari ke darmiyan) aata tha, aur namaz ke waqt woh apne neeche ek bartan rakh leti."
