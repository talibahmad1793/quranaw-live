Sahih al-Bukhari 311

Book 6: Menstrual Periods · Hadith 16

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا مُعْتَمِرٌ، عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّ بَعْضَ، أُمَّهَاتِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ اعْتَكَفَتْ وَهْىَ مُسْتَحَاضَةٌ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:One of the mothers of the faithful believers (i.e. the wives of the Prophet (ﷺ) ) did I`tikaf while she was having bleeding in between her periods

Ayesha (رضی اللہ عنہا) se riwayat hai ke Nabi ki ek biwi ne I'tikaf kiya jabke unhe apne haiz ke darmiyan khoon aata tha.
