Sahih al-Bukhari 7249

Book 95: Accepting Information Given by a Truthful Person · Hadith 4

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا حَفْصُ بْنُ عُمَرَ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ صَلَّى بِنَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم الظُّهْرَ خَمْسًا فَقِيلَ أَزِيدَ فِي الصَّلاَةِ قَالَ ‏ "‏ وَمَا ذَاكَ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالُوا صَلَّيْتَ خَمْسًا‏.‏ فَسَجَدَ سَجْدَتَيْنِ بَعْدَ مَا سَلَّمَ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) led us in Zuhr prayer and prayer five rak`at. Somebody asked him whether the prayer had been increased." He (the Prophet (ﷺ) ) said, "And what is that?" They (the people) replied, "You have prayed five rak`at." Then the Prophet (ﷺ) offered two prostrations (of Sahu) after he had finished his prayer with the Taslim

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne humein Zuhr ki namaz padhaayi aur paanch rakat padhi. Kisi ne unse pucha ke kya namaz badha di gayi hai. Unhone (Nabi SAW ne) kaha, 'Wo kya hai?' Unhone (logon ne) jawab diya, 'Aapne paanch rakat padhi.' Phir Nabi (SAW) ne Taslim se apni namaz khatam karne ke baad do (Sahu) sajde kiye.
