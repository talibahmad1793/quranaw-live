Sahih al-Bukhari 5363

Book 69: Supporting the Family · Hadith 13

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَرْعَرَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ بْنِ عُتَيْبَةَ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنِ الأَسْوَدِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، سَأَلْتُ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ مَا كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَصْنَعُ فِي الْبَيْتِ قَالَتْ كَانَ فِي مِهْنَةِ أَهْلِهِ، فَإِذَا سَمِعَ الأَذَانَ خَرَجَ‏.‏

Narrated Al-Aswad bin Yazid:I asked `Aisha "What did the Prophet (ﷺ) use to do at home?" She said, "He used to work for his family, and when he heard the Adhan (call for the prayer), he would go out

Al-Aswad bin Yazid bataate hain: Maine Aisha se pucha, 'Nabi (SAW) ghar mein kya kaam kiya karte the?' Unhone kaha, 'Wo apne khaandaan ke kaamon mein haath bataate the, aur jab Azaan sunte, to (namaz ke liye) baahar chale jaate.'
