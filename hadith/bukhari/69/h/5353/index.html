Sahih al-Bukhari 5353

Book 69: Supporting the Family · Hadith 3

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ قَزَعَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ ثَوْرِ بْنِ زَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي الْغَيْثِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ السَّاعِي عَلَى الأَرْمَلَةِ وَالْمِسْكِينِ كَالْمُجَاهِدِ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ، أَوِ الْقَائِمِ اللَّيْلَ الصَّائِمِ النَّهَارَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The one who looks after a widow or a poor person is like a Mujahid (warrior) who fights for Allah's Cause, or like him who performs prayers all the night and fasts all the day

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo koi biwaa ya gareeb ki dekhbhaal karta hai, wo Allah ki raah mein jihaad karne wale Mujahid jaisa hai, ya us shaks jaisa hai jo poori raat namaz padhta hai aur poore din roza rakhta hai.'
