Sahih al-Bukhari 2377

Book 42: Distribution of Water · Hadith 25

Hadith text

وَقَالَ اللَّيْثُ عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ دَعَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم الأَنْصَارَ لِيُقْطِعَ لَهُمْ بِالْبَحْرَيْنِ، فَقَالُوا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنْ فَعَلْتَ فَاكْتُبْ لإِخْوَانِنَا مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ بِمِثْلِهَا، فَلَمْ يَكُنْ ذَلِكَ عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّكُمْ سَتَرَوْنَ بَعْدِي أَثَرَةً فَاصْبِرُوا حَتَّى تَلْقَوْنِي ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas (ra):The Prophet (ﷺ) called the Ansar so as to grant them a portion of (the land of) Bahrain. They said, "O Allah's Messenger ! If you grant this to us, write a similar document on our Quraish (emigrant) brothers." But the Prophet (ﷺ) did not have enough grants and he said: "After me you will see the people giving preference (to others), so be patient till you meet me

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne Ansaar ko bulaya taake unhe Bahrain (ki zameen) ka ek hissa den. Unhone kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Agar aap yeh humein den, humare Quraish (Muhajir) bhaion ke liye bhi isi jaisi dastavez likhiye." Magar Nabi ke paas dene ke liye kaafi nahi tha aur unhone kaha: "Mere baad tum dekhoge ke log doosron ko tarjih denge, isliye sabr karo jab tak tum mujhse (Qiyamat ke din) na milo."
