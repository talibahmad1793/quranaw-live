Sahih al-Bukhari 3166

Book 58: Jizyah and Mawaada'ah · Hadith 8

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قَيْسُ بْنُ حَفْصٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَاحِدِ، حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو، حَدَّثَنَا مُجَاهِدٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَنْ قَتَلَ مُعَاهَدًا لَمْ يَرَحْ رَائِحَةَ الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّ رِيحَهَا تُوجَدُ مِنْ مَسِيرَةِ أَرْبَعِينَ عَامًا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Amr:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Whoever killed a person having a treaty with the Muslims, shall not smell the smell of Paradise though its smell is perceived from a distance of forty years

Abdullah bin Amr bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Jisne bhi kisi Musalmanon ke saath sandhi (muahida) wale shaks ko qatl kiya, woh Jannat ki khushbu bhi nahi soonghega, chahe uski khushbu chaalis saal ki doori se mahsoos ki jaati ho."
