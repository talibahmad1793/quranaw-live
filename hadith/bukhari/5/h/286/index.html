Sahih al-Bukhari 286

Book 5: Bathing (Ghusl) · Hadith 38

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامٌ، وَشَيْبَانُ، عَنْ يَحْيَى، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، قَالَ سَأَلْتُ عَائِشَةَ أَكَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَرْقُدُ وَهْوَ جُنُبٌ قَالَتْ نَعَمْ وَيَتَوَضَّأُ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Salama:I asked `Aisha "Did the Prophet (ﷺ) use to sleep while he was Junub?" She replied, "Yes, but he used to perform ablution (before going to bed)

Abu Salama bataate hain ke maine Ayesha se pucha: "Kya Nabi Junub halat mein sote the?" Unhone jawab diya: "Haan, magar sone se pehle wudu kar lete."
