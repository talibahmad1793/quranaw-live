Sahih al-Bukhari 5266

Book 68: Divorce · Hadith 16

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي الْحَسَنُ بْنُ صَبَّاحٍ، سَمِعَ الرَّبِيعَ بْنَ نَافِعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُعَاوِيَةُ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ أَبِي كَثِيرٍ، عَنْ يَعْلَى بْنِ حَكِيمٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، أَنَّهُ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ، سَمِعَ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ، يَقُولُ إِذَا حَرَّمَ امْرَأَتَهُ لَيْسَ بِشَىْءٍ‏.‏ وَقَالَ ‏{‏لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللَّهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ‏}‏

Narrated Sa`id bin Jubair:that he heard Ibn `Abbas saying, "If a man makes his wife unlawful for him, it does not mean that she is divorced." He added, "Indeed in the Messenger of Allah , you have a good example to follow

Saeed bin Jubair bataate hain: Unhone Ibn Abbas ko ye kehte suna, 'Agar koi apni biwi ko apne liye haraam kar le, iska matlab ye nahi ke wo talaaq-shuda ho gayi.' Unhone aage kaha, 'Waaqai, Allah ke Rasool (SAW) mein tumhaare liye ek behtareen namoona hai jise apnaao.'
