Sahih al-Bukhari 5324

Book 68: Divorce · Hadith 71

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ الْقَاسِمِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّهَا قَالَتْ مَا لِفَاطِمَةَ أَلاَ تَتَّقِي اللَّهَ، يَعْنِي فِي قَوْلِهَا لاَ سُكْنَى وَلاَ نَفَقَةَ

Narrated Al-Qasim:Aisha said, "What is wrong with Fatima? Why doesn't she fear Allah?" by saying that a divorced lady is not entitled to be provided with residence and sustenance (by her husband)

Al-Qasim bataate hain: Aisha ne kaha, 'Fatima ko kya ho gaya hai? Wo Allah se kyun nahi darti,' ye kehte hue ke ek talaaq-shuda aurat ko apne shohar se rehne ki jagah aur kharche ka haq nahi hai.
