Sahih al-Bukhari 5253

Book 68: Divorce · Hadith 3

Hadith text

وَقَالَ أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ، قَالَ حُسِبَتْ عَلَىَّ بِتَطْلِيقَةٍ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:(Divorcing my wife during her menses) was counted as one legal divorce

Ibn Umar bataate hain: (Meri biwi ko uski haiz ke dauraan talaaq dena) ek qaanooni talaaq ke taur par gina gaya.
