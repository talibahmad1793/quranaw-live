Sahih al-Bukhari 5281

Book 68: Divorce · Hadith 30

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الأَعْلَى بْنُ حَمَّادٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وُهَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، قَالَ ذَاكَ مُغِيثٌ عَبْدُ بَنِي فُلاَنٍ ـ يَعْنِي زَوْجَ بَرِيرَةَ ـ كَأَنِّي أَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهِ يَتْبَعُهَا فِي سِكَكِ الْمَدِينَةِ، يَبْكِي عَلَيْهَا‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:That was Mughith, the slave of Bani so-and-so, i.e., Barira's husband as if I am now looking at him following her (Barira) along the streets of Medina

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Wo Mughith the, falaan qabeele ke ghulaam, yaani Barira ke shohar, aisa lagta hai jaise main abhi bhi unhein Madina ki galiyon mein Barira ke peechhe peechhe chalte dekh raha hoon.
