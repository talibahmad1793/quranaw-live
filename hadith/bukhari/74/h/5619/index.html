Sahih al-Bukhari 5619

Book 74: Drinks · Hadith 45

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أُتِيَ بِلَبَنٍ قَدْ شِيبَ بِمَاءٍ، وَعَنْ يَمِينِهِ أَعْرَابِيٌّ وَعَنْ شِمَالِهِ أَبُو بَكْرٍ، فَشَرِبَ، ثُمَّ أَعْطَى الأَعْرَابِيَّ، وَقَالَ ‏ "‏ الأَيْمَنَ الأَيْمَنَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:Milk mixed with water was brought to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) while a bedouin was on his right and Abu Bakr was on his left. He drank (of it) and then gave (it) to the bedouin and said, 'The right" "The right (first)

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Doodh mein paani milaakar Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ke paas laaya gaya jabke ek badawi unke daaen taraf tha aur Abu Bakr unke baaen taraf. Unhone piya, phir badawi ko diya aur kaha, 'Daayin taraf! Daayin taraf pehle!'
