Sahih al-Bukhari 5591

Book 74: Drinks · Hadith 17

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ سَهْلاً، يَقُولُ أَتَى أَبُو أُسَيْدٍ السَّاعِدِيُّ فَدَعَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي عُرْسِهِ، فَكَانَتِ امْرَأَتُهُ خَادِمَهُمْ وَهْىَ الْعَرُوسُ‏.‏ قَالَتْ أَتَدْرُونَ مَا سَقَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْقَعْتُ لَهُ تَمَرَاتٍ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ فِي تَوْرٍ‏.‏

Narrated Sahl:Abu Usaid As-Sa`idi came and invited Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) on the occasion of his wedding. His wife who was the bride, was serving them. Do you know what drink she prepared for Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) ? She had soaked some dates in water in a Tur overnight

Sahl bataate hain: Abu Usaid As-Sa'idi aaye aur apni shaadi ke mauqe par Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko dawat di. Unki biwi, jo dulhan thi, unki khidmat kar rahi thi. Kya tumhein pata hai unhone Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ke liye kaunsa sharbat taiyaar kiya tha? Unhone raat bhar kuch khajoorein ek Tur (bartan) mein paani mein bhigo kar rakhi thin.
