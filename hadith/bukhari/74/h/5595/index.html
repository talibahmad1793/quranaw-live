Sahih al-Bukhari 5595

Book 74: Drinks · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عُثْمَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، قُلْتُ لِلأَسْوَدِ هَلْ سَأَلْتَ عَائِشَةَ أُمَّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ عَمَّا يُكْرَهُ أَنْ يُنْتَبَذَ فِيهِ فَقَالَ نَعَمْ قُلْتُ يَا أُمَّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ عَمَّا نَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْ يُنْتَبَذَ فِيهِ قَالَتْ نَهَانَا فِي ذَلِكَ أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ أَنْ نَنْتَبِذَ فِي الدُّبَّاءِ وَالْمُزَفَّتِ‏.‏ قُلْتُ أَمَا ذَكَرْتِ الْجَرَّ وَالْحَنْتَمَ قَالَ إِنَّمَا أُحَدِّثُكَ مَا سَمِعْتُ، أَفَأُحَدِّثُ مَا لَمْ أَسْمَعْ

Narrated Ibrahim:I asked Al-Aswad, "Did you ask `Aisha, Mother of the Believers, about the containers in which it is disliked to prepare (non-alcoholic) drinks?" He said, "Yes, I said to her, 'O Mother of the Believers! What containers did the Prophet (ﷺ) forbid to use for preparing (non-alcoholic) drinks?" She said, 'The Prophet forbade us, (his family), to prepare (non-alcoholic) drinks in Ad-Dubba and Al-Muzaffat.' I asked, 'Didn't you mention Al Jar and Al Hantam?' She said, 'I tell what I have heard; shall I tell you what I have not heard?

Ibrahim bataate hain: Maine Al-Aswad se pucha, 'Kya aapne Aisha, Ummul-Momineen, se un bartanon ke baare mein pucha jinmein (bagair sharaab wale) sharbat banaana napasand kiya jaata hai?' Unhone kaha, 'Haan, maine unse pucha, "Aye Ummul-Momineen! Nabi (SAW) ne kaunse bartanon mein (bagair sharaab wale) sharbat banaane se mana kiya?" Unhone kaha, "Nabi (SAW) ne humein (apne khaandaan ko) Ad-Dubba aur Al-Muzaffat mein sharbat banaane se mana kiya." Maine pucha, "Aapne Al-Jar aur Al-Hantam ka zikr nahi kiya?" Unhone kaha, "Main wahi bataati hoon jo maine suna hai; kya main tumhein wo bhi bataun jo maine nahi suna?"'
