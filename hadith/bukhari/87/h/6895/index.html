Sahih al-Bukhari 6895

Book 87: Blood Money (Ad-Diyat) · Hadith 33

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ هَذِهِ وَهَذِهِ سَوَاءٌ ‏"‏، يَعْنِي الْخِنْصَرَ وَالإِبْهَامَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "This and this are the same." He meant the little finger and the thumb

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Ye aur ye barabar hain.' Unka ishara chhoti ungli aur angoothe ki taraf tha.
