Book 87: Blood Money (Ad-Diyat) · Hadith 26

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الزِّنَادِ، أَنَّ الأَعْرَجَ، حَدَّثَهُ أَنَّهُ، سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، يَقُولُ إِنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏"‏ نَحْنُ الآخِرُونَ السَّابِقُونَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَبِإِسْنَادِهِ ‏"‏ لَوِ اطَّلَعَ فِي بَيْتِكَ أَحَدٌ وَلَمْ تَأْذَنْ لَهُ، خَذَفْتَهُ بِحَصَاةٍ فَفَقَأْتَ عَيْنَهُ، مَا كَانَ عَلَيْكَ مِنْ جُنَاحٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:That he heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) saying, "We (Muslims) are the last (to come) but (will be) the foremost (on the Day of Resurrection)." And added, "If someone is peeping (looking secretly) into your house without your permission, and you throw a stone at him and destroy his eyes, there will be no blame on you

Abu Huraira bataate hain ke unhone Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko ye kehte suna, 'Hum (Musalmaan) aakhir mein aane wale hain magar (Qayamat ke din) sabse aage hone wale honge.' Aur izaafa kiya, 'Agar koi tumhaari ijaazat ke bagair tumhaare ghar mein chupke se jhaank raha ho, aur tum uspar pathhar phenko aur uski aankhein zaaya kar do, to tumpar koi ilzaam nahi hoga.'
