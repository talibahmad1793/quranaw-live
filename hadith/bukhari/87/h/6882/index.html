Book 87: Blood Money (Ad-Diyat) · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي حُسَيْنٍ، حَدَّثَنَا نَافِعُ بْنُ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ أَبْغَضُ النَّاسِ إِلَى اللَّهِ ثَلاَثَةٌ مُلْحِدٌ فِي الْحَرَمِ، وَمُبْتَغٍ فِي الإِسْلاَمِ سُنَّةَ الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ، وَمُطَّلِبُ دَمِ امْرِئٍ بِغَيْرِ حَقٍّ لِيُهَرِيقَ دَمَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The most hated persons to Allah are three: (1) A person who deviates from the right conduct, i.e., an evil doer, in the Haram (sanctuaries of Mecca and Medina); (2) a person who seeks that the traditions of the Pre-Islamic Period of Ignorance, should remain in Islam (3) and a person who seeks to shed somebody's blood without any right

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah ke nazdeek sabse zyaada napasandidaa teen log hain: (1) wo shaks jo Haram (Makkah aur Madina ki panaah-gaahon) mein seedhe raaste se bhatak jaaye, yaani ek badkaar shaks; (2) wo shaks jo chaahe ke Jaahiliyat (Islam se pehle) ke daur ki riwaayaten Islam mein bani rahein; (3) aur wo shaks jo bina kisi haq ke kisi ka khoon bahaana chaahe.'
