Sahih al-Bukhari 5662

Book 75: Patients · Hadith 22

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ، حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم دَخَلَ عَلَى رَجُلٍ يَعُودُهُ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ لاَ بَأْسَ طَهُورٌ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ كَلاَّ بَلْ حُمَّى تَفُورُ عَلَى شَيْخٍ كَبِيرٍ كَيْمَا تُزِيرَهُ الْقُبُورَ‏.‏ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ فَنَعَمْ إِذًا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) entered upon sick man to pay him a visit, and said to him, "Don't worry, Allah willing, (your sickness will be) an expiation for your sins." The man said, "No, it is but a fever that is boiling within an old man and will send him to his grave." On that, the Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Then yes, it is so

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ek bimaar aadmi ke paas gaye unse milne, aur unse kaha, 'Pareshaan mat ho, Insha-Allah, (tumhaari bimaari) tumhaare gunaahon ka kaffara banegi.' Us aadmi ne kaha, 'Nahi, ye to bas ek bukhaar hai jo ek budhe aadmi ke andar ubal raha hai aur usse uski qabr tak bhej dega.' Isper Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'To, haan, phir aisa hi hoga.'
