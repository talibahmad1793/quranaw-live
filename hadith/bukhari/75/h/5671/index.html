Sahih al-Bukhari 5671

Book 75: Patients · Hadith 31

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا ثَابِتٌ الْبُنَانِيُّ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ لاَ يَتَمَنَّيَنَّ أَحَدُكُمُ الْمَوْتَ مِنْ ضُرٍّ أَصَابَهُ، فَإِنْ كَانَ لاَ بُدَّ فَاعِلاً فَلْيَقُلِ اللَّهُمَّ أَحْيِنِي مَا كَانَتِ الْحَيَاةُ خَيْرًا لِي، وَتَوَفَّنِي إِذَا كَانَتِ الْوَفَاةُ خَيْرًا لِي ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "None of you should wish for death because of a calamity befalling him; but if he has to wish for death, he should say: "O Allah! Keep me alive as long as life is better for me, and let me die if death is better for me

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Tum mein se koi bhi kisi museebat ki wajah se maut ki tamanna na kare; magar agar maut ki tamanna karna zaroori ho, to use kehna chaahiye, "Aye Allah! Mujhe zinda rakh jab tak zindagi mere liye behtar ho, aur mujhe maut de agar maut mere liye behtar ho."'
