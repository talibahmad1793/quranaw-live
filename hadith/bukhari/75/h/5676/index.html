Sahih al-Bukhari 5676

Book 75: Patients · Hadith 36

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ الْمُنْكَدِرِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ دَخَلَ عَلَىَّ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَنَا مَرِيضٌ فَتَوَضَّأَ فَصَبَّ عَلَىَّ أَوْ قَالَ صُبُّوا عَلَيْهِ فَعَقَلْتُ فَقُلْتُ لاَ يَرِثُنِي إِلاَّ كَلاَلَةٌ، فَكَيْفَ الْمِيرَاثُ فَنَزَلَتْ آيَةُ الْفَرَائِضِ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) came to me while I was ill. He performed ablution and threw the remaining water on me (or said, "Pour it on him) " When I came to my senses I said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! I have no son or father to be my heir, so how will be my inheritance?" Then the Verse of inheritance was revealed

Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) mere paas aaye jab main bimar tha. Unhone wuzu kiya aur baacha hua paani mujhpar daala (ya kaha, 'Ise usper daalo'). Jab mujhe hosh aaya, maine kaha, 'Aye Allah ke Rasool! Mera koi beta ya waalid nahi jo mera waaris ho, to meri miraas kaise hogi?' Isper miraas ki Aayat naazil hui.
