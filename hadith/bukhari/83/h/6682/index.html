Sahih al-Bukhari 6682

Book 83: Oaths and Vows · Hadith 59

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ فُضَيْلٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عُمَارَةُ بْنُ الْقَعْقَاعِ، عَنْ أَبِي زُرْعَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ كَلِمَتَانِ خَفِيفَتَانِ عَلَى اللِّسَانِ، ثَقِيلَتَانِ فِي الْمِيزَانِ، حَبِيبَتَانِ إِلَى الرَّحْمَنِ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "(The following are) two words (sentences or utterances) that are very easy for the tongue to say, and very heavy in the balance (of reward), and most beloved to the Gracious Almighty (And they are): Subhan Allahi wa bi-hamdihi; Subhan Allahi-l-'Adhim

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, '(Ye) do jumle (vaakya ya bol) hain jo zubaan ke liye bahut aasaan hain, aur (ajr ki) tarazu mein bahut bhaari hain, aur Rehman-o-Kareem (Allah) ko sabse pyaare hain (aur wo hain): Subhan Allahi Wa Bihamdihi; Subhan Allahil-Azeem.'
