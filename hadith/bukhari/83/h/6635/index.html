Sahih al-Bukhari 6635

Book 83: Oaths and Vows · Hadith 14

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وَهْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ أَبِي يَعْقُوبَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ أَبِي بَكْرَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ أَرَأَيْتُمْ إِنْ كَانَ أَسْلَمُ وَغِفَارُ وَمُزَيْنَةُ وَجُهَيْنَةُ خَيْرًا مِنْ تَمِيمٍ وَعَامِرِ بْنِ صَعْصَعَةَ وَغَطَفَانَ وَأَسَدٍ، خَابُوا وَخَسِرُوا ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالُوا نَعَمْ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ إِنَّهُمْ خَيْرٌ مِنْهُمْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Bakra:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Do you think if the tribes of Aslam, Ghifar, Muzaina and Juhaina are better than the tribes of Tamim, 'Amir bin Sa'sa'a, Ghatfan and Asad, they (the second group) are despairing and losing?" They (the Prophet's companions) said, "Yes, (they are)." He said, "By Him in Whose Hand my soul is, they (the first group) are better than them (the second group)

Abu Bakra bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Kya tumhein lagta hai agar Aslam, Ghifar, Muzaina aur Juhaina (qabeele) Tamim, Aamir bin Sa'sa'a, Ghatfan aur Asad qabeelon se behtar hain, to wo (doosre giroh) nirash aur ghaate mein hain?' Unhone (Nabi ke sahaaba ne) kaha, 'Haan, (wo hain).' Unhone kaha, 'Us Zaat ki qasam jiske haath mein meri jaan hai, wo (pehla giroh) inse (doosre giroh se) behtar hai.'
