Sahih al-Bukhari 6656

Book 83: Oaths and Vows · Hadith 35

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ يَمُوتُ لأَحَدٍ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ ثَلاَثَةٌ مِنَ الْوَلَدِ، تَمَسُّهُ النَّارُ، إِلاَّ تَحِلَّةَ الْقَسَمِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Any Muslim who has lost three of his children will not be touched by the Fire except that which will render Allah's oath fulfilled

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Koi bhi Musalmaan jisne apne teen bacche khoye hon, use Aag nahi chhuegi siwaaye us hadd tak jo Allah ki qasam poori karne ke liye zaroori ho (yaani qasam poori karne ke liye maamooli sa asar).'
