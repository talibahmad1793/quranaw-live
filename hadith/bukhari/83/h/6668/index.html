Sahih al-Bukhari 6668

Book 83: Oaths and Vows · Hadith 46

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا فَرْوَةُ بْنُ أَبِي الْمَغْرَاءِ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُسْهِرٍ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتْ هُزِمَ الْمُشْرِكُونَ يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ هَزِيمَةً تُعْرَفُ فِيهِمْ، فَصَرَخَ إِبْلِيسُ أَىْ عِبَادَ اللَّهِ أُخْرَاكُمْ، فَرَجَعَتْ أُولاَهُمْ فَاجْتَلَدَتْ هِيَ وَأُخْرَاهُمْ، فَنَظَرَ حُذَيْفَةُ بْنُ الْيَمَانِ فَإِذَا هُوَ بِأَبِيهِ فَقَالَ أَبِي أَبِي‏.‏ قَالَتْ فَوَاللَّهِ مَا انْحَجَزُوا حَتَّى قَتَلُوهُ، فَقَالَ حُذَيْفَةُ غَفَرَ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ‏.‏ قَالَ عُرْوَةُ فَوَاللَّهِ مَا زَالَتْ فِي حُذَيْفَةَ مِنْهَا بَقِيَّةٌ حَتَّى لَقِيَ اللَّهَ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:When the pagans were defeated during the (first stage) of the battle of Uhud, Satan shouted, "O Allah's slaves! Beware of what is behind you!" So the front files of the Muslims attacked their own back files. Hudhaifa bin Al-Yaman looked and on seeing his father he shouted: "My father! My father!" By Allah! The people did not stop till they killed his father. Hudhaifa then said, "May Allah forgive you." `Urwa (the sub-narrator) added, "Hudhaifa continued asking Allah forgiveness for the killers of his father till he met Allah (till he died)

Aisha bataati hain: Jab mushrik jang-e-Uhud ke (pehle marhale) mein haraa diye gaye, Shaitaan ne chillaaya, 'Aye Allah ke bandon! Apne peechhe se khabardaar raho!' To Musalmaanon ki agli qataarein apni hi peechhe waali qataaron par hamla karne lagi. Hudhaifa bin Al-Yaman ne dekha aur jab unhone apne waalid ko dekha, unhone chillaaya, 'Mere waalid! Mere waalid!' Allah ki qasam! Log nahi ruke jab tak unhone unke waalid ko qatl nahi kar diya. Hudhaifa ne phir kaha, 'Allah tumhein maaf kare.' Ravi Urwa ne izaafa kiya, 'Hudhaifa apne waalid ke qaatilon ke liye Allah se maafi maangte rahe yahaan tak ke wo Allah se mile (yaani unka intekaal ho gaya).'
