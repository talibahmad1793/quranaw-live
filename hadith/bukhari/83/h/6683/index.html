Sahih al-Bukhari 6683

Book 83: Oaths and Vows · Hadith 60

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَاحِدِ، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، عَنْ شَقِيقٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَلِمَةً وَقُلْتُ أُخْرَى ‏ "‏ مَنْ مَاتَ يَجْعَلُ لِلَّهِ نِدًّا أُدْخِلَ النَّارَ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَقُلْتُ أُخْرَى مَنْ مَاتَ لاَ يَجْعَلُ لِلَّهِ نِدًّا أُدْخِلَ الْجَنَّةَ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said a sentence and I said another. He said, "Whoever dies while he is setting up rivals along with Allah (i.e. worshipping others along with Allah) shall be admitted into the (Hell) Fire." And I said the other: "Whoever dies while he is not setting up rivals along with Allah (i.e. worshipping none except Allah) shall be admitted into Paradise

Abdullah bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne ek baat kahi aur maine doosri kahi. Unhone kaha, 'Jo koi is haalat mein mare ke wo Allah ke saath shareek thehraata ho (yaani Allah ke ilaawa doosron ki bhi ibaadat karta ho), wo Jahannam ki Aag mein daakhil kiya jaayega.' Aur maine doosri baat kahi: 'Jo koi is haalat mein mare ke wo Allah ke saath koi shareek na thehraata ho (yaani sirf Allah ki ibaadat karta ho), wo Jannat mein daakhil kiya jaayega.'
