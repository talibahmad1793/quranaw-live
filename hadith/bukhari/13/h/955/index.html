Sahih al-Bukhari 955

Book 13: The Two Festivals (Eids) · Hadith 7

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُثْمَانُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ بْنِ عَازِبٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ خَطَبَنَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ الأَضْحَى بَعْدَ الصَّلاَةِ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ مَنْ صَلَّى صَلاَتَنَا وَنَسَكَ نُسُكَنَا فَقَدْ أَصَابَ النُّسُكَ، وَمَنْ نَسَكَ قَبْلَ الصَّلاَةِ فَإِنَّهُ قَبْلَ الصَّلاَةِ، وَلاَ نُسُكَ لَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ أَبُو بُرْدَةَ بْنُ نِيَارٍ خَالُ الْبَرَاءِ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، فَإِنِّي نَسَكْتُ شَاتِي قَبْلَ الصَّلاَةِ، وَعَرَفْتُ أَنَّ الْيَوْمَ يَوْمُ أَكْلٍ وَشُرْبٍ، وَأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ تَكُونَ شَاتِي أَوَّلَ مَا يُذْبَحُ فِي بَيْتِي، فَذَبَحْتُ شَاتِي وَتَغَدَّيْتُ قَبْلَ أَنْ آتِيَ الصَّلاَةَ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ شَاتُكَ شَاةُ لَحْمٍ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، فَإِنَّ عِنْدَنَا عَنَاقًا لَنَا جَذَعَةً هِيَ أَحَبُّ إِلَىَّ مِنْ شَاتَيْنِ، أَفَتَجْزِي عَنِّي قَالَ ‏"‏ نَعَمْ، وَلَنْ تَجْزِيَ عَنْ أَحَدٍ بَعْدَكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Al-Bara' bin `Azib:The Prophet (ﷺ) delivered the Khutba after offering the prayer on the Day of Nahr and said, "Whoever offers the prayer like us and slaughters like us then his Nusuk (sacrifice) will be accepted by Allah. And whoever slaughters his sacrifice before the `Id prayer then he has not done the sacrifice." Abi Burda bin Niyar, the uncle of Al-Bara' said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! I have slaughtered my sheep before the `Id prayer and I thought today as a day of eating and drinking (not alcoholic drinks), and I liked that my sheep should be the first to be slaughtered in my house. So I slaughtered my sheep and took my food before coming for the prayer." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The sheep which you have slaughtered is just mutton (not a Nusuk)." He (Abu Burda) said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! I have a young she-goat which is dearer to me than two sheep. Will that be sufficient as a Nusuk on my behalf? "The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Yes, it will be sufficient for you but it will not be sufficient (as a Nusuk) for anyone else after you

Al-Bara bin Azib (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne Yaum-un-Nahr (Eid-ul-Adha) ke din namaz padhne ke baad khutba diya aur farmaya: "Jo humari tarah namaz padhe aur humari tarah zabah kare, uska Nusuk (qurbani) Allah ke haan qubool hogi. Aur jo apni qurbani Eid ki namaz se pehle zabah kare, usne qurbani nahi ki." Al-Bara ke chacha Abi Burda bin Niyar ne kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Maine apni bhed Eid ki namaz se pehle zabah kar li thi kyunke maine socha aaj khane-peene ka din hai (haram sharab nahi), aur main chahta tha meri bhed sabse pehle mere ghar mein zabah ho. Isliye maine apni bhed zabah karke namaz se pehle khana khaya." Nabi ne farmaya: "Jo bhed tumne zabah ki hai woh sirf gosht hai (Nusuk nahi)." Unhone (Abu Burda ne) kaha: "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Mere paas ek jawan bakri hai jo do bhedon se zyada pyari hai. Kya woh mere taraf se Nusuk ke tor par kaafi hogi?" Nabi ne farmaya: "Haan, yeh tumhare liye kaafi hai magar tumhare baad kisi aur ke liye Nusuk ke tor par kaafi nahi hogi."
