Sahih al-Bukhari 532

Book 9: Times of the Prayers · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا حَفْصُ بْنُ عُمَرَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا قَتَادَةُ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ اعْتَدِلُوا فِي السُّجُودِ، وَلاَ يَبْسُطْ ذِرَاعَيْهِ كَالْكَلْبِ، وَإِذَا بَزَقَ فَلاَ يَبْزُقَنَّ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ وَلاَ عَنْ يَمِينِهِ، فَإِنَّهُ يُنَاجِي رَبَّهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Do the prostration properly and do not put your forearms flat with elbows touching the ground like a dog. And if you want to spit, do not spit in front, nor to the right for the person in prayer is speaking in private to his Lord

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai ke Nabi ne farmaya: "Sajda achhi tarah karo, apni kohniyan zameen par kutte ki tarah bichha kar mat rakho. Aur agar thookna ho to saamne ya dahine mat thooko, kyunke namaz padhne wala apne Rab se sirgoshi kar raha hota hai."
