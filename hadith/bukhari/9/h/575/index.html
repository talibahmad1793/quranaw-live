Book 9: Times of the Prayers · Hadith 51

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَاصِمٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا هَمَّامٌ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، أَنَّ زَيْدَ بْنَ ثَابِتٍ، حَدَّثَهُ أَنَّهُمْ، تَسَحَّرُوا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ثُمَّ قَامُوا إِلَى الصَّلاَةِ‏.‏ قُلْتُ كَمْ بَيْنَهُمَا قَالَ قَدْرُ خَمْسِينَ أَوْ سِتِّينَ ـ يَعْنِي آيَةً ـ ح‏.‏

Narrated Anas:Zaid bin Thabit said, "We took the "Suhur" (the meal taken before dawn while fasting is observed) with the Prophet (ﷺ) and then stood up for the (morning) prayer." I asked him how long the interval between the two (Suhur and prayer) was. He replied, 'The interval between the two was just sufficient to recite fifty to sixty 'Ayat

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain, Zaid bin Thabit ne kaha: "Humne Nabi ke saath Sehri khai, phir (Fajr ki) namaz ke liye khade hue." Maine unse pucha in dono (Sehri aur namaz) ke darmiyan kitna waqfa tha. Unhone jawab diya: "Itna waqfa jitne mein pachaas se saath ayaat padhi ja sakein."
