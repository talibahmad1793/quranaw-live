Sahih al-Bukhari 2275

Book 37: Hiring · Hadith 15

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، عَنْ مُسْلِمٍ، عَنْ مَسْرُوقٍ، حَدَّثَنَا خَبَّابٌ، قَالَ كُنْتُ رَجُلاً قَيْنًا فَعَمِلْتُ لِلْعَاصِ بْنِ وَائِلٍ فَاجْتَمَعَ لِي عِنْدَهُ فَأَتَيْتُهُ أَتَقَاضَاهُ فَقَالَ لاَ وَاللَّهِ لاَ أَقْضِيكَ حَتَّى تَكْفُرَ بِمُحَمَّدٍ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ أَمَا وَاللَّهِ حَتَّى تَمُوتَ ثُمَّ تُبْعَثَ فَلاَ‏.‏ قَالَ وَإِنِّي لَمَيِّتٌ ثُمَّ مَبْعُوثٌ قُلْتُ نَعَمْ‏.‏ قَالَ فَإِنَّهُ سَيَكُونُ لِي ثَمَّ مَالٌ وَوَلَدٌ فَأَقْضِيكَ‏.‏ فَأَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى ‏{‏أَفَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي كَفَرَ بِآيَاتِنَا وَقَالَ لأُوتَيَنَّ مَالاً وَوَلَدًا‏}‏

Narrated Khabbab:I was a blacksmith and did some work for Al-`As bin Wail. When he owed me some money for my work, I went to him to ask for that amount. He said, "I will not pay you unless you disbelieve in Muhammad." I said, "By Allah! I will never do that till you die and be resurrected." He said, "Will I be dead and then resurrected after my death?" I said, "Yes." He said, "There I will have property and offspring and then I will pay you your due." Then Allah revealed. 'Have you seen him who disbelieved in Our signs, and yet says: I will be given property and offspring?

Khabbab (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Main ek lohar tha aur Al-Asi bin Wail ke liye kaam karta tha. Jab unpar mere kaam ka kuch paisa qarz tha, main woh maangne unke paas gaya. Unhone kaha: "Main tumhe tab tak nahi dunga jab tak tum Muhammad ko na maano." Maine kaha: "Allah ki qasam! Main aisa kabhi nahi karunga jab tak tum mar na jaao aur dobara zinda na ho jaao." Unhone kaha: "Kya main marunga aur phir apni maut ke baad dobara zinda hounga?" Maine haan mein jawab diya. Unhone kaha: "Wahan mere paas jaidad aur aulad hogi aur phir main tumhara paisa ada karunga." Phir Allah ne yeh nazil ki: "Kya tumne use dekha jisne humari nishaniyon ka inkaar kiya, aur phir kaha: Mujhe zaroor maal aur aulad di jaayegi?"
