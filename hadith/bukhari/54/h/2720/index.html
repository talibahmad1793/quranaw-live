Sahih al-Bukhari 2720

Book 54: Conditions · Hadith 9

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى، حَدَّثَنَا جُوَيْرِيَةُ بْنُ أَسْمَاءَ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ أَعْطَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَيْبَرَ الْيَهُودَ أَنْ يَعْمَلُوهَا وَيَزْرَعُوهَا، وَلَهُمْ شَطْرُ مَا يَخْرُجُ مِنْهَا‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) gave the land of Khaibar to the Jews on the condition that they would work on it and cultivate it and they would get half of its yield

Abdullah bin Umar bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne Khaibar ki zameen Yahudiyon ko is shart par di ke woh isme kaam aur kheti karenge, aur unhe iski paidawaar ka aadha hissa milega.
