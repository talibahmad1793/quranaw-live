Book 27: Pilgrims Prevented from Completing the Pilgrimage · Hadith 3

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا جُوَيْرِيَةُ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، أَنَّ بَعْضَ بَنِي عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ لَهُ لَوْ أَقَمْتَ‏.‏ بِهَذَا‏.‏

Narrated Nafi`:Some of the sons of `Abdullah told him (i.e. `Abdullah) if he had stayed (and not performed Hajj that year)

Nafi bataate hain: Abdullah ke kuch bete ne unse kaha agar woh (us saal Hajj na karke) ruk gaye hote (to behtar hota).
