Book 27: Pilgrims Prevented from Completing the Pilgrimage · Hadith 6

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مَحْمُودٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنِ الْمِسْوَرِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَحَرَ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَحْلِقَ، وَأَمَرَ أَصْحَابَهُ بِذَلِكَ‏.‏

Narrated Al-Miswar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) slaughtered (the Hadi) before he had his head shaved and then he ordered his Companions to do the same

Al-Miswar bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne apna sar munavaane se pehle (Hadi) zabah ki, phir apne sahaba ko wahi karne ka hukum diya.
