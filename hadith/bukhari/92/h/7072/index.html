Sahih al-Bukhari 7072

Book 92: Afflictions and the End of the World · Hadith 23

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدٌ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هَمَّامٍ، سَمِعْتُ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ يُشِيرُ أَحَدُكُمْ عَلَى أَخِيهِ بِالسِّلاَحِ، فَإِنَّهُ لاَ يَدْرِي لَعَلَّ الشَّيْطَانَ يَنْزِعُ فِي يَدِهِ، فَيَقَعُ فِي حُفْرَةٍ مِنَ النَّارِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "None of you should point out towards his Muslim brother with a weapon, for he does not know, Satan may tempt him to hit him and thus he would fall into a pit of fire (Hell)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Tum mein se koi bhi apne Musalmaan bhai ki taraf hathiyaar se ishara na kare, kyunke use nahi pata, shaayad Shaitaan use usse maarne ke liye uksaaye aur wo (natije mein) Aag ke gaddhe mein gir jaaye (Jahannam mein).'
