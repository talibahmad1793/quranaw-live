Sahih al-Bukhari 7066

Book 92: Afflictions and the End of the World · Hadith 17

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ وَاصِلٍ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، وَأَحْسِبُهُ، رَفَعَهُ قَالَ ‏ "‏ بَيْنَ يَدَىِ السَّاعَةِ أَيَّامُ الْهَرْجِ، يَزُولُ الْعِلْمُ، وَيَظْهَرُ فِيهَا الْجَهْلُ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو مُوسَى وَالْهَرْجُ الْقَتْلُ بِلِسَانِ الْحَبَشَةِ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Near the establishment of the Hour, there will be the days of Al-Harj, and the religious knowledge will be taken away (vanish i.e. by the death of Religious scholars) and general ignorance will spread." Abu Musa said, "Al-Harj, in the Ethiopian language, means killing

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Qayamat ke qareeb, Al-Harj ke din aayenge, aur deeni ilm uthaa liya jaayega (yaani aalimon ki maut se gaayab ho jaayega) aur aam jahaalat phailegi.' Abu Musa ne kaha, 'Al-Harj, Habshi (Ethiopian) zubaan mein, qatl ka matlab hai.'
