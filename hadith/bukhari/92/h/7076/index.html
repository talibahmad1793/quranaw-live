Sahih al-Bukhari 7076

Book 92: Afflictions and the End of the World · Hadith 27

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصٍ، حَدَّثَنِي أَبِي، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ، حَدَّثَنَا شَقِيقٌ، قَالَ قَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ سِبَابُ الْمُسْلِمِ فُسُوقٌ، وَقِتَالُهُ كُفْرٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet, said, "Abusing a Muslim is Fusuq (evil doing) and killing him is Kufr (disbelief)

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Kisi Musalmaan ko bura-bhala kehna Fusuq (badkaari) hai, aur use qatl karna Kufr (na-shukri) hai.'
