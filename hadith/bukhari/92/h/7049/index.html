Sahih al-Bukhari 7049

Book 92: Afflictions and the End of the World · Hadith 2

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ، عَنْ مُغِيرَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، قَالَ قَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أَنَا فَرَطُكُمْ عَلَى الْحَوْضِ، لَيُرْفَعَنَّ إِلَىَّ رِجَالٌ مِنْكُمْ حَتَّى إِذَا أَهْوَيْتُ لأُنَاوِلَهُمُ اخْتُلِجُوا دُونِي فَأَقُولُ أَىْ رَبِّ أَصْحَابِي‏.‏ يَقُولُ لاَ تَدْرِي مَا أَحْدَثُوا بَعْدَكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "I am your predecessor at the Lake-Fount (Kauthar) and some men amongst you will be brought to me, and when I will try to hand them some water, they will be pulled away from me by force whereupon I will say, 'O Lord, my companions!' Then the Almighty will say, 'You do not know what they did after you left, they introduced new things into the religion after you

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Main Hauz (Al-Kauthar) par tumse pehle jaane wala hoon aur tum mein se kuch logon ko mere paas laaya jaayega, aur jab main unhein paani dene ki koshish karunga, unhein zabardasti mujhse dhakel diya jaayega, isper main kahoonga, "Aye Rab, mere saathi!" Phir Sarv-shaktimaan kahega, "Tumhein nahi pata unhone tumhaare jaane ke baad kya kiya, unhone deen mein naye kaam (bidaat) shuru kiye."'
