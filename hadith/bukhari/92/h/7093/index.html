Sahih al-Bukhari 7093

Book 92: Afflictions and the End of the World · Hadith 44

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا لَيْثٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهْوَ مُسْتَقْبِلٌ الْمَشْرِقَ يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ أَلاَ إِنَّ الْفِتْنَةَ هَا هُنَا مِنْ حَيْثُ يَطْلُعُ قَرْنُ الشَّيْطَانِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:I heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) while he was facing the East, saying, "Verily! Afflictions are there, from where the side of the head of Satan comes out

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Maine Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko, jab wo Poorab ki taraf muh kiye hue the, ye kehte suna, 'Beshak! Museebaten wahaan hain, jahaan se Shaitaan ke sar ka hissa nikalta hai.'
