Sahih al-Bukhari 7104

Book 92: Afflictions and the End of the World · Hadith 53

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا بَدَلُ بْنُ الْمُحَبَّرِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، أَخْبَرَنِي عَمْرٌو، سَمِعْتُ أَبَا وَائِلٍ، يَقُولُ دَخَلَ أَبُو مُوسَى وَأَبُو مَسْعُودٍ عَلَى عَمَّارٍ حَيْثُ بَعَثَهُ عَلِيٌّ إِلَى أَهْلِ الْكُوفَةِ يَسْتَنْفِرُهُمْ فَقَالاَ مَا رَأَيْنَاكَ أَتَيْتَ أَمْرًا أَكْرَهَ عِنْدَنَا مِنْ إِسْرَاعِكَ فِي هَذَا الأَمْرِ مُنْذُ أَسْلَمْتَ‏.‏ فَقَالَ عَمَّارٌ مَا رَأَيْتُ مِنْكُمَا مُنْذُ أَسْلَمْتُمَا أَمْرًا أَكْرَهَ عِنْدِي مِنْ إِبْطَائِكُمَا عَنْ هَذَا الأَمْرِ‏.‏ وَكَسَاهُمَا حُلَّةً حُلَّةً، ثُمَّ رَاحُوا إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Wail:Abu Musa and Abu Mas`ud went to `Ammar when `Ali had sent him to Kufa to exhort them to fight (on `Ali's side). They said to him, "Since you have become a Muslim, we have never seen you doing a deed more criticizable to us than your haste in this matter." `Ammar said, "Since you (both) became Muslims, I have never seen you doing a deed more criticizable to me than your keeping away from this matter." Then Abu Mas`ud provided `Ammar and Abu Musa with two-piece outfits to wear, and one of them went to the mosque (of Kufa)

Abu Wail bataate hain: Abu Musa aur Abu Mas'ud Ammar ke paas gaye jab Ali ne unhein Kufa (jang mein Ali ka saath dene ki logon ko taakeed karne) bheja tha. Unhone unse kaha, 'Jab se aap Musalmaan bane hain, humne aapko kabhi is maamle mein aapki jaldi se zyaada koi aisa amal karte nahi dekha jise hum galat samajhein.' Ammar ne kaha, 'Jab se tum dono Musalmaan bane ho, maine tumhein kabhi is maamle se door rehne se zyaada koi aisa amal karte nahi dekha jise main galat samajhoon.' Phir Abu Mas'ud ne Ammar aur Abu Musa ko pehne jaane ke liye do-tukda kapde diye, aur unmein se ek Masjid (Kufa ki) mein gaya.
