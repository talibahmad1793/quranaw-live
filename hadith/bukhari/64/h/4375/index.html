Sahih al-Bukhari 4375

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 400

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ نَصْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هَمَّامٍ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ يَقُولُ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ بَيْنَا أَنَا نَائِمٌ أُتِيتُ بِخَزَائِنِ الأَرْضِ، فَوُضِعَ فِي كَفِّي سِوَارَانِ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ، فَكَبُرَا عَلَىَّ فَأُوحِيَ إِلَىَّ أَنِ انْفُخْهُمَا، فَنَفَخْتُهُمَا فَذَهَبَا فَأَوَّلْتُهُمَا الْكَذَّابَيْنِ اللَّذَيْنِ أَنَا بَيْنَهُمَا صَاحِبَ صَنْعَاءَ، وَصَاحِبَ الْيَمَامَةِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "While I was sleeping, I was given the treasures of the earth and two gold bangles were put in my hands, and I did not like that, but I received the inspiration that I should blow on them, and I did so, and both of them vanished. I interpreted it as referring to the two liars between whom I am present; the ruler of Sana and the Ruler of Yamama

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Jabke main so raha tha, mujhe zameen ke khazanon di gaee, aur mere haath mein sone ki do chudiyaan rakhi gaee, aur mujhe yeh achha nahi laga, magar mujhe hidaayat mili ke main unpar phoonk maar dun, aur maine aisa hi kiya, aur donon gaayab ho gaee. Maine ise samjha do jhooton ke sandarbh mein jinke beech main hoonga; Sana ka hukmaran aur Yamama ka hukmaran."
