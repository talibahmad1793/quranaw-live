Sahih al-Bukhari 4124

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 168

Hadith text

وَزَادَ إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ طَهْمَانَ عَنِ الشَّيْبَانِيِّ، عَنْ عَدِيِّ بْنِ ثَابِتٍ، عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ بْنِ عَازِبٍ، قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ قُرَيْظَةَ لِحَسَّانَ بْنِ ثَابِتٍ ‏ "‏ اهْجُ الْمُشْرِكِينَ، فَإِنَّ جِبْرِيلَ مَعَكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Al-Bara' bin `Azib said (through another chain of sub-narrators):"On the day of Quraiza's (siege), Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said to Hassan bin Thabit, 'Abuse them (with your poems), and Jibril is with you

Al-Bara bin Azib ne kaha (ek doosri chain ke saath): "Quraiza ke ghersne ke din, Allah ke Rasool ne Hassan bin Thabit se kaha, 'Unki buraee karo (apni kavita se), aur Jibreel tumhare saath hai.'"
