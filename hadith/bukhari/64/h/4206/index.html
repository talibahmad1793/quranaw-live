Sahih al-Bukhari 4206

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 246

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا الْمَكِّيُّ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ أَبِي عُبَيْدٍ، قَالَ رَأَيْتُ أَثَرَ ضَرْبَةٍ فِي سَاقِ سَلَمَةَ، فَقُلْتُ يَا أَبَا مُسْلِمٍ، مَا هَذِهِ الضَّرْبَةُ قَالَ هَذِهِ ضَرْبَةٌ أَصَابَتْنِي يَوْمَ خَيْبَرَ، فَقَالَ النَّاسُ أُصِيبَ سَلَمَةُ‏.‏ فَأَتَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَنَفَثَ فِيهِ ثَلاَثَ نَفَثَاتٍ، فَمَا اشْتَكَيْتُهَا حَتَّى السَّاعَةِ‏.‏

Narrated Yazid bin Abi Ubaid:I saw the trace of a wound in Salama's leg. I said to him, "O Abu Muslim! What is this wound?" He said, "This was inflicted on me on the day of Khaibar and the people said, 'Salama has been wounded.' Then I went to the Prophet (ﷺ) and he puffed his saliva in it (i.e. the wound) thrice., and since then I have not had any pain in it till this hour

Yazid bin Abi Ubaid bataate hain: Maine Salama ki taang par ek zakhm ka nishaan dekha. Maine unse kaha, "Aye Abu Muslim! Yeh kaisa zakhm hai?" Unhone kaha, "Yeh mujhe Khaibar ke din laga aur logon ne kaha, 'Salama zakhmi ho gaye hain.' Phir main Nabi ke paas gaya aur unhone usmein teen baar apna laar phoonka, aur tab se ab tak mujhe ismein koee dard mahsoos nahi hua."
