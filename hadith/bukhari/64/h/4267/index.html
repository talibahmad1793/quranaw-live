Sahih al-Bukhari 4267

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 301

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عِمْرَانُ بْنُ مَيْسَرَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ فُضَيْلٍ، عَنْ حُصَيْنٍ، عَنْ عَامِرٍ، عَنِ النُّعْمَانِ بْنِ بَشِيرٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ أُغْمِيَ عَلَى عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ رَوَاحَةَ، فَجَعَلَتْ أُخْتُهُ عَمْرَةُ تَبْكِي وَاجَبَلاَهْ وَاكَذَا وَاكَذَا‏.‏ تُعَدِّدُ عَلَيْهِ فَقَالَ حِينَ أَفَاقَ مَا قُلْتِ شَيْئًا إِلاَّ قِيلَ لِي آنْتَ كَذَلِكَ‏.‏

Narrated An-Nu`man bin Bashir:`Abdullah bin Rawaha fell down unconscious and his sister `Amra started crying and was saying loudly, "O Jabala! Oh so-and-so! Oh so-and-so! and went on calling him by his (good ) qualities one by one). When he came to his senses, he said (to his sister), "When-ever you said something, I was asked, 'Are you really so (i.e. as she says)?

An-Nu'man bin Bashir bataate hain: Abdullah bin Rawaha behosh hokar gir gaye aur unki behn Amra rone lagi aur zor se kahne lagi, "Aye Jabala! Oh falaan-falaan! Oh falaan-falaan!" aur unke achhe gunon ka ek-ek karke naam lekar unhe pukaar rahi thi. Jab unhe hosh aaya, unhone (apni behn se) kaha, "Jab bhi tumne kuch kaha, mujhse pucha gaya, 'Kya tum sach mein aise ho (jaisi woh kah rahi hai)?'"
