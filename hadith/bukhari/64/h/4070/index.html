Sahih al-Bukhari 4070

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 115

Hadith text

وَعَنْ حَنْظَلَةَ بْنِ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ، سَمِعْتُ سَالِمَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، يَقُولُ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَدْعُو عَلَى صَفْوَانَ بْنِ أُمَيَّةَ وَسُهَيْلِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو وَالْحَارِثِ بْنِ هِشَامٍ فَنَزَلَتْ ‏{‏لَيْسَ لَكَ مِنَ الأَمْرِ شَىْءٌ‏}‏ إِلَى قَوْلِهِ ‏{‏فَإِنَّهُمْ ظَالِمُونَ‏}‏‏.‏

Salim bin `Abdullah said' "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) used to invoke evil upon Safwan bin Umaiya, Suhail bin `Amr and Al-Harith bin Hisham. So the Verse was revealed:-- "Not for you (O Muhammad!)......(till the end of Verse) For they are indeed wrong-doers

Salim bin Abdullah bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool Safwan bin Umaiya, Suhail bin Amr, aur Al-Harith bin Hisham ke khilaf bad-dua kiya karte the. Ispar Ayat naazil huee: "Yeh maamla aapke haath mein nahi hai (Aye Muhammad!)...." (aakhri tak) "...beshak, woh zaalim hain."
