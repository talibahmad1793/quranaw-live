Sahih al-Bukhari 4105

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 149

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي الْحَكَمُ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رَضِيَ الله عنهما ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ نُصِرْتُ بِالصَّبَا وَأُهْلِكَتْ عَادٌ بِالدَّبُورِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "I have been made victorious by As-Saba (i.e. an easterly wind) and the Ad nation was destroyed by Ad-Dabur (i.e. a westerly wind)

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Mujhe As-Saba (yaani ek purvi hawa) ke zariye fatah di gaee hai, aur Aad qaum ko Ad-Dabur (yaani ek pashchimi hawa) ne barbaad kiya."
