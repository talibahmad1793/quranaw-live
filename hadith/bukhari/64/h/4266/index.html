Sahih al-Bukhari 4266

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 300

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي قَيْسٌ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ خَالِدَ بْنَ الْوَلِيدِ، يَقُولُ لَقَدْ دُقَّ فِي يَدِي يَوْمَ مُوتَةَ تِسْعَةُ أَسْيَافٍ، وَصَبَرَتْ فِي يَدِي صَفِيحَةٌ لِي يَمَانِيَةٌ‏.‏

Narrated Khalid bin Al-Walid:On the day of Mu'tah, nine swords were broken in my hand and only a Yemenite sword of mine remained in my hand

Khalid bin Al-Walid bataate hain: Mu'tah ke din, mere haath mein nau talwaren toot gaee aur sirf meri ek Yemeni talwaar hi baaki bachi mere haath mein.
