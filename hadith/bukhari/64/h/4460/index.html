Sahih al-Bukhari 4460

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 476

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكُ بْنُ مِغْوَلٍ، عَنْ طَلْحَةَ، قَالَ سَأَلْتُ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ أَبِي أَوْفَى ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَوْصَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ لاَ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ كَيْفَ كُتِبَ عَلَى النَّاسِ الْوَصِيَّةُ أَوْ أُمِرُوا بِهَا قَالَ أَوْصَى بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ‏.‏

Narrated Talha:I asked `Abdullah bin Abu `Aufa "Did the Prophet (ﷺ) make a will? ' He replied, "No." I further asked, "How come is it that the making of a will was enjoined on the people or that they were ordered to make it? " He said, "The Prophet (ﷺ) made a will concerning Allah's Book

Talha bataate hain: Maine Abdullah bin Abu Aufa se pucha, 'Kya Nabi (SAW) ne koi wasiyat ki thi?' Unhone jawab diya, 'Nahi.' Maine phir pucha, 'To phir logon ko wasiyat karne ka hukm kyun diya gaya, ya unhe iska aadesh kyun diya gaya?' Unhone kaha, 'Nabi (SAW) ne Allah ki Kitaab ke baare mein wasiyat ki thi.'
