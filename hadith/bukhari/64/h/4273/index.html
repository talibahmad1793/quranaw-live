Sahih al-Bukhari 4273

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 307

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ مَسْعَدَةَ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي عُبَيْدٍ، عَنْ سَلَمَةَ بْنِ الأَكْوَعِ، قَالَ غَزَوْتُ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم سَبْعَ غَزَوَاتٍ‏.‏ فَذَكَرَ خَيْبَرَ وَالْحُدَيْبِيَةَ وَيَوْمَ حُنَيْنٍ وَيَوْمَ الْقَرَدِ‏.‏ قَالَ يَزِيدُ وَنَسِيتُ بَقِيَّتَهُمْ‏.‏

Narrated Yazid bin Abi Ubaid:Salama bin Al-Akwa` said, "I fought in seven Ghazwat along with the Prophet." He then mentioned Khaibar, Al-Hudaibiya, the day (i.e. battle) of Hunain and the day of Al-Qarad. I forgot the names of the other Ghazwat

Yazid bin Abi Ubaid bataate hain: Salama bin Al-Akwa ne kaha, "Maine saat Ghazwaat mein Nabi ke saath jung ki." Phir unhone Khaibar, Al-Hudaibiya, Hunain ke din, aur Al-Qarad ke din ka zikar kiya. Main baaki Ghazwaton ke naam bhool gaya.
