Sahih al-Bukhari 4233

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 271

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، سَمِعَ حَفْصَ بْنَ غِيَاثٍ، حَدَّثَنَا بُرَيْدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ أَبِي بُرْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى، قَالَ قَدِمْنَا عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَعْدَ أَنِ افْتَتَحَ خَيْبَرَ، فَقَسَمَ لَنَا، وَلَمْ يَقْسِمْ لأَحَدٍ لَمْ يَشْهَدِ الْفَتْحَ غَيْرَنَا‏.‏

Narrated Abu Musa:We came upon the Prophet (ﷺ) after he had conquered Khaibar. He then gave us a share (from the booty), but apart from us he did not give to anybody else who did not attend the Conquest

Abu Musa bataate hain: Hum Nabi ke paas aaye Khaibar ki fatah ke baad. Unhone humein ek hissa diya (maal-e-ghanimat se), magar humare ilawa unhone kisi aur ko nahi diya jo fatah ke waqt maujood nahi tha.
