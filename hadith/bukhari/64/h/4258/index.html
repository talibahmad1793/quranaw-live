Sahih al-Bukhari 4258

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 292

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا وُهَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، قَالَ تَزَوَّجَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَيْمُونَةَ وَهْوَ مُحْرِمٌ، وَبَنَى بِهَا وَهْوَ حَلاَلٌ وَمَاتَتْ بِسَرِفَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) married Maimuna while he was in the state of Ihram but he consummated that marriage after finishing that state. Maimuna died at Sarif (i.e. a place near Mecca)

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi ne Maimuna se shaadi ki jabke woh Ihram ki haalat mein the, magar unhone apni shaadi Ihram poora hone ke baad poori ki. Maimuna ka intiqal Sarif (yaani Mecca ke qareeb ek jagah) mein hua.
