Sahih al-Bukhari 4118

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 162

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرُ بْنُ حَازِمٍ، عَنْ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ هِلاَلٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كَأَنِّي أَنْظُرُ إِلَى الْغُبَارِ سَاطِعًا فِي زُقَاقِ بَنِي غَنْمٍ مَوْكِبِ جِبْرِيلَ حِينَ سَارَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلَى بَنِي قُرَيْظَةَ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:As if I am just now looking at the dust rising in the street of Banu Ghanm (in Medina) because of the marching of Gabriel's regiment when Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) set out to Banu Quraiza (to attack them)

Anas bataate hain: Mano abhi bhi main Banu Ghanm ke gali mein dhool udte dekh raha hoon, Jibreel ki tukdi ke kooch karne ki wajah se, jab Allah ke Rasool Banu Quraiza ki taraf (unpar hamla karne) nikle.
