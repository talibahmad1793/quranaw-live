Sahih al-Bukhari 4309

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 340

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي بِشْرٍ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، قُلْتُ لاِبْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ أَنْ أُهَاجِرَ إِلَى الشَّأْمِ‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ هِجْرَةَ وَلَكِنْ جِهَادٌ، فَانْطَلِقْ فَاعْرِضْ نَفْسَكَ، فَإِنْ وَجَدْتَ شَيْئًا وَإِلاَّ رَجَعْتَ‏.‏

Narrated Mujahid:I said to Ibn `Umar, "I want to migrate to Sham." He said, "There is no migration, but Jihad (for Allah's Cause). Go and offer yourself for Jihad, and if you find an opportunity for Jihad (stay there) otherwise, come back

Mujahid bataate hain: Maine Ibn Umar se kaha, "Main Sham hijrat karna chahta hoon." Unhone kaha, "Ab koee hijrat nahi hai, magar Jihad (Allah ki raah mein) hai. Jaao aur Jihad ke liye khud ko pesh karo, aur agar tumhe Jihad ka koee mauka milta hai (wahaan ruko) warna, wapas aa jao."
