Sahih al-Bukhari 4165

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 207

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قَبِيصَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ طَارِقٍ، قَالَ ذُكِرَتْ عِنْدَ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ الشَّجَرَةُ فَضَحِكَ فَقَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي أَبِي وَكَانَ، شَهِدَهَا‏.‏

Narrated Tariq:(The tree where the Ridwan Pledge of allegiance was taken by the Prophet) was mentioned before Sa`id bin Al-Musaiyab. On that he smiled and said, "My father informed me (about it) and he had witnessed it (i.e. the Pledge)

Tariq bataate hain: (Woh darakht jahaan Ridwan bai'at Nabi dwaara li gaee thi) Sa'id bin Al-Musaiyab ke saamne iska zikar hua. Ispar woh muskurae aur kaha, "Mere waalid ne mujhe (iske baare mein) bataya aur unhone ise dekha tha (yaani us bai'at ko)."
