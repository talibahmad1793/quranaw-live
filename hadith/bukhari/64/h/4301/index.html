Sahih al-Bukhari 4301

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 334

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُوسَى، أَخْبَرَنَا هِشَامٌ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ سُنَيْنٍ أَبِي جَمِيلَةَ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا وَنَحْنُ، مَعَ ابْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ قَالَ وَزَعَمَ أَبُو جَمِيلَةَ أَنَّهُ أَدْرَكَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، وَخَرَجَ مَعَهُ عَامَ الْفَتْحِ‏.‏

Narrated Az-Zuhri:While we were in the company of Ibn Al-Musaiyab, Sunain Abi Jamila informed us (a Hadith), Abu Jamila said that he lived during the lifetime of the Prophet (ﷺ) and that he had accompanied him ( to Mecca) during the year of the Conquest (of Mecca)

Az-Zuhri bataate hain: Jabke hum Ibn Al-Musaiyab ki sohbat mein the, Sunain Abi Jamila ne humein ek Hadith sunaee. Abu Jamila ne kaha ke woh Nabi ki zindagi mein rahte the aur unhone Fatah ke saal unke saath (Mecca) jane ka saath diya tha.
