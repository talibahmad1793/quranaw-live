Sahih al-Bukhari 4336

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 365

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ لَمَّا كَانَ يَوْمُ حُنَيْنٍ آثَرَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَاسًا، أَعْطَى الأَقْرَعَ مِائَةً مِنَ الإِبِلِ، وَأَعْطَى عُيَيْنَةَ مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، وَأَعْطَى نَاسًا، فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ مَا أُرِيدَ بِهَذِهِ الْقِسْمَةِ وَجْهُ اللَّهِ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ لأُخْبِرَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ رَحِمَ اللَّهُ مُوسَى‏.‏ قَدْ أُوذِيَ بِأَكْثَرَ مِنْ هَذَا فَصَبَرَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:When it was the day of Hunain, the Prophet (ﷺ) favored some people over some others (in the distribution of the booty). He gave Al-Aqra' one-hundred camels and gave Uyaina the same, and also gave other people (of Quraish). A man said, "Allah's Pleasure was not the aim, in this distribution." I said, "I will inform the Prophet (about your statement)." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "May Allah bestow Mercy on Moses, for he was troubled more than this but he remained patient

Abdullah bataate hain: Jab Hunain ka din tha, Nabi ne (maal-e-ghanimat ki taqseem mein) kuch logon ko doosron par tarjih di. Unhone Al-Aqra ko sau unt diye aur Uyaina ko bhi utne hi diye, aur doosron (Quraish) ko bhi diye. Ek shaks ne kaha, "Is taqseem mein Allah ki khushi pane ka irada nahi tha." Maine kaha, "Main Nabi ko (aapki baat) bataunga." Nabi ne kaha, "Allah Musa par apni rehmat naazil kare, kyunke unhe isse zyada takleef di gaee thi, magar woh sabr karte rahe."
