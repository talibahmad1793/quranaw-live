Sahih al-Bukhari 3971

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 24

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي يُوسُفُ بْنُ الْمَاجِشُونِ، عَنْ صَالِحِ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَوْفٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَدِّهِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، قَالَ كَاتَبْتُ أُمَيَّةَ بْنَ خَلَفٍ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ يَوْمُ بَدْرٍ، فَذَكَرَ قَتْلَهُ وَقَتْلَ ابْنِهِ، فَقَالَ بِلاَلٌ لاَ نَجَوْتُ إِنْ نَجَا أُمَيَّةُ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur-Rahman bin `Auf:"I had an agreement with Umaiya bin Khalaf (that he would look after my relatives and property in Mecca, and I would look after his relatives and property in Medina)." `Abdur-Rahman then mentioned the killing of Umaiya and his son on the day of Badr, and Bilal said, "Woe to me if Umaiya remains safe (i.e. alive)

Abdur-Rahman bin Auf bataate hain: "Mera Umaiya bin Khalaf ke saath ek muahida tha (ke woh Mecca mein mere rishtedaron aur jaidad ka khyaal rakhega, aur main Madina mein unke rishtedaron aur jaidad ka khyaal rakhunga)." Abdur-Rahman ne phir Umaiya aur unke bete ki Badr ki jung ke din hone waali maut ka zikar kiya, aur Bilal ne kaha, "Mujhe afsos hoga agar Umaiya surakshit (yaani zinda) rah gaya."
