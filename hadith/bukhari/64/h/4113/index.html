Sahih al-Bukhari 4113

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 157

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُنْكَدِرِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ جَابِرًا، يَقُولُ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ الأَحْزَابِ ‏"‏ مَنْ يَأْتِينَا بِخَبَرِ الْقَوْمِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ الزُّبَيْرُ أَنَا‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏"‏ مَنْ يَأْتِينَا بِخَبَرِ الْقَوْمِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ الزُّبَيْرُ أَنَا‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏"‏ مَنْ يَأْتِينَا بِخَبَرِ الْقَوْمِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ الزُّبَيْرُ أَنَا‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏"‏ إِنَّ لِكُلِّ نَبِيٍّ حَوَارِيًّا، وَإِنَّ حَوَارِيَّ الزُّبَيْرُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jabir:On the day of Al-Ahzab (i.e. clans), Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, 'Who will bring us the news of the people (i.e. the clans of Quraish infidels)?" Az-Zubair said, "I." The Prophet (ﷺ) again said, "Who will bring us the news of the people?" Az-Zubair said, "I." The Prophet (ﷺ) again said, "Who will bring us the news of the people?" Az-Zubair said, "I." The Prophet (ﷺ) then said, "Every prophet has his Hawari (i.e. disciple - special helper); my disciple is Az-Zubair

Jabir bataate hain: Al-Ahzab (yaani giroh) ke din, Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Kaun humein logon (yaani Quraish kaafiron ke girohon) ki khabar laega?" Az-Zubair ne kaha, "Main." Nabi ne phir kaha, "Kaun humein logon ki khabar laega?" Az-Zubair ne kaha, "Main." Nabi ne phir kaha, "Kaun humein logon ki khabar laega?" Az-Zubair ne kaha, "Main." Nabi ne phir kaha, "Har Nabi ka apna Hawaari (yaani khaas shaagird) hota hai, aur mera Hawaari Az-Zubair hai."
