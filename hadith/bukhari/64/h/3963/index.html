Sahih al-Bukhari 3963

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 15

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي عَدِيٍّ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ التَّيْمِيِّ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، رضى الله عنه قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ بَدْرٍ ‏ "‏ مَنْ يَنْظُرُ مَا فَعَلَ أَبُو جَهْلٍ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَانْطَلَقَ ابْنُ مَسْعُودٍ، فَوَجَدَهُ قَدْ ضَرَبَهُ ابْنَا عَفْرَاءَ حَتَّى بَرَدَ، فَأَخَذَ بِلِحْيَتِهِ فَقَالَ أَنْتَ أَبَا جَهْلٍ قَالَ وَهَلْ فَوْقَ رَجُلٍ قَتَلَهُ قَوْمُهُ أَوْ قَالَ قَتَلْتُمُوهُ‏

Narrated Anas:On the day of Badr, the Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Who will go and see what has happened to Abu Jahl?" Ibn Mas`ud went and found that the two sons of 'Afra had struck him fatally. `Abdullah bin Mas`ud got hold of his beard and said, "'Are you Abu Jahl?" He replied, "Can there be a man more superior to one whom his own folk have killed (or you have killed)?

Anas bataate hain: Badr ki jung ke din, Nabi ne kaha, "Kaun jaakar dekhega Abu Jahl ka kya hua?" Ibn Mas'ud gaye aur paaya ke Afra ke do beton ne unhe maar-maar kar maut ke qareeb la diya tha. Abdullah bin Mas'ud ne unki daadhi pakad li aur kaha, "Kya aap Abu Jahl hain?" Unhone jawab diya, "Kya koee shaks isse zyada izzatdaar ho sakta hai jise (tumne) qatl kiya hai ya jise uski apni qaum ne qatl kiya hai?"
