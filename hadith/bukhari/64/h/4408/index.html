Sahih al-Bukhari 4408

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 430

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي الأَسْوَدِ، مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ نَوْفَلٍ عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتْ خَرَجْنَا مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَمِنَّا مَنْ أَهَلَّ بِعُمْرَةٍ، وَمِنَّا مَنْ أَهَلَّ بِحَجَّةٍ، وَمِنَّا مَنْ أَهَلَّ بِحَجٍّ وَعُمْرَةٍ، وَأَهَلَّ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالْحَجِّ، فَأَمَّا مَنْ أَهَلَّ بِالْحَجِّ أَوْ جَمَعَ الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ فَلَمْ يَحِلُّوا حَتَّى يَوْمَ النَّحْرِ‏.‏ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ وَقَالَ مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي حَجَّةِ الْوَدَاعِ‏.‏ حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ مِثْلَهُ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:We set out with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ), and some of us assumed the Ihram for `Umra, some assumed it for Hajj, and some assumed it for both Hajj and `Umra. Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) assumed the Ihram for Hajj. So those who had assumed the Ihram for Hajj or for both Hajj and `Umra, did not finish their Ihram till the day of An-Nahr (i.e. slaughter of sacrifices). Malik also narrated as above, saying, "(We set out) with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) in Hajjat-ul-Wada`...)" This hadith also reaches us through another chain

Ayesha bataati hain: Hum Allah ke Rasool ke saath nikle, aur humme se kuch ne Umrah ke liye Ihram dhaaran kiya, kuch ne Hajj ke liye, aur kuch ne Hajj aur Umrah donon ke liye. Allah ke Rasool ne Hajj ke liye Ihram dhaaran kiya. Isliye jinhone Hajj ke liye ya Hajj aur Umrah donon ke liye Ihram dhaaran kiya tha, unhone An-Nahr (yaani qurbaniyon ki zabah) ke din tak apna Ihram khatm nahi kiya. Malik ne bhi upar jaisa hi riwaayat ki, kehte huye, "(Hum nikle) Allah ke Rasool ke saath Hajjat-ul-Wada mein...)" Yeh Hadith humtak ek doosri chain se bhi pahunchti hai.
