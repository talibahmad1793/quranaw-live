Sahih al-Bukhari 4063

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 108

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا وَكِيعٌ، عَنْ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، عَنْ قَيْسٍ، قَالَ رَأَيْتُ يَدَ طَلْحَةَ شَلاَّءَ، وَقَى بِهَا النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ‏.‏

Narrated Qais:I saw Talha's paralyzed hand with which he had protected the Prophet (ﷺ) on the day of Uhud

Qais bataate hain: Maine Talha ka lakva hua haath dekha jisse unhone Uhud ki jung ke din Nabi ki hifaazat ki thi.
