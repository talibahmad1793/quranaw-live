Sahih al-Bukhari 4208

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 248

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ الْخُزَاعِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا زِيَادُ بْنُ الرَّبِيعِ، عَنْ أَبِي عِمْرَانَ، قَالَ نَظَرَ أَنَسٌ إِلَى النَّاسِ يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ، فَرَأَى طَيَالِسَةً فَقَالَ كَأَنَّهُمُ السَّاعَةَ يَهُودُ خَيْبَرَ‏.‏

Narrated Abu `Imran:Anas looked at the people wearing Tailsans (i.e. a special kind of head covering worn by Jews in old days). On that Anas said, "At this moment they (i.e. those people) look like the Jews of Khaibar

Abu Imran bataate hain: Anas ne un logon ko dekha jo Tailsan (yaani purane zamane mein Yahudiyon dwaara pahna jane wala ek khaas sar ka kapda) pahne hue the. Ispar Anas ne kaha, "Is waqt woh (yaani woh log) Khaibar ke Yahudiyon jaise dikh rahe hain."
