Sahih al-Bukhari 4316

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 347

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، قِيلَ لِلْبَرَاءِ وَأَنَا أَسْمَعُ، أَوَلَّيْتُمْ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ حُنَيْنٍ فَقَالَ أَمَّا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَلاَ، كَانُوا رُمَاةً فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ أَنَا النَّبِيُّ لاَ كَذِبْ أَنَا ابْنُ عَبْدِ الْمُطَّلِبْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu 'Is-haq:Al-Bara' was asked while I was listening, "Did you flee (before the enemy) along with the Prophet (ﷺ) on the day of (the battle of) Hunain?" He replied, "As for the Prophet, he did not (flee). The enemy were good archers and the Prophet (ﷺ) was saying, "I am the Prophet (ﷺ) undoubtedly; I am the son of `Abdul Muttalib

Abu Ishaq bataate hain: Al-Bara se puchha gaya jabke main sun raha tha, "Kya aap Nabi ke saath Hunain ke din bhage the?" Unhone jawab diya, "Jahaan tak Nabi ka sawal hai, woh nahi bhage. Dushman achhe tirandaaz the aur Nabi kah rahe the, 'Main bilkul Nabi hoon; main Abdul Muttalib ka beta hoon.'"
