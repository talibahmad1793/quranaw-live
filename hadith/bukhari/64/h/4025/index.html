Sahih al-Bukhari 4025

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 74

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا الْحَجَّاجُ بْنُ مِنْهَالٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عُمَرَ النُّمَيْرِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا يُونُسُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ الزُّهْرِيَّ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عُرْوَةَ بْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ، وَسَعِيدَ بْنَ الْمُسَيَّبِ، وَعَلْقَمَةَ بْنَ وَقَّاصٍ، وَعُبَيْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ حَدِيثِ، عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ زَوْجِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم كُلٌّ ـ حَدَّثَنِي طَائِفَةً مِنَ الْحَدِيثِ ـ قَالَتْ فَأَقْبَلْتُ أَنَا وَأُمُّ مِسْطَحٍ فَعَثَرَتْ أُمُّ مِسْطَحٍ فِي مِرْطِهَا فَقَالَتْ تَعِسَ مِسْطَحٌ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ بِئْسَ مَا قُلْتِ، تَسُبِّينَ رَجُلاً شَهِدَ بَدْرًا فَذَكَرَ حَدِيثَ الإِفْكِ‏.‏

Narrated Yunus bin Yazid:I heard Az-Zuhri saying, "I heard `Urwa bin Az-Zubair. Sa`id bin Al-Musaiyab, 'Alqama bin Waqqas and 'Ubaidullah bin `Abdullah each narrating part of the narrative concerning `Aisha the wife of the Prophet. `Aisha said: When I and Um Mistah were returning, Um Mistah stumbled by treading on the end of her robe, and on that she said, 'May Mistah be ruined.' I said, 'You have said a bad thing, you curse a man who took part in the battle of Badr!." Az-Zuhri then narrated the narration of the Lie (forged against `Aisha)

Yunus bin Yazid bataate hain: Maine Az-Zuhri ko kehte suna, "Maine Urwa bin Az-Zubair, Sa'id bin Al-Musaiyab, Alqama bin Waqqas, aur Ubaidullah bin Abdullah, har ek ko Nabi ki biwi Ayesha se sambandhit kahani ka ek hissa riwaayat karte suna. Ayesha ne kaha: Jab main aur Um Mistah wapas aa rahi thi, Um Mistah apni chaadar ke kinare par thokar khakar girne lagi, aur ispar unhone kaha, 'Mistah barbaad ho.' Maine kaha, 'Aapne ek buri baat kahi hai, aap ek aise shaks ko bad-dua deti hain jinhone Badr ki jung mein hissa liya!'" Az-Zuhri ne phir jhoote ilzaam (Ayesha ke khilaf gadha gaya) ki poori kahani riwaayat ki.
