Sahih al-Bukhari 4420

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 442

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ بُكَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ دِينَارٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لأَصْحَابِ الْحِجْرِ ‏ "‏ لاَ تَدْخُلُوا عَلَى هَؤُلاَءِ الْمُعَذَّبِينَ إِلاَّ أَنْ تَكُونُوا بَاكِينَ، أَنْ يُصِيبَكُمْ مِثْلُ مَا أَصَابَهُمْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said to his companions who were at Al-Hijr, "Do not enter upon these people who are being punished, except in a weeping state, lest the same calamity as of theirs should befall you

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne apne sathiyon se, jo Al-Hijr mein the, kaha, "In logon ke paas mat jao jinke saath saza ho rahi hai, siwaye rote hue haalat mein, is dar se ke tumpar bhi wahi vinaash na aa jaaye jaisa unpar aaya tha."
