Sahih al-Bukhari 4073

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 118

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ نَصْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هَمَّامٍ، سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ اشْتَدَّ غَضَبُ اللَّهِ عَلَى قَوْمٍ فَعَلُوا بِنَبِيِّهِ ـ يُشِيرُ إِلَى رَبَاعِيَتِهِ ـ اشْتَدَّ غَضَبُ اللَّهِ عَلَى رَجُلٍ يَقْتُلُهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) (pointing to his broken canine tooth) said, "Allah's Wrath has become severe on the people who harmed His Prophet. Allah's Wrath has become severe on the man who is killed by the Apostle of Allah in Allah's Cause

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne (apne toote hue daadha ke daant ki taraf ishara karte huye) kaha, "Allah ka gussa un logon par sakht ho gaya jinhone unke Nabi ko dukh diya. Allah ka gussa us shaks par sakht ho gaya jise Allah ke Rasool ne Allah ki raah mein qatl kiya."
