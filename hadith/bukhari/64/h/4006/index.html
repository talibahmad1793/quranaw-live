Sahih al-Bukhari 4006

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 57

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَدِيٍّ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، سَمِعَ أَبَا مَسْعُودٍ الْبَدْرِيَّ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ نَفَقَةُ الرَّجُلِ عَلَى أَهْلِهِ صَدَقَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Masud Al-Badri:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "A man's spending on his family is a deed of charity

Abu Mas'ud Al-Badri bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Ek shaks ka apne pariwara par kharch karna ek charitable amal hai."
