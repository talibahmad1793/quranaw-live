Sahih al-Bukhari 4338

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 367

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو النُّعْمَانِ، حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ بَعَثَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم سَرِيَّةً قِبَلَ نَجْدٍ، فَكُنْتُ فِيهَا، فَبَلَغَتْ سِهَامُنَا اثْنَىْ عَشَرَ بَعِيرًا، وَنُفِّلْنَا بَعِيرًا بَعِيرًا، فَرَجَعْنَا بِثَلاَثَةَ عَشَرَ بَعِيرًا‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:The Prophet (ﷺ) sent a Sariya towards Najd and I was in it, and our share from the booty amounted to twelve camels each, and we were given an additional camel each. So we returned with thirteen camels each

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Nabi ne Najd ki taraf ek Sariya (yaani chhota dasta) bheja aur main usmein tha, aur humara maal-e-ghanimat ka hissa baarah unt har ek ke liye tha, aur humein ek atirikt unt bhi diya gaya har ek ko. Isliye hum terah-terah unton ke saath wapas aaye.
