Sahih al-Bukhari 4057

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 103

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا لَيْثٌ، عَنْ يَحْيَى، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ قَالَ سَعْدُ بْنُ أَبِي وَقَّاصٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ لَقَدْ جَمَعَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ أَبَوَيْهِ كِلَيْهِمَا‏.‏ يُرِيدُ حِينَ قَالَ ‏ "‏ فِدَاكَ أَبِي وَأُمِّي ‏"‏‏.‏ وَهُوَ يُقَاتِلُ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Al Musaiyab:Sa`d bin Abi Waqqas said, "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) mentioned both his father and mother for me on the day of the battle of Uhud." He meant when the Prophet (ﷺ) said (to Sa`d) while the latter was fighting. "Let my father and mother be sacrificed for you

Ibn Al-Musaiyab bataate hain: Sa'd bin Abi Waqqas ne kaha, "Allah ke Rasool ne Uhud ki jung ke din mere liye apne donon maa-baap ka zikar kiya." Unka matlab tha jab Nabi ne (Sa'd se) kaha jabke woh jung kar rahe the, "Mere maa-baap tumhare liye qurbaan hon."
