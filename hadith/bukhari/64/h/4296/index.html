Sahih al-Bukhari 4296

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 329

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي حَبِيبٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ أَبِي رَبَاحٍ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ عَامَ الْفَتْحِ وَهْوَ بِمَكَّةَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ حَرَّمَ بَيْعَ الْخَمْرِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:That he heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) saying in the year of the Conquest (of Mecca) while he was in Mecca, "Allah and His Apostle have made the selling of wine (i.e. alcoholic drinks) unlawful

Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Unhone Allah ke Rasool ko Fatah ke saal, jabke woh Mecca mein the, yeh kahte huye suna, "Allah aur uske Rasool ne sharab (yaani nashile pey padaarthon) ka vyaapaar haram kar diya hai."
