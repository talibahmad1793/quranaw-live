Sahih al-Bukhari 4265

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 299

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، عَنْ قَيْسِ بْنِ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ خَالِدَ بْنَ الْوَلِيدِ، يَقُولُ لَقَدِ انْقَطَعَتْ فِي يَدِي يَوْمَ مُوتَةَ تِسْعَةُ أَسْيَافٍ، فَمَا بَقِيَ فِي يَدِي إِلاَّ صَفِيحَةٌ يَمَانِيَةٌ‏.‏

Narrated Khalid bin Al-Walid:On the day (of the battle of) Mu'tah, nine swords were broken in my hand, and nothing was left in my hand except a Yemenite sword of mine

Khalid bin Al-Walid bataate hain: Mu'tah ke din (jung mein), mere haath mein nau talwaren toot gaee, aur mere haath mein sirf meri ek Yemeni talwaar baaki bachi.
