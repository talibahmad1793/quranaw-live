Sahih al-Bukhari 4046

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 92

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، سَمِعَ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَجُلٌ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ أَرَأَيْتَ إِنْ قُتِلْتُ فَأَيْنَ أَنَا قَالَ ‏ "‏ فِي الْجَنَّةِ ‏"‏ فَأَلْقَى تَمَرَاتٍ فِي يَدِهِ، ثُمَّ قَاتَلَ حَتَّى قُتِلَ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:On the day of the battle of Uhud, a man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, "Can you tell me where I will be if I should get martyred?" The Prophet (ﷺ) replied, "In Paradise." The man threw away some dates he was carrying in his hand, and fought till he was martyred

Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Uhud ki jung ke din, ek shaks Nabi ke paas aaya aur kaha, "Kya aap mujhe bata sakte hain main kahaan hoonga agar main shaheed ho jaoon?" Nabi ne jawab diya, "Jannat mein." Us shaks ne apne haath mein pakdi kuch khajooren phenk di aur jung karte rahe jab tak woh shaheed hue.
