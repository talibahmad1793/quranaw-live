Sahih al-Bukhari 4051

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 97

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ جَابِرٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ نَزَلَتْ هَذِهِ الآيَةُ فِينَا ‏{‏إِذْ هَمَّتْ طَائِفَتَانِ مِنْكُمْ أَنْ تَفْشَلاَ‏}‏ بَنِي سَلِمَةَ وَبَنِي حَارِثَةَ، وَمَا أُحِبُّ أَنَّهَا لَمْ تَنْزِلْ، وَاللَّهُ يَقُولُ ‏{‏وَاللَّهُ وَلِيُّهُمَا‏}‏

Narrated Jabir:This Verse: "When two of your parties almost Decided to fall away..." was revealed in our connection, i.e. Bani Salama and Bani Haritha and I would not have liked that, if it was not revealed, for Allah said:-- But Allah was their Protector

Jabir bataate hain: Yeh Ayat: "Jab tumhare do giroh lagbhag (himmat) khone wale the..." humare sandarbh mein naazil huee, yaani Bani Salama aur Bani Haritha ke sandarbh mein, aur main isse khush nahi hota agar yeh naazil na huee hoti, kyunke Allah ne kaha: "Magar Allah unka rakshak tha."
