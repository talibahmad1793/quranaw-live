Sahih al-Bukhari 4164

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 206

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا طَارِقٌ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيَّبِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّهُ كَانَ مِمَّنْ بَايَعَ تَحْتَ الشَّجَرَةِ، فَرَجَعْنَا إِلَيْهَا الْعَامَ الْمُقْبِلَ فَعَمِيَتْ عَلَيْنَا‏.‏

Narrated Sa`id bin Al-Musaiyab:That his father was amongst those who had given the Pledge of allegiance (to the Prophet (ﷺ) ) beneath the Tree, and the next year when they went towards the Tree, they were not able to recognize it

Sa'id bin Al-Musaiyab bataate hain (apne waalid se): Woh un logon mein the jinhone darakht ke neeche (Nabi ko) bai'at di thi, aur agle saal jab woh darakht ki taraf gaye, woh use pahchaanne mein asamartha rahe.
