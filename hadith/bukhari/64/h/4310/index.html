Sahih al-Bukhari 4310

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 341

Hadith text

وَقَالَ النَّضْرُ أَخْبَرَنَا شُعْبَةُ، أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو بِشْرٍ، سَمِعْتُ مُجَاهِدًا، قُلْتُ لاِبْنِ عُمَرَ فَقَالَ لاَ هِجْرَةَ الْيَوْمَ، أَوْ بَعْدَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَهُ‏.‏

(In another narration) Ibn `Umar said:"There is no migration today or after Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)." (and completed his statement as above)

(ek doosri riwaayat mein) Ibn Umar ne kaha: "Aaj ya Allah ke Rasool ke baad koee hijrat nahi hai." (aur upar jaisa hi apni baat poori ki)
