Sahih al-Bukhari 4470

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 485

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَصْبَغُ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي ابْنُ وَهْبٍ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي عَمْرٌو، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي حَبِيبٍ، عَنْ أَبِي الْخَيْرِ، عَنِ الصُّنَابِحِيِّ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ لَهُ مَتَى هَاجَرْتَ قَالَ خَرَجْنَا مِنَ الْيَمَنِ مُهَاجِرِينَ، فَقَدِمْنَا الْجُحْفَةَ، فَأَقْبَلَ رَاكِبٌ فَقُلْتُ لَهُ الْخَبَرَ فَقَالَ دَفَنَّا النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مُنْذُ خَمْسٍ‏.‏ قُلْتُ هَلْ سَمِعْتَ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ شَيْئًا قَالَ نَعَمْ أَخْبَرَنِي بِلاَلٌ مُؤَذِّنُ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ فِي السَّبْعِ فِي الْعَشْرِ الأَوَاخِرِ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Abu Habib:Abu Al-Khair said, "As-Sunabihi asked (me), 'When did you migrate?' I (i.e. Abu Al-Khair) said, 'We went out from Yemen as emigrants and arrived at Al-Juhfa, and there came a rider whom I asked about the news. The rider said: We buried the Prophet (ﷺ) five days ago." I asked (As-Sunabihi), 'Did you hear anything about the night of Qadr?' He replied, 'Bilal, the Mu'adh-dhin of the Prophet (ﷺ) informed me that it is on one of the seven nights of the last ten days (of Ramadan)

Ibn Abu Habib bataate hain: Abu Al-Khair ne kaha, 'As-Sunabihi ne mujhse pucha, "Tum kab hijrat karke aaye?" Maine (yaani Abu Al-Khair ne) kaha, "Hum Yemen se hijrat karke nikle aur Al-Juhfa pahunche, wahaan ek sawaar aaya jisse maine khabar poochi. Us sawaar ne kaha: Humne Nabi (SAW) ko paanch din pehle dafn kiya."' Maine (As-Sunabihi se) pucha, 'Kya tumne Laila-tul-Qadr ke baare mein kuch suna?' Unhone jawab diya, 'Nabi (SAW) ke Muazzin Bilal ne mujhe bataya ke ye (Ramzan ke) aakhri dus dinon ki saat raaton mein se ek hai.'
