Sahih al-Bukhari 4169

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 211

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمٌ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي عُبَيْدٍ، قَالَ قُلْتُ لِسَلَمَةَ بْنِ الأَكْوَعِ عَلَى أَىِّ شَىْءٍ بَايَعْتُمْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ الْحُدَيْبِيَةِ‏.‏ قَالَ عَلَى الْمَوْتِ‏.‏

Narrated Yazid bin Abi Ubaid:I said to Salama bin Al-Akwa`, "For what did you give the Pledge of allegiance to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) on the day of Al-Hudaibiya?" He replied, "For death (in the Cause of Islam)

Yazid bin Abi Ubaid bataate hain: Maine Salama bin Al-Akwa se pucha, "Aapne Al-Hudaibiya ke din Allah ke Rasool ko kis cheez ke liye Bai'at di?" Unhone jawab diya, "Maut ke liye (Islam ki khaatir)."
