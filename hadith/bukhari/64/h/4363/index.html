Sahih al-Bukhari 4363

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 389

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ دَاوُدَ أَبُو الرَّبِيعِ، حَدَّثَنَا فُلَيْحٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ أَبَا بَكْرٍ الصِّدِّيقَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ بَعَثَهُ فِي الْحَجَّةِ الَّتِي أَمَّرَهُ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَبْلَ حَجَّةِ الْوَدَاعِ يَوْمَ النَّحْرِ فِي رَهْطٍ يُؤَذِّنُ فِي النَّاسِ لاَ يَحُجُّ بَعْدَ الْعَامِ مُشْرِكٌ وَلاَ يَطُوفُ بِالْبَيْتِ عُرْيَانٌ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:That during the Hajj in which the Prophet (ﷺ) had made Abu Bakr As Siddiq as chief of the, Hajj before the Hajj-ul-Wida,' on the day of Nahr, Abu Bakr sent him along with a group of persons to announce to the people. "No pagan is permitted to perform Hajj after this year, and nobody is permitted to perform the Tawaf of the Ka`ba naked

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Us Hajj ke dauran jismein Nabi ne Abu Bakr As-Siddiq ko Hajj-ul-Wida se pahle Hajj ka pramukh banaya tha, Nahr (yaani qurbani) ke din, Abu Bakr ne unhe kuch logon ke ek giroh ke saath bheja logon ko elaan karne ke liye, "Is saal ke baad koee bhi mushrik Hajj nahi karega, aur koee bhi vyakti nagn hokar Ka'ba ka Tawaf nahi karega."
