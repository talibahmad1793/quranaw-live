Sahih al-Bukhari 4109

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 153

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ صُرَدٍ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَ الأَحْزَابِ ‏ "‏ نَغْزُوهُمْ وَلاَ يَغْزُونَنَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Sulaiman bin Surad:On the day of Al-Ahzab (i.e. clans) the Prophet (ﷺ) said, (After this battle) we will go to attack them(i.e. the infidels) and they will not come to attack us

Sulaiman bin Surad bataate hain: Al-Ahzab (yaani giroh) ke din, Nabi ne kaha, "(Is jung ke baad) hum unpar (yaani kaafiron par) hamla karne jaenge, aur woh humpar hamla karne nahi aenge."
