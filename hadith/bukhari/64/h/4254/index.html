Sahih al-Bukhari 4254

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 288

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عُثْمَانُ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، قَالَ دَخَلْتُ أَنَا وَعُرْوَةُ بْنُ الزُّبَيْرِ الْمَسْجِدَ، فَإِذَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ جَالِسٌ إِلَى حُجْرَةِ عَائِشَةَ ثُمَّ قَالَ كَمِ اعْتَمَرَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ أَرْبَعًا ‏{‏إِحْدَاهُنَّ فِي رَجَبٍ‏}‏ ثُمَّ سَمِعْنَا اسْتِنَانَ، عَائِشَةَ قَالَ عُرْوَةُ يَا أُمَّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَلاَ تَسْمَعِينَ مَا يَقُولُ أَبُو عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ إِنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم اعْتَمَرَ أَرْبَعَ عُمَرٍ‏.‏ فَقَالَتْ مَا اعْتَمَرَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عُمْرَةً إِلاَّ وَهْوَ شَاهِدُهُ، وَمَا اعْتَمَرَ فِي رَجَبٍ قَطُّ‏.‏

Narrated Mujahid:`Urwa and I entered the Mosque and found `Abdullah bin `Umar sitting beside the dwelling place of `Aisha. `Urwa asked (Ibn `Umar), "How many `Umras did the Prophet (ﷺ) perform?" Ibn `Umar replied, "Four, one of which was in Rajab." Then we heard `Aisha brushing her teeth whereupon `Urwa said, "O mother of the believers! Don't you hear what Abu `Abdur-Rahman is saying? He is saying that the Prophet performed four `Umra, one of which was in Rajab." `Aisha said, "The Prophet (ﷺ) did not perform any `Umra but he (i.e. Ibn `Umar) witnessed it. And he (the Prophet (ﷺ) ) never did any `Umra in (the month of) Rajab

Mujahid bataate hain: Urwa aur main masjid mein daakhil hue aur Abdullah bin Umar ko Ayesha ke rehne ki jagah ke bagal baithe paaya. Urwa ne (Ibn Umar se) pucha, "Nabi ne kitne Umrah kiye?" Ibn Umar ne jawab diya, "Chaar, jinmein se ek Rajab mein tha." Phir humne Ayesha ko apne daant safaee karte suna, ispar Urwa ne kaha, "Aye mominon ki maa! Kya aap sun rahi hain Abu Abdur-Rahman kya kah rahe hain? Woh keh rahe hain Nabi ne chaar Umrah kiye, jinmein se ek Rajab mein tha." Ayesha ne kaha, "Nabi ne koee bhi Umrah nahi kiya jiske woh (yaani Ibn Umar) gawah nahi the. Aur unhone kabhi Rajab (mahine) mein Umrah nahi kiya."
