Sahih al-Bukhari 4436

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 457

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ سَعْدٍ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ لَمَّا مَرِضَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم الْمَرَضَ الَّذِي مَاتَ فِيهِ جَعَلَ يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ فِي الرَّفِيقِ الأَعْلَى ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:When the Prophet (ﷺ) fell ill in his fatal illness, he started saying, "With the highest companion

Aisha bataati hain: Jab Nabi (SAW) apni aakhri bimari mein bimar pade, to wo kehne lage, '(Mujhe) sabse buland saathi ke saath (rakh).'
