Sahih al-Bukhari 4144

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 188

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا وَكِيعٌ، عَنْ نَافِعِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ كَانَتْ تَقْرَأُ ‏{‏إِذْ تَلِقُونَهُ بِأَلْسِنَتِكُمْ‏}‏ وَتَقُولُ الْوَلْقُ الْكَذِبُ‏.‏ قَالَ ابْنُ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ وَكَانَتْ أَعْلَمَ مِنْ غَيْرِهَا بِذَلِكَ لأَنَّهُ نَزَلَ فِيهَا‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Abi Mulaika:`Aisha used to recite this Verse:-- 'Ida taliqunahu bi-alsinatikum' (24.15) "(As you tell lie with your tongues.)" and used to say "Al-Walaq" means "telling of a lie. "She knew this Verse more than anybody else as it was revealed about her

Ibn Abi Mulaika bataate hain: Ayesha yeh Ayat padha karti thi: "Ida taliqunahu bi-alsinatikum" (24:15) (yaani jab tum apni zubanon se jhoot bolte ho), aur woh kahti thi ke "Al-Walaq" ka matlab hai "jhoot bolna." Woh is Ayat ko kisi se zyada achhi tarah jaanti thi kyunke yeh unke baare mein naazil huee thi.
