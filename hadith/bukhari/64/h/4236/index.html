Sahih al-Bukhari 4236

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 274

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ مَهْدِيٍّ، عَنْ مَالِكِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ لَوْلاَ آخِرُ الْمُسْلِمِينَ مَا فُتِحَتْ عَلَيْهِمْ قَرْيَةٌ إِلاَّ قَسَمْتُهَا، كَمَا قَسَمَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَيْبَرَ‏.‏

Narrated `Umar:But for the other Muslims (i.e. coming generations) I would divide (the land of) whatever villages the Muslims might conquer (among the fighters), as the Prophet (ﷺ) divided (the land of) Khaibar

Umar bataate hain: Magar aane wali peedhiyon ke Musalmanon ke liye, main woh gaanv taqseem kar deta jise Musalman fatah karenge (yoddhaon ke beech), jaise Nabi ne Khaibar ki zameen taqseem ki.
