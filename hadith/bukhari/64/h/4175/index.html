Sahih al-Bukhari 4175

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 217

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي عَدِيٍّ، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ بُشَيْرِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنْ سُوَيْدِ بْنِ النُّعْمَانِ ـ وَكَانَ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ الشَّجَرَةِ ـ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَصْحَابُهُ أُتُوا بِسَوِيقٍ فَلاَكُوهُ‏.‏ تَابَعَهُ مُعَاذٌ عَنْ شُعْبَةَ‏.‏

Narrated Suwaid bin An-Nu`man:who was one of those who witnessed (the Pledge of allegiance beneath) the Tree: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and his companions were given Sawiq and they chewed it

Suwaid bin An-Nu'man bataate hain (jo un logon mein se the jinhone darakht ke neeche (Bai'at) dekhi thi): Allah ke Rasool aur unke sahaba ko Sawiq (yaani pisa hua jau) diya gaya aur unhone use chabaya.
