Sahih al-Bukhari 4214

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 254

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ‏.‏ وَحَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وَهْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ هِلاَلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مُغَفَّلٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كُنَّا مُحَاصِرِي خَيْبَرَ فَرَمَى إِنْسَانٌ بِجِرَابٍ فِيهِ شَحْمٌ، فَنَزَوْتُ لآخُذَهُ، فَالْتَفَتُّ فَإِذَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم�� فَاسْتَحْيَيْتُ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin Mughaffal:While we were besieging Khaibar, a person threw a leather container containing some fat and I ran to take it. Suddenly I looked behind, and behold! The Prophet (ﷺ) was there. So I felt shy (to take it then)

Abdullah bin Mughaffal bataate hain: Jabke hum Khaibar ko gher rahe the, ek shaks ne ghi se bhara ek chamde ka bartan phenka, aur main use lene ke liye dauda. Achanak maine peeche mudkar dekha, aur dekho! Nabi wahaan the. Isliye main sharminda ho gaya (use us waqt lene se).
