Sahih al-Bukhari 3958

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 10

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ رَجَاءٍ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْرَائِيلُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ، قَالَ كُنَّا أَصْحَابَ مُحَمَّدٍ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَتَحَدَّثُ أَنَّ عِدَّةَ أَصْحَابِ بَدْرٍ عَلَى عِدَّةِ أَصْحَابِ طَالُوتَ الَّذِينَ جَاوَزُوا مَعَهُ النَّهَرَ، وَلَمْ يُجَاوِزْ مَعَهُ إِلاَّ مُؤْمِنٌ، بِضْعَةَ عَشَرَ وَثَلاَثَمِائَةٍ‏.‏

Narrated Al-Bara:We, the Companions of Muhammad used to say that the number of the warriors of Badr was the same as the number of Saul's companions who crossed the river (of Jordan) with him, and none crossed the river with him but a believer, and they were over three-hundred-and-ten men

Al-Bara bataate hain: Hum, Muhammad ke sahaba, kahte the ke Badr ke yoddhaon ki sankhya Saul ke sathiyon jitni thi jinhone unke saath nadi paar ki, aur koee bhi unke saath nadi paar nahi kiya siwaye ek sachche momin ke, aur woh teen sau das se zyada the.
