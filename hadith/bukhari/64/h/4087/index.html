Sahih al-Bukhari 4087

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 131

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، سَمِعَ جَابِرًا، يَقُولُ الَّذِي قَتَلَ خُبَيْبًا هُوَ أَبُو سِرْوَعَةَ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir:The person who killed Khubaib was Abu Sirwa' (i.e. `Uqba bin Al-Harith)

Jabir bataate hain: Jinhone Khubaib ko qatl kiya woh Abu Sirwa (yaani Uqba bin Al-Harith) the.
