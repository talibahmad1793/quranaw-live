Sahih al-Bukhari 4356

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 382

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، حَدَّثَنَا قَيْسٌ، قَالَ قَالَ لِي جَرِيرٌ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ لِي النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ أَلاَ تُرِيحُنِي مِنْ ذِي الْخَلَصَةِ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَكَانَ بَيْتًا فِي خَثْعَمَ يُسَمَّى الْكَعْبَةَ الْيَمَانِيَةَ، فَانْطَلَقْتُ فِي خَمْسِينَ وَمِائَةِ فَارِسٍ مِنْ أَحْمَسَ، وَكَانُوا أَصْحَابَ خَيْلٍ، وَكُنْتُ لاَ أَثْبُتُ عَلَى الْخَيْلِ، فَضَرَبَ فِي صَدْرِي حَتَّى رَأَيْتُ أَثَرَ أَصَابِعِهِ فِي صَدْرِي، وَقَالَ ‏"‏ اللَّهُمَّ ثَبِّتْهُ، وَاجْعَلْهُ هَادِيًا مَهْدِيًّا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَانْطَلَقَ إِلَيْهَا فَكَسَرَهَا وَحَرَّقَهَا، ثُمَّ بَعَثَ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ رَسُولُ جَرِيرٍ وَالَّذِي بَعَثَكَ بِالْحَقِّ، مَا جِئْتُكَ حَتَّى تَرَكْتُهَا كَأَنَّهَا جَمَلٌ أَجْرَبُ‏.‏ قَالَ فَبَارَكَ فِي خَيْلِ أَحْمَسَ وَرِجَالِهَا خَمْسَ مَرَّاتٍ‏.‏

Narrated Qais:Jarir said to me, The Prophet (ﷺ) said to me, "Won't you relieve me from Dhu-l-Khalasa?" And that was a house (in Yemen belonging to the tribe of) Khatham called Al-Ka`ba Al Yamaniya. I proceeded with one-hundred and-fifty cavalry from Ahmas (tribe) who were horse riders. I used not to sit firm on horses, so the Prophet (ﷺ) stroke me over my chest till I saw the mark of his fingers over my chest, and then he said, 'O Allah! Make him (i.e. Jarir) firm and one who guides others and is guided on the right path." So Jarir proceeded to it dismantled and burnt it, and then sent a messenger to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ). The messenger of Jarir said (to the Prophet), "By Him Who sent you with the Truth, I did not leave that place till it was like a scabby camel." The Prophet (ﷺ) blessed the horses of Ahmas and their men five times

Qais bataate hain: Jarir ne mujhse kaha, Nabi ne mujhe kaha, "Kya tum mujhe Dhu-l-Khalasa se rahat nahi doge?" Aur yeh (Yemen mein) Khatham qabile ka ek ghar tha jise Al-Ka'ba Al Yamaniya kaha jata tha. Main paanch sau pachas ghudswaron ke saath badha jo Ahmas (qabile) se the aur ghode ke sawar the. Main ghodon par sthir baithna nahi jaanta tha, isliye Nabi ne mere seene par apni ungli se thap-thapaya jab tak maine unki ungliyon ka nishaan apne seene par dekha, aur phir unhone kaha, 'Aye Allah! Ise (yaani Jarir ko) sthir aur ek aisa shaks banaa jo doosron ko sahi raste par le jaye aur khud bhi sahi raste par ho.'" Isliye Jarir uski taraf badhe, use dhwast kiya aur jala diya, aur phir Allah ke Rasool ke paas ek sandesh-vahak bheja. Jarir ke sandesh-vahak ne (Nabi se) kaha, "Us Zaat ki qasam jisne aapko Haqq ke saath bheja hai, maine woh jagah tab tak nahi chhoda jab tak yeh ek kharaab untni jaisa nahi ban gaya." Nabi ne Ahmas ke ghodon aur unke logon ko paanch baar barkat di.
