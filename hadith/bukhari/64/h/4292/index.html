Sahih al-Bukhari 4292

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 325

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى، مَا أَخْبَرَنَا أَحَدٌ، أَنَّهُ رَأَى النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُصَلِّي الضُّحَى غَيْرَ أُمِّ هَانِئٍ، فَإِنَّهَا ذَكَرَتْ أَنَّهُ يَوْمَ فَتْحِ مَكَّةَ اغْتَسَلَ فِي بَيْتِهَا ثُمَّ صَلَّى ثَمَانِيَ رَكَعَاتٍ، قَالَتْ لَمْ أَرَهُ صَلَّى صَلاَةً أَخَفَّ مِنْهَا غَيْرَ أَنَّهُ يُتِمُّ الرُّكُوعَ وَالسُّجُودَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Abi Laila:None informed us that he saw the Prophet (ﷺ) offering the Duha (i.e. forenoon) prayer, except Um Hani who mentioned that the Prophet (ﷺ) took a bath in her house on the day of the Conquest (of Mecca) and then offered an eight rak`at prayer. She added, "I never saw the Prophet (ﷺ) offering a lighter prayer than that prayer, but he was performing perfect bowing and prostrations

Ibn Abi Laila bataate hain: Humein kisi ne bhi yeh nahi bataya ke unhone Nabi ko Duha (yaani dopahar se pahle ki) namaz padhte dekha, siwaye Um Hani ke jinhone zikar kiya ke Nabi ne unke ghar mein Fatah ke din ghusal kiya aur phir aath rakaton ki namaz padhi. Unhone joda, "Maine Nabi ko us namaz se halki koee namaz kabhi nahi padhte dekha, magar woh poori tarah ruku aur sajda kar rahe the."
