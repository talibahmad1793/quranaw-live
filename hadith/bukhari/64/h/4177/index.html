Sahih al-Bukhari 4177

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 219

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ يَسِيرُ فِي بَعْضِ أَسْفَارِهِ، وَعُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ يَسِيرُ مَعَهُ لَيْلاً، فَسَأَلَهُ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ عَنْ شَىْءٍ فَلَمْ يُجِبْهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ثُمَّ سَأَلَهُ فَلَمْ يُجِبْهُ، ثُمَّ سَأَلَهُ فَلَمْ يُجِبْهُ وَقَالَ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ ثَكِلَتْكَ أُمُّكَ يَا عُمَرُ، نَزَرْتَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ثَلاَثَ مَرَّاتٍ، كُلُّ ذَلِكَ لاَ يُجِيبُكَ‏.‏ قَالَ عُمَرُ فَحَرَّكْتُ بَعِيرِي ثُمَّ تَقَدَّمْتُ أَمَامَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَخَشِيتُ أَنْ يَنْزِلَ فِيَّ قُرْآنٌ، فَمَا نَشِبْتُ أَنْ سَمِعْتُ صَارِخًا يَصْرُخُ بِي ـ قَالَ ـ فَقُلْتُ لَقَدْ خَشِيتُ أَنْ يَكُونَ نَزَلَ فِيَّ قُرْآنٌ‏.‏ وَجِئْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَسَلَّمْتُ عَلَيْهِ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ لَقَدْ أُنْزِلَتْ عَلَىَّ اللَّيْلَةَ سُورَةٌ لَهِيَ أَحَبُّ إِلَىَّ مِمَّا طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِ الشَّمْسُ، ثُمَّ قَرَأَ ‏{‏إِنَّا فَتَحْنَا لَكَ فَتْحًا مُبِينًا‏}‏‏.‏‏"‏

Narrated Zaid bin Aslam:My father said, "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) was proceeding at night on one of his journeys and `Umar bin Al- Khattab was going along with him. `Umar bin Al-Khattab asked him (about something) but Allah's Apostle did not answer him. `Umar asked him again, but he did not answer him. He asked him again (for the third time) but he did not answer him. On that `Umar bin Al-Khattab addressed himself saying, "May your mother be bereaved of you, O `Umar, for you have asked Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) thrice, yet he has not answered you." `Umar said, "Then I made my camel run fast and took it in front of the other Muslims, and I was afraid that something might be revealed in my connection. I had hardly waited for a moment when I heard somebody calling me. I said, 'I was afraid that something might have been revealed about me.' Then I came to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and greeted him. He (i.e. the Prophet) said, 'Tonight there has been revealed to me, a Sura which is dearer to me than (all the world) on which the sun rises,' and then he recited: 'Verily! We have granted you (O Muhammad) A manifest victory

Zaid bin Aslam bataate hain (apne waalid se): Allah ke Rasool apne ek safar ki raat mein aage badh rahe the aur Umar bin Al-Khattab unke saath ja rahe the. Umar bin Al-Khattab ne unse (kuch) pucha, magar Allah ke Rasool ne unhe jawab nahi diya. Umar ne phir pucha, magar unhone phir jawab nahi diya. Unhone phir pucha (teesri baar), magar unhone phir jawab nahi diya. Ispar Umar bin Al-Khattab ne khud se kaha, "Teri maa tumhe khoe, Aye Umar, kyunke tumne Allah ke Rasool se teen baar pucha, phir bhi unhone tumhe jawab nahi diya." Umar ne kaha, "Phir maine apne unt ko tezi se daudaya aur use doosre Musalmanon se aage le liya, aur mujhe dar tha ke mere sandarbh mein kuch naazil ho sakta hai. Main mushkil se ek pal ruka tha jab maine kisi ko mujhe bulate suna. Maine kaha, 'Mujhe dar tha mere baare mein kuch naazil hua hoga.' Phir main Allah ke Rasool ke paas aaya aur unhe salaam kiya. Unhone (yaani Nabi ne) kaha, 'Aaj raat mujhpar ek Surah naazil huee hai jo mujhe (saari duniya) se, jispar suraj chadhta hai, zyada pyari hai,' aur phir unhone padha: 'Beshak, Humne aapko (Aye Muhammad) ek saaf Vijay diya hai...'"
