Sahih al-Bukhari 4263

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 297

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّابِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ يَحْيَى بْنَ سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَتْنِي عَمْرَةُ، قَالَتْ سَمِعْتُ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ تَقُولُ لَمَّا جَاءَ قَتْلُ ابْنِ حَارِثَةَ وَجَعْفَرِ بْنِ أَبِي طَالِبٍ وَعَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ رَوَاحَةَ ـ رضى الله عنهم ـ جَلَسَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُعْرَفُ فِيهِ الْحُزْنُ ـ قَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ ـ وَأَنَا أَطَّلِعُ مِنْ صَائِرِ الْبَابِ ـ تَعْنِي مِنْ شَقِّ الْبَابِ ـ فَأَتَاهُ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ أَىْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّ نِسَاءَ جَعْفَرٍ قَالَ وَذَكَرَ بُكَاءَهُنَّ، فَأَمَرَهُ أَنْ يَنْهَاهُنَّ قَالَ فَذَهَبَ الرَّجُلُ ثُمَّ أَتَى فَقَالَ قَدْ نَهَيْتُهُنَّ‏.‏ وَذَكَرَ أَنَّهُ لَمْ يُطِعْنَهُ قَالَ فَأَمَرَ أَيْضًا فَذَهَبَ ثُمَّ أَتَى فَقَالَ وَاللَّهِ لَقَدْ غَلَبْنَنَا‏.‏ فَزَعَمَتْ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ فَاحْثُ فِي أَفْوَاهِهِنَّ مِنَ التُّرَابِ ‏"‏ قَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ فَقُلْتُ أَرْغَمَ اللَّهُ أَنْفَكَ، فَوَاللَّهِ مَا أَنْتَ تَفْعَلُ، وَمَا تَرَكْتَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنَ الْعَنَاءِ‏.‏

Narrated `Amra:I heard `Aisha saying, "When the news of the martyrdom of Ibn Haritha, Ja`far bin Abi Talib and `Abdullah bin Rawaha reached, Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) sat with sorrow explicit on his face." `Aisha added, "I was then peeping through a chink in the door. A man came to him and said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! The women of Ja`far are crying.' Thereupon the Prophet (ﷺ) told him to forbid them to do so. So the man went away and returned saying, "I forbade them but they did not listen to me." The Prophet (ﷺ) ordered him again to go (and forbid them). He went again and came saying, 'By Allah, they overpowered me (i.e. did not listen to me)." `Aisha said that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said (to him), "Go and throw dust into their mouths." Aisha added, "I said, May Allah put your nose in the dust! By Allah, neither have you done what you have been ordered, nor have you relieved Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) from trouble

Amra bataate hain: Maine Ayesha ko kehte suna, "Jab Ibn Haritha, Ja'far bin Abi Talib, aur Abdullah bin Rawaha ki shahadat ki khabar pahunchi, Allah ke Rasool udaasi ke saath baith gaye, jo unke chehre par saaf dikh raha tha." Ayesha ne joda, "Main us waqt darwaze ke ek chhed se jhaank rahi thi. Ek shaks unke paas aaya aur kaha, 'Aye Allah ke Rasool! Ja'far ki aurten ro rahi hain.' Ispar Nabi ne unhe unhe aisa karne se rokne ka hukum diya. Isliye shaks chala gaya aur wapas aakar kaha, 'Maine unhe roka magar unhone meri baat nahi maani.' Nabi ne unhe phir jane ka hukum diya. Woh phir gaye aur wapas aakar kaha, 'Allah ki qasam, unhone mujhe haavi kar diya (yaani meri baat nahi suni).'" Ayesha ne kaha ke Allah ke Rasool ne (unse) kaha, "Jao aur unke munhon mein mitti daalo." Ayesha ne joda, "Maine kaha, 'Allah tumhari naak ko mitti mein sane!' Allah ki qasam, na tumne woh kiya jiska tumhe hukum diya gaya, na tumne Allah ke Rasool ko takleef se rahat di."
