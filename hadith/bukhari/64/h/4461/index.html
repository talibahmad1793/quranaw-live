Sahih al-Bukhari 4461

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 477

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الأَحْوَصِ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ الْحَارِثِ، قَالَ مَا تَرَكَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم دِينَارًا وَلاَ دِرْهَمًا وَلاَ عَبْدًا وَلاَ أَمَةً، إِلاَّ بَغْلَتَهُ الْبَيْضَاءَ الَّتِي كَانَ يَرْكَبُهَا، وَسِلاَحَهُ، وَأَرْضًا جَعَلَهَا لاِبْنِ السَّبِيلِ صَدَقَةً‏.‏

Narrated 'Amir bin Al-Harith:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) did not leave a Dinar or a Dirham or a male or a female slave. He left only his white mule on which he used to ride, and his weapons, and a piece of land which he gave in charity for the needy travelers

Amir bin Al-Harith bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne peeche na koi Dinar chhoda, na Dirham, na koi ghulaam, na koi launDi. Unhone sirf apna safed khachar chhoda jispar wo sawaar hote the, apne hathiyaar, aur zameen ka ek tukda jo unhone zaroorat-mand musaafiron ke liye sadqa kar diya tha.
