Sahih al-Bukhari 4132

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 176

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي سَالِمٌ، أَنَّ ابْنَ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ غَزَوْتُ مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قِبَلَ نَجْدٍ، فَوَازَيْنَا الْعَدُوَّ فَصَافَفْنَا لَهُمْ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:I took part in a Ghazwa towards Najd along with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and we clashed with the enemy, and we lined up for them

Ibn Umar bataate hain: Maine Najd ki taraf ek Ghazwe mein hissa liya Allah ke Rasool ke saath, aur hum dushman se mile aur unke muqaable mein safon mein khade hue.
