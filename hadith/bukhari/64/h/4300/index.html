Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 333

Hadith text

وَقَالَ اللَّيْثُ حَدَّثَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، أَخْبَرَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ ثَعْلَبَةَ بْنِ صُعَيْرٍ، وَكَانَ النَّبِيُّ، صلى الله عليه وسلم قَدْ مَسَحَ وَجْهَهُ عَامَ الْفَتْحِ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin Tha`laba bin Su`air whose face was rubbed by the Prophet during the year of the Conquest (of Makkah)

Abdullah bin Tha'laba bin Su'air bataate hain, jinke chehre ko Nabi ne Fatah ke saal (apne haathon se pyaar se) chhuya tha.
