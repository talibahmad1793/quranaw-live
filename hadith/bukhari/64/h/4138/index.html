Sahih al-Bukhari 4138

Book 64: Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi) · Hadith 182

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، عَنْ رَبِيعَةَ بْنِ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ يَحْيَى بْنِ حَبَّانَ، عَنِ ابْنِ مُحَيْرِيزٍ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ دَخَلْتُ الْمَسْجِدَ فَرَأَيْتُ أَبَا سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيَّ فَجَلَسْتُ إِلَيْهِ فَسَأَلْتُهُ عَنِ الْعَزْلِ،، قَالَ أَبُو سَعِيدٍ خَرَجْنَا مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي غَزْوَةِ بَنِي الْمُصْطَلِقِ، فَأَصَبْنَا سَبْيًا مِنْ سَبْىِ الْعَرَبِ، فَاشْتَهَيْنَا النِّسَاءَ وَاشْتَدَّتْ عَلَيْنَا الْعُزْبَةُ، وَأَحْبَبْنَا الْعَزْلَ، فَأَرَدْنَا أَنْ نَعْزِلَ، وَقُلْنَا نَعْزِلُ وَرَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْنَ أَظْهُرِنَا قَبْلَ أَنْ نَسْأَلَهُ فَسَأَلْنَاهُ عَنْ ذَلِكَ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ مَا عَلَيْكُمْ أَنْ لاَ تَفْعَلُوا، مَا مِنْ نَسَمَةٍ كَائِنَةٍ إِلَى يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ إِلاَّ وَهْىَ كَائِنَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Muhairiz:I entered the Mosque and saw Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri and sat beside him and asked him about Al-Azl (i.e. coitus interruptus). Abu Sa`id said, "We went out with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) for the Ghazwa of Banu Al-Mustaliq and we received captives from among the Arab captives and we desired women and celibacy became hard on us and we loved to do coitus interruptus. So when we intended to do coitus interruptus, we said, 'How can we do coitus interruptus before asking Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) who is present among us?" We asked (him) about it and he said, 'It is better for you not to do so, for if any soul (till the Day of Resurrection) is predestined to exist, it will exist

Ibn Muhairiz bataate hain: Main masjid mein daakhil hua aur Abu Saeed Al-Khudri ko dekha aur unke paas baith gaya aur unse Al-Azl (yaani sambhog ke dauran beech mein nikal jane ki riwaj) ke baare mein pucha. Abu Saeed ne kaha, "Hum Allah ke Rasool ke saath Ghazwa Banu Al-Mustaliq ke liye nikle aur humein Arab qaidiyon mein se kuch qaidi mile aur humein aurton ki iccha huee aur brahmacharya humare liye mushkil ho gaya aur humein Al-Azl karna achha lagne laga. Isliye jab humne Al-Azl karne ka irada kiya, humne kaha, 'Hum Al-Azl kaise kar sakte hain isse pahle ke hum Allah ke Rasool se puchen jo humare beech maujood hain?' Humne unse iske baare mein pucha aur unhone kaha, 'Tumhare liye behtar hai ke tum aisa na karo, kyunke agar koee bhi jaan (Qiyamat ke din tak) astitva mein aane ke liye niyat hai, woh astitva mein aayegi hi.'"
