Sahih al-Bukhari 5916

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 131

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، عَنْ حَفْصَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ زَوْجِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَتْ قُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ مَا شَأْنُ النَّاسِ حَلُّوا بِعُمْرَةٍ، وَلَمْ تَحْلِلْ أَنْتَ مِنْ عُمْرَتِكَ قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنِّي لَبَّدْتُ رَأْسِي، وَقَلَّدْتُ هَدْيِي، فَلاَ أَحِلُّ حَتَّى أَنْحَرَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Hafsa:(the wife of the Prophet) I said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Why have the people finished their Ihram after performing the `Umra while you have not finished your Ihram after your `Umra?" He said, "I have done Talbid (of my hair) and have decorated my Hadis with garlands, so I shall not finish my Ihram till I have slaughtered my Hadi (animal for sacrifice)

Hafsa (Nabi ki zawja) bataati hain: Maine kaha, 'Aye Allah ke Rasool! Logon ne Umra karne ke baad apna Ihram khatam kar liya hai, jabke aapne apni Umra ke baad apna Ihram khatam nahi kiya?' Unhone kaha, 'Maine apne baalon ka Talbid kiya hai aur apne Hadi (qurbaani ke jaanwar) ko haaron se sajaaya hai, isliye main apna Ihram tab tak khatam nahi karunga jab tak apna Hadi zibah na kar loon.'
