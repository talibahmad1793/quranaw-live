Sahih al-Bukhari 5946

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 162

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا زُهَيْرُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، عَنْ عُمَارَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي زُرْعَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ أُتِيَ عُمَرُ بِامْرَأَةٍ تَشِمُ فَقَامَ فَقَالَ أَنْشُدُكُمْ بِاللَّهِ مَنْ سَمِعَ مِنَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي الْوَشْمِ فَقَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ فَقُمْتُ فَقُلْتُ يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَنَا سَمِعْتُ‏.‏ قَالَ مَا سَمِعْتَ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ لاَ تَشِمْنَ وَلاَ تَسْتَوْشِمْنَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:A woman who used to practise tattooing was brought to `Umar. `Umar got up and said, "I beseech you by Allah, which of you heard the Prophet (ﷺ) saying something about tattooing?" I got up and said, "O chief of the Believers! I heard something." He said, "What did you hear?" I said, "I heard the Prophet (addressing the ladies), saying, 'Do not practise tattooing and do not get yourselves tattooed

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Gudna banaane wali ek khaatoon ko Umar ke paas laaya gaya. Umar khade hue aur kaha, 'Main tumhein Allah ki qasam deta hoon, tum mein se kisne Nabi (SAW) ko gudna ke baare mein kuch kehte suna hai?' Main khada hua aur kaha, 'Aye Momineen ke Ameer! Maine kuch suna hai.' Unhone pucha, 'Tumne kya suna?' Maine kaha, 'Maine Nabi (SAW) ko (auraton se khitaab karte hue) ye kehte suna, "Gudna mat banaao aur apne aap ko gudna mat banwaao."'
