Sahih al-Bukhari 5914

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 129

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي سَالِمُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، أَنَّ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ عُمَرَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ يَقُولُ مَنْ ضَفَّرَ فَلْيَحْلِقْ، وَلاَ تَشَبَّهُوا بِالتَّلْبِيدِ‏.‏ وَكَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ يَقُولُ لَقَدْ رَأَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مُلَبِّدًا‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:I heard `Umar saying, "Whoever braids his hair should shave it (on finishing Ihram). You'd better not do, something like Talbid." Ibn `Umar used to say: "I saw Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) with his hair stuck together with gum

Abdullah bin Umar bataate hain: Maine Umar ko ye kehte suna, 'Jo koi apne baal gunthta hai (braids), use (Ihram khatam karte waqt) mundwaana chaahiye. Behtar hai ke tum Talbid jaisa kuch na karo.' Ibn Umar kaha karte the, 'Maine Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko apne baal gond se chipke hue dekha.'
