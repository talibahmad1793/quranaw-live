Sahih al-Bukhari 5878

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 95

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الأَنْصَارِيُّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ ثُمَامَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، أَنَّ أَبَا بَكْرٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ لَمَّا اسْتُخْلِفَ كَتَبَ لَهُ، وَكَانَ نَقْشُ الْخَاتَمِ ثَلاَثَةَ أَسْطُرٍ‏.‏ مُحَمَّدٌ سَطْرٌ، وَرَسُولُ سَطْرٌ، وَاللَّهِ سَطْرٌ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:that when Abu Bakr became the Caliph, he wrote a letter to him (and stamped it with the Prophet's ring) and the engraving of the ring was in three lines: Muhammad in one line, 'Apostle' in another line, and 'Allah' in a third line

Anas bataate hain: Jab Abu Bakr Khalifa bane, unhone unhein ek khat likha (aur Nabi ki anguthi se mohar lagaayi) aur anguthi ka naqsh teen lakeeron mein tha: 'Muhammad' ek lakeer mein, 'Rasool' doosri lakeer mein, aur 'Allah' teesri lakeer mein.
