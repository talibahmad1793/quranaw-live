Sahih al-Bukhari 5895

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 112

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ زَيْدٍ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ، قَالَ سُئِلَ أَنَسٌ عَنْ خِضَابِ النَّبِيِّ، صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ إِنَّهُ لَمْ يَبْلُغْ مَا يَخْضِبُ، لَوْ شِئْتُ أَنْ أَعُدَّ شَمَطَاتِهِ فِي لِحْيَتِهِ‏.‏

Narrated Thabit:Anas was asked whether the Prophet (ﷺ) used a a hair dye or not. He replied, "The Prophet (ﷺ) did not have enough grey hair to dye, (such that) if I wanted to count the fading hairs in his beard (I could have)

Thabit bataate hain: Anas se pucha gaya ke kya Nabi (SAW) baal rangne wali dawa (hair dye) istemaal karte the ya nahi. Unhone jawab diya, 'Nabi (SAW) ke paas rangne laayak itne safed baal nahi the, (itne kam the ke) agar main unki daadi ke halke baal ginana chahta (to gin sakta tha).'
