Sahih al-Bukhari 5912

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 127

Hadith text

وَقَالَ أَبُو هِلاَلٍ حَدَّثَنَا قَتَادَةُ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ،‏.‏ أَوْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ضَخْمَ الْكَفَّيْنِ وَالْقَدَمَيْنِ، لَمْ أَرَ بَعْدَهُ شَبَهًا لَهُ‏.‏

Narrated Anas or Jabir bin `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) had big hands and feet and I have not seen anybody like him after him

Anas ya Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ke haath aur pair bade the, aur maine unke baad kabhi kisi ko unke jaisa nahi dekha.
