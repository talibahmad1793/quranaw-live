Sahih al-Bukhari 5869

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 86

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، أَخْبَرَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ زُرَيْعٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا حُمَيْدٌ، قَالَ سُئِلَ أَنَسٌ هَلِ اتَّخَذَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَاتَمًا قَالَ أَخَّرَ لَيْلَةً صَلاَةَ الْعِشَاءِ إِلَى شَطْرِ اللَّيْلِ، ثُمَّ أَقْبَلَ عَلَيْنَا بِوَجْهِهِ، فَكَأَنِّي أَنْظُرُ إِلَى وَبِيصِ خَاتَمِهِ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ النَّاسَ قَدْ صَلَّوْا وَنَامُوا، وَإِنَّكُمْ لَمْ تَزَالُوا فِي صَلاَةٍ مَا انْتَظَرْتُمُوهَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Humaid:Anas was asked, "Did the Prophet (ﷺ) wear a ring?" Anas said, "Once he delayed the: `Isha' prayer till midnight. Then he came, facing us ..... as if I am now Looking at the glitter of his ring ..... and said, "The people have offered their prayers and slept but you have been in prayer as you have been waiting for it

Humaid bataate hain: Anas se pucha gaya, 'Kya Nabi (SAW) anguthi pehante the?' Anas ne kaha, 'Ek baar unhone Isha ki namaz aadhi raat tak der kar di. Phir wo aaye, hamaari taraf muh karke ..... jaise ke main abhi bhi unki anguthi ki chamak dekh raha hoon ..... aur kaha, "Logon ne apni namaz padh li hai aur so gaye hain, magar tum namaz mein rahe ho kyunke tum iska intezaar kar rahe the."'
