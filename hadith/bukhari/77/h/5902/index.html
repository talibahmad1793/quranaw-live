Sahih al-Bukhari 5902

Book 77: Dress · Hadith 119

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ أُرَانِي اللَّيْلَةَ عِنْدَ الْكَعْبَةِ، فَرَأَيْتُ رَجُلاً آدَمَ كَأَحْسَنِ مَا أَنْتَ رَاءٍ مِنْ أُدْمِ الرِّجَالِ، لَهُ لِمَّةٌ كَأَحْسَنِ مَا أَنْتَ رَاءٍ مِنَ اللِّمَمِ، قَدْ رَجَّلَهَا، فَهْىَ تَقْطُرُ مَاءً مُتَّكِئًا عَلَى رَجُلَيْنِ، أَوْ عَلَى عَوَاتِقِ رَجُلَيْنِ، يَطُوفُ بِالْبَيْتِ فَسَأَلْتُ مَنْ هَذَا فَقِيلَ الْمَسِيحُ ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ‏.‏ وَإِذَا أَنَا بِرَجُلٍ جَعْدٍ، قَطَطٍ، أَعْوَرِ الْعَيْنِ الْيُمْنَى كَأَنَّهَا عِنَبَةٌ طَافِيَةٌ، فَسَأَلْتُ مَنْ هَذَا فَقِيلَ الْمَسِيحُ الدَّجَّالُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Today I saw myself in a dream near the Ka`ba. I saw a whitish brown man, the handsomest of all brown men you might ever see. He had the most beautiful Limma (hair hanging down to the earlobes) you might ever see. He had combed it and it was dripping water; and he was performing the Tawaf around the Ka`ba leaning on two men or on the shoulders of two men. I asked, "Who is this?" It was said. "Messiah, the son of Mary." Suddenly I saw a curly-haired man, blind in the right eye which looked like a protruding out grape. I asked, "Who is this?" It was said, "He is Masiah Ad-Dajjal

Abdullah bin Umar bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Aaj maine khud ko khwaab mein Kaaba ke qareeb dekha. Maine ek halke bhoore rang ka aadmi dekha, jaisa khoobsoorat bhoora aadmi tumne kabhi dekha ho. Unke baal sabse khoobsoorat Limma (kaan ki lau tak latakte baal) the jaise tumne kabhi dekhe hon. Unhone unhein kanghi ki thi aur usse paani tapak raha tha; aur wo do aadmiyon ke sahaare ya do aadmiyon ke kandhon par tek lagaakar Kaaba ka Tawaf kar rahe the. Maine pucha, "Ye kaun hai?" Kaha gaya, "Maseeh, Maryam ke bete." Achaanak maine ek ghunghraale baalon wala aadmi dekha, jiski daayin aankh andhi thi aur ubhri hui angoor jaisi lag rahi thi. Maine pucha, "Ye kaun hai?" Kaha gaya, "Ye Maseeh Ad-Dajjal hai."'
