Sahih al-Bukhari 7228

Book 94: Wishes · Hadith 3

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ نَصْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هَمَّامٍ، سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لَوْ كَانَ عِنْدِي أُحُدٌ ذَهَبًا، لأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ لاَ يَأْتِيَ ثَلاَثٌ وَعِنْدِي مِنْهُ دِينَارٌ، لَيْسَ شَىْءٌ أُرْصِدُهُ فِي دَيْنٍ عَلَىَّ أَجِدُ مَنْ يَقْبَلُهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "If I had gold equal to the mountain of Uhud, I would love that, before three days had passed, not a single Dinar thereof remained with me if I found somebody to accept it excluding some amount that I would keep for the payment of my debts

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Agar mere paas Uhud pahaad jitna sona ho, mujhe pasand aata ke teen din guzarne se pehle, mere paas usmein se ek bhi Dinar na bache, agar mujhe koi use qubool karne wala mile, siwaaye us thodi si raqam ke jo main apna qarz chukaane ke liye rakhunga.'
