Sahih al-Bukhari 5730

Book 76: Medicine · Hadith 45

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَامِرٍ، أَنَّ عُمَرَ، خَرَجَ إِلَى الشَّأْمِ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ بِسَرْغَ بَلَغَهُ أَنَّ الْوَبَاءَ قَدْ وَقَعَ بِالشَّأْمِ، فَأَخْبَرَهُ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَوْفٍ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا سَمِعْتُمْ بِهِ بِأَرْضٍ فَلاَ تَقْدَمُوا عَلَيْهِ وَإِذَا وَقَعَ بِأَرْضٍ وَأَنْتُمْ بِهَا فَلاَ تَخْرُجُوا فِرَارًا مِنْهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin 'Amir:`Umar went to Sham and when he reached Sargh, he got the news that an epidemic (of plague) had broken out in Sham. `Abdur-Rahman bin `Auf told him that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "If you hear that it (plague) has broken out in a land, do not go to it; but if it breaks out in a land where you are present, do not go out escaping from it

Abdullah bin Aamir bataate hain: Umar Sham ke liye gaye, aur jab wo Sargh pahunche, unhein khabar mili ke Sham mein mahamaari phail gayi hai. Abdur Rahman bin Auf ne unse kaha ke Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya tha, 'Agar tum suno ke kisi ilaake mein ye (mahamaari) phail gayi hai, to wahaan mat jaao; magar agar ye kisi ilaake mein phail jaaye jahaan tum maujood ho, to wahaan se bhaag kar mat nikalo.'
