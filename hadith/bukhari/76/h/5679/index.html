Sahih al-Bukhari 5679

Book 76: Medicine · Hadith 2

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا بِشْرُ بْنُ الْمُفَضَّلِ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ ذَكْوَانَ، عَنْ رُبَيِّعَ بِنْتِ مُعَوِّذٍ ابْنِ عَفْرَاءَ، قَالَتْ كُنَّا نَغْزُو مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَسْقِي الْقَوْمَ، وَنَخْدُمُهُمْ، وَنَرُدُّ الْقَتْلَى وَالْجَرْحَى إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ‏.‏

Narrated Rubai bint Mu`adh bin Afra:We used to go for Military expeditions along with Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and provide the people with water, serve them and bring the dead and the wounded back to Medina

Rubai bint Mu'adh bin Afra bataati hain: Hum Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ke saath fauji mohimon par jaaya karte the aur logon ko paani dete, unki khidmat karte, aur murda aur zakhmiyon ko Madina wapas laate.
