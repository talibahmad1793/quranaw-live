Sahih al-Bukhari 1016

Book 15: Invoking Allah for Rain (Istisqaa) · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ شَرِيكِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ هَلَكَتِ الْمَوَاشِي وَتَقَطَّعَتِ السُّبُلُ‏.‏ فَدَعَا، فَمُطِرْنَا مِنَ الْجُمُعَةِ إِلَى الْجُمُعَةِ، ثُمَّ جَاءَ فَقَالَ تَهَدَّمَتِ الْبُيُوتُ وَتَقَطَّعَتِ السُّبُلُ، وَهَلَكَتِ الْمَوَاشِي فَادْعُ اللَّهَ يُمْسِكْهَا‏.‏ فَقَامَ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ اللَّهُمَّ عَلَى الآكَامِ وَالظِّرَابِ وَالأَوْدِيَةِ وَمَنَابِتِ الشَّجَرِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَانْجَابَتْ عَنِ الْمَدِينَةِ انْجِيَابَ الثَّوْبِ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, "Livestock are destroyed and the roads are cut off." So Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) invoked Allah for rain and it rained from that Friday till the next Friday. The same person came again and said, "Houses have collapsed, roads are cut off, and the livestock are destroyed. Please pray to Allah to withhold the rain." Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) (stood up and) said, "O Allah! (Let it rain) on the plateaus, on the hills, in the valleys and over the places where trees grow." So the clouds cleared away from Medina as clothes are taken off

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Ek shaks Nabi ke paas aaya aur kaha: "Maal-maweshi barbaad ho gaye hain aur raaste band hain." To Allah ke Rasool ne Allah se baarish ki dua ki aur us Jumme se agle Jumme tak baarish hoti rahi. Wahi shaks phir aaya aur kaha: "Ghar gir gaye hain, raaste band hain, aur maal-maweshi barbaad ho gaye hain. Kripya Allah se baarish rokne ki dua kijiye." Allah ke Rasool ne (khade hokar) kaha: "Aye Allah! Pathareeli zameenon par, tibbon par, wadiyon mein, aur un jagahon par jahan darakht ugte hain." To baadal Madina se aise hat gaye jaise kapde utaar diye jaate hain.
