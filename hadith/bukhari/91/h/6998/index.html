Sahih al-Bukhari 6998

Book 91: Interpretation of Dreams · Hadith 17

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ الْمِقْدَامِ الْعِجْلِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ الطُّفَاوِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أُعْطِيتُ مَفَاتِيحَ الْكَلِمِ، وَنُصِرْتُ بِالرُّعْبِ، وَبَيْنَمَا أَنَا نَائِمٌ الْبَارِحَةَ إِذْ أُتِيتُ بِمَفَاتِيحِ خَزَائِنِ الأَرْضِ حَتَّى وُضِعَتْ فِي يَدِي ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ فَذَهَبَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَنْتُمْ تَنْتَقِلُونَهَا‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "I have been given the keys of eloquent speech and given victory with awe (cast into the hearts of the enemy), and while I was sleeping last night, the keys of the treasures of the earth were brought to me till they were put in my hand." Abu Huraira added: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) left (this world) and now you people are carrying those treasures from place to place

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Mujhe fasaahat (khubsoorat baat karne) ki chaabiyaan di gayi hain, aur mujhe jeet di gayi hai dahshat (dushman ke dilon mein daali gayi khauf) ke saath, aur kal raat jab main so raha tha, zameen ke khazaanon ki chaabiyaan mere paas laayi gayi yahaan tak ke unhein mere haath mein rakh diya gaya.' Abu Huraira ne izaafa kiya: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) (is duniya se) chale gaye aur ab tum log un khazaanon ko ek jagah se doosri jagah le jaa rahe ho.
