Sahih al-Bukhari 7045

Book 91: Interpretation of Dreams · Hadith 59

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ حَمْزَةَ، حَدَّثَنِي ابْنُ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، وَالدَّرَاوَرْدِيُّ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ خَبَّابٍ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ إِذَا رَأَى أَحَدُكُمُ الرُّؤْيَا يُحِبُّهَا، فَإِنَّهَا مِنَ اللَّهِ، فَلْيَحْمَدِ اللَّهَ عَلَيْهَا، وَلْيُحَدِّثْ بِهَا، وَإِذَا رَأَى غَيْرَ ذَلِكَ مِمَّا يَكْرَهُ، فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ، فَلْيَسْتَعِذْ مِنْ شَرِّهَا، وَلاَ يَذْكُرْهَا لأَحَدٍ، فَإِنَّهَا لَنْ تَضُرَّهُ ‏"‏

Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:I heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) saying, "If anyone of you saw a dream which he liked, then that was from Allah, and he should thank Allah for it and tell it to others; but if he saw something else, i.e, a dream which he did not like, then that is from Satan and he should seek refuge with Allah from it and should not tell it to anybody for it will not harm him

Abu Saeed Al-Khudri bataate hain: Maine Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ko ye kehte suna, 'Agar tum mein se koi aisa khwaab dekhe jo use pasand ho, to wo Allah ki taraf se hai, aur use iske liye Allah ka shukriya adaa karna chaahiye aur ise doosron ko batana chaahiye; magar agar wo kuch aur dekhe, yaani aisa khwaab jo use napasand ho, to wo Shaitaan ki taraf se hai aur use isse Allah ki panaah maangni chaahiye aur ise kisi ko nahi batana chaahiye, kyunke isse use nuqsaan nahi hoga.'
