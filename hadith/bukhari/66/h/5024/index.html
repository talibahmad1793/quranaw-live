Sahih al-Bukhari 5024

Book 66: Virtues of the Qur'an · Hadith 46

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَا أَذِنَ اللَّهُ لِشَىْءٍ مَا أَذِنَ لِلنَّبِيِّ أَنْ يَتَغَنَّى بِالْقُرْآنِ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ سُفْيَانُ تَفْسِيرُهُ يَسْتَغْنِي بِهِ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Allah does not listen to a prophet as He listens to a prophet who recites the Qur'an in a loud and pleasant tone." Sufyan said, "This saying means: a prophet who regards the Qur'an as something that makes him dispense with many worldly pleasures

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Allah kisi Nabi ki utni ghor se nahi sunta jitni ghor se wo us Nabi ki sunta hai jo Quran ko buland aur khushnuma aawaaz mein padhta hai.' Ravi Sufyan kehte hain, 'Iska matlab hai wo Nabi jo Quran ko aisi cheez samajhta hai jiski wajah se wo duniya ki bahut si lazzaton se be-niyaz ho jaata hai.'
