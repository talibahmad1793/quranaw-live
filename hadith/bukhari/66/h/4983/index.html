Sahih al-Bukhari 4983

Book 66: Virtues of the Qur'an · Hadith 5

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ الأَسْوَدِ بْنِ قَيْسٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ جُنْدَبًا، يَقُولُ اشْتَكَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَلَمْ يَقُمْ لَيْلَةً أَوْ لَيْلَتَيْنِ فَأَتَتْهُ امْرَأَةٌ فَقَالَتْ يَا مُحَمَّدُ مَا أُرَى شَيْطَانَكَ إِلاَّ قَدْ تَرَكَكَ، فَأَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ ‏{‏وَالضُّحَى * وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا سَجَى * مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَى‏}‏

Narrated Jundub:Once the Prophet (ﷺ) fell ill and did not offer the night prayer (Tahajjud prayer) for a night or two. A woman (the wife of Abu Lahab) came to him and said, "O Muhammad ! I do not see but that your Satan has left you." Then Allah revealed (Surat-Ad-Duha): 'By the fore-noon, and by the night when it darkens (or is still); Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor hated you

Jundub bataate hain: Ek baar Nabi (SAW) bimar pad gaye aur ek ya do raat Tahajjud ki namaz nahi padh sake. Ek aurat (Abu Lahab ki biwi) unke paas aayi aur kaha, 'Aye Muhammad! Mujhe lagta hai aapke Shaitaan ne aapko chhod diya hai.' Isper Allah ne (Surah Ad-Duha) naazil ki: 'Chaasht ki qasam, aur raat ki jab wo chhaa jaaye (ya thehar jaaye); tumhaare Rab ne na tumhein chhoda hai aur na hi tumse naaraaz hua hai.'
