Sahih al-Bukhari 5032

Book 66: Virtues of the Qur'an · Hadith 54

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَرْعَرَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ بِئْسَ مَا لأَحَدِهِمْ أَنْ يَقُولَ نَسِيتُ آيَةَ كَيْتَ وَكَيْتَ بَلْ نُسِّيَ، وَاسْتَذْكِرُوا الْقُرْآنَ فَإِنَّهُ أَشَدُّ تَفَصِّيًا مِنْ صُدُورِ الرِّجَالِ مِنَ النَّعَمِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "It is a bad thing that some of you say, 'I have forgotten such-and-such verse of the Qur'an,' for indeed, he has been caused (by Allah) to forget it. So you must keep on reciting the Qur'an because it escapes from the hearts of men faster than camel do

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Ye buri baat hai ke tum mein se koi kahe, "Main falaan Aayat bhool gaya," kyunke haqeeqat mein Allah ne hi use bhulaaya hai. Isliye tumhein Quran ki tilawat jaari rakhni chaahiye, kyunke ye logon ke dilon se oonton ke rassi todkar bhaagne se bhi tez nikal jaata hai.'
