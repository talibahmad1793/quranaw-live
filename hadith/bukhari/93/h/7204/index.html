Sahih al-Bukhari 7204

Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 64

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، حَدَّثَنَا هُشَيْمٌ، أَخْبَرَنَا سَيَّارٌ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، عَنْ جَرِيرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ بَايَعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى السَّمْعِ وَالطَّاعَةِ، فَلَقَّنَنِي، فِيمَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، وَالنُّصْحِ لِكُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:I gave the Pledge of allegiance to the Prophet (ﷺ) that I would listen and obey, and he told me to add: 'As much as I can, and will give good advice to every Muslim

Jabir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Maine Nabi (SAW) se sunne aur maanne ki baiyat li, aur unhone mujhe izaafa karne ko kaha: 'Jitni meri taaqat mein ho, aur har Musalmaan ko achhi salaah deta rahunga.'
