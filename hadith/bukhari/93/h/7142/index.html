Sahih al-Bukhari 7142

Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 6

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي التَّيَّاحِ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ اسْمَعُوا وَأَطِيعُوا وَإِنِ اسْتُعْمِلَ عَلَيْكُمْ عَبْدٌ حَبَشِيٌّ كَأَنَّ رَأْسَهُ زَبِيبَةٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "You should listen to and obey, your ruler even if he was an Ethiopian (black) slave whose head looks like a raisin

Anas bin Malik bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Tumhein apne hukmraan ki baat sunni aur uski farmaanbardaari karni chaahiye, chaahe wo ek Habashi (kaale) ghulaam ho jiska sar kishmish jaisa dikhta ho.'
