Sahih al-Bukhari 7144

Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 8

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنِي نَافِعٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ السَّمْعُ وَالطَّاعَةُ عَلَى الْمَرْءِ الْمُسْلِمِ، فِيمَا أَحَبَّ وَكَرِهَ، مَا لَمْ يُؤْمَرْ بِمَعْصِيَةٍ، فَإِذَا أُمِرَ بِمَعْصِيَةٍ فَلاَ سَمْعَ وَلاَ طَاعَةَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "A Muslim has to listen to and obey (the order of his ruler) whether he likes it or not, as long as his orders involve not one in disobedience (to Allah), but if an act of disobedience (to Allah) is imposed one should not listen to it or obey it. (See Hadith No. 203, Vol)

Abdullah bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Musalmaan ko apne hukmraan ki (hukm) sunni aur maanni chaahiye chaahe wo use pasand ho ya na ho, jab tak uske hukm mein Allah ki nafarmaani shaamil na ho, magar agar Allah ki nafarmaani ka koi hukm diya jaaye, to use na sunna chaahiye na maanna chaahiye.' (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees No. 203, Jild dekhein.)
