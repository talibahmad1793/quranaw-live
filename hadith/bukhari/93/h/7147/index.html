Sahih al-Bukhari 7147

Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، حَدَّثَنَا يُونُسُ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ سَمُرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ يَا عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنَ سَمُرَةَ، لاَ تَسْأَلِ الإِمَارَةَ، فَإِنْ أُعْطِيتَهَا عَنْ مَسْأَلَةٍ وُكِلْتَ إِلَيْهَا، وَإِنْ أُعْطِيتَهَا عَنْ غَيْرِ مَسْأَلَةٍ أُعِنْتَ عَلَيْهَا، وَإِذَا حَلَفْتَ عَلَى يَمِينٍ فَرَأَيْتَ غَيْرَهَا خَيْرًا مِنْهَا، فَأْتِ الَّذِي هُوَ خَيْرٌ، وَكَفِّرْ عَنْ يَمِينِكَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur-Rahman bin Samura:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "O `Abdur-Rahman bin Samura! Do not seek to be a ruler, for if you are given authority on your demand, you will be held responsible for it, but if you are given it without asking for it, then you will be helped (by Allah) in it. If you ever take an oath to do something and later on you find that something else is better, then do what is better and make expiation for your oath

Abdur Rahman bin Samura bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Aye Abdur Rahman bin Samura! Hukmraan banne ki koshish mat karo, kyunke agar tumhein ye zimmedaari maang kar di jaaye, to tum iske liye zimmedaar thehraaye jaaoge, magar agar tumhein ye bina maange di jaaye, to tumhein isme (Allah ki taraf se) madad milegi. Agar tum kabhi kisi kaam ke liye qasam khaao aur baad mein tumhein pata chale ke koi doosri cheez behtar hai, to jo behtar hai wo karo aur apni qasam ka kaffara adaa karo.'
