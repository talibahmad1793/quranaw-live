Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 60

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي عُبَادَةُ بْنُ الْوَلِيدِ، أَخْبَرَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ عُبَادَةَ بْنِ الصَّامِتِ، قَالَ "بَايَعْنَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى السَّمْعِ وَالطَّاعَةِ فِي الْمَنْشَطِ وَالْمَكْرَهِ‏.‏ ‏‏وَأَنْ لاَ نُنَازِعَ الأَمْرَ أَهْلَهُ، وَأَنْ نَقُومَ ـ أَوْ نَقُولَ ـ بِالْحَقِّ حَيْثُمَا كُنَّا لاَ نَخَافُ فِي اللَّهِ لَوْمَةَ لاَئِمٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated 'Ubada bin As-Samit:We gave the oath of allegiance to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) that we would listen to and obey him both at the time when we were active and at the time when we were tired and that we would not fight against the ruler or disobey him, and would stand firm for the truth or say the truth wherever we might be, and in the Way of Allah we would not be afraid of the blame of the blamers. (See Hadith No. 178 and)

Ubada bin As-Samit bataate hain: Humne Allah ke Rasool (SAW) se baiyat li ke hum unhein sunenge aur maanenge, chaahe hum betaab hon ya thake hue, aur hum hukmraan ke khilaaf nahi ladhenge ya uski nafarmaani nahi karenge, aur hum sach ke liye mazbooti se khade rahenge ya sach kahenge jahaan bhi hum hon, aur Allah ki raah mein hum aalochana karne walon ki aalochana se nahi darenge. (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees No. 178 dekhein.)
