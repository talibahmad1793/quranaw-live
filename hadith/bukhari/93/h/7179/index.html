Sahih al-Bukhari 7179

Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 42

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي حَبِيبٍ، عَنْ عِرَاكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ إِنْ شَرَّ النَّاسِ ذُو الْوَجْهَيْنِ، الَّذِي يَأْتِي هَؤُلاَءِ بِوَجْهٍ وَهَؤُلاَءِ بِوَجْهٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "The worst of all mankind is the double-faced one, who comes to some people with one face and to others, with another face

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Sab insaanon mein sabse buraa dohra-chehra waala shaks hai, jo kuch logon ke paas ek chehra lekar aata hai aur doosron ke paas ek doosra chehra.'
