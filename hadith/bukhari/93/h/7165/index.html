Sahih al-Bukhari 7165

Book 93: Judgments (Ahkaam) · Hadith 29

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ الزُّهْرِيُّ عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، قَالَ شَهِدْتُ الْمُتَلاَعِنَيْنِ وَأَنَا ابْنُ خَمْسَ، عَشْرَةَ فُرِّقَ بَيْنَهُمَا‏.‏

Narrated Sahl bin Sa`d:I witnessed a husband and a wife who were involved in a case of Lian. Then (the judgment of) divorce was passed. I was fifteen years of age, at that time

Sahl bin Sa'd bataate hain: Maine ek shohar aur biwi ko Li'an ke ek maamle mein shaamil dekha. Phir (talaaq ka) faisla diya gaya. Main us waqt pandrah saal ka tha.
