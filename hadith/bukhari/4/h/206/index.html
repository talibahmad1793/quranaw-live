Sahih al-Bukhari 206

Book 4: Ablutions (Wudu') · Hadith 72

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا زَكَرِيَّاءُ، عَنْ عَامِرٍ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ كُنْتُ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي سَفَرٍ، فَأَهْوَيْتُ لأَنْزِعَ خُفَّيْهِ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ دَعْهُمَا، فَإِنِّي أَدْخَلْتُهُمَا طَاهِرَتَيْنِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَمَسَحَ عَلَيْهِمَا‏.‏

Narrated `Urwa bin Al-Mughira:My father said, "Once I was in the company of the Prophet (ﷺ) on a journey and I dashed to take off his Khuffs (socks made from thick fabric or leather). He ordered me to leave them as he had put them after performing ablution. So he passed wet hands over them

Urwa bin Al-Mughira bataate hain, apne waalid ke hawale se: "Main ek safar mein Nabi ke saath tha, main foran unke Khuff utaarne laga. Aap ne mujhe rukne ka hukum diya kyunke unhone wudu ke baad hi Khuff pehne the. Aap ne un par haath phera."
