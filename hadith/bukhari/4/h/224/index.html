Sahih al-Bukhari 224

Book 4: Ablutions (Wudu') · Hadith 90

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ، قَالَ أَتَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم سُبَاطَةَ قَوْمٍ فَبَالَ قَائِمًا، ثُمَّ دَعَا بِمَاءٍ، فَجِئْتُهُ بِمَاءٍ فَتَوَضَّأَ‏.‏

Narrated Hudhaifa:Once the Prophet (ﷺ) went to the dumps of some people and passed urine while standing. He then asked for water and so I brought it to him and he performed ablution

Hudhaifa (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain ke Nabi kuch logon ke kachre ke dher ke paas gaye aur khade hokar peshab kiya. Phir paani mangwaya, main laaya aur Aap ne wudu kiya.
