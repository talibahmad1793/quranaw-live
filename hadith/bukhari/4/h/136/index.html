Sahih al-Bukhari 136

Book 4: Ablutions (Wudu') · Hadith 2

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ بُكَيْرٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ أَبِي هِلاَلٍ، عَنْ نُعَيْمٍ الْمُجْمِرِ، قَالَ رَقِيتُ مَعَ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ عَلَى ظَهْرِ الْمَسْجِدِ، فَتَوَضَّأَ فَقَالَ إِنِّي سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ إِنَّ أُمَّتِي يُدْعَوْنَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ غُرًّا مُحَجَّلِينَ مِنْ آثَارِ الْوُضُوءِ، فَمَنِ اسْتَطَاعَ مِنْكُمْ أَنْ يُطِيلَ غُرَّتَهُ فَلْيَفْعَلْ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Nu`am Al-Mujmir:Once I went up the roof of the mosque, along with Abu Huraira. He performed ablution and said, "I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) saying, "On the Day of Resurrection, my followers will be called "Al-Ghurr-ul- Muhajjalun" from the trace of ablution and whoever can increase the area of his radiance should do so (i.e. by performing ablution regularly)

Nu'am Al-Mujmir bataate hain ke main Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) ke saath masjid ki chhat par gaya. Unhone wudu kiya aur kaha: "Maine Nabi (ﷺ) ko farmate suna: 'Qiyamat ke din meri ummat ko wudu ke asraat ki wajah se Al-Ghurr-ul-Muhajjalun (chamakte hue chehre-haath-paon wale) kaha jayega, jo apni chamak barhaana chahe, zyada wudu kare.'"
