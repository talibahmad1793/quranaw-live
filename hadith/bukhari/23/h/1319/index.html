Sahih al-Bukhari 1319

Book 23: Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz) · Hadith 76

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا الشَّيْبَانِيُّ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي مَنْ، شَهِدَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ أَتَى عَلَى قَبْرٍ مَنْبُوذٍ فَصَفَّهُمْ وَكَبَّرَ أَرْبَعًا‏.‏ قُلْتُ مَنْ حَدَّثَكَ قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ رضى الله عنهما‏.‏

Narrated Ash-Shaibani:Ash-Shu`bi said, "I was informed by a man who had seen the Prophet (ﷺ) going to a grave that was separate from the other graves and he aligned the people in rows and said four Takbir." I said, "O Abu `Amr! who narrated (that) to you"? He said, "Ibn `Abbas

Ash-Shaibani bataate hain: Ash-Shu'bi ne kaha: "Mujhe ek shaks ne bataya jisne Nabi ko dekha tha ek aisi qabar ki taraf jate huye jo baaki qabron se alag thi, aur unhone logon ki safein banayi aur chaar Takbirein kahin." Maine pucha: "Aye Abu Amr! Yeh aapko kisne bataya?" Unhone kaha: "Ibn Abbas."
