Sahih al-Bukhari 1270

Book 23: Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz) · Hadith 31

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكُ بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، سَمِعَ جَابِرًا ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ أَتَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ أُبَىٍّ بَعْدَ مَا دُفِنَ فَأَخْرَجَهُ، فَنَفَثَ فِيهِ مِنْ رِيقِهِ وَأَلْبَسَهُ قَمِيصَهُ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir:The Prophet (ﷺ) came to (the grave of) `Abdullah bin Ubai after his body was buried. The body was brought out and then the Prophet (ﷺ) put his saliva over the body and clothed it in his shirt

Jabir (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi Abdullah bin Ubai ki (qabar par) uski dafn ke baad gaye. Jism ko bahar nikala gaya aur Nabi ne apna thook uspar lagaya aur use apni qamees pehnayi.
