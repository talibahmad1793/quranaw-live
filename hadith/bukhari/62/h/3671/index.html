Sahih al-Bukhari 3671

Book 62: Companions of the Prophet · Hadith 21

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا جَامِعُ بْنُ أَبِي رَاشِدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو يَعْلَى، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ ابْنِ الْحَنَفِيَّةِ، قَالَ قُلْتُ لأَبِي أَىُّ النَّاسِ خَيْرٌ بَعْدَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ‏.‏ قُلْتُ ثُمَّ مَنْ قَالَ ثُمَّ عُمَرُ‏.‏ وَخَشِيتُ أَنْ يَقُولَ عُثْمَانُ قُلْتُ ثُمَّ أَنْتَ قَالَ مَا أَنَا إِلاَّ رَجُلٌ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ‏.‏

Narrated Muhammad bin Al-Hanafiya:I asked my father (`Ali bin Abi Talib), "Who are the best people after Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) ?" He said, "Abu Bakr." I asked, "Who then?" He said, "Then `Umar. " I was afraid he would say "Uthman, so I said, "Then you?" He said, "I am only an ordinary person

Muhammad bin Al-Hanafiya bataate hain: Maine apne waalid (Ali bin Abi Talib) se pucha, "Allah ke Rasool ke baad sab logon mein sabse behtar kaun hai?" Unhone kaha, "Abu Bakr." Maine pucha, "Phir kaun?" Unhone kaha, "Phir Umar." Mujhe dar tha woh Uthman kahenge, isliye maine kaha, "Phir aap?" Unhone kaha, "Main sirf ek aam shaks hoon."
