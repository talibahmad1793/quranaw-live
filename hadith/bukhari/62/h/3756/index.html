Sahih al-Bukhari 3756

Book 62: Companions of the Prophet · Hadith 101

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ،، قَالَ ضَمَّنِي النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلَى صَدْرِهِ وَقَالَ ‏ "‏ اللَّهُمَّ عَلِّمْهُ الْحِكْمَةَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Once the Prophet (ﷺ) embraced me (pressed me to his chest) and said, "O Allah, teach him wisdom (i.e. the understanding of the knowledge of Qur'an)

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Ek baar Nabi ne mujhe gale lagaya (apne seene se lagaya) aur kaha, "Aye Allah, ise hikmat sikha (yaani Quran ke gyan ki samajh)."
