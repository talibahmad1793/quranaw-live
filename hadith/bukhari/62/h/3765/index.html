Sahih al-Bukhari 3765

Book 62: Companions of the Prophet · Hadith 110

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي مَرْيَمَ، حَدَّثَنَا نَافِعُ بْنُ عُمَرَ، حَدَّثَنِي ابْنُ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ، قِيلَ لاِبْنِ عَبَّاسٍ هَلْ لَكَ فِي أَمِيرِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ مُعَاوِيَةَ، فَإِنَّهُ مَا أَوْتَرَ إِلاَّ بِوَاحِدَةٍ‏.‏ قَالَ إِنَّهُ فَقِيهٌ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn Abi Mulaika:Somebody said to Ibn `Abbas, "Can you speak to the chief of the believers Mu`awiyah, as he does not pray except one rak`a as witr?" Ibn `Abbas replied, "He is a Faqih (i.e. a learned man who can give religious verdicts)

Ibn Abu Mulaika bataate hain: Kisi ne Ibn Abbas se kaha, "Kya aap mominon ke Ameer Muawiya se baat kar sakte hain, kyunke woh sirf ek rakat Witr padhte hain?" Ibn Abbas ne jawab diya, "Woh ek Faqih hain (yaani ek gyaanvan shaks jo deeni faisle de sakte hain)."
