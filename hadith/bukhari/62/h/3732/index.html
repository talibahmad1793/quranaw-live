Sahih al-Bukhari 3732

Book 62: Companions of the Prophet · Hadith 79

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا لَيْثٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، رضى الله عنها أَنَّ قُرَيْشًا، أَهَمَّهُمْ شَأْنُ الْمَخْزُومِيَّةِ، فَقَالُوا مَنْ يَجْتَرِئُ عَلَيْهِ إِلاَّ أُسَامَةُ بْنُ زَيْدٍ، حِبُّ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏

Narrated 'Aisha: The people of the Quraish tribe were worried about the Makhzumiya woman. They said. "Nobody dare speak to him (i.e. the Prophet (ﷺ) ) except Usama bin Zaid as he is the most beloved to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)

Ayesha bataati hain: Quraish qabile ke log Makhzumiya aurat ke maamle se pareshaan the. Unhone kaha, "Koi bhi unse (yaani Nabi se) baat karne ki himmat nahi karega siwaye Usama bin Zaid ke, kyunke woh Allah ke Rasool ko sabse pyare hain."
