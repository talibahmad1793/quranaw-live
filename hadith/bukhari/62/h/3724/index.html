Sahih al-Bukhari 3724

Book 62: Companions of the Prophet · Hadith 71

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي خَالِدٍ، عَنْ قَيْسِ بْنِ أَبِي حَازِمٍ،، قَالَ رَأَيْتُ يَدَ طَلْحَةَ الَّتِي وَقَى بِهَا النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَدْ شَلَّتْ‏.‏

Narrated Qais bin Abi Hazim:I saw Talha's paralyzed hand with which he had protected the Prophet (from an arrow)

Qais bin Abi Hazim bataate hain: Maine Talha ke lakve (yaani padaghaat) hue haath ko dekha jisse unhone Nabi ki (ek teer se) hifaazat ki thi.
