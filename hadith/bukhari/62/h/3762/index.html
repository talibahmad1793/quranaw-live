Sahih al-Bukhari 3762

Book 62: Companions of the Prophet · Hadith 107

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ،، قَالَ سَأَلْنَا حُذَيْفَةَ عَنْ رَجُلٍ، قَرِيبِ السَّمْتِ وَالْهَدْىِ مِنَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى نَأْخُذَ عَنْهُ فَقَالَ مَا أَعْرِفُ أَحَدًا أَقْرَبَ سَمْتًا وَهَدْيًا وَدَلاًّ بِالنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنِ ابْنِ أُمِّ عَبْدٍ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur-Rahman bin Yazid:We asked Hudhaifa to tell us of a person resembling (to some extent) the Prophet (ﷺ) in good appearance and straight forward behavior so that we may learn from him (good manners and acceptable conduct). Hudhaifa replied, "I do not know anybody resembling the Prophet (to some extent) in appearance and conduct more than Ibn Um `Abd

Abdur-Rahman bin Yazid bataate hain: Humne Hudhaifa se kaha ke woh humein aise kisi shaks ke baare mein bataen jo (kuch had tak) Nabi jaise achhe roop aur seedhi soch mein milte-julte hon, taake hum unse (achhe adab aur qubool karne laayak vyavhaar) seekh saken. Hudhaifa ne jawab diya, "Mujhe koi bhi shaks Nabi jaise roop aur vyavhaar mein Ibn Um Abd se zyada milta-julta nahi lagta."
