Sahih al-Bukhari 6717

Book 84: Expiation for Unfulfilled Oaths · Hadith 10

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنِ الأَسْوَدِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّهَا أَرَادَتْ أَنْ تَشْتَرِيَ، بَرِيرَةَ فَاشْتَرَطُوا عَلَيْهَا الْوَلاَءَ، فَذَكَرَتْ ذَلِكَ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ اشْتَرِيهَا إِنَّمَا الْوَلاَءُ لِمَنْ أَعْتَقَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:that she intended to buy Barira (a slave girl) and her masters stipulated that they would have her Wala'. When `Aisha mentioned that to the Prophet (ﷺ) ; he said, "Buy her, for the Wala' is for the one who manumits

Aisha bataati hain ke unhone Barira (ek launDi) ko khareedne ka iraada kiya aur uske maalikon ne shart rakhi ke Wala' (azaad kiye jaane ke rishte ka haq) unke paas rahega. Jab Aisha ne ye baat Nabi (SAW) ko bataayi, unhone kaha, 'Use khareed lo, kyunke Wala' us shaks ke liye hai jo azaad karta hai.'
