Sahih al-Bukhari 2245

Book 35: Sales in which a Price is paid for Goods to be Delivered Later (As-Salam) · Hadith 8

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ، حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنِ الشَّيْبَانِيِّ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ أَبِي مُجَالِدٍ، بِهَذَا وَقَالَ فَنُسْلِفُهُمْ فِي الْحِنْطَةِ وَالشَّعِيرِ‏.‏ وَقَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ الْوَلِيدِ عَنْ سُفْيَانَ حَدَّثَنَا الشَّيْبَانِيُّ وَقَالَ وَالزَّيْتِ‏.‏ حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ عَنِ الشَّيْبَانِيِّ وَقَالَ فِي الْحِنْطَةِ وَالشَّعِيرِ وَالزَّبِيبِ‏.‏

Narrated Muhammad bin Abi Al-Mujalid:as above (446) and said, "We used to pay them in advance for wheat and barley (to be delivered later). Narrated Ash-Shaibani--"And also for oil." Narrated Ash-Shaibani: who said "We used to pay in advance for wheat barley and dried grapes

Muhammad bin Abi Al-Mujalid bataate hain: (yehi upar wali riwayat) aur kaha, "Hum unhe gehun aur jau ki qeemat pehle dete the (baad mein delivery ke liye)." Ash-Shaibani ke hawale se: "Aur tel ke liye bhi." Ash-Shaibani ke hawale se: "Hum gehun, jau aur sookhi angoor ki qeemat pehle dete the."
