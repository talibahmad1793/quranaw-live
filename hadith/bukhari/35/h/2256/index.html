Book 35: Sales in which a Price is paid for Goods to be Delivered Later (As-Salam) · Hadith 16

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، أَخْبَرَنَا جُوَيْرِيَةُ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كَانُوا يَتَبَايَعُونَ الْجَزُورَ إِلَى حَبَلِ الْحَبَلَةِ، فَنَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْهُ‏.‏ فَسَّرَهُ نَافِعٌ أَنْ تُنْتَجَ النَّاقَةُ مَا فِي بَطْنِهَا‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:The people used to sell camels on the basis of Habal-al-Habala. The Prophet (ﷺ) forbade such sale. Nafi` explained Habal-al-Habala by saying. "The camel is to be delivered to the buyer after the she-camel gives birth

Abdullah (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Log Habal-al-Habala ki bunyaad par oont bechte the. Nabi ne aisi bikri se mana kiya. Nafi ne Habal-al-Habala ka matlab bataya: "Yeh matlab hai ke oont, oontni ke bachche janne ke baad khareedar ko diya jaayega."
