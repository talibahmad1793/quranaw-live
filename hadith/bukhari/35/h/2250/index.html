Book 35: Sales in which a Price is paid for Goods to be Delivered Later (As-Salam) · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ أَبِي الْبَخْتَرِيِّ، سَأَلْتُ ابْنَ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ عَنِ السَّلَمِ، فِي النَّخْلِ فَقَالَ نَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ بَيْعِ الثَّمَرِ حَتَّى يَصْلُحَ، وَنَهَى عَنِ الْوَرِقِ بِالذَّهَبِ نَسَاءً بِنَاجِزٍ‏.‏ وَسَأَلْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ فَقَالَ نَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ بَيْعِ النَّخْلِ حَتَّى يَأْكُلَ أَوْ يُؤْكَلَ، وَحَتَّى يُوزَنَ‏.‏ قُلْتُ وَمَا يُوزَنُ قَالَ رَجُلٌ عِنْدَهُ حَتَّى يُحْرَزَ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Al-Bakhtari:I asked Ibn `Umar about Salam for dates. Ibn `Umar replied, "The Prophet (ﷺ) forbade the sale (of the fruits) of date-palms until they were fit for eating and also forbade the sale of silver for gold on credit." I also asked Ibn `Abbas about it. Ibn `Abbas replied, "The Prophet (ﷺ) forbade the sale of dates till they were fit for eating, and could be weighed." I asked him, "What is to be weighed (as the dates are on the trees)?" A man sitting by Ibn `Abbas said, "It means till they are cut and stored

Abu Al-Bakhtari bataate hain: Maine Ibn Umar se khajoor ke Salam ke baare mein pucha. Ibn Umar ne jawab diya: "Nabi ne khajoor (phalon) ko bechne se mana kiya jab tak woh khane ke laayak na ho jaaye, aur chandi ko sone ke badle udhaar par bechne se bhi mana kiya." Maine Ibn Abbas se bhi iske baare mein pucha. Ibn Abbas ne jawab diya: "Nabi ne khajoor bechne se mana kiya jab tak woh khane ke laayak na ho aur unka wazan na kiya ja sake." Maine unse pucha: "Wazan kaise ho sakta hai (jabke woh darakhton par hai)?" Ibn Abbas ke bagal mein baithe ek shaks ne kaha: "Iska matlab hai jab tak woh kaati aur jama na ho jaaye."
