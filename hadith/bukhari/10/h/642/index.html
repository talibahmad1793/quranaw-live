Sahih al-Bukhari 642

Book 10: Call to Prayers (Adhaan) · Hadith 39

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَمْرٍو، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ صُهَيْبٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ أُقِيمَتِ الصَّلاَةُ وَالنَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُنَاجِي رَجُلاً فِي جَانِبِ الْمَسْجِدِ، فَمَا قَامَ إِلَى الصَّلاَةِ حَتَّى نَامَ الْقَوْمُ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:Once the Iqama was pronounced and the Prophet (ﷺ) was talking to a man (in a low voice) in a corner of the mosque and he did not lead the prayer till (some of) the people had slept (dozed in a sitting posture)

Anas (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai ke ek baar Iqama ho chuki thi aur Nabi masjid ke ek kone mein kisi shaks se (dheemi awaaz mein) baat kar rahe the, Aap ne imamat tab tak nahi ki jab tak (kuch) log baithe-baithe (thodi der) so nahi gaye.
