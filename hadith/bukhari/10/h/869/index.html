Sahih al-Bukhari 869

Book 10: Call to Prayers (Adhaan) · Hadith 260

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ عَمْرَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتْ لَوْ أَدْرَكَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَا أَحْدَثَ النِّسَاءُ لَمَنَعَهُنَّ كَمَا مُنِعَتْ نِسَاءُ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ‏.‏ قُلْتُ لِعَمْرَةَ أَوَ مُنِعْنَ قَالَتْ نَعَمْ‏.‏

Narrated `Aisha:Had Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) known what the women were doing, he would have forbidden them from going to the mosque as the women of Bani Israel had been forbidden. Yahya bin Sa`id (a sub-narrator) asked `Amra (another sub-narrator), "Were the women of Bani Israel forbidden?" She replied "Yes

Ayesha (رضی اللہ عنہا) se riwayat hai: "Agar Allah ke Rasool ko pata hota ke aurtein (baad mein) kya karengi, to Aap unhe masjid jaane se mana karte, jaise Bani Israel ki aurton ko mana kiya gaya tha." Yahya bin Saeed ne Amra se pucha: "Kya Bani Israel ki aurton ko mana kiya gaya tha?" Unhone kaha: "Haan."
