Sahih al-Bukhari 848

Book 10: Call to Prayers (Adhaan) · Hadith 240

Hadith text

وَقَالَ لَنَا آدَمُ حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، قَالَ كَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ يُصَلِّي فِي مَكَانِهِ الَّذِي صَلَّى فِيهِ الْفَرِيضَةَ‏.‏ وَفَعَلَهُ الْقَاسِمُ‏.‏ وَيُذْكَرُ عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَفَعَهُ لاَ يَتَطَوَّعُ الإِمَامُ فِي مَكَانِهِ‏.‏ وَلَمْ يَصِحَّ‏.‏

Narrated Nafi:Ibn Umar used to offer prayers (Nawafil) at the place where he had offered the compulsory prayer. Al-Qasim (bin Muhammad bin Abi Bakr) did the same. The narration coming from Abu Hurairah (from the Prophet (ﷺ)) forbidding the Imam from offering prayers (optional prayer) at the same place where he was offered the compulsory prayer is incorrect

Nafi bataate hain ke Ibn Umar farz namaz padhne ki usi jagah nafil namaz bhi padhte the. Al-Qasim (bin Muhammad bin Abi Bakr) bhi aisa hi karte the. Abu Huraira se (Nabi ke hawale se) yeh riwayat ki gayi hai ke Imam usi jagah nafil na padhe jahan usne farz padhi ho, magar yeh riwayat sahih nahi hai.
