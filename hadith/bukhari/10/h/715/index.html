Sahih al-Bukhari 715

Book 10: Call to Prayers (Adhaan) · Hadith 110

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْوَلِيدِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ سَعْدِ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ صَلَّى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم الظُّهْرَ رَكْعَتَيْنِ، فَقِيلَ صَلَّيْتَ رَكْعَتَيْنِ‏.‏ فَصَلَّى رَكْعَتَيْنِ، ثُمَّ سَلَّمَ ثُمَّ سَجَدَ سَجْدَتَيْنِ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) prayed two rak`at of Zuhr prayer (instead of four) and he was told that he had prayed two rak`at only. Then he prayed two more rak`at and finished them with the Taslim followed by two prostrations

Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai ke Nabi ne Zuhr mein do rakat (chaar ke bajaye) padhi, unhe bataya gaya ke Aap ne sirf do rakat padhi hain. Phir Aap ne do aur rakat padhi aur Taslim se khatam karke do sajde kiye.
