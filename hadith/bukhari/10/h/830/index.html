Sahih al-Bukhari 830

Book 10: Call to Prayers (Adhaan) · Hadith 224

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا بَكْرٌ، عَنْ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ رَبِيعَةَ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ابْنِ بُحَيْنَةَ، قَالَ صَلَّى بِنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم الظُّهْرَ فَقَامَ وَعَلَيْهِ جُلُوسٌ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ فِي آخِرِ صَلاَتِهِ سَجَدَ سَجْدَتَيْنِ وَهْوَ جَالِسٌ‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin Malik bin Buhaina:Once Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) led us in the Zuhr prayer and got up (after the prostrations of the second rak`a) although he should have sat (for the Tashahhud). So at the end of the prayer, he prostrated twice while sitting (prostrations of Sahu)

Abdullah bin Malik bin Buhaina (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai ke ek baar Allah ke Rasool ne Zuhr ki imamat ki aur (doosri rakat ke sajdon ke baad) khade ho gaye jabke unhe (Tashahhud ke liye) baithna chahiye tha. Isliye namaz ke aakhir mein, Aap ne baithe-baithe do sajde kiye (Sahu ke sajde).
