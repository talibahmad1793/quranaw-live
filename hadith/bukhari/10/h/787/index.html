Sahih al-Bukhari 787

Book 10: Call to Prayers (Adhaan) · Hadith 182

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَوْنٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا هُشَيْمٌ، عَنْ أَبِي بِشْرٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، قَالَ رَأَيْتُ رَجُلاً عِنْدَ الْمَقَامِ يُكَبِّرُ فِي كُلِّ خَفْضٍ وَرَفْعٍ وَإِذَا قَامَ وَإِذَا وَضَعَ، فَأَخْبَرْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ أَوَلَيْسَ تِلْكَ صَلاَةَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم لاَ أُمَّ لَكَ‏.‏

Narrated `Ikrima:I saw a person praying at Muqam-Ibrahim (the place of Abraham by the Ka`ba) and he was saying Takbir on every bowing, rising, standing and sitting. I asked Ibn `Abbas (about this prayer). He admonished me saying: "Isn't that the prayer of the Prophet?

Ikrima bataate hain: "Maine ek shaks ko Maqam-e-Ibrahim par namaz padhte dekha, woh har ruku, uthne, khade hone aur baithne par Takbir kehta tha. Maine Ibn Abbas se (iske baare mein) pucha. Unhone naseehat karte huye kaha: 'Kya yeh Nabi ki namaz nahi hai?'"
