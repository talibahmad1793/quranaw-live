Sahih al-Bukhari 1175

Book 19: Prayer at Night (Tahajjud) · Hadith 53

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، عَنْ تَوْبَةَ، عَنْ مُوَرِّقٍ، قَالَ قُلْتُ لاِبْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَتُصَلِّي الضُّحَى قَالَ لاَ‏.‏ قُلْتُ فَعُمَرُ‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ‏.‏ قُلْتُ فَأَبُو بَكْرٍ‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ‏.‏ قُلْتُ فَالنَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ لاَ إِخَالُهُ‏.‏

Narrated Muwarriq:I asked Ibn `Umar "Do you offer the Duha prayer?" He replied in the negative. I further asked, "Did `Umar use to pray it?" He (Ibn `Umar) replied in the negative. I again asked, "Did Abu Bakr use to pray it?" He replied in the negative. I again asked, "Did the Prophet (ﷺ) use to pray it?" Ibn `Umar replied, "I don't think he did

Muwarriq bataate hain: Maine Ibn Umar se pucha: "Kya aap Duha ki namaz padhte hain?" Unhone na mein jawab diya. Maine phir pucha: "Kya Umar ise padhte the?" Unhone na kaha. Maine phir pucha: "Kya Abu Bakr ise padhte the?" Unhone na kaha. Maine phir pucha: "Kya Nabi ise padhte the?" Ibn Umar ne jawab diya: "Mujhe nahi lagta unhone padhi."
