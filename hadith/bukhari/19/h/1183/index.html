Sahih al-Bukhari 1183

Book 19: Prayer at Night (Tahajjud) · Hadith 60

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، عَنِ الْحُسَيْنِ، عَنِ ابْنِ بُرَيْدَةَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ الْمُزَنِيُّ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ صَلُّوا قَبْلَ صَلاَةِ الْمَغْرِبِ ‏"‏‏.‏ ـ قَالَ فِي الثَّالِثَةِ ـ لِمَنْ شَاءَ كَرَاهِيَةَ أَنْ يَتَّخِذَهَا النَّاسُ سُنَّةً‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah Al-Muzani:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Pray before the Maghrib (compulsory) prayer." He (said it thrice) and in the third time, he said, "Whoever wants to offer it can do so." He said so because he did not like the people to take it as a tradition

Abdullah Al-Muzani bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "Maghrib (ki farz namaz) se pehle (nafil) padho." (Aap ne yeh teen baar kaha) aur teesri baar farmaya: "Jo padhna chahe, padh sakta hai." Aap ne yeh isliye kaha kyunke Aap nahi chahte the log ise ek pakki riwaayat samajh len.
