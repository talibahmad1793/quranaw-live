Sahih al-Bukhari 1125

Book 19: Prayer at Night (Tahajjud) · Hadith 5

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ الأَسْوَدِ بْنِ قَيْسٍ، عَنْ جُنْدَبِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ احْتَبَسَ جِبْرِيلُ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَتِ امْرَأَةٌ مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ أَبْطَأَ عَلَيْهِ شَيْطَانُهُ‏.‏ فَنَزَلَتْ ‏{‏وَالضُّحَى * وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا سَجَى * مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَى‏}‏

Narrated Jundab bin `Abdullah:Gabriel did not come to the Prophet (for some time) and so one of the Quraish women said, "His Satan has deserted him." So came the Divine Revelation: "By the forenoon And by the night When it is still! Your Lord (O Muhammad) has neither Forsaken you Nor hated you

Jundab bin Abdullah (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Jibreel Nabi ke paas (kuch waqt tak) nahi aaye, isliye Quraish ki ek aurat ne kaha: "Unke shaitan ne unhe chhod diya hai." Isi waqt yeh wahi nazil hui: "Chasht ki qasam, aur raat ki jab woh sukoon paaye! Tumhare Rab (Aye Muhammad) ne na tumhe chhoda hai na tumse naraz hue hain."
