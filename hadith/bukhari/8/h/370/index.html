Sahih al-Bukhari 370

Book 8: Prayers (Salat) · Hadith 22

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي ابْنُ أَبِي الْمَوَالِي، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ الْمُنْكَدِرِ، قَالَ دَخَلْتُ عَلَى جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ وَهُوَ يُصَلِّي فِي ثَوْبٍ مُلْتَحِفًا بِهِ وَرِدَاؤُهُ مَوْضُوعٌ، فَلَمَّا انْصَرَفَ قُلْنَا يَا أَبَا عَبْدِ اللَّهِ تُصَلِّي وَرِدَاؤُكَ مَوْضُوعٌ قَالَ نَعَمْ، أَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ يَرَانِي الْجُهَّالُ مِثْلُكُمْ، رَأَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُصَلِّي هَكَذَا‏.‏

Narrated Muhammad bin Al-Munkadir:I went to Jabir bin `Abdullah and he was praying wrapped in a garment and his Rida was Lying beside him. When he finished the prayers, I said "O `Abdullah! You pray (in a single garment) while your Rida' is lying beside you." He replied, "Yes, I did it intentionally so that the ignorant ones like you might see me. I saw the Prophet (ﷺ) praying like this

Muhammad bin Al-Munkadir bataate hain ke main Jabir bin Abdullah ke paas gaya, woh ek kapde mein lipat kar namaz padh rahe the jabke unka Rida bagal mein pada tha. Namaz khatam hone par maine kaha: "Aye Abdullah! Aap ek kapde mein namaz padhte hain jabke Rida bagal mein hai." Unhone jawab diya: "Haan, maine jaan-boojh kar kiya taake tumhare jaise nadaan mujhe (aise) dekh sakein. Maine Nabi ko isi tarah namaz padhte dekha."
