Sahih al-Bukhari 392

Book 8: Prayers (Salat) · Hadith 44

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا نُعَيْمٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ الْمُبَارَكِ، عَنْ حُمَيْدٍ الطَّوِيلِ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أُمِرْتُ أَنْ أُقَاتِلَ النَّاسَ حَتَّى يَقُولُوا لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ‏.‏ فَإِذَا قَالُوهَا وَصَلَّوْا صَلاَتَنَا، وَاسْتَقْبَلُوا قِبْلَتَنَا، وَذَبَحُوا ذَبِيحَتَنَا، فَقَدْ حَرُمَتْ عَلَيْنَا دِمَاؤُهُمْ وَأَمْوَالُهُمْ إِلاَّ بِحَقِّهَا، وَحِسَابُهُمْ عَلَى اللَّهِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas bin Malik:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "I have been ordered to fight the people till they say: 'None has the right to be worshipped but Allah.' And if they say so, pray like our prayers, face our Qibla and slaughter as we slaughter, then their blood and property will be sacred to us and we will not interfere with them except legally and their reckoning will be with Allah

Anas bin Malik (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai ke Allah ke Rasool ne farmaya: "Mujhe hukum diya gaya hai ke logon se jung karoon jab tak woh yeh na kahen: 'Allah ke siwa koi mabood nahi.' Agar woh yeh kahen, humari tarah namaz padhen, humara Qibla apnayen, aur humari tarah zabah karen, to unka khoon aur maal humare liye mehfooz ho jayega, siwaye haqq ke, aur unka hisaab Allah ke zimme hai."
