Sahih al-Bukhari 449

Book 8: Prayers (Salat) · Hadith 98

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا خَلاَّدٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَاحِدِ بْنُ أَيْمَنَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَابِرٍ، أَنَّ امْرَأَةً، قَالَتْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَلاَ أَجْعَلُ لَكَ شَيْئًا تَقْعُدُ عَلَيْهِ، فَإِنَّ لِي غُلاَمًا نَجَّارًا قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِنْ شِئْتِ ‏"‏‏.‏ فَعَمِلَتِ الْمِنْبَرَ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir:A woman said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Shall I get something constructed for you to sit on as I have a slave who is a carpenter?" He replied, "Yes, if you like." So she had that pulpit constructed

Jabir (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain ke ek aurat ne kaha: "Ya Rasoolallah! Kya main aapke baithne ke liye kuch banwa dun, mere paas ek badhai gulam hai?" Aap ne jawab diya: "Haan, agar tum chaho." To usne woh mimbar banwaya.
