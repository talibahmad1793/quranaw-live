Sahih al-Bukhari 499

Book 8: Prayers (Salat) · Hadith 146

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَوْنُ بْنُ أَبِي جُحَيْفَةَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَبِي قَالَ، خَرَجَ عَلَيْنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالْهَاجِرَةِ، فَأُتِيَ بِوَضُوءٍ فَتَوَضَّأَ فَصَلَّى بِنَا الظُّهْرَ وَالْعَصْرَ وَبَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ عَنَزَةٌ، وَالْمَرْأَةُ وَالْحِمَارُ يَمُرُّونَ مِنْ وَرَائِهَا‏.‏

Narrated `Aun bin Abi Juhaifa:that he had heard his father saying, "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) came to us at midday and water was brought for his ablution. He performed ablution and led us in Zuhr and `Asr prayers with a short spear (or stick) planted in front of him (as a Sutra), while women and donkeys were passing beyond it

Aun bin Abi Juhaifa, apne waalid ke hawale se: "Allah ke Rasool dopahar ko humare paas aaye, wudu ke liye paani laaya gaya. Aap ne wudu kiya aur humari Zuhr-Asr namaz ki imamat ki, saamne ek chhota neza gaada hua tha, jabke aurtein aur gadhe uske peechhe se guzarte the."
