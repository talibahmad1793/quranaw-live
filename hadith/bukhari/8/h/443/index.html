Sahih al-Bukhari 443

Book 8: Prayers (Salat) · Hadith 92

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا خَلاَّدُ بْنُ يَحْيَى، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا مِسْعَرٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا مُحَارِبُ بْنُ دِثَارٍ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ أَتَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ ـ قَالَ مِسْعَرٌ أُرَاهُ قَالَ ضُحًى ـ فَقَالَ ‏ "‏ صَلِّ رَكْعَتَيْنِ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَكَانَ لِي عَلَيْهِ دَيْنٌ فَقَضَانِي وَزَادَنِي‏.‏

Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:I went to the Prophet (ﷺ) in the mosque (the sub-narrator Mis`ar thought that Jabir had said, "In the forenoon.") He ordered me to pray two rak`at. He owed me some money and he repaid it to me and gave more than what was due to me

Jabir bin Abdullah (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: "Main masjid mein Nabi ke paas gaya (Mis'ar ko lagta hai Jabir ne 'subah' kaha), Aap ne mujhe do rakat padhne ka hukum diya. Unpar mera kuch qarz tha, unhone woh ada kiya aur mujhe jitna haqq tha usse zyada diya."
