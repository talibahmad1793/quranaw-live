Sahih al-Bukhari 1228.2

Book 22: Forgetfulness in Prayer · Hadith 6

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادٌ، عَنْ سَلَمَةَ بْنِ عَلْقَمَةَ، قَالَ قُلْتُ لِمُحَمَّدٍ فِي سَجْدَتَىِ السَّهْوِ تَشَهُّدٌ قَالَ لَيْسَ فِي حَدِيثِ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ‏.‏

Narrated Salama bin 'Alqama:I asked Muhammad (bin Seereen) whether Tashah-hud should be recited after the two prostrations of Sahu. He replied, "It is not (mentioned) in Abu Huraira's narration

Salama bin Alqama bataate hain: Maine Muhammad (bin Seereen) se pucha kya do Sahu ke sajdon ke baad Tashahhud padhna chahiye. Unhone jawab diya: "Yeh Abu Huraira ki riwayat mein (zikar) nahi hai."
