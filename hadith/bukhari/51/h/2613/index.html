Sahih al-Bukhari 2613

Book 51: Gifts · Hadith 46

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ أَبُو جَعْفَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ فُضَيْلٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ أَتَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْتَ فَاطِمَةَ فَلَمْ يَدْخُلْ عَلَيْهَا، وَجَاءَ عَلِيٌّ فَذَكَرَتْ لَهُ ذَلِكَ فَذَكَرَهُ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ إِنِّي رَأَيْتُ عَلَى بَابِهَا سِتْرًا مَوْشِيًّا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ مَا لِي وَلِلدُّنْيَا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَأَتَاهَا عَلِيٌّ فَذَكَرَ ذَلِكَ لَهَا فَقَالَتْ لِيَأْمُرْنِي فِيهِ بِمَا شَاءَ‏.‏ قَالَ تُرْسِلُ بِهِ إِلَى فُلاَنٍ‏.‏ أَهْلِ بَيْتٍ بِهِمْ حَاجَةٌ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:Once the Prophet (ﷺ) went to the house of Fatima but did not enter it. `Ali came and she told him about that. When 'Ali asked the Prophet (ﷺ) about it, he said, "I saw a (multicolored) decorated curtain on her door. I am not interested in worldly things." `Ali went to Fatima and told her about it. Fatima said, "I am ready to dispense with it in the way he suggests." The Prophet (ﷺ) ordered her to send it to such-andsuch needy people

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Ek baar Nabi Fatima ke ghar gaye magar andar nahi gaye. Ali aaye aur usne unhe iske baare mein bataya. Jab Ali ne Nabi se iske baare mein pucha, unhone kaha: "Maine unke darwaze par ek (rangeen) sajaawati parda dekha. Mujhe duniyawi cheezon mein dilchaspi nahi." Ali Fatima ke paas gaye aur unhe iske baare mein bataya. Fatima ne kaha: "Main isse jaisi bhi unki khwahish ho, chhod dene ko taiyaar hoon." Nabi ne unhe hukum diya ke woh ise falaan-falaan zaroorat-mand logon ko bhej den.
