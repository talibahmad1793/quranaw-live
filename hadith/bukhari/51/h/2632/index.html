Sahih al-Bukhari 2632

Book 51: Gifts · Hadith 64

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، حَدَّثَنَا الأَوْزَاعِيُّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي عَطَاءٌ، عَنْ جَابِرٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كَانَتْ لِرِجَالٍ مِنَّا فُضُولُ أَرَضِينَ فَقَالُوا نُؤَاجِرُهَا بِالثُّلُثِ وَالرُّبُعِ وَالنِّصْفِ‏.‏ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ مَنْ كَانَتْ لَهُ أَرْضٌ فَلْيَزْرَعْهَا أَوْ لِيَمْنَحْهَا أَخَاهُ، فَإِنْ أَبَى فَلْيُمْسِكْ أَرْضَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Jabir:Some men had superfluous land and they said that they would give it to others to cultivate on the condition that they would get one-third or one-fourth or one half of its yield. The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Whoever has land should cultivate it himself or give it to his brother or keep it uncultivated

Jabir (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Kuch logon ke paas zyada zameen thi aur unhone kaha ke woh doosron ko zameen denge kheti karne ke liye is shart par ke unhe paidawaar ka ek-tihai, ek-chauthai, ya aadha milega. Nabi ne kaha: "Jiske paas zameen ho, use khud kheti karni chahiye ya apne bhai ko de deni chahiye, ya use un-kheti hui chhod dena chahiye."
