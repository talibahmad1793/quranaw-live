Sahih al-Bukhari 3839

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 64

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، قَالَ قُلْتُ لأَبِي أُسَامَةَ حَدَّثَكُمْ يَحْيَى بْنُ الْمُهَلَّبِ، حَدَّثَنَا حُصَيْنٌ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، ‏{‏وَكَأْسًا دِهَاقًا‏}‏ قَالَ مَلأَى مُتَتَابِعَةً‏.‏

Narrated Husain:That `Ikrima said, "Kasan Dihaqa means glass full (of something) followed successively with other full glasses

Husain bataate hain: Ikrima ne kaha, "Kasan Dihaqa ka matlab hai kaanch ka pyaala bhara hua (kisi cheez se), lagataar doosre bhare hue pyaalon ke saath."
