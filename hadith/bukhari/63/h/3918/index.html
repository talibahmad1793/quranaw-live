Sahih al-Bukhari 3918

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 143

Hadith text

قَالَ الْبَرَاءُ فَدَخَلْتُ مَعَ أَبِي بَكْرٍ عَلَى أَهْلِهِ، فَإِذَا عَائِشَةُ ابْنَتُهُ مُضْطَجِعَةٌ، قَدْ أَصَابَتْهَا حُمَّى، فَرَأَيْتُ أَبَاهَا فَقَبَّلَ خَدَّهَا، وَقَالَ كَيْفَ أَنْتِ يَا بُنَيَّةُ

Al-Bara added:I then went with Abu Bakr into his home (carrying that saddle) and there I saw his daughter `Aisha Lying in a bed because of heavy fever and I saw her father Abu Bakr kissing her cheek and saying, "How are you, little daughter?

Al-Bara ne joda: Phir main Abu Bakr ke saath unke ghar gaya (woh jin lekar), aur wahaan maine unki beti Ayesha ko tez bukhaar ki wajah se bistar mein lete dekha, aur maine unke waalid Abu Bakr ko unke gaal ko choomte aur kahte dekha, "Kaisi ho, meri chhoti beti?"
