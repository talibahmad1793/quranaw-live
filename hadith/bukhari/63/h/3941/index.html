Sahih al-Bukhari 3941

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 165

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، حَدَّثَنَا قُرَّةُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ لَوْ آمَنَ بِي عَشَرَةٌ مِنَ الْيَهُودِ لآمَنَ بِي الْيَهُودُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Had only ten Jews (amongst their chiefs) believed me, all the Jews would definitely have believed me

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Agar sirf das Yahudi (unke sardaron mein se) mujhpar vishwas karte, sab Yahudi zaroor mujhpar vishwas karte."
