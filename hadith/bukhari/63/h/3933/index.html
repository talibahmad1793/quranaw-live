Sahih al-Bukhari 3933

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 158

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ حَمْزَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ حُمَيْدٍ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عُمَرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، يَسْأَلُ السَّائِبَ ابْنَ أُخْتِ النَّمِرِ مَا سَمِعْتَ فِي، سُكْنَى مَكَّةَ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ الْعَلاَءَ بْنَ الْحَضْرَمِيِّ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ ثَلاَثٌ لِلْمُهَاجِرِ بَعْدَ الصَّدَرِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdur-Rahman bin Humaid Az-Zuhri:I heard `Umar bin `Abdul-Aziz asking As-Sa'ib, the nephew of An-Namir, "What have you heard about residing in Mecca?" The other said, "I heard Al-Ala bin Al-Hadrami saying, Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said: An Emigrant is allowed to stay in Mecca for three days after departing from Mina (i.e. after performing all the ceremonies of Hajj)

Abdur-Rahman bin Humaid Az-Zuhri bataate hain: Maine Umar bin Abdul Aziz ko As-Saib se, jo An-Namir ke bhatije the, puchte suna, "Aapne Mecca mein rehne ke baare mein kya suna hai?" Doosre ne kaha, "Maine Al-Ala bin Al-Hadrami ko kehte suna, 'Allah ke Rasool ne kaha: Ek Muhajir ko Mecca mein teen din tak Mina se lautne ke baad (yaani Hajj ki sab riwajen poori karne ke baad) rehne ki ijazat hai.'"
