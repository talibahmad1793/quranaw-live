Sahih al-Bukhari 3823

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 48

Hadith text

وَعَنْ قَيْسٍ، عَنْ جَرِيرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ كَانَ فِي الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ بَيْتٌ يُقَالَ لَهُ ذُو الْخَلَصَةِ، وَكَانَ يُقَالُ لَهُ الْكَعْبَةُ الْيَمَانِيَةُ، أَوِ الْكَعْبَةُ الشَّأْمِيَّةُ، فَقَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ هَلْ أَنْتَ مُرِيحِي مِنْ ذِي الْخَلَصَةِ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ فَنَفَرْتُ إِلَيْهِ فِي خَمْسِينَ وَمِائَةِ فَارِسٍ مِنْ أَحْمَسَ ـ قَالَ ـ فَكَسَرْنَا، وَقَتَلْنَا مَنْ وَجَدْنَا عِنْدَهُ، فَأَتَيْنَاهُ، فَأَخْبَرْنَاهُ، فَدَعَا لَنَا وَلأَحْمَسَ‏.‏

Jarir bin 'Abdullah narrated:There was a house called Dhul-Khalasa in the Pre-Islamic Period and it was also called Al-Ka'ba Al-Yamaniya or Al-Ka'ba Ash-Shamiya. Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said to me, "Will you relieve me from Dhul-Khalasa?" So I left for it with 150 cavalrymen from the tribe of Ahmas and then we destroyed it and killed whoever we found there. Then we came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and informed him about it. He invoked good upon us and upon the tribe of Ahmas

Jarir bin Abdullah bataate hain: Jahiliyat ke daur mein ek ghar Dhul-Khalasa naam ka tha, aur use Al-Ka'ba Al-Yamaniya ya Al-Ka'ba Ash-Shamiya bhi kaha jata tha. Allah ke Rasool ne mujhse kaha, "Kya tum mujhe Dhul-Khalasa se aaram doge?" Isliye main Ahmas qabile ke dedh sau ghudsawaar logon ke saath wahaan gaya aur humne use toda aur jo bhi wahaan mila use qatl kiya. Phir hum Nabi ke paas wapas aaye aur unhe iski khabar di. Unhone humare aur Ahmas qabile ke logon ke liye achhi dua ki.
