Sahih al-Bukhari 3934

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 159

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، قَالَ مَا عَدُّوا مِنْ مَبْعَثِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَلاَ مِنْ وَفَاتِهِ، مَا عَدُّوا إِلاَّ مِنْ مَقْدَمِهِ الْمَدِينَةَ‏.‏

Narrated Sahl bin Sa`d:The Prophet's companions did not take as a starting date for the Muslim calendar, the day, the Prophet (ﷺ) had been sent as an Apostle or the day of his death, but the day of his arrival at Medina

Sahl bin Sa'd bataate hain: Nabi ke sahaba ne Musalman calendar (yaani hijri sanvat) ki shuruaat ki taarikh ke tor par woh din nahi chuna jab Nabi ko Rasool banaya gaya tha ya unki maut ka din, balki unke Madina aagman ka din.
