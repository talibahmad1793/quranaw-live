Sahih al-Bukhari 3937

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 162

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ حُمَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَدِمَ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَوْفٍ، فَآخَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَ سَعْدِ بْنِ الرَّبِيعِ الأَنْصَارِيِّ، فَعَرَضَ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يُنَاصِفَهُ أَهْلَهُ وَمَالَهُ، فَقَالَ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بَارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكَ فِي أَهْلِكَ وَمَالِكَ، دُلَّنِي عَلَى السُّوقِ‏.‏ فَرَبِحَ شَيْئًا مِنْ أَقِطٍ وَسَمْنٍ، فَرَآهُ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَعْدَ أَيَّامٍ وَعَلَيْهِ وَضَرٌ مِنْ صُفْرَةٍ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ مَهْيَمْ يَا عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، تَزَوَّجْتُ امْرَأَةً مِنَ الأَنْصَارِ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ فَمَا سُقْتَ فِيهَا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ وَزْنَ نَوَاةٍ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ‏.‏ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ أَوْلِمْ وَلَوْ بِشَاةٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Anas:When `Abdur-Rahman bin `Auf came to Medina and the Prophet (ﷺ) established the bond of brotherhood between him and Sa`d bin Ar-Rabi-al-Ansari, Sa`d suggested that `Abdur-Rahman should accept half of his property and family. `Abdur Rahman said, "May Allah bless you in your family and property; guide me to the market." So `Abdur-Rahman (while doing business in the market) made some profit of some condensed dry yoghurt and butter. After a few days the Prophet (ﷺ) saw him wearing clothes stained with yellow perfume. The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, "What is this, O `Abdur-Rahman?" He said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! I have married an Ansar' woman." The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, "What have you given her as Mahr?" He (i.e. `Abdur-Rahman) said, "A piece of gold, about the weight of a date stone." Then the Prophet said, Give a banquet, even though of a sheep

Anas bataate hain: Jab Abdur-Rahman bin Auf Madina aaye aur Nabi ne unke aur Sa'd bin Ar-Rabi Al-Ansaari ke beech bhaeechaara ka bandhan banaya, Sa'd ne sujhaav diya ke Abdur-Rahman unki jaidad aur pariwara ka aadha qubool kar len. Abdur-Rahman ne kaha, "Allah tumhare pariwara aur jaidad mein barkat de; mujhe bazaar dikha do." Isliye Abdur-Rahman ne (bazaar mein vyaapaar karte hue) kuch dahi aur ghi ka munafa kamaya. Kuch dinon baad Nabi ne unhe peele rang ki khushbu wale kapde pahne dekha. Nabi ne pucha, "Yeh kya hai, Aye Abdur-Rahman?" Unhone kaha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Maine ek Ansaari aurat se shaadi ki hai." Nabi ne pucha, "Tumne unhe Mahr mein kya diya?" Unhone (yaani Abdur-Rahman ne) kaha, "Ek khajoor-gutli ke barabar wazan ka sona." Phir Nabi ne kaha, "Ek shaadi ki dawat do, chahe ek bhed se hi ho."
