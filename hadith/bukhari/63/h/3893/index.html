Sahih al-Bukhari 3893

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 118

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي حَبِيبٍ، عَنْ أَبِي الْخَيْرِ، عَنِ الصُّنَابِحِيِّ، عَنْ عُبَادَةَ بْنِ الصَّامِتِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّهُ قَالَ إِنِّي مِنَ النُّقَبَاءِ الَّذِينَ بَايَعُوا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏ وَقَالَ بَايَعْنَاهُ عَلَى أَنْ لاَ نُشْرِكَ بِاللَّهِ شَيْئًا، وَلاَ نَسْرِقَ، وَلاَ نَزْنِيَ، وَلاَ نَقْتُلَ النَّفْسَ الَّتِي حَرَّمَ اللَّهُ، وَلاَ نَنْتَهِبَ، وَلاَ نَعْصِيَ بِالْجَنَّةِ إِنْ فَعَلْنَا ذَلِكَ، فَإِنْ غَشِينَا مِنْ ذَلِكَ شَيْئًا كَانَ قَضَاءُ ذَلِكَ إِلَى اللَّهِ‏.‏

Narrated 'Ubada bin As Samit:I was one of the Naqibs who gave the ('Aqaba) Pledge of Allegiance to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ). We gave the pledge of allegiance to him that we would not worship anything other than Allah, would not steal, would not commit illegal sexual intercourse, would not kill a person whose killing Allah has made illegal except rightfully, would not rob each other, and we would not be promised Paradise if we did the above sins, then if we committed one of the above sins, Allah will give His Judgment concerning it

Ubada bin As-Samit bataate hain: Main un Naqib (yaani sardaron) mein se ek tha jinhone Allah ke Rasool ko (Al-Aqaba ki) bai'at di. Humne unhe bai'at di ke hum Allah ke siwa kisi ki ibadat nahi karenge, chori nahi karenge, na-jaiz sambandh nahi banaenge, kisi aisi jaan ko qatl nahi karenge jise Allah ne haram kiya hai siwaye uchit kaaranon ke, ek-doosre ko lootenge nahi, aur agar hum in gunahon ko karte hain to humein Jannat ka waada nahi kiya jaayega; phir agar humne un gunahon mein se koee gunah kiya, Allah is baare mein apna faisla dega.
