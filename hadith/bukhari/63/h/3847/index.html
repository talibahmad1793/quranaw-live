Sahih al-Bukhari 3847

Book 63: Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar) · Hadith 72

Hadith text

وَقَالَ ابْنُ وَهْبٍ أَخْبَرَنَا عَمْرٌو، عَنْ بُكَيْرِ بْنِ الأَشَجِّ، أَنَّ كُرَيْبًا، مَوْلَى ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ حَدَّثَهُ أَنَّ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ لَيْسَ السَّعْىُ بِبَطْنِ الْوَادِي بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ سُنَّةً، إِنَّمَا كَانَ أَهْلُ الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ يَسْعَوْنَهَا وَيَقُولُونَ لاَ نُجِيزُ الْبَطْحَاءَ إِلاَّ شَدًّا

Narrated Ibn 'Abbas:To run along the valley between two green pillars of Safa and Marwa (mountains) was not Sunna, but the people in the pre-islamic period of ignorance used to run along it, and used to say: "We do not cross this rain stream except running strongly

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Safa aur Marwa (pahad) ke do hare khambon ke beech waadi mein daudna Sunnat nahi thi, magar jahiliyat ke daur ke log uske saath daudte the aur kahte the, "Hum is barish ki dhara ko paar nahi karte siwaye zordaar daudkar."
