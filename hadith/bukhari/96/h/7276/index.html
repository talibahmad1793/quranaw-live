Sahih al-Bukhari 7276

Book 96: Holding Fast to the Qur'an and Sunnah · Hadith 9

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ سَأَلْتُ الأَعْمَشَ فَقَالَ عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ وَهْبٍ، سَمِعْتُ حُذَيْفَةَ، يَقُولُ حَدَّثَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ أَنَّ الأَمَانَةَ نَزَلَتْ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ فِي جَذْرِ قُلُوبِ الرِّجَالِ، وَنَزَلَ الْقُرْآنُ فَقَرَءُوا الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلِمُوا مِنَ السُّنَّةِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Hudhaifa:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said to us, "Honesty descended from the Heavens and settled in the roots of the hearts of men (faithful believers), and then the Qur'an was revealed and the people read the Qur'an, (and learnt it from it) and also learnt it from the Sunna." Both Qur'an and Sunna strengthened their (the faithful believers') honesty. (See Hadith No)

Hudhaifa bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne humse kaha, 'Amaanat (imaandaari) aasman se utri aur logon (sacche Momineen) ke dilon ki jadon mein baithi, aur phir Quran naazil hua aur log Quran padhne lage, (aur usse seekha) aur Sunnat se bhi seekha.' Quran aur Sunnat, dono ne unki (Momineen ki) amaanat ko mazboot kiya. (Poori tafseel ke liye Hadees No. dekhein.)
