Sahih al-Bukhari 7334

Book 96: Holding Fast to the Qur'an and Sunnah · Hadith 63

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي مَرْيَمَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو غَسَّانَ، حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو حَازِمٍ، عَنْ سَهْلٍ، أَنَّهُ كَانَ بَيْنَ جِدَارِ الْمَسْجِدِ مِمَّا يَلِي الْقِبْلَةَ وَبَيْنَ الْمِنْبَرِ مَمَرُّ الشَّاةِ‏.‏

Narrated Sahl:The distance between the pulpit and the wall of the mosque on the side of the Qibla was just sufficient for a sheep to pass through

Sahl bataate hain: Mimbar aur Masjid ki Qibla waali deewaar ke beech ki doori itni thi ke usmein se ek bakri guzar sakti thi.
