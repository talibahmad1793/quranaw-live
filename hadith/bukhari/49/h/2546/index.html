Sahih al-Bukhari 2546

Book 49: Manumission of Slaves · Hadith 29

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ الْعَبْدُ إِذَا نَصَحَ سَيِّدَهُ وَأَحْسَنَ عِبَادَةَ رَبِّهِ كَانَ لَهُ أَجْرُهُ مَرَّتَيْنِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Umar:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "If a slave is honest and faithful to his master and worships his Lord (Allah) in a perfect manner, he will get a double reward

Ibn Umar (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne farmaya: "Agar ek gulaam imaandaar aur wafadaar hai apne maalik ke prati, aur apne Rab (Allah) ki ibadat achhi tarah karta hai, use dohra ajr milega."
