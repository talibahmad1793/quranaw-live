Sahih al-Bukhari 1001

Book 14: Witr Prayer · Hadith 12

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ زَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ، قَالَ سُئِلَ أَنَسٌ أَقَنَتَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي الصُّبْحِ قَالَ نَعَمْ‏.‏ فَقِيلَ لَهُ أَوَقَنَتَ قَبْلَ الرُّكُوعِ قَالَ بَعْدَ الرُّكُوعِ يَسِيرًا‏.‏

Narrated Muhammad bin Seereen:Anas was asked, "Did the Prophet (ﷺ) recite Qunut in the Fajr prayer?" Anas replied in the affirmative. He was further asked, "Did he recite Qunut before bowing?" Anas replied, "He recited Qunut after bowing for some time (for one month)

Muhammad bin Seereen bataate hain: Anas se pucha gaya: "Kya Nabi Fajr ki namaz mein Qunut padhte the?" Anas ne haan mein jawab diya. Unse phir pucha gaya: "Kya woh ruku se pehle Qunut padhte the?" Anas ne jawab diya: "Aap ne ruku ke baad, kuch waqt tak (ek mahine ke liye), Qunut padhi."
