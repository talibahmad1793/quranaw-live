Sahih al-Bukhari 1203

Book 21: Actions while Praying · Hadith 7

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا الزُّهْرِيُّ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ التَّسْبِيحُ لِلرِّجَالِ وَالتَّصْفِيقُ لِلنِّسَاءِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The saying 'Subhan Allah' is for men and clapping is for women." (If something happens in the prayer, the men can invite the attention of the Imam by saying "Subhan Allah". And women, by clapping their hands)

Abu Huraira (رضی اللہ عنہ) bataate hain: Nabi ne farmaya: "'Subhan Allah' kehna mardon ke liye hai aur taaliyan bajanaa aurton ke liye." (Agar namaz mein kuch ho jaaye, mard 'Subhan Allah' kehkar Imam ka dhyan khinch sakte hain, aur aurtein taaliyan bajakar.)
