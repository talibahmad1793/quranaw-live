Sahih al-Bukhari 4893

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 413

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وَهْبُ بْنُ جَرِيرٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي قَالَ، سَمِعْتُ الزُّبَيْرَ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، فِي قَوْلِهِ تَعَالَى ‏{‏وَلاَ يَعْصِينَكَ فِي مَعْرُوفٍ‏}‏ قَالَ إِنَّمَا هُوَ شَرْطٌ شَرَطَهُ اللَّهُ لِلنِّسَاءِ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Regarding the saying of Allah: 'And they will not disobey you in any just matter.' (60.12) That was one of the conditions which Allah imposed on The believing) women (who came to take the oath of allegiance to the Prophet)

Ibn Abbas Allah ke is farmaan ke baare mein bataate hain: 'Aur wo kisi jaayez baat mein aapki na-farmaani nahi karengi' (60:12) - ye un shartton mein se ek thi jo Allah ne un momin auraton par rakhi thin jo Nabi (SAW) se baiat karne aati thin.
