Sahih al-Bukhari 4773

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 295

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ مُقَاتِلٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا يَعْلَى، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ الْعُصْفُرِيُّ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، ‏{‏لَرَادُّكَ إِلَى مَعَادٍ‏}‏ قَالَ إِلَى مَكَّةَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Qur'an 28.85'...will bring you home' means to Mecca

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Quran 28:85 mein, '...tumhein tumhaare (asli) ghar wapas le aayega,' iska matlab hai Makkah.
