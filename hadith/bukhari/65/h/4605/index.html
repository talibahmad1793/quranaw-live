Sahih al-Bukhari 4605

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 127

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، سَمِعْتُ الْبَرَاءَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ آخِرُ سُورَةٍ نَزَلَتْ بَرَاءَةَ، وَآخِرُ آيَةٍ نَزَلَتْ ‏{‏يَسْتَفْتُونَكَ ‏}‏

Narrated Al-Bara:The last Sura that was revealed was Bara'a, and the last Verse that was revealed was: "They ask you for a legal verdict, Say: Allah directs (thus) about those who leave no descendants or ascendants as heirs

Al-Bara bataate hain: Sabse aakhir mein naazil hone wali Surah 'Bara'a' (Surah At-Tauba) thi, aur sabse aakhir mein naazil hone wali Aayat ye thi: 'Wo aapse (miraas ke baare mein) fatwa poochte hain. Kaho: Allah tumhein un logon ke baare mein hukm deta hai jinke koi aulaad ya waalidain waaris na hon...'
