Sahih al-Bukhari 4933

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 454

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَابِسٍ، سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ ‏{‏تَرْمِي بِشَرَرٍ‏}‏ كُنَّا نَعْمِدُ إِلَى الْخَشَبَةِ ثَلاَثَةَ أَذْرُعٍ وَفَوْقَ ذَلِكَ، فَنَرْفَعُهُ لِلشِّتَاءِ فَنُسَمِّيهِ الْقَصَرَ‏.‏ ‏{‏كَأَنَّهُ جِمَالاَتٌ صُفْرٌ‏}‏ حِبَالُ السُّفْنِ تُجْمَعُ حَتَّى تَكُونَ كَأَوْسَاطِ الرِّجَالِ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn 'Abbas: (regarding) the explanation of "... It throws sparks as Al-Qasr ..." (V. 77:32): We used to collect logs of wood, three cubits long or longer, to store for heating purposes in winter, and we used to call it Al- Qasr, it also means a castle or a fort. "As if they were Jimalatun Sufr (yellow camels or bundles of ropes)" (V.77:33): means the ropes of a ship which are made in bundles till it become as wide as men's waists

Ibn Abbas is Aayat ki tafseer mein bataate hain: '...wo Al-Qasr jaisi chingariyaan uchaalti hai...' (77:32) - hum lakdi ke kunde (teen haath lambe ya usse zyaada) sardi mein garmaane ke liye jama karke rakhte the, aur hum ise 'Al-Qasr' kehte the, iska matlab qila ya kila bhi hota hai. '...jaise wo Jimaalaatun Sufr (peele oont ya rasson ke bandhe hue gattar) hon...' (77:33) - iska matlab hai kashti ki rassiyaan jo bandal (gattar) mein banaayi jaati hain yahaan tak ke wo mardon ki kamar jitni moti ho jaati hain.
