Sahih al-Bukhari 4587

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 109

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ، قَالَ كُنْتُ أَنَا وَأُمِّي، مِنَ الْمُسْتَضْعَفِينَ‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:My mother and I were among the weak and oppressed (Muslims at Mecca)

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Meri maa aur main un kamzor aur mazloom (Musalmaanon) mein se the (jo Makkah mein reh gaye the).
