Sahih al-Bukhari 4685

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 207

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ زُرَيْعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدٌ، وَهِشَامٌ، قَالاَ حَدَّثَنَا قَتَادَةُ، عَنْ صَفْوَانَ بْنِ مُحْرِزٍ، قَالَ بَيْنَا ابْنُ عُمَرَ يَطُوفُ إِذْ عَرَضَ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ يَا أَبَا عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ ـ أَوْ قَالَ يَا ابْنَ عُمَرَ ـ سَمِعْتَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي النَّجْوَى فَقَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ يُدْنَى الْمُؤْمِنُ مِنْ رَبِّهِ ـ وَقَالَ هِشَامٌ يَدْنُو الْمُؤْمِنُ ـ حَتَّى يَضَعَ عَلَيْهِ كَنَفَهُ، فَيُقَرِّرُهُ بِذُنُوبِهِ تَعْرِفُ ذَنْبَ كَذَا يَقُولُ أَعْرِفُ، يَقُولُ رَبِّ أَعْرِفُ مَرَّتَيْنِ، فَيَقُولُ سَتَرْتُهَا فِي الدُّنْيَا وَأَغْفِرُهَا لَكَ الْيَوْمَ ثُمَّ تُطْوَى صَحِيفَةُ حَسَنَاتِهِ، وَأَمَّا الآخَرُونَ أَوِ الْكُفَّارُ فَيُنَادَى عَلَى رُءُوسِ الأَشْهَادِ هَؤُلاَءِ الَّذِينَ كَذَبُوا عَلَى رَبِّهِمْ ‏"‏‏.‏ وَقَالَ شَيْبَانُ عَنْ قَتَادَةَ حَدَّثَنَا صَفْوَانُ‏.‏

Narrated Safwan bin Muhriz:While Ibn `Umar was performing the Tawaf (around the Ka`ba), a man came up to him and said, "O Abu `AbdurRahman!" or said, "O Ibn `Umar! Did you hear anything from the Prophet (ﷺ) about An-Najwa?" Ibn `Umar said, "I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) saying, 'The Believer will be brought near his Lord." (Hisham, a sub-narrator said, reporting the Prophet's words), "The believer will come near (his Lord) till his Lord covers him with His screen and makes him confess his sins. (Allah will ask him), 'Do you know (that you did) 'such-and-such sin?" He will say twice, 'Yes, I do.' Then Allah will say, 'I concealed it in the world and I forgive it for you today.' Then the record of his good deeds will be folded up. As for the others, or the disbelievers, it will be announced publicly before the witnesses: 'These are ones who lied against their Lord

Safwan bin Muhriz bataate hain: Jab Ibn Umar Kaaba ka Tawaf kar rahe the, ek shaks unke paas aaya aur kaha, 'Aye Abu Abdur Rahman!' ya kaha, 'Aye Ibn Umar! Kya aapne Nabi (SAW) se An-Najwa (khufiya guftgu/hisaab-kitaab) ke baare mein kuch suna hai?' Ibn Umar ne kaha, 'Maine Nabi (SAW) ko ye farmaate suna, "Momin ko apne Rab ke qareeb laaya jaayega."' (Ravi Hisham kehte hain, Nabi (SAW) ke alfaaz naql karte hue:) 'Momin apne Rab ke qareeb aayega yahaan tak ke uska Rab use apni chaadar (parda) se dhaank lega aur use apne gunaahon ka iqraar karwaayega. (Allah usse poochega,) "Kya tumhein pata hai (tumne) falaan falaan gunaah kiya tha?" Wo do baar kahega, "Haan, mujhe pata hai." Phir Allah farmaayega, "Maine duniya mein use chhupaaya tha, aur aaj main tumhein maaf karta hoon." Phir uske nek amaal ka register (record) lapet diya jaayega. Magar doosron ke liye, yaani kaafiron ke liye, gawaahon ke saamne khule aam elaan kiya jaayega: "Yahi wo log hain jinhone apne Rab par jhoot bola tha."'
