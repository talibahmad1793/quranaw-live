Sahih al-Bukhari 4627

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 149

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ سَالِمِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَيْبِ خَمْسٌ إِنَّ اللَّهَ عِنْدَهُ عِلْمُ السَّاعَةِ، وَيُنَزِّلُ الْغَيْثَ، وَيَعْلَمُ مَا فِي الأَرْحَامِ، وَمَا تَدْرِي نَفْسٌ مَاذَا تَكْسِبُ غَدًا، وَمَا تَدْرِي نَفْسٌ بِأَىِّ أَرْضٍ تَمُوتُ، إِنَّ اللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرٌ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "The keys of the Unseen are five: Verily with Allah (Alone) is the knowledge of the Hour He sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs. No soul knows what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul knows in what land it will die. Verily, Allah is All-Knower, All-Aware

Abdullah bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Ghaib ki chaabiyaan paanch hain: Beshak sirf Allah ko hi Qayamat (ke waqt) ka ilm hai, wahi baarish barsaata hai, aur wo jaanta hai jo (maaon ke) rehm mein hai. Koi bhi shaks nahi jaanta ke wo kal kya kamaayega, aur koi bhi shaks nahi jaanta ke wo kis zameen mein marega. Beshak Allah khoob jaanne wala, khoob khabar rakhne wala hai.'
