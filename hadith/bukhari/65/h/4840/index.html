Sahih al-Bukhari 4840

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 361

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ جَابِرٍ، قَالَ كُنَّا يَوْمَ الْحُدَيْبِيَةِ أَلْفًا وَأَرْبَعَمِائَةٍ‏.‏

Narrated Jabir:We were one thousand and four hundred on the Day of Al-Hudaibiya

Jabir bataate hain: Hum Hudaibiya ke din chaudah sau (1400) log the.
