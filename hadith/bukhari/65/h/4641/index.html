Sahih al-Bukhari 4641

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 163

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ هَمَّامِ بْنِ مُنَبِّهٍ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ يَقُولُ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ قِيلَ لِبَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ ‏{‏ادْخُلُوا الْبَابَ سُجَّدًا وَقُولُوا حِطَّةٌ نَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ خَطَايَاكُمْ‏}‏ فَبَدَّلُوا فَدَخَلُوا يَزْحَفُونَ عَلَى أَسْتَاهِهِمْ وَقَالُوا حَبَّةٌ فِي شَعَرَةٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "It was said to the children of Israel, 'Enter the gate in prostration and say Hittatun. We shall forgive you your faults.' (7.161) But they changed (Allah's Order) and entered, dragging themselves on their buttocks and said, 'Habbatun (a grain) in a Sha'ratin (hair)

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Bani Israel se kaha gaya tha, "Darwaaze mein jhukte hue daakhil ho aur kaho: Hittatun (yaani Aye Allah! Hamare gunaah maaf kar de), Hum tumhari khataayen maaf kar denge."' (7:161) Magar unhone (Allah ke hukm ko) badal diya aur apne chootadon ke bal ghisadte hue daakhil hue aur kaha, 'Habbatun (yaani ek daana) Sha'ratin (baal) mein' (yaani tazheek ke taur par ek bemaani baat kah di).
