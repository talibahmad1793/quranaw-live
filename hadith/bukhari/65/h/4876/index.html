Sahih al-Bukhari 4876

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 397

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُوسَى، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَنَّ ابْنَ جُرَيْجٍ، أَخْبَرَهُمْ قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي يُوسُفُ بْنُ مَاهَكَ، قَالَ إِنِّي عِنْدَ عَائِشَةَ أُمِّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ قَالَتْ لَقَدْ أُنْزِلَ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِمَكَّةَ، وَإِنِّي لَجَارِيَةٌ أَلْعَبُ ‏{‏بَلِ السَّاعَةُ مَوْعِدُهُمْ وَالسَّاعَةُ أَدْهَى وَأَمَرُّ‏}‏

Narrated Yusuf bin Mahak:I was in the house of `Aisha, the mother of the Believers. She said, "This revelation: "Nay, but the Hour is their appointed time (for their full recompense); and the Hour will be more previous and most bitter." (54.46) was revealed to Muhammad at Mecca while I was a playful little girl

Yusuf bin Mahak bataate hain: Main Aisha, Ummul-Momineen, ke ghar mein tha. Unhone kaha, 'Ye wahi: "Balke Qayamat hi unke (poore badle ke) muqarrar waqt ki jagah hai; aur Qayamat bahut hi sakht aur kadwi hogi" (54:46), Makkah mein Muhammad (SAW) par naazil hui thi jab main ek khelti-koodti chhoti ladki thi.'
