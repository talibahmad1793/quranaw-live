Sahih al-Bukhari 4852

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 373

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا وَرْقَاءُ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ أَمَرَهُ أَنْ يُسَبِّحَ، فِي أَدْبَارِ الصَّلَوَاتِ كُلِّهَا‏.‏ يَعْنِي قَوْلَهُ ‏{‏وَأَدْبَارَ السُّجُودِ‏}‏

Narrated Mujahid:Ibn `Abbas said, "Allah ordered His Prophet to celebrate Allah's praises after all prayers." He refers to His Statement: 'After the prayers

Mujahid bataate hain: Ibn Abbas ne kaha, 'Allah ne apne Nabi ko hukm diya ke wo har namaz ke baad Allah ki tasbeeh karein.' Ye Allah ke is farmaan ki taraf ishara hai: 'Namazon ke baad...'
