Sahih al-Bukhari 4634

Book 65: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh)) · Hadith 156

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا حَفْصُ بْنُ عُمَرَ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ ‏ "‏ لاَ أَحَدَ أَغْيَرُ مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَلِذَلِكَ حَرَّمَ الْفَوَاحِشَ مَا ظَهَرَ مِنْهَا وَمَا بَطَنَ، وَلاَ شَىْءَ أَحَبُّ إِلَيْهِ الْمَدْحُ مِنَ اللَّهِ، لِذَلِكَ مَدَحَ نَفْسَهُ ‏"‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ سَمِعْتَهُ مِنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ قَالَ نَعَمْ‏.‏ قُلْتُ وَرَفَعَهُ قَالَ نَعَمْ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Wail:`Abdullah (bin Mas`ud) said, "None has more sense of ghaira than Allah therefore - He prohibits shameful sins (illegal sexual intercourse, etc.) whether committed openly or secretly. And none loves to be praised more than Allah does, and for this reason He praises Himself." I asked Abu Wail, "Did you hear it from `Abdullah?" He said, "Yes," I said, "Did `Abdullah ascribe it to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)?" He said, "Yes

Abu Wail bataate hain: Abdullah bin Masud ne kaha, 'Allah se zyaada ghairat (namoos) ki hifaazat karne wala koi nahi, isi wajah se wo besharmi ke gunaahon ko, chaahe khule mein hon ya chhupe mein, haraam qaraar deta hai. Aur Allah se zyaada koi apni taareef pasand karne wala nahi, isi wajah se wo khud apni taareef karta hai.' (Ravi kehte hain:) Maine Abu Wail se pucha, 'Kya aapne ye khud Abdullah se suna?' Unhone kaha, 'Haan.' Maine pucha, 'Kya Abdullah ne ise Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ki taraf mansoob kiya?' Unhone kaha, 'Haan.'
