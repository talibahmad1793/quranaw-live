Sahih al-Bukhari 6855

Book 86: Limits and Punishments set by Allah (Hudood) · Hadith 77

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الْقَاسِمِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ، قَالَ ذَكَرَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ الْمُتَلاَعِنَيْنِ فَقَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ شَدَّادٍ هِيَ الَّتِي قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ لَوْ كُنْتُ رَاجِمًا امْرَأَةً عَنْ غَيْرِ بَيِّنَةٍ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ، تِلْكَ امْرَأَةٌ أَعْلَنَتْ‏.‏

Narrated Al-Qasim bin Muhammad:Ibn `Abbas mentioned the couple who had taken the oath of Lian. `Abdullah bin Shaddad said (to him), "Was this woman about whom Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, 'If I were ever to stone to death any woman without witnesses. (I would have stoned that woman to death)?' Ibn `Abbas replied," No, that lady exposed herself (by her suspicious behavior)

Al-Qasim bin Muhammad bataate hain: Ibn Abbas ne us jodi ka zikr kiya jinhone Li'an ki qasam li thi. Abdullah bin Shaddad ne (unse) kaha, 'Kya ye wahi khaatoon thi jinke baare mein Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ne kaha tha, "Agar main kabhi kisi khaatoon ko bina gawaahon ke sangsaar karta (to main us khaatoon ko sangsaar karta)"?' Ibn Abbas ne jawab diya, 'Nahi, wo khaatoon apne aap ko zaahir kar chuki thi (apne shakki suluk se).'
