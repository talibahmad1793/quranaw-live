Sahih al-Bukhari 6810

Book 86: Limits and Punishments set by Allah (Hudood) · Hadith 39

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ ذَكْوَانَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ لاَ يَزْنِي الزَّانِي حِينَ يَزْنِي وَهْوَ مُؤْمِنٌ، وَلاَ يَسْرِقُ حِينَ يَسْرِقُ وَهْوَ مُؤْمِنٌ، وَلاَ يَشْرَبُ حِينَ يَشْرَبُهَا وَهْوَ مُؤْمِنٌ، وَالتَّوْبَةُ مَعْرُوضَةٌ بَعْدُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The one who commits an illegal sexual intercourse is not a believer at the time of committing illegal sexual intercourse and a thief is not a believer at the time of committing theft and a drinker of alcoholic drink is not a believer at the time of drinking. Yet, (the gate of) repentance is open thereafter

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo koi zina karta hai wo us waqt Momin nahi hota jab wo zina kar raha hota hai, aur ek chor us waqt Momin nahi hota jab wo chori kar raha hota hai, aur sharaab peene wala us waqt Momin nahi hota jab wo peeta hai. Phir bhi, (tauba ka darwaaza) uske baad khula hota hai.'
