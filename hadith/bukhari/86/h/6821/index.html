Sahih al-Bukhari 6821

Book 86: Limits and Punishments set by Allah (Hudood) · Hadith 49

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَجُلاً، وَقَعَ بِامْرَأَتِهِ فِي رَمَضَانَ، فَاسْتَفْتَى رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏"‏ هَلْ تَجِدُ رَقَبَةً ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ هَلْ تَسْتَطِيعُ صِيَامَ شَهْرَيْنِ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ فَأَطْعِمْ سِتِّينَ مِسْكِينًا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira: A person had sexual relation with his wife in the month of Ramadan (while he was fasting), and he came to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) seeking his verdict concerning that action. The Prophet (ﷺ) said (to him), "Can you afford to manumit a slave?" The man said, "No." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Can you fast for two successive months?" He said, "No." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Then feed sixty poor persons

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Ek shaks ne apni biwi se Ramzan ke mahine mein (roze ki haalat mein) humbistari ki, aur wo Allah ke Rasool (SAW) ke paas is amal ke baare mein faisla lene aaya. Nabi (SAW) ne (usse) kaha, 'Kya tum ek ghulaam azaad karne ka intezaam kar sakte ho?' Us shaks ne kaha, 'Nahi.' Nabi (SAW) ne kaha, 'Kya tum lagataar do maheene roza rakh sakte ho?' Usne kaha, 'Nahi.' Nabi (SAW) ne kaha, 'To sath gareebon ko khaana khilaao.'
