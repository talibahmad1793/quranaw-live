Sahih al-Bukhari 6807

Book 86: Limits and Punishments set by Allah (Hudood) · Hadith 36

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ أَبِي بَكْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ‏.‏ وَحَدَّثَنِي خَلِيفَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو حَازِمٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيِّ، قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ مَنْ تَوَكَّلَ لِي مَا بَيْنَ رِجْلَيْهِ وَمَا بَيْنَ لَحْيَيْهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ لَهُ بِالْجَنَّةِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Sahl bin Sa`d:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Whoever guarantees me (the chastity of) what is between his legs (i.e. his private parts), and what is between his jaws (i.e., his tongue), I guarantee him Paradise

Sahl bin Sa'd bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Jo koi mujhe (apni) taangon ke beech ki cheez (yaani sharmgaah) aur (apne) jabdon ke beech ki cheez (yaani zubaan) ki zimmedaari le, main uske liye Jannat ki zimmedaari leta hoon.'
