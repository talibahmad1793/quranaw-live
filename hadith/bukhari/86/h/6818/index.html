Sahih al-Bukhari 6818

Book 86: Limits and Punishments set by Allah (Hudood) · Hadith 46

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ زِيَادٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ الْوَلَدُ لِلْفِرَاشِ، وَلِلْعَاهِرِ الْحَجَرُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The boy is for (the owner of) the bed and the stone is for the person who commits illegal sexual intercourse

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne farmaya, 'Ladka bistar ke (maalik) ke liye hota hai, aur pathhar us shaks ke liye hai jo zina karta hai.'
