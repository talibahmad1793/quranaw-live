Sahih al-Bukhari 6776

Book 86: Limits and Punishments set by Allah (Hudood) · Hadith 5

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسْلِمٌ، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامٌ، حَدَّثَنَا قَتَادَةُ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ جَلَدَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي الْخَمْرِ بِالْجَرِيدِ وَالنِّعَالِ، وَجَلَدَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ أَرْبَعِينَ‏.‏

Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) lashed a drunk with dateleaf stalks and shoes. And Abu Bakr gave a drunk forty lashes

Anas bataate hain: Nabi (SAW) ne ek nashe mein dhut shaks ko khajoor ke patton ki dandiyon aur jootiyon se kode maare. Aur Abu Bakr ne ek nashe mein dhut shaks ko chaalis kode maare.
