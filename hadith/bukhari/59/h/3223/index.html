Sahih al-Bukhari 3223

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 34

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْيَمَانِ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ الْمَلاَئِكَةُ يَتَعَاقَبُونَ، مَلاَئِكَةٌ بِاللَّيْلِ وَمَلاَئِكَةٌ بِالنَّهَارِ، وَيَجْتَمِعُونَ فِي صَلاَةِ الْفَجْرِ وَالْعَصْرِ، ثُمَّ يَعْرُجُ إِلَيْهِ الَّذِينَ بَاتُوا فِيكُمْ، فَيَسْأَلُهُمْ وَهْوَ أَعْلَمُ، فَيَقُولُ كَيْفَ تَرَكْتُمْ ‏{‏عِبَادِي‏}‏ فَيَقُولُونَ تَرَكْنَاهُمْ يُصَلُّونَ، وَأَتَيْنَاهُمْ يُصَلُّونَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Angels keep on descending from and ascending to the Heaven in turn, some at night and some by daytime, and all of them assemble together at the time of the Fajr and `Asr prayers. Then those who have stayed with you over-night, ascend unto Allah Who asks them, and He knows the answer better than they, "How have you left My slaves?" They reply, "We have left them praying as we found them praying." If anyone of you says "Amin" (during the Prayer at the end of the recitation of Surat-al-Fatiha), and the angels in Heaven say the same, and the two sayings coincide, all his past sins will be forgiven

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Farishte baari-baari aasman se utarte aur aasman ki taraf chadhte rahte hain, kuch raat ko aur kuch din mein, aur woh sab Fajr aur Asr ki namaz ke waqt ek saath jama hote hain. Phir woh jinhone tumhare saath raat guzari, Allah ki taraf chadhte hain jo unse pucchta hai (aur woh iska jawab unse zyada jaanta hai), 'Tumne mere bandon ko kis haalat mein chhoda?' Woh jawab dete hain, 'Humne unhe namaz padhte hue chhoda jaisa humne unhe namaz padhte hue paaya.'" Agar tum mein se koi "Ameen" kehta hai (Surah Al-Fatiha padhte waqt), aur aasman ke farishte bhi wahi kehte hain, aur donon ke kehne ka waqt mil jata hai, uske sab pichle gunah maaf kiye jaayenge.
