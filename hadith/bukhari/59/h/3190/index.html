Sahih al-Bukhari 3190

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 1

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ جَامِعِ بْنِ شَدَّادٍ، عَنْ صَفْوَانَ بْنِ مُحْرِزٍ، عَنْ عِمْرَانَ بْنِ حُصَيْنٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ قَالَ جَاءَ نَفَرٌ مِنْ بَنِي تَمِيمٍ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏"‏ يَا بَنِي تَمِيمٍ، أَبْشِرُوا ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالُوا بَشَّرْتَنَا فَأَعْطِنَا‏.‏ فَتَغَيَّرَ وَجْهُهُ، فَجَاءَهُ أَهْلُ الْيَمَنِ، فَقَالَ ‏"‏ يَا أَهْلَ الْيَمَنِ، اقْبَلُوا الْبُشْرَى إِذْ لَمْ يَقْبَلْهَا بَنُو تَمِيمٍ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالُوا قَبِلْنَا‏.‏ فَأَخَذَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُحَدِّثُ بَدْءَ الْخَلْقِ وَالْعَرْشِ، فَجَاءَ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ يَا عِمْرَانُ، رَاحِلَتُكَ تَفَلَّتَتْ، لَيْتَنِي لَمْ أَقُمْ‏.‏

Narrated `Imran bin Husain:Some people of Bani Tamim came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and he said (to them), "O Bani Tamim! rejoice with glad tidings." They said, "You have given us glad tidings, now give us something." On hearing that the color of his face changed then the people of Yemen came to him and he said, "O people of Yemen ! Accept the good tidings, as Bani Tamim has refused them." The Yemenites said, "We accept them. Then the Prophet (ﷺ) started taking about the beginning of creation and about Allah's Throne. In the mean time a man came saying, "O `Imran! Your she-camel has run away!'' (I got up and went away), but I wish I had not left that place (for I missed what Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) had said

Imran bin Husain bataate hain: Bani Tamim qabile ke kuch log Nabi ke paas aaye aur unhonne (unse) kaha, "Aye Bani Tamim! Achhi khabar se khush ho jao." Unhonne kaha, "Aapne humein achhi khabar di hai, ab humein kuch dijiye." Yeh sunkar unke chehre ka rang badal gaya, phir Yemen ke log unke paas aaye aur unhonne kaha, "Aye Yemen ke logo! Achhi khabar qubool karo, kyunke Bani Tamim ne use radd kiya hai." Yemen walon ne kaha, "Hum ise qubool karte hain." Phir Nabi ne srishti ki shuruaat aur Allah ke Arsh ke baare mein baat karna shuru kiya. Isi dauran ek shaks aaya aur kaha, "Aye Imran! Tumhari untni bhaag gaee hai!" (Main uthkar chala gaya), magar kaash main woh jagah na chhodta (kyunke maine woh miss kar diya jo Allah ke Rasool ne kaha).
