Sahih al-Bukhari 3256

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 66

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ صَفْوَانَ بْنِ سُلَيْمٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ إِنَّ أَهْلَ الْجَنَّةِ يَتَرَاءَيُونَ أَهْلَ الْغُرَفِ مِنْ فَوْقِهِمْ كَمَا يَتَرَاءَيُونَ الْكَوْكَبَ الدُّرِّيَّ الْغَابِرَ فِي الأُفُقِ مِنَ الْمَشْرِقِ أَوِ الْمَغْرِبِ، لِتَفَاضُلِ مَا بَيْنَهُمْ ‏"‏‏.‏ قَالُوا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، تِلْكَ مَنَازِلُ الأَنْبِيَاءِ لاَ يَبْلُغُهَا غَيْرُهُمْ قَالَ ‏"‏ بَلَى وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، رِجَالٌ آمَنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَصَدَّقُوا الْمُرْسَلِينَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "The people of Paradise will look at the dwellers of the lofty mansions (i.e. a superior place in Paradise) in the same way as one looks at a brilliant star far away in the East or in the West on the horizon; all that is because of their superiority over one another (in rewards)." On that the people said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Are these lofty mansions for the prophets which nobody else can reach? The Prophet (ﷺ) replied," No! "By Allah in whose Hands my life is, these are for the men who believed in Allah and also believed in the Apostles

Abu Saeed Al-Khudri bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Jannat wale oonche mahalon (yaani Jannat ki ek oonchi jagah) ke nivaasiyon ko usi tarah dekhenge jaise koi ek chamakdar sitare ko door purab ya pashchim mein kshitij par dekhta hai; yeh sab isliye kyunke unme se ek-doosre par ajr mein superiority (barhta) hogi." Isspar logon ne kaha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Kya yeh oonche mahal sirf Nabiyon ke liye hain jinke paas koi aur nahi pahunch sakta?" Nabi ne jawab diya, "Nahi! Us Zaat ki qasam jiske haathon mein meri zindagi hai, yeh un logon ke liye hain jo Allah par iman laaye aur Rasoolon par bhi iman laaye."
