Sahih al-Bukhari 3236

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 47

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى، حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو رَجَاءٍ، عَنْ سَمُرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ "‏ رَأَيْتُ اللَّيْلَةَ رَجُلَيْنِ أَتَيَانِي قَالاَ الَّذِي يُوقِدُ النَّارَ مَالِكٌ خَازِنُ النَّارِ، وَأَنَا جِبْرِيلُ، وَهَذَا مِيكَائِيلُ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Samura:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Last night I saw (in a dream) two men coming to me. One of them said, "The person who kindles the fire is Malik, the gate-keeper of the (Hell) Fire, and I am Gabriel, and this is Michael

Samura bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Kal raat maine (khwab mein) do log dekhe mere paas aate huye. Unme se ek ne kaha, 'Jo shaks aag jalane wala hai, woh Malik hai, Dozakh ka darbaan, aur main Jibreel hoon, aur yeh Mikaeel hai.'"
