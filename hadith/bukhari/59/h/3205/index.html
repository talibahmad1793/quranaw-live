Sahih al-Bukhari 3205

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 16

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ، عَنْ مُجَاهِدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ نُصِرْتُ بِالصَّبَا، وَأُهْلِكَتْ عَادٌ بِالدَّبُورِ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "I have been made victorious with the Saba (i.e. easterly wind) and the people of 'Ad were destroyed with the Dabur (i.e. westerly wind)

Ibn Abbas bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "Mujhe Saba (yaani purvi hawa) ke zariye fatah di gaee hai, aur Aad qabile ke log Dabur (yaani pashchimi hawa) ke zariye barbaad kiye gaye."
