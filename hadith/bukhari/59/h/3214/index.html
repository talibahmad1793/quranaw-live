Sahih al-Bukhari 3214

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 25

Hadith text

وَحَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ، أَخْبَرَنَا وَهْبُ بْنُ جَرِيرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي قَالَ، سَمِعْتُ حُمَيْدَ بْنَ هِلاَلٍ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كَأَنِّي أَنْظُرُ إِلَى غُبَارٍ سَاطِعٍ فِي سِكَّةِ بَنِي غَنْمٍ‏.‏ زَادَ مُوسَى مَوْكِبَ جِبْرِيلَ‏.‏

Narrated Jarir:as below. Narrated Humaid bin Hilal: Anas bin Malik said, "As if I see a cloud of dust swirling up in the lane of Bani Ghanim." Musa added, "That was caused by the procession of Gabriel

Jarir bataate hain: (jaisa neeche hai). Humaid bin Hilal bataate hain: Anas bin Malik ne kaha, "Mano main dhool ka ek baadal dekh raha hoon jo Bani Ghanim ki gali mein uth raha hai." Musa ne joda, "Yeh Jibreel ke jaane se hua tha."
