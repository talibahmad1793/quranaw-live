Sahih al-Bukhari 3265

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 75

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ أَبِي أُوَيْسٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ نَارُكُمْ جُزْءٌ مِنْ سَبْعِينَ جُزْءًا مِنْ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ ‏"‏‏.‏ قِيلَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنْ كَانَتْ لَكَافِيَةً‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ فُضِّلَتْ عَلَيْهِنَّ بِتِسْعَةٍ وَسِتِّينَ جُزْءًا، كُلُّهُنَّ مِثْلُ حَرِّهَا ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Your (ordinary) fire is one of 70 parts of the (Hell) Fire." Someone asked, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) This (ordinary) fire would have been sufficient (to torture the unbelievers)," Allah's Apostle said, "The (Hell) Fire has 69 parts more than the ordinary (worldly) fire, each part is as hot as this (worldly) fire

Abu Huraira bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Tumhari (aam duniyavi) aag Dozakh ki aag ke sattar hisso mein se ek hissa hai." Kisi ne pucha, "Ya Allah ke Rasool! Yeh (duniyavi) aag hi kaafi hoti (kaafiron ko saza dene ke liye)." Allah ke Rasool ne kaha, "Dozakh ki aag mein duniyavi aag se unsath (69) hisse zyada hain, har hissa isi (duniyavi) aag jitna garam hai."
