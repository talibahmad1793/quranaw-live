Sahih al-Bukhari 3197

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 8

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّابِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ سِيرِينَ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي بَكْرَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي بَكْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏ "‏ الزَّمَانُ قَدِ اسْتَدَارَ كَهَيْئَتِهِ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ، السَّنَةُ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا، مِنْهَا أَرْبَعَةٌ حُرُمٌ، ثَلاَثَةٌ مُتَوَالِيَاتٌ ذُو الْقَعْدَةِ وَذُو الْحِجَّةِ وَالْمُحَرَّمُ، وَرَجَبُ مُضَرَ الَّذِي بَيْنَ جُمَادَى وَشَعْبَانَ ‏"‏‏.‏

Narrated Abu Bakra:The Prophet (ﷺ) said. "(The division of time has turned to its original form which was current when Allah created the Heavens and the Earths. The year is of twelve months, out of which four months are sacred: Three are in succession Dhul-Qa' da, Dhul-Hijja and Muharram, and (the fourth is) Rajab of (the tribe of) Mudar which comes between Jumada-ath-Thaniyah and Sha`ban

Abu Bakra bataate hain: Nabi ne kaha, "(Waqt ka baantvara apni asli shakal mein wapas aa gaya hai jaisa jab Allah ne aasmanon aur zameenon ko banaya tha. Saal baarah mahinon ka hota hai, jinme se chaar mahine muqaddas hain: teen lagatar hain—Dhul-Qa'da, Dhul-Hijja, aur Muharram—aur (chautha hai) Rajab (Bani) Mudar ka, jo Jumada-ath-Thaniyah aur Sha'ban ke beech aata hai."
