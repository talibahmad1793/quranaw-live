Sahih al-Bukhari 3227

Book 59: Beginning of Creation · Hadith 38

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ سُلَيْمَانَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي ابْنُ وَهْبٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي عُمَرُ، عَنْ سَالِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ وَعَدَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم جِبْرِيلُ فَقَالَ إِنَّا لاَ نَدْخُلُ بَيْتًا فِيهِ صُورَةٌ وَلاَ كَلْبٌ‏.‏

Narrated Salim's father:Once Gabriel promised the Prophet (that he would visit him, but Gabriel did not come) and later on he said, "We, angels, do not enter a house which contains a picture or a dog

Salim ke waalid bataate hain: Ek baar Jibreel ne Nabi se waada kiya (ke woh unse milne aaenge, magar Jibreel nahi aaye), aur baad mein unhone kaha, "Hum, farishte, aise ghar mein daakhil nahi hote jismein tasveer ya kutta ho."
