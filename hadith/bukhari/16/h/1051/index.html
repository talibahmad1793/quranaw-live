Sahih al-Bukhari 1051

Book 16: Eclipses · Hadith 11

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا شَيْبَانُ، عَنْ يَحْيَى، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو، أَنَّهُ قَالَ لَمَّا كَسَفَتِ الشَّمْسُ عَلَى عَهْدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نُودِيَ إِنَّ الصَّلاَةَ جَامِعَةٌ فَرَكَعَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم رَكْعَتَيْنِ فِي سَجْدَةٍ ثُمَّ قَامَ فَرَكَعَ رَكْعَتَيْنِ فِي سَجْدَةٍ، ثُمَّ جَلَسَ، ثُمَّ جُلِّيَ عَنِ الشَّمْسِ‏.‏ قَالَ وَقَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ مَا سَجَدْتُ سُجُودًا قَطُّ كَانَ أَطْوَلَ مِنْهَا‏.‏

Narrated `Abdullah bin `Amr:When the sun eclipsed in the lifetime of Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and an announcement was made that the prayer was to be held in congregation. The Prophet (ﷺ) performed two bowing in one rak`a. Then he stood up and performed two bowing in one rak`a. Then he sat down and finished the prayer; and by then the (eclipse) had cleared `Aisha said, "I had never performed such a long prostration

Abdullah bin Amr (رضی اللہ عنہما) bataate hain: Allah ke Rasool ke zamane mein jab suraj grahan hua aur elaan kiya gaya ke namaz jamaat mein hogi. Nabi ne ek rakat mein do ruku kiye. Phir khade hue aur (doosri rakat mein) do ruku kiye. Phir baithe aur namaz khatam ki; is waqt tak (eclipse) saaf ho chuka tha. Ayesha ne kaha: "Maine kabhi itna lamba sajda nahi kiya tha."
