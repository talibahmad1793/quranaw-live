Sahih al-Bukhari 1078

Book 17: Prostration During Recital of Qur'an · Hadith 12

Hadith text

حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا مُعْتَمِرٌ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَبِي قَالَ، حَدَّثَنِي بَكْرٌ، عَنْ أَبِي رَافِعٍ، قَالَ صَلَّيْتُ مَعَ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ الْعَتَمَةَ فَقَرَأَ ‏{‏إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انْشَقَّتْ‏}‏ فَسَجَدَ فَقُلْتُ مَا هَذِهِ قَالَ سَجَدْتُ بِهَا خَلْفَ أَبِي الْقَاسِمِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَلاَ أَزَالُ أَسْجُدُ فِيهَا حَتَّى أَلْقَاهُ‏.‏

Narrated Abu Rafi`:I offered the `Isha' prayer behind Abu Huraira and he recited Idhas-Sama' Un-Shaqqat, and prostrated. I said, "What is this?" Abu Huraira said, "I prostrated behind Abul-Qasim and I will do the same till I meet him

Abu Rafi bataate hain: Maine Abu Huraira ke peechhe Isha padhi, unhone "Idhas-Sama' Un-Shaqqat" tilawat ki aur sajda kiya. Maine pucha: "Yeh kya hai?" Abu Huraira ne kaha: "Maine Abul-Qasim (Nabi) ke peechhe sajda kiya tha aur jab tak unse na miloon, karta rahunga."
